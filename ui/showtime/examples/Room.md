# Room

Three slow, blurred lights in `accent` and `accent-2` with a dark vignette. The background of the live screen.

Set `--accent` and `--accent-2` on the room (team colors, a recipe's main ingredient, a project color). They are registered properties, so changing them fades over one second. Inside a room the look is always dark.

Needs: `showtime.css` (or `bundle.css` plus `tokens.css`) and Archivo from Google Fonts.
