# Poster

2:3 cover cards on a sideways shelf, like a streaming app's row.

- `.st-shelf` gives a title row; `.st-rail` scrolls with snap.
- Each poster sets its own `--accent`. Put a photo in as an `<img>` and the gradient becomes the fallback.
- `.st-ranked` puts a huge outlined number behind the poster for top-10 shelves.

Needs: `showtime.css` (or `bundle.css` plus `tokens.css`) and Archivo from Google Fonts.
