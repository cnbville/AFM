# Button

Pill-soft buttons that grow a little on hover and press in on click.

- `.st-btn.primary`: the one main action on a screen. Filled with `fg`, text in `on-fg`.
- `.st-btn.glass`: everything else. A frosted `fill-2` that works over imagery.
- `.small` for toolbars, `.block` for full width in cards. `.st-icon-btn` is the round close or arrow.

One primary per view. Labels say what happens: "Start mock draft", not "Go".

Needs: `showtime.css` (or `bundle.css` plus `tokens.css`) and Archivo from Google Fonts.
