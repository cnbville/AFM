# Glass

A frosted panel over color. The signature surface of the live studio.

- Always put glass over something with color (a `.st-room`, a hero, a photo). Over flat `ground` it reads as a grey card.
- `.st-panel` adds padding and a header with a small right-aligned note.

Needs: `showtime.css` (or `bundle.css` plus `tokens.css`) and Archivo from Google Fonts.
