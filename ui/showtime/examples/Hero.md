# Hero

The cinematic opener: subject color light, a slow beam, a giant outlined number and a huge name.

- One hero per screen, at the top. Size it to content (`min-height` is a floor), never a full 100vh.
- The outlined number is the subject's rank, pick, jersey or step. It is the system's signature shape.
- Copy rises in with a short stagger. Text on the hero is always white.

Needs: `showtime.css` (or `bundle.css` plus `tokens.css`) and Archivo from Google Fonts.
