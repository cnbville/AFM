# Legacy data (update 2, lane 2)

- `coach_halls.csv`: the state high school coaches' halls of fame whose
  lines we found published, with the source for each (researched
  2026-10-25). `min_years` is years coaching, `in_state_years` years in
  the state, `min_head_years` years as head coach, `min_wins` head coach
  wins; `retired_rule` is `any`, `retired`, `retired_or_50` (or 50 years
  old), `retired_or_60`, `retired_or_30` (or 30 years coaching),
  `retired_15_active_30` (15 years retired, 30 active). Michigan's two
  lines are either-or; every other state's are all needed. States not
  listed use a proposed line (25 seasons, 150 head coach wins) that the
  screens mark "line proposed, to research".
- `greats.csv`: the reference list of real all-time greats from before the
  world's history (names and numbers only), shown beside the race.
