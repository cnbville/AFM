# The line bank: the fans (people topic 9, "Fan voices"; the fan lane
# 2026-10-30, then lane 13f). Format in src/world/folk/voice.rs. The places
# are in src/world/folk/fans.rs: the town after your game, the call-in show
# and the message board on the News page, and the crowd on game day.
#
# Who speaks: a fan carries `fan`, his level (hs, college, nfl), what he is
# (ticket: a season-ticket holder; student; alum; parent; booster; local),
# young (under 30) or old (60 and up), she, hot or calm, homer (always
# sees the bright side) or doomer (always the dark one), his region and
# era (pre2000, pre2010, social, modern, nil). The radio host is `host`; the
# crowd in the stands is `crowd` with its level, region and era.
#
# Facts every fan line can use: {team} how the fans say their own team
# (the Bills, Alabama, Lincoln), {mascot}, {town}, {year}, {color} (some
# colleges), {coach} (Coach Hale) and {coach_last} (Hale), mood=high, good,
# mixed, low or angry, {record} once a game is played, {streak} (three
# straight) with run=won or lost from three in a row.
# After a game: {them}, {score} (ours first), result=win, loss or tie,
# margin=big (17+), close (7 or less) or mid, at=home or away, rival=yes,
# upset=won or lost (against the line), {qb}, {star} (our best player that
# day), {goat} with cost=pick, fumble, miss or drop (our costliest
# mistake), defense=good (held them to 10) or bad (gave up 28). {next} is the next opponent inside two weeks.
# Recruiting: {player} the recruit, move=commit, decommit or sign, and on a
# high school's side {other} the college. The draft: draft=before or after,
# {player} the pick, and on a college's side {other} the club.
# Names in {qb}, {star}, {goat} and {player} are last names, linked.
#
# The subjects (fans.<subject>) are said in every place; place=town, radio
# or board marks a version for one place (a board post is written, short).
