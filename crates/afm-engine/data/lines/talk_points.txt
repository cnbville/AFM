# The line bank: one-on-ones on his talking points (talk facts lane L4,
# item 6; design in /afm/talk-facts/proposal.md, section 8). Written by hand
# for the most common parts; the bank run (lane L12) fills the rest. A part
# with no lines falls back to today's line for the move.
#
# talk.point.open.<point>.<stance>: he opens on the point, in his stance.
# talk.point.open.good.<stance>: any good news with no part of its own
# (an award, a job won, a big game, coming back): it names nothing.
# talk.point.ask.<point>: his question to you.
# talk.point.again: he hears praise for the same thing a second time.
#
# Facts: the player's (name, full, coach, you, pos, team, friend, gone),
# the point's holes and checks (talk_points.rs, claims/player.rs), stance,
# went (good, flat, bad) on a reply. A line that denies or admits his own
# conduct outright carries blameless=yes or at_fault=yes: the game sets them
# only when the world backs the line (an open case sets neither, unless he
# owns it and did it, or denies it and didn't).
