# Research area 5 (in progress): high school playoff formats by state

Started 2026-10-14 for open realism question Q2 (`docs/handoff/open-questions.md`): use each state's real playoff format per classification (field size, rounds, how teams qualify, private-school rules). **In progress: 10 of 51 states so far.** All figures are **[published]** (association pages and news, linked). Where a source left something out, it says so.

`data/real-world/hs_states.csv` already has classes, final dates and sites for every state (SI 2026 guide); this file adds how teams qualify.

Patterns so far: almost every state qualifies by district or region finish plus a computer rating (RPI-style formulas or MaxPreps rankings). Fields take roughly half to three quarters of teams. Several states recently split or limited private schools (AL, GA, IL multiplier, LA select/non-select).
