# Section 4: calendar additions (confirmed)

Status: **confirmed 2026-10-15.** Cain: "add the 46 and the 11 agreed topics that are already decided into one big build plan" (thread "Calendar immersion"). These 46 items add to `docs/design/calendar.md`; the build order is in `docs/design/calendar-build-plan.md`. Real dates marked "to check" get source-checked when building.

Scope: only what section 4 owns. Section 4 is the clock and the frame of the year. It decides **when** something happens, **what is allowed** that day, **what needs you**, and **how loud** it is. How a thing works inside (a contract, a voter, a personality) belongs to its own section; those are listed at the end.

Bundles hold small items of one kind; each part is named.

## A. NFL dates missing from the spec

1. **Roster bonus due dates.** Real teams cut players just before a roster bonus comes due early in the league year (to check: commonly days 3 to 5). Game: contracts can carry a dated roster bonus, and the calendar marks the day it comes due, so cuts cluster before it for a reason.
2. **The June 1 release date.** Cuts after June 1 spread dead money over two years, so some teams wait. Game: June 1 goes on the calendar as the date that changes the cap cost of a release, plus the real "post-June 1 designation" (a cut made earlier that counts as after June 1, a limited number per team, to check).
3. **Workout bonus attendance dates.** Many veterans attend "voluntary" OTAs to earn a workout bonus that requires a share of sessions. Game: the OTA days count attendance per player, so attendance has a real money reason.
4. **The camp report date as the holdout line.** A player who doesn't report to camp is holding out, with daily CBA fines (to check: about $50k a day, not waivable for some contracts). Game: report day is the date a holdout starts, and fines accrue each day after it.
5. **Hold-in days.** Modern players often report but refuse to practice. Game: from report day, a reported player can sit out practice while under contract dispute, with the real rules on fines for that (to check).
6. **The RFA offer sheet deadline.** Restricted free agents can sign offer sheets until a set April date (to check). Game: a dated deadline, after which the RFA's tender stands.
7. **Per-game roster bonus game days.** Some deals pay per game on the active roster. Game: each game day is the day that bonus is earned or lost, so inactive lists cost or save money on that day.
8. **Incentive cut-off at week 18.** Incentives are judged on the season's final stats. Game: the last regular-season game is the deadline for every incentive, so late-season snap and target calls have a dated stake.
9. **The college all-star games.** Late January, the first time prospects work under NFL coaches. Game: each game's practice week and game on the calendar, with its own invite list from the world; scouts can attend.
   - The Senior Bowl (Mobile, NFL staff coach the teams, to check the current set-up).
   - The East-West Shrine Bowl.
   - The HBCU Legacy Bowl.
   - The NFLPA Collegiate Bowl.
10. **Combine invite list day.** The invite list (about 330, to check) comes out in early February. Game: a dated news item; not being invited is a real setback a prospect and his agent react to.
11. **Combine week as the agents' meeting place.** Agents and teams meet in Indianapolis that week and free agency deals often take shape there (illegal to finalize, common in practice). Game: combine week is a window where whispers about upcoming free agency and trades appear.
12. **Pro day schedule per school.** Each school holds its pro day on its own date in March and early April. Game: every program gets a pro day date; scouts can't be at two at once.
13. **Top-30 visit window.** Teams' 30 official visits run through March and April, ending before the draft (to check the cut-off). Game: a dated window with the real limit.
14. **The rookie program in June.** Game: the dated league rookie program, which removes rookies from team activity for its days.
15. **NFL awards and Hall of Fame dates.** Game: each on its real date, as news days and ceremonies.
   - Pro Bowl fan voting window, November to December (to check); prime-time games inside it are seen by more voters.
   - All-Pro announcement in January.
   - NFL Honors, the Thursday before the Super Bowl.
   - Hall of Fame finalists in January (to check), the class announced at NFL Honors.
   - Hall of Fame enshrinement in Canton in early August, the weekend of the Hall of Fame Game.
16. **Ring ceremony.** The Super Bowl champion's ring night, usually in June. Game: a dated team event.
17. **Season ticket renewal deadline.** Teams set it in spring (dates vary by team). Game: a dated day per team for fan mood to show; the money side waits for full finances (section 11).

## B. College dates missing from the spec

18. **Poll and ranking releases.** Game: each on its real day; they feed the stakes test (item 41).
   - AP preseason poll, mid-August.
   - Weekly AP poll, every Sunday in season.
   - Weekly Coaches Poll (to check its day).
   - CFP committee rankings, Tuesdays from early November (to check the first date), last ranking on selection day.
19. **CFP selection day.** The Sunday after conference title games. Game: dated, already implied by the spec; made explicit as its own big day.
20. **Bowl selection day.** The same day, bowls fill under their conference tie-ins. Game: dated.
21. **Bowl practices.** Bowl teams get extra practices between the season and the bowl (to check: up to about 15). Game: a dated block for bowl teams only. It helps good teams, so it gets watched against the dynasty bar.
22. **Finals week.** Most schools hold fall finals in early-to-mid December, during bowl prep. Game: dated per school; practice time shrinks that week.
23. **Term-end academic eligibility check.** Grades and degree progress are checked after each term. Game: dated per school after fall and spring terms; a player can become ineligible for the bowl or for spring ball on that date.
24. **December graduation.** Game: dated per school; graduates gain the real graduate-transfer options.
25. **College awards dates.** Game: each on its real date, as news days and ceremonies.
   - Heisman finalists announced, early December (to check).
   - The Heisman ceremony, second Saturday of December (to check).
   - The college awards show, mid-December, most position awards.
   - All-America teams, December, several selectors on their own days (to check).
26. **Conference media days.** July. Game: dated per conference; preseason predictions released there.
27. **Marked college games.** Game: the calendar marks each one.
   - Senior day, each school's last home game.
   - Homecoming, one home game each school picks in the fall.
28. **Rivalry week.** Already in the spec; made explicit as the week rivalry games are played, with each rivalry's trophy and all-time record attached.

## C. High school dates missing from the spec

29. **Grade eligibility checks per state.** States check grades each grading period (Texas "no pass, no play", to check: every six weeks). Game: dated per state; a player can be ineligible for the next games, including playoffs.
30. **SAT and ACT test dates.** Fixed national dates. Game: dated; a recruit's scores arrive on them.
31. **Marked high school games.** Game: the calendar marks each one.
   - Homecoming, one home game per school.
   - Senior night, the last home game per school.
32. **All-state teams announced.** After each state's season. Game: dated per state.
33. **State media polls.** Weekly in many states. Game: dated weekly release per state.

## D. Weather from the date and the place

34. **Daily weather per town from real climate.** Game: each town's weather on each day comes from its real climate norms for that date, so late-season northern cold and September southern heat come from the calendar.
35. **Heat index on practice days.** August camp is run in heat. Game: each practice day gets a heat reading from item 34.
36. **State heat rules for high school practice.** Some states restrict practice by heat measures (for example Georgia, to check). Game: on hot days those rules cut or move practice.
37. **Lightning delays.** Lower levels stop play for 30 minutes after each strike nearby (to check the rule per level). Game: storms on game day can delay a game by the real rule.
38. **Weather postponements and relocations.** Real games have been moved for snow and hurricanes (the 2022 Bills home game moved to Detroit). Game: rare severe weather moves or postpones a game under each level's real rules.
39. **High school forfeits and no contests.** A cancelled high school game becomes a forfeit or a no contest by state rules, which counts in playoff points. Game: follows each state's rule.

## E. Which games count as big (topic 8's stakes test)

40. **Storyline tags on games.** Game: each tag is attached to a game from the hidden history and people, and counts toward the stakes test.
   - Revenge game: a player facing the team that cut or traded him.
   - Coach's return: a coach facing his former team.
   - Hometown game: a player playing near home with family attending.
   - Rivalry: with the rivalry's trophy and all-time record.
   - Milestone: a career or season record within reach in that game.
41. **Poll stakes.** A college game between ranked teams or with a playoff spot on the line. Game: counts toward the stakes test.
42. **"On this day" anniversaries.** The 30 years of history give dated memories ("ten years ago today..."). Game: a news line on the anniversary, never a stakes factor.

## F. Timing for other sections' events

43. **Personal events.** Players' own lives land on realistic dates. Game: the calendar gives each its window and real absences; what the events are and what they do belongs to section 6 (and 6b for the throwing sessions).
   - A game missed for the birth of a child.
   - A game missed for a funeral.
   - Weddings in the offseason.
   - Player-run summer throwing sessions (quarterbacks gathering receivers).
   - Youth camps and charity events in summer.
44. **Owner meetings.** Game: dated meetings; how goals are set and judged is section 6.
   - Preseason meeting, where goals are set.
   - Bye-week check-in.
   - End-of-season review, the day before Black Monday.
45. **Betting market releases.** Game: dated releases; how the market sets numbers is a later mechanic.
   - Preseason win totals, in spring after the schedule release (to check).
   - Weekly lines, opening after the previous week's games.
46. **Whisper windows.** Game: windows when whispers become more frequent; how deception works is section 7.
   - Trade rumors, the two weeks before the trade deadline.
   - Draft smokescreens, April, when teams leak false interest.
