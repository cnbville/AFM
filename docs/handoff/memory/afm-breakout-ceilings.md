Decided 2026-10-10: every attribute has a hidden ceiling, but ceilings are not walls (Raichel hated FM strikers scoring 35 goals with capped finishing). Production above what the ratings predict, sustained over enough snaps, raises the ceilings behind it. A proven breakout can lift a ceiling by up to about 10 points a year, past the normal 5-point limit. It can happen at any age, by smaller amounts as players get older.

**Why:** realism. Production that keeps beating the ratings means the ratings were wrong.
**How to apply:** The match engine must output per-player expected-vs-actual metrics that the development step consumes. This overrides the season calendar doc's "one band per checkpoint" rule for proven breakouts. Full list: /mnt/project-files/design/player-model-decisions.md.
