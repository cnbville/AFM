Trial 3 (2026-10-10) answered Raichel's notes "too easy to be 99 rated HS and NCAA" and "D2 rarely or never gets drafted (UDFA), and if they do it's day 3 max". Researched benchmarks (verified by a second agent):
- Non-D1 (D2/D3/NAIA) picks: 4.4/yr 2010-25, 0 in 2025 and 2026, 83% on day 3, none in round 1. D2 UDFAs about 10-20/yr.
- FCS direct picks: 5.8% of picks 2010-25, about 10/yr in the 2020s, 79% on day 3. FCS-background incl. movers 12-15/yr.
- Portal up-moves: FCS>FBS 300-500/yr, D2>FBS 130-165/yr. D2 starters who moved up went in round 3 in 2024, 2025 and 2026 (Puni, TeSlaa, Hurst).
- EA College Football: 0, 0, 1 players at 99 at launch (CFB 25/26/27), about 10 at 95+, about 100 at 90+ of ~11,000; typical FBS player low 70s. On3: 5-star draft rate about 63%.
- Real draft round ends (2025): 32, 64, 102, 138, 176, 216, 257.

Sim lands near all of these; 5-star draft rate runs 70-79% (high).
