2026-10-11: Raichel answered trial 3's question 1 with A: a five-star must dominate high school, and a five-star bust can only start once he reaches FBS (injury, trouble, stalled body). They also asked for a sanity check of realism and logic; a multi-agent review found ~24 issues.

Trial 4 built (uncommitted, see [[afm-trial2-unpushed]]): stars from three averaged film reports with position-aligned ranks, offers in star order, position-relative level ratings (fixed as a bug; Raichel may still prefer one scale), college suspensions/dismissals (~2.6% of college seasons), stalled bodies, per-team draft boards graded once. Four-seed results: lowest five-star L87-90 among seniors, 0 of the top 500 seniors unranked, 14-25 blue-chip QBs a class. Still off: five-stars drafted 67-94% (real ~63%) because HS players arrive at college nearly finished.

Seven calls for trial 5 are on the artifact page (https://claude.ai/artifact/HjXeV3d1q74zH9AmpVPiSP) and in /mnt/project-files/design/player-model-decisions.md: 1 college decides more (rawer arrivals), 2 early entry by projection, 3 pro cuts/contracts/free agency/position aging tuned so 85+ ~ Pro Bowl, 4 injuries cost games and ability, 5 depth charts with position slots, 6 positional value in the draft, 7 sideways/down transfers. Recommendation is A on each; none is decided yet.

Bug-fix round (2026-10-11, after Raichel asked which bugs were still open): every plain bug on /mnt/project-files/design/open-bugs-after-trial4.md fixed except two partial ones (about 35 of the true top 500 seniors are still two-stars from scouts' film error, tied to call 1; specialists have no draft list of their own, tied to call 5). Artifact is now version 5 with ten new careers; calls 1-7 still unanswered.
