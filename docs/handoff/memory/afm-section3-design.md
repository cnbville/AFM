Section 3 design runs in the thread "Let's move on to section 3" (started 2026-10-13). HS stats are covered in the stats database topic (Raichel asked, confirmed yes).

Topic 1, how a play is decided (CONFIRMED 2026-10-13): every play is simultaneous one-on-one battles (blocks, routes vs coverage, QB read vs coverage, runner vs tacklers) decided by attributes, body, fatigue, scheme fit and play call; battles chain (missed block -> pressure -> early throw); no flat outcome rolls. Every player tracked several times a second (Next Gen Stats style) so stats and 2D replay are honest. Randomness lives inside each battle, not a final luck roll. Notable plays keep their why.

Topic 2, movement and contact (proposal: physics movement from attributes+body, reaction from awareness, contact = momentum+leverage+strength+technique, in-game fatigue). Q1 vision CONFIRMED 2026-10-13: players react only to what they can see; play recognition / film IQ lets a player 'see' more, on offense and defense; still some luck and physics (a fallen player can't tackle). Goal: reward smart players over dumb or lazy ones. Work rate decides pursuit effort (proposed, not objected). Q2 CONFIRMED: tackles play out step by step (contact, churn, help arrives). Topic 3 play calls and schemes CONFIRMED 2026-10-13: plays are full assignments from formations; real schemes with player fit; playbook learning via IQ; AI coordinators call by situation from hidden ability; pre-snap checks. Q1: players freelance within a play (WR bails out QB on a scramble drill, DB jumps a route or breaks assignment) on BOTH sides, gated by IQ and by a new coach trait, Strictness (some coaches just distrust players; NOT hidden, visible, and coaches build a reputation for it), with dynamic per-player trust. Freelancing separates good players from system players and lets good players on bad teams show in stats. Q2: coordinators adjust in-game and at halftime; Raichel wants substantial real research and a play-calling database behind it (e.g. nflverse play-by-play) when built. Topic 4 penalties, momentum, game feel CONFIRMED 2026-10-13: penalties emerge from battles (holding when beaten, discipline, focus, crowd), checked vs real rates per level; officiating crews have tendencies; weather, surface, altitude, crowd feed battles. Momentum: small hidden per-player swing driven by existing composure/confidence/mental health traits (no new rating); scouts read it with noise from next-snap film, stat splits and interviews ('bounces back well'); stats DB keeps after-turnover and trailing splits. Clock management and 4th-down calls are a coaching skill built on real win-probability data; weak coaches err. Topic 5 level differences (proposal: real rules per level and state, gaps come from players not level modifiers, real roster sizes). Q1 CONFIRMED: HS head coach decides two-way players by talent and depth; fatigue and injury risk rise with snaps. Raichel raised player creation: code picks position first (player.rs generate_prospect); Claude proposed athlete-first generation, coach assigns position by fit and need, positions change over a career, real shares checked not forced. CONFIRMED 2026-10-13 after a SWOT: build it as its own step BEFORE the match engine (it re-tunes talent model, body by position, five-star/FCS calibration). Raichel's example: a WR with high play recognition but bad hands could become a 4.2 40-yard safety. Q2 CONFIRMED: HS mostly simpler schemes from coach background, BUT also linked to coach level/ability and the players available (no 3-4 Okie with bad edges and great DTs); better coaches fit scheme to personnel. Topic 6 injuries and subs CONFIRMED 2026-10-13: injuries from play physics (force, angle, turf, fatigue, proneness) with real injury data per position; hurt players play on with lower ratings; concussion protocol per level; real rotations and packages. Playing through pain is the player's choice (toughness, stakes, coach pressure) with risk of worsening; serious injuries leave lasting marks (ACL speed loss, re-injury risk) varied by player. Topic 7 special teams CONFIRMED 2026-10-13: real ball flight (leg, accuracy, wind, weather, altitude); snap, hold and protection are battles; punts with hang time, gunners vs jammers, returner judgment; returns are full plays; real kickoff rules per level; fakes/onside are calls by coach aggressiveness. Long snappers and holders are real rated players; pressure kicks depend on composure. CORRECTED by Raichel: for kickers and punters the mental side is BIG, not small (anyone can kick 50; the mental part separates the best). Kickers/punters get specialization profiles by distance: e.g. one lethal inside 30 but lacking power, another blasting from 55 but inconsistent from 20-40. Topic 8 stats database (full proposal /mnt/project-files/design/section3-topic8-stats-proposal.md; it's about logging/storage, GUI is section 10). CONFIRMED 2026-10-13: one play log per play feeds every stat; classic+advanced+grades+splits+coach stats; one career record across levels with percentiles vs level/state/schedule and era adjustment; feeds coach buzz, gem flags, awards, records, HoF. Q1: keep player stats forever; full replay tracking only for own games and notable plays. Raichel assumed washouts (no college, no pro, cut after UDFA/camps) get deleted; Raichel then said 'basically as much logging as possible': rule = log and keep as much as the game can handle; trim only what save size/speed truly requires (washouts' game-level detail and old HS play logs first; their season totals, awards, records stay). Topic 9 watching and calling CONFIRMED 2026-10-13: every game in the world runs the full engine in parallel; own game ~15 min in 2D with chosen highlight level and NGS overlays; between-play changes and halftime notes. Live calling: as HC you can take over any play, or let OC/DC pick; their pick is shown visualised and you accept or deny it; call quality depends on coach quality and on real researched play-vs-counter knowledge. Other games: no live watching, but any game can be replayed in 2D afterwards (deterministic seed re-run, so no stored tracking needed). Leave room for better visuals later (3D could render the same tracking data). Topic 10 calibration: checks are a development safeguard only, never an in-game nudge; fix causes in the engine. Era change emerges from coaches adopting what works via coaching trees, checked vs real trends (proposed). Raichel insists outliers must happen (e.g. Derrick Henry's 153 HS touchdowns); Claude proposed tests also check the extremes (best seasons/careers near real records, not capped). CONFIRMED 2026-10-13 ('as long as it's in development'): 2021-25 real targets, emergent era change, automatic tests on every engine change, development-only. ALL 10 TOPICS DONE; next a separate thread writes the spec in docs/design/ from this thread, keeping Raichel's wording. Build order: athlete-first player generation first, then the match engine. Q2: HS stats as reported, but mild distortion only: mostly accurate, a minority late/missing/padded, so scouts don't keep finding 'lies'.

Raichel's rule (2026-10-13): always match ultra realism unless it breaks the game or makes it impossible.
