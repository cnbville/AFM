ALL 11 TOPICS DONE 2026-10-14; next a separate thread writes the spec to docs/design/ from this thread. Section 4 design ran in the thread "Let's work section 4 in the mean time" (started 2026-10-13). Old draft: docs/design/season-calendar-and-pacing.md (answers here override it).

Topics: 1 clock, 2 NFL, 3 college, 4 HS, 5 recruiting, 6 week, 7 training slots, 8 pacing, 9 waits, 10 staff/time controls, 11 rule changes.

Topic 1 clock (CONFIRMED 2026-10-13): one-day ticks; one real clock for all levels (HS Fri, college Sat, NFL Sun + Thu/Mon/holiday games); dates derived yearly from real anchors; save starts day after Super Bowl. Big days run hour by hour (FA open, trade deadline, cutdowns, draft, UDFA rush).

Topic 2 NFL year (CONFIRMED 2026-10-14): real CBA calendar. CBA offseason limits bind your training: yes. Raichel wants room for a creative way to see and monitor players' offseason work (own workouts, trainers, conditioning); design it later (likely 6b), leave room in the calendar. Year one uses the real 2026 schedule, later years the real opponent formula: yes.

Verified dates (2026-10-14): portal Jan 2-16, CFP teams +5 days after season, coach-change 15 days from NEW coach hire. NFL: soft deadline Jan 6, hard Jan 15, CFP title players ~Jan 24 +72h revoke, opt-out deadline Feb 7. Unverified: NCAA eligibility-return withdrawal deadline. Verify dates with sources before stating them (Raichel asked).
Topic 3 portal (CONFIRMED 2026-10-14, Raichel asked for event-driven portal 2026-10-14): season-long hidden lean from events (snaps, 5-star signed at position in Dec, HC/position coach change, scheme fit, NIL drop/tampering via handlers, family/life, draft grade, team results); redshirt-after-4-games exits; stars enter day 1 with deals, fringe wait, portal regret; personality weights events. Whispers on a player's lean: YES, part of the game like locker room vibe. Coaches' existing recruiting skill (and player relationships) sway portal outcomes both ways: pulling transfers in and talking own players into staying.
Topic 3 college year CONFIRMED 2026-10-14: real NCAA calendars per division (FBS/FCS/D2/D3/NAIA/JUCO); one combined January choice per player (draft/portal/stay) weighed from grade, NIL, snaps, degree, personality; bowl opt-outs from draft grade, personality, stakes.

Topic 4 HS year (CONFIRMED 2026-10-14): per-state calendars from hs_states.csv + start dates, game counts, spring football, summer dead periods, all-star games.
Topic 4 answers: spring ball = breakout stage for true freshmen, redshirt freshmen and sophomores (no older players), in BOTH college spring practice and HS spring ball: right combination (work ethic, coachability, position coach, open reps, body maturing, ceiling headroom) can give a sudden real jump; notices first ('if X keeps up x and y he'll likely make huge steps'). Independents like IMG yes, but CAREFUL with pull (NCAA dynasty calibration was hard): few such schools, tuition/boarding/roster limits cap pull, no extra effect on college choice beyond exposure.
Topic 5 recruiting (CONFIRMED 2026-10-14): real FBS recruiting calendar (2025-26 per texasfootball.com: quiet Mar 2-Apr 14, contact Apr 15-30, dead May 24-27, quiet/camps/official visits May 28-Jun 21, dead Jun 22-Jul 31); official visits from Apr 1 junior year; early signing Dec + Feb. Commits/decommits/flips event-driven like portal lean: yes. Commit timing by personality and family: yes. Added by Raichel: committed and local kids allow more contact (HS practice visits in evaluation weeks, game-day/unofficial visits, family at games, unlimited contact after signing, committed kids recruit peers). Raichel: build these as full recruiting actions AI college staffs use, through the same actions a human could use, because a playable NCAA coach career may come later.
