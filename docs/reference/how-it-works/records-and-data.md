## Quick answers

- Where are the record books? `w.stats.books`, keyed by (scope, book): a college division, a conference, a state's high schools or the NFL, and a list kind (`world/records.rs`, `Scope`, `Book`).
- How deep is each list? A division's, the NFL's and a state's best 25; a conference's 10 (`world/records.rs`, `DIVISION_TOP`, `CONFERENCE_TOP`).
- What does a team's own book keep? Career, season and single-game marks in the 13 record stats: a college program's and an NFL club's best 10, a high school's best 3 (`world/marks.rs`, `Tier::team_top`, `Marks`).
- Do bowls and playoffs count? College counts its bowls and playoff games, high school its playoffs; the NFL counts the regular season only (`world/marks.rs`, module notes; `nfl_game` skips once `NflSeason::booked`).
- When do single-game marks go in? After every engine game: college in `world/college.rs` (after the streaks), the NFL in `world/pro/games.rs`, `record`, high school in `world/hs_year.rs`'s game loop (`world/marks.rs`, `college_game`, `nfl_game`, `hs_game`). A neutral-site game (a bowl, an international game) reads "vs" for both teams (each hook's `neutral`).
- What is the era column? 100 is what an average regular at his position and level would have done in the same games: his number over the season's per-game norm times his games (`world/marks.rs`, `era`; college's norms `w.stats.norms`, the NFL's and the states' `w.marks.norms`, `norms_of`). A career that began before the store's first season, or has a season with no norm, shows none (`nfl_close`; `world/hs_year.rs`, `file_lines`).
- Who counts as a regular for the norms? NFL: 8+ games and 15+ snaps a game; high school: 5+ games and 15+ snaps a game (`world/marks.rs`, `norms_of`, `nfl_close`, `hs_norms`).
- What are the rate minimums? NFL seasons: 14 attempts, 6.25 carries, 1.875 catches and one field goal try a team game; careers 1,500 attempts, 750 carries, 200 catches, 100 field goals. College and high school seasons use 15 attempts a game and the NFL's other per-game marks, over the team's games with bowls and playoffs; the NFL's season length comes from the rule book of that year (`w.changes.rules(year).nfl_games`, `season_games`); careers 325 completions, 300 carries, 100 catches, 40 field goal tries (high school half). The college and high school numbers are proposed, not confirmed (`world/marks.rs`, `season_qualifies`, `career_qualifies`). The Leaderboards count the same NFL minimums per team game, with 2.5 punts, the most games any club has played; when that count is gone (a season's table not kept) his own games count (minor bug 222, B38; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_core/tables.rs`, `qualifies`, `nfl_team_games`).
- Does a player's Stats tab show a college year without games? Yes, from his college coaches' book (`Person::college_staff`, one row a season on a roster): a redshirt year reads "Redshirt year: practiced, no games", a year he missed games and played none "Did not play: hurt", any other "Did not play". Only for a year the stat book kept lines for at that program's level, so a history played on the stand-in shows no such rows (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_core/pages.rs`, `seasons`).
- Is college's passer rating the NFL's? No: the rate book uses the NCAA's passing efficiency (`world/analytics.rs`, `passing_efficiency`; `world/marks.rs`, `rate`).
- When does a record count as fallen? When a game takes first place from someone else, or a season or career running total passes the holder on the day, once a level's books have five seasons behind them (counted from the first season that filed anything); each list and player is told once a season (`world/marks.rs`, `tell`, `check_running`, `Marks::established`).
- Who is passed? The holder's total today: his stored total plus his running season when he still plays there. A record already broken this season falls again only to someone past the best mark told, and he is named as the one passed (`world/marks.rs`, `holder_run`, `Marks::best_told`, `retell`).
- Are a high school's own fallen records told? No, only the state's (`world/marks.rs`, `game_marks`, `check_running`).
- How long are fallen records kept? This season and last (`world/marks.rs`, `end_season`, `hs_new_season`).
- What does "chasing now" show? This season's paces at least three games in that reach nine tenths of the season record, and careers of men still playing at that level within a quarter of the career record (proposed, not confirmed; `world/marks.rs`, `chasing`, `active_in`). A level's career lists drop the "still being played" mark at each close for men who left (`refresh_live_lists`).
- Where do a departed player's names come from? The people still in the world, then the store's own names (kept before `World::compact` drops anyone a team book or analytics row still needs), then the stat book's honors (`world/marks.rs`, `name_of`, `keep_names`).
- Does any of this change the games? No. The store only reads games and seasons; nothing in the simulation reads it (`world/marks.rs`, module notes).
- What does hovering a stat's name show? Its plain-words card (Cain, 2026-11-03): what it is, how the game works it out and which way is better; on Player analytics also the middle value among the season's qualifiers. One entry per stat in `crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/glossary.rs` (`TERMS`, about 360 with lane NB7's team numbers), sent once by `stat_glossary`. A page that knows the stat marks it by key (Player analytics' columns and card, Leaderboards' columns, Stat history's lists, chips and leader column); any other table header is found by its text under the table's first header, and a game's team totals by the row's first cell (`HEADS`; `app/ui/screens/help.js`, H8, `hpStatHeads`, `hpStatHtml`). It follows the Hover cards setting.
- What counts as an explosive run? 10 yards or more, the common analytics cutoff, on the game line, Leaderboards' "Exp" and Player analytics' "10+%"; the labels said 15 until minor bug 181 (`game/stats.rs`, `explosive_runs`).
- Does EPA a dropback count scrambles? Yes: EPA on his throws, sacks and scrambles over dropbacks. The stat book keeps scramble EPA as its own stat (`Stat::ScrambleEpa`, filled from `StatLine::scramble_epa`); before minor bug 183 scrambles counted as dropbacks but their EPA was left out (`world/analytics.rs`, `Cm::EpaDb`; `game/stats.rs`; `world/records.rs`, `from_line`).
- How deep is my charting and how soon does it come? Your analytics head's rating sets both; the NFL's tracking is full depth the morning after for every seat. The details are in [tallies.md](tallies.md), "Charting depth and delay" (`world/analytics.rs`, `charting`).
- What is PD in a box score? Passes defended: interceptions plus passes broken up, as the NFL and NCAA count them; it was passes broken up only until minor bug 182 (`crates/afm-game/src/views.rs`, the game page's defense rows).

## The level books

- College: season, career and streak lists per division since before lane L20; single games, rate seasons and rate careers added (`world/records.rs`, `record_books`; `world/marks.rs`, `college_game`, `college_close`, `season_rates`, `career_rates`).
- Conferences: seasons, single games and rate seasons, no careers (`world/marks.rs`, `college_scopes`, `college_close`).
- The NFL: seasons, careers and single games of the regular season, rate records, each year's leader and runner-up, filled at Black Monday after the league's own record book (`world/pro/honors.rs`, `season_records`; `world/marks.rs`, `nfl_close`).
- High school: each state's season and career lists (seasons in the state), single games, rate records and each year's leader, filed when the state's season ends (`world/hs_year.rs`, `file_lines`; `world/marks.rs`, `hs_close`).
- An entry keeps its value, holder, team, season (a career's first and last), games, position, era and, for a single game, the date, opponent, home or away and score (`world/records.rs`, `RecordEntry`, `GameRef`).
- Entries filed before lane L20 carry no games, position or era; a state's old entries have the school in the name and no team.

## Team books

- Kept per team in `w.marks.college`, `nfl` and `hs`, each sorted by list (kind and stat), best first (`world/marks.rs`, `Mark`, `team_list`, `place_mark`).
- A career mark is his total with that team; a career still being played is marked live, and the mark clears when he leaves (`world/marks.rs`, `refresh_live`, `refresh_live_schools`).
- An NFL club's season and career marks come from the games themselves, so a man traded in the season has a line with each club (`world/marks.rs`, `Marks::club_season`, filled in `nfl_game`); games the store never saw, in a file loaded mid-season, go to his last club. Career totals per player and club are in `club_careers`, the first season there in `club_first`; they go when he leaves football (`nfl_close`, `keep_names`).
- A career mark's game count has no cap: it is packed with the first season in `Mark::aux` (`career_aux`, `Mark::game_count`, `Mark::first_year`).

## Leaders

- College's leader and runner-up each year are read from the stat book, which keeps every season (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/books.rs`, `leaders`).
- The NFL's and each state's are kept in `w.marks.leaders`, since those seasons' lines don't all stay in the world (`world/marks.rs`, `leaders_of`).

## Analytics

- The position groups and their cards: QB, RB, WR, TE, OL, DL, EDGE, LB, CB, S, K, P; the long snapper has none (`world/analytics.rs`, `GROUPS`, `group_of`).
- Two cards per group: the charted one (EPA, CPOE, pressures, separation, trench wins, coverage) and the box score one (`world/analytics.rs`, `charted`, `boxed`).
- Who sees the charted card: every seat for the NFL (league-wide tracking), a college seat for its own division, an NFL seat for FBS and FCS; high school is box scores everywhere. The NFL seat's college charting and every seat's NFL tracking are proposed, not confirmed (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/mod.rs`, `charted`, `sources`).
- How much of the charted card you see, and when this season's games reach it, follows your analytics head (lane NB1): numbers past your depth are left out, and games not charted yet are left out of this season's lines (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/analytics.rs`, `card_of`, `charted_now`; [tallies.md](tallies.md)).
- A season qualifies on its group's volume: dropbacks, carries, routes, pass-block snaps, pass rushes, defensive snaps, coverage snaps, field goal tries, punts; the default is about two thirds of a starter's (NFL 1.35 times), cut to the share of the season played while it runs (`world/analytics.rs`, `default_min`; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/analytics.rs`, `min_for`).
- Percentiles are among that season's qualifiers at the level (a division, the NFL, a state), turned so 100 is always the best (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/analytics.rs`, `percentiles`).
- Can I see only freshmen or seniors? Yes: the table filters by class, redshirt and age (Cain, 2026-11-02). College class is Fr to Sr by seasons of eligibility used before that season, plus that one; a year at school that didn't count (no games, or four or fewer from 2018) makes him a redshirt from then on, and under the five-year model (Division I from 2026) every year counts. High school goes by grade from his class; the NFL by year in the league (rookie to 5th and more). Age is on 31 August of the season, from his birth date (from his class when the world has none). A player the world no longer knows (dropped from people and alumni) has no class, so a filter leaves him out (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/analytics.rs`, `Filter`, `class_in`, `age_in`, `passes`).
- Does the filter change the percentiles? No: they stay among every qualifier, so a freshman's shade says where he stands in the whole field; the table says how many of the qualifiers match (`table`, `qualified`).
- How is CPOE worked out? Completions less expected completions, over attempts (throwaways count in attempts only). Each throw's expected completion comes from its level's own model, fitted on the engine's throws by `xcomp_fit`: a depth band (behind the line, 0-4, 5-9, 10-14, 15-19, 20-29, 30-39, 40+ air yards), a separation band (under half a yard, an interception counting as none; half a yard to one; one to one and a half; more) and pressure. So a level's CPOE centres on zero, as real models fitted on their own league do. Before minor bug 178 one hand-set model for every level put every passer 12 to 24 points under zero (`game/stats.rs`, `XCOMP`, `xcomp_features`, `expected_completion_at`; `GameStats::level`; `crates/afm-tools/src/bin/xcomp_fit.rs`).
- Grade is the game's own 0-100 grade from the charting, as the game pages show it (`world/analytics.rs`, `Cm::value`; `game/stats.rs`, `StatLine::grade`).
- A card opens on the table's season, division or state, and its group follows the position he was listed at that season (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/analytics.rs`, `card`, `season_pos`, `now_in`).
- The NFL keeps each regular season's charted card values for every player in `w.marks.pro_rows`, so past seasons outlive the players (`world/marks.rs`, `ProRow`, `nfl_close`).
- Seasons like his: every other season's qualifiers at the level, each read against its own field; closeness is the mean gap in percentiles over the numbers both have (at least half the card), shown as 100 minus the gap; the best five, one season per player (proposed, not confirmed; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/analytics.rs`, `card`).

## The position cards and the player's Data tab (lane NB4)

- Each group's charted card has views: the overview (the card above) first, then its position's depth cards. QB: accuracy and depth, pressure and blitz, play action and scrambles, grades, by zone. RB: running, against the box, receiving and usage, blocking, grades. WR and TE: routes and separation, where he lines up, blocking, grades, by zone. OL: pass protection, discipline and spots, grades. DL and EDGE: pass rush, rush moves, run defense, grades. LB: coverage, rush and blitz, run defense, where he lines up, grades, by zone. CB and S: coverage, run support and blitz, where he lines up, grades, by zone. K: kicking. P: punting (`world/analytics.rs`, `views`).
- Returners are their own group (RET): anyone with a kick or punt return, whatever his position, qualifying on returns (`GROUPS`, `RET`, `volume`; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/analytics.rs`, `pool`).
- The new numbers read the players tally's depth counts, so they begin with the first tallied season; older seasons show them blank (`Cm::deep`, `Cm::value_d`; [tallies.md](tallies.md), "The depth counts").
- Formulas follow their sources: PFF's pass-blocking efficiency (100 - (sacks + 0.75 x (hits + hurries)) / pass-block snaps x 100), pass-rush productivity ((sacks + 0.75 x (hits + hurries)) / pass rushes x 100), elusive rating (missed tackles forced a touch x yards after contact a carry x 100) and breakaway % (yards on runs of 15+ over all rushing yards); Pro Football Reference's ANY/A ((yards + 20 TD - 45 INT - sack yards) / (attempts + sacks)); Next Gen Stats' aggressiveness (throws into under a yard of separation) and completed air yards (`Cm::value_d`).
- Rush yards over expected and YAC over expected: each run's and catch's expected yards come from the level's own model, fitted on the engine's plays by `xyards_fit` (least squares). Runs read men in the box (6, 7, 8+), inside or outside, ten yards or less to the goal, 11-20, and two or less to go; catches read behind the line, 10-19 and 20+ air yards, separation (to five yards), man coverage and the middle third. On 600 games a level the fit matches every level's average (NFL 4.19 yards a carry, the fit 4.17; YAC 4.75 both; refitted after lane NB5's passing changes and again after minor bugs batch 9 on 2026-11-07). The features are proposed, not confirmed (`world/tally/expect.rs`, `XRUSH`, `XYAC`; `crates/afm-tools/src/bin/xyards_fit.rs`).
- Get-off is the seconds from the snap to a rusher's first contact with a blocker (the engine's stand-in for NGS's time to cross the line); time to pressure is the sim's time of his pressure, else the throw's time (proposed, not confirmed; `world/tally/depth.rs`, `blocks_and_rush`).
- Where he lines up comes from each snap's spots (`ORole` on offense, `DSlot` on defense). A safety counts in the box when the box had more men than the front, the strong safety first (proposed, not confirmed; `depth.rs`, `game`).
- Facet grades: the game's own per-play grades kept by facet (passing, running, receiving, pass blocking, run blocking, pass rush, run defense, coverage, tackling, special teams), each read 0-100 the way the overall grade is. Every graded play goes to one facet, so the facets add up to the overall grade. A tackle that stops a play goes to run defense on a run and coverage on a pass, other tackles to tackling; a penalty to the job he had that snap, a pre-snap foul to pass blocking or the pass rush (proposed, not confirmed; `game/stats.rs`, `FACETS`, `facet`, `book`).
- Level average (item 3): every column's hover gives the average among the season's qualifiers and the middle one, with which way is better; a number that only says how he plays (time to throw, where he lines up, how often he blitzes) has no better way, and its shade reads as plain percentile (`analytics.rs`, `columns`, `means`; `Cm::neutral`; `app/ui/screens/help.js`, `hpStatHtml`).
- Era + (item 12): a rate's index is 100 at the qualifiers' average that season and 10 a standard deviation, turned so higher is better, as Pro Football Reference's "+" stats read. Counts stay as they are. The scale is proposed, not confirmed (`analytics.rs`, `era_vals`).
- Opponent-adjusted (item 13): each adjustable number is read against the opponents he faced, from the season's games file: a man on offense against what offenses did to the defenses he faced, a man on defense against what the offenses he faced did to everyone. Each game weighs by his snaps on that side, and an opponent's season leaves out its games against his team. EPA, shares and success rates move by the gap between his opponents' rate and the level's; yardage rates scale by the level's over his opponents'. The level's rate is the average his field faced, so the field's average stays put. Raw sits under each adjusted number. The mapping and the method are proposed, not confirmed (`world/analytics.rs`, `opp_adjust`, `OppRate`; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/deep.rs`, `sched`, `Sched::faced`; `analytics.rs`, `adj_vals`).
- More filters (item 14): team, conference (the NFL's AFC or NFC), the spot he played most (tackle, guard, center, wide, slot, in line, backfield; edge, interior line, off-ball linebacker, outside corner, slot corner, deep safety, box safety), draft eligible next spring (three years out of high school), transfer (more than one school by then), recruiting stars and home state. Saved views keep the level, group, view, reading, minimum and filters in the browser's own settings, so they come back each visit (`analytics.rs`, `Filter`, `passes`, `spots_for`; `world/analytics.rs`, `SPOTS_O`, `SPOTS_D`, `spot_snaps`; `app/ui/screens/data.js`, `dtViewsBar`, `daviewsave`).
- The player page's Data tab (items 11, 15, 28; minor bug 44), one season at a time at college or the NFL: facet grades, where he lined up, the throw map by zone (4 depth rows by 3 thirds: attempts, completion %, yards, EPA a throw; as the passer, the target or the man covering, whichever he had most), run paths by gap, the route map, the pressure map by the blocker he beat, his splits (down, distance, field zone, quarter, two-minute, score, home, road, neutral, rain or snow, wind 15+ mph, 40 F or colder, indoors; college against ranked and unranked teams, the NFL by club and against playoff teams) and his games. Film-deep parts show only where your charting reaches film depth (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/deep.rs`, `player_data`; `app/ui/screens/data.js`, the Data tab).
- The Glossary page in Data lists every stat's plain words, how it's worked out and which way is better, with a search (`app/ui/screens/data.js`, `screens.glossary`; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/glossary.rs`).
- Separation at the catch point is summed on catches, so it is now divided by catches (it was divided by targets, which read it low); Sep/T is a new number over every target (`world/analytics.rs`, `Cm::Sep`; `world/records.rs`, `SeparationPerTarget`).

## Team data (lane NB7)

- Where? Team data (any team: its page's Go to line, or the Data section for yours), Stats by season (board 44) and Team leaderboards (with the Tiers and Power tabs), all in the Data section (`app/ui/screens/data.js`, `screens.teamdata`, `teamseasons`, `teamlead`; commands `team_data`, `team_seasons`, `team_leaders` in `crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/teams.rs`).
- What does the team's Data page show? Five tabs. Analytics: 28 numbers a side for offense and defense (a defense's are what it allowed), the whole team's turnover margin, expected margin and luck, penalties and time of possession, and special teams, each with its rank in its group and the group's average. Tendencies: run or pass in 21 situations against the level's expected pass rate, personnel, backfield, play action, motion, RPOs, no huddle, runs into loaded boxes; the defense's coverage shells and coverages, man rate, blitz by down, packages and box counts. Games: each game's result, yards, EPA a play and the offense's and defense's grades, then every man's snap share game by game. Roster: returning production, starters and starts back (NFL: snaps back), the roster's stars and blue-chip share (high school: regular starters and seniors). Power: the rating now with its rank, the preseason projection, home field, the coming games' win chances and the odds (`teams.rs`, `team_data`, `tendencies`, `games_list`, `back_json`, `team_power`).
- Who is it ranked against? Its college division that season, the NFL, or its state's high schools of its class (proposed, not confirmed; `teams.rs`, `group_of`).
- What follows the charting? Each number has a depth (`teams.rs`, `STATS`): box scores (points, yards, third downs, the red zone, turnovers, sacks, penalties, kicking), play-by-play (EPA, success, explosive plays, line yards, stuffs, havoc, scoring chances, average start, three and outs, pass rate over expected, expected turnovers, the calls by situation, snap shares) and film (pressure rate, personnel, backfield, motion, play action, coverages, blitzes, boxes, unit grades). A level your seat charts nothing at shows the box score version; high school is always box scores (`lane_data/mod.rs`, `charting`).
- Pass rate over expected: the offense's dropbacks less the expected dropbacks over its snaps, on nflfastR's neutral early downs (first and second down, a win chance from 20 to 80%, outside the last two minutes of a half), the expected rate from the level's own model ([tallies.md](tallies.md), "The expected pass model").
- Expected turnovers: Bill Connelly's, half of all fumbles and the level's own share of passes defensed that were picked off that season (`teams.rs`, `expected_to`, `level_numbers`).
- Havoc rate: tackles for loss (sacks too), forced fumbles, interceptions and passes broken up over snaps; an offense's is the havoc it suffered (`tally/games.rs`, `adv`).
- Unit grades: the snap-weighted 0-100 game grade of every man on the unit, each man counted on the side he played most that game (`teams.rs`, `games_list`; `game/stats.rs`, `StatLine::grade`).
- Returning production: Connelly's shares, his weights where he gives them (receiving 23.5% of the offense; passes defensed 33%, tackles 19%, tackles for loss 15% of the defense), the rest proposed, not confirmed: passing 42%, rushing 13% and the offense's starts 21.5%, the defense's starts 33% (`teams.rs`, `BACK_O`, `BACK_D`). Who is back: the roster today for the season being played, else every man with a stat book line or a start that season (`on_team`).
- Stats by season: one row a season with the record, the head coach and the unit's coordinator (none for special teams: the world keeps no special teams coordinator), seven numbers with their ranks, the talent (stars on that side; high school its seniors) and the starters back; per game or totals; the current coordinator's seasons highlighted (`teams.rs`, `team_seasons`, `BY_UNIT`).
- Team leaderboards: every team in a division, the NFL or a state ranked by any number at your depth, any counted season, offense or defense; Tiers plots offense against defense in EPA a play (points a game where your seat has box scores only) with each team's logo; Power ranks the power rating with the odds (`teams.rs`, `team_leaders`, `power_table`).
- The power rating: offense and defense in points against an average team, fitted on every score with the opponent and home field taken out, pulled toward the preseason projection by three games' worth; the projection reads last season's and the season before's ratings, the production back and the stars, its weights fitted on the world's last ten closed seasons (fallback: most of last season); the win chance reads the margin against the spread fitted on last season's games; the odds play the rest of the regular season 2,000 times (`world/power.rs`; `teams.rs`, `inputs`, `weights`, `power_season`). For high school no field is read: the states' own formulas pick it, so it shows a winning record's odds (proposed, not confirmed).
- Does any of it read a hidden rating? No: scores, the box, the charting, the stat book and the recruiting stars only.

## Game pages, situations and decisions (lane NB8)

- Any game in the books has a page (item 24): the score by quarter, the team stats and the leading lines for anyone; where your staff charts the game (the NFL always, a college game with a side in a division you chart), the five factors, the win chance chart and every 4th down and try against the analysts' model. Until your charting reaches the game, the page says when it comes in. High school games are box scores, and your own kept games link to their full page (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/tgame.rs`, `tally_game`, `charted`; `app/ui/screens/situations.js`, `screens.tgame`). The player Data tab's games link to it (`lane_data/deep.rs`, `games`).
- Your own game page's Advanced tab adds the five factors (success rate by EPA, explosiveness, field position, points a trip inside the 40, turnovers), havoc rate, line yards, stuffs, three-and-outs and post-game win expectancy, and weighs every 4th down and try on the same analysts' model the Decisions page uses (it used the engine coaches' own read) (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_gameday/page.rs`, `game_page`, `fourths`, `tries`; `app/ui/screens/gameday.js`, `Advanced`).
- Success rate on the five factors is EPA above zero (cfbfastR's), which post-game win expectancy was fitted on; the older "Success rate" row on your game page is the 40/60/100 yardage rule. Both are shown, each named (`tgame.rs`, `adv_box`).
- Average start on your game page was the opponent's side of the field (about 75 for a drive from your 25); it now reads the drive's start from its own goal (minor bug 259; `page.rs`, `advanced`).
- Situations (item 25), for your team, any team or the league: down and distance, field zones, the red zone (inside the 20 and goal to go, with trips, touchdowns, field goals and points a trip), the two-minute drill against the rest of the game and by quarter, the score (ahead 9+, within 8, behind 9+), garbage time and the numbers without it; with the ball or on defense; each number beside the level's average team (`lane_data/situ.rs`, `situations`).
- Decisions (item 26): a team's every 4th down and try with the model's win chance of each choice, its best, the cost and what happened; the model's own call in a tied game at 10:00 of the 2nd quarter by yards to go and spot, with how often the level (and the team) went for it there; each head coach's season against the model (went when it said go, agreed, win chance lost a game, two-point tries, challenges won) with the level's median; timeouts used, timeouts left in close losses, challenges and wrong rulings not challenged (`situ.rs`, `decisions`, `model_grid`, `coach_of`). A 4th down or try where every choice was within a point of the best (a decided game or a toss-up) reads "Even" instead of a call, and any choice agrees with it (`world/tally/calls.rs`, `Judge::even`). Rows run newest first, each game's snaps from its last back.
- Concepts against coverages (item 27): each concept's success rate, EPA or yards a play against each coverage, for the league, a team's offense (what it ran against what it saw) or its defense (what was run at it against what it played); faint under 20 snaps. Tiles: the coverage seen most, the best answer to it, the costliest pairing and the concept the level is copying (the biggest rise in share of snaps on last season). Your next opponent's coverages on all downs and 3rd down, with the level's best answer to each; your calls against what you saw (`situ.rs`, `concepts`, `best_answer`).
- What your seat sees: the charted levels only (the NFL's tracking, your own division in college, FBS and FCS for an NFL club); concepts need film charting (an analytics head of 8 or more). High school is your own film of your own games, nobody else's. This season's numbers leave out the games your staff hasn't charted yet (`situ.rs`, `scope`; teams `Season::charted_by`; calls `sums_of`). No hidden rating shows.

## Trends, Compare, Career comps and the Stat finder (lane NB10)

- Trends' League tab (item 29, board 58): up to three numbers by season for a college division, the NFL or a state's high schools: the average team, and the middle half of teams (the 25th to the 75th percentile) shaded. The numbers are the team numbers of Team data (offense side, special teams), the trends file's style numbers (deep throw rate, throws behind the line, depth of target, time to throw, play action, shotgun and pistol, under center, 11, 12 and 21 personnel, motion, no huddle, RPOs, runs into 8+ in the box, the blitz, man coverage, nickel and dime) and the starters' height and weight at ten position groups. Style numbers need the depth your seat charts at that level (film for formation, personnel, play action and coverage; tracking for time to throw); high school shows box score numbers and sizes only (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/trends.rs`, `catalogue`, `STYLE`, `SIZES`, `league`; the file in [tallies.md](tallies.md), "The trends file").
- Trends' Team tab: a team's last 10 seasons (or every season) on up to three numbers, both sides of the ball, each ranked in its league that season, beside its league's average and middle half (`trends.rs`, `team`).
- Trends' Player tab, and a player page's Trends tab: his seasons on his position card's stat book numbers by age, against the usual curve: the average qualifier at his position and level at each age, himself left out, shown once 8 seasons count at that age (proposed, not confirmed; `careers.rs`, `player_trend`, `CURVE_MIN`). Numbers that need the depth counts are left out of every cross-season tool, so the curve reaches back to the stat book's first season (proposed, not confirmed; `careers.rs`, `field`).
- Career comps (item 31, boards 52 and 56): the players whose first (or latest) one to four seasons at the level read most like his: for each season the mean gap in percentile across the card's numbers and his playing time (more than half of them must be known), averaged, plus 3 points a year of age apart at the first season. "Match" is 1 minus that gap over 100: .94 is 6 points of percentile apart on average (proposed, not confirmed; `careers.rs`, `career_comps`, `season_gap`, `AGE_GAP`). Candidates' careers must have gone past the seasons matched: a college player still in school counts only with more seasons than the match uses (first seasons) or not at all (latest seasons); "Drafted only" keeps the drafted. Each comp says what came next: his draft slot, NFL starts, Pro Bowls and All-Pros, or where he is now (`outcome`).
- Compare (item 30, board 54): two to four players on the first one's position card at his level, each read in his own division (or state): percentile bars for one season lined up by class, by age or by year, or career numbers (no percentile); every season lined up by class or age; what came next. Two to four teams: every team number with its rank and percentile in its own league, each team's latest season or one year (`compare.rs`, `players`, `teams`, `compare_find`). The older Compare under My players compares ratings, not stats.
- The Stat finder (item 32, board 55): seasons, careers, games or streaks at one level, in one division or all, a state at high school, any years the game has kept (the season being played from its lines so far, until the stat book files it at the close), a position (OL, DL and DB as groups), class and redshirt (college), and age; up to eight conditions, each a number at least or at most a value, on the stat book's totals, sums (yards from scrimmage, touches, total touchdowns) and rates (`finder.rs`, `STATS`, `query`). Careers sum the seasons that meet the filters, then test the conditions. Games and streaks read the games file's lines (college and the NFL; high school games keep boxes only); a streak is games in a row he played that each meet every condition, at least 3 by default, and is marked running when it holds through his last game and that game is this season (`games`, `streaks`). Results show the best 100 by the first condition (up to 500), with the total found.
- Saved searches live in the app's settings on that computer, up to 20, each with the matches seen when it was last checked; opening the Stat finder after a game day has passed checks each again and marks the new matches (proposed, not confirmed; `app/ui/screens/trends.js`, `sfCheckSaved`).
- Every number on these pages has its plain words on hover: new glossary entries for the style numbers, starters' sizes, the band, the usual curve, Match and the finder's sums (`glossary.rs`, a test checks every Trends number and finder number).

## Scouting, recruiting and money (lane NB11)

Items 33-41 of the Data overhaul. The screens are in `app/ui/screens/desk.js`; the commands in `crates/afm-game/src/lane_data/` (`yours.rs`, `prospects.rs`, `drafts.rs`, `coaches.rs`, `money.rs`). They read the tallies (three new files: prospects, moves and money, [tallies.md](tallies.md)), the stat book, the classes and your staff's files. No hidden rating or true potential shows anywhere.

- Your numbers (item 41, board 45): eight tiles for your team this season, each with its rank in your group, where it sits between the group's worst and best, and its last five seasons. College tiles: EPA a play on offense and defense, success rate, explosive plays, points a drive, havoc, turnover margin, special teams; NFL adds pass rate over expected; a level your seat charts only by box score (high school always) gets points, yards a play, third downs, red zone, turnovers and the like. A number your charting can't reach is swapped for one it can. A verdict line says which side carries you (the offense's four tiles against the defense's two, 12 percentile points apart) and names the leak (a tile in the bottom third). Then five seasons of offense and defense, what drives it (your two best and two worst numbers beyond the tiles, and your 4th downs against the analysts' model), what moved in the last seven days and the players who stand out (90th percentile and up in their group) or worry (10th and down). Thresholds proposed, not confirmed (`yours.rs`, `your_numbers`, `tiles`, `verdict`, `drivers`, `movers`, `alerts`).
- Next opponent (item 33): their offense against your defense and yours against theirs, number by number with the edge; their tendencies; each of their position groups' qualified men by mean percentile; their last four games; and the matchups that tilt (your edge rushers against their line, corners against receivers and the rest; a group's mean percentile gap of 15 or more tilts, proposed, not confirmed). Your game day page links to it (`yours.rs`, `next_opponent`, `MATCHUPS`).
- Prospects (items 34-35): the draft class (the public board, then the declared off it, top 300) or the high school class (the followed kids' rankings: the class still to sign, by state and position). Each links to the prospect card (`prospects.rs`, `prospects`, `college_list`, `hs_list`).
- The college prospect card (item 34, board 56): each college season's line with the dominator rating (WR and TE share of the team's receiving yards and touchdowns, RB of its scrimmage yards and touchdowns, EDGE, DT and LB of its sacks and tackles for loss), the age-adjusted dominator (times 1 + 0.1 a year under 21, held between half and one and a half), the breakout age (his age on the draft day of his first season at 20% dominator, 15% for a tight end), and the college markers that carry to the NFL at his spot (passers: yards an attempt adjusted, CPOE, big-time throws and turnover-worthy plays; backs: yards after contact, missed tackles forced, explosive runs; receivers: yards and targets a route run, drops; the line: pressures allowed, run and pass block win rates; rushers: pressure rate, stops, missed tackles). Testing comes from your staff's combine and pro day looks, each as a percentile of the real combine at his spot (`data/real-world/combine_by_position.csv`, normal on its mean and spread); size the same. Comps are the past seasons most like his, with how each turned out. Your staff's read shows as their range (`prospects.rs`, `college_card`, `dominator`, `breakout_mark`, `age_adjusted`, `markers`, `real_pct`; the percents proposed, not confirmed, from PlayerProfiler's and PFF's published lines).
- The high school card (item 35): every game's line on the engine with the opponent, his season lines with his percentile in his state and in his class at his position, camp times, offers and visits with dates, his commitment and signing, and his ranking (stars, national, position and state rank) over time (`prospects.rs`, `hs_card`; the prospects tally).
- Draft data (item 36, boards 48 and 57): every draft class by round, position, conference, school or club: picks, the share who started 32 or more NFL games (a hit, proposed, not confirmed), the share with four or more seasons, games, starts and Pro Bowls a pick, and value over slot (starts against the average pick at that slot); a class counts toward hit rates after four seasons and games after eight. Rounds show the real NFL's 1980-2015 classes beside it. Your club's picks, the steals and busts, and your board's record against the public board's (`drafts.rs`, `draft_data`, `REAL_ROUNDS`, `SLOTS`, `board_record`).
- Recruiting data (item 37): each class's team ranking (247Sports' team score: each signee's rating times 100, weighted by his place on a bell curve with a spread of 10, proposed, not confirmed), stars and blue-chip share; how each star band turned out; who develops most over his stars (starts and draft over what the stars predicted); pipelines by state with the share who stayed home (`drafts.rs`, `recruit_data`, `team_score`, `rating_of`).
- Portal data (item 38): each program's moves in and out that year with how many played and started, moves by direction (up, across, down a level) and by reason (`drafts.rs`, `portal_data`; the moves tally).
- Judging coaches (item 39, board 48): every OC, DC or head coach at a level ranked by his unit's EPA a play percentile (high school: points for and against within the state's class), over one season, three or his whole time; with the talent he had (stars on his side, the NFL's cap on his side), how far he did better than it, his tendency (OC passes over expected, DC havoc, head coach the model's 4th down call) and his contract (`coaches.rs`, `coach_data`, `talent`, `tendency`).
- Money (item 40, board 50): any team's money by position group against its conference or the NFL, each beside the result it buys (passing EPA for quarterbacks, run success for backs, sack rate for the line and so on; box score stand-ins where charting doesn't reach); money against winning over seasons (a scatter with its fit); value for money, snaps a $1M (NFL) or a $100K (college) for the best and worst paid men. High schools show their budget line by line (`money.rs`, `money_data`, `RESULTS`, `value`, `hs_money`). College money is the player pay pots split by each man's share (proposed, not confirmed).
