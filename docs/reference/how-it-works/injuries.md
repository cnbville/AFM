There are three injury sources, and every injury they make reaches the yearly step:

1. The match engine: hits on each snap, at every level (`game/sim.rs`, `injury_check`).
2. NFL practice: every club, on its own practice days from veterans' report to the Super Bowl, when its practice ends (`world/pro/games.rs`, `practice_injuries`; `training/days.rs`, `nfl_practice`).
3. College and high school practices, yours and every AI team's (`training/seat.rs`, `practice_injuries`, `heat_week`, `report_day`; `training/week.rs`, `monday`).

Each one, when it lands, goes in the year's injury log (`training/seat.rs`, `SeatTraining::log_hurt`, `year_hurt`; realism audit item 1). The yearly development step reads the worst one since his last step: what it was and how long it kept him out decide the growth lost, the burst lost and the ceilings gone (`development.rs`, `develop_season`, `YearHurt`). The old yearly roll is left only where no season played on the engine: the class sim, a season on the stand-in, a year away, a prep school or U Sports (`world/season.rs`, `real_hurts`).

## Quick answers

- Does an engine game injury carry past the game? Yes, at every level unless he played on. In the NFL (and the other pro leagues) it sets `out_days`, counted down day by day, with `out_weeks` the weeks still left (lane 4, B1: `world/pro/games.rs`, `record`, `record_preseason`; `world/pro/other.rs`; `ProCareer::hurt_for`). In college and high school (JV games aside) he is out ceil(weeks × (1 − 0.12 × medical) × 7) days, at least 1, from the day after the game; medical = (the program's medical rating − 10) / 10 in college (a top staff 12% shorter, a poor one up to 11% longer), 0 in high school. Your players get a `Hurt` row ("game") and a trainers' note; everyone else goes in `training.others_hurt`, which `hurt_out` reads, so AI teams sit them too (`training/seat.rs`, `game_injuries`; `world/college.rs`, `play_day`; `world/hs_year.rs`).
- And an NFL season row's games missed? Counted game by game: each regular-season game his team plays while he is on its roster and still out hurt (`world/pro/games.rs`, `record`). Men on injured reserve don't count, as on real injury reports (nflverse's 1.2 games a player-season); the yearly roll's figure is cleared at camp (`world/pro/mod.rs`, `camp`; minor bug 96: the rows kept the roll, 0.56).
- Then where do college and high school "games missed" come from? On a season the engine plays, game by game, as at the NFL (minor bug 315): every game his team plays while he is out hurt (a game or practice injury of any team, `training.hurt` or `others_hurt`) counts on his season row, college at kickoff (`world/college.rs`, `play_games`, `missed_game`), high school when the school would have dressed him (its varsity at your school, freshmen only at a school short of 30 older players; `world/hs_year.rs`, `engine_games`, `hurt_dressed`). When the season ends his row names the worst injury of it from the year's log, by its class (below; a torn ACL in the last game is a major injury with no games missed), or by the games he missed when that says more (one or two a minor injury, more a moderate one), and the log remembers the row has it (`world/season.rs`, `mark_real_injury`). The yearly step then rolls nothing and makes no injury at camp (`camp_injury` returns at once when the step read real injuries). Only where the step still rolls (the first season after a stand-in one) does camp clear the roll's minor and moderate labels and make a major roll a real one, out from camp until the day after the last game it costs, a torn ACL 70% or a broken bone on a keyed draw (`world/season.rs`, `camp_injury`).
- Does toughness make injuries rarer? No. Toughness only decides whether he plays on hurt (`game/sim.rs`, `injury_check`, `plays_on`).
- Does fatigue raise injury odds? Yes: the chance × (1 + 0.8 × tired), tired 0-1 from his energy (`game/sim.rs`, `injury_check`). Fatigue also scales ratings (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`).
- Does body weight matter? Yes, through the hit's force: half the sum of each man's mass times speed (`game/sim.rs`, `hit_effects`). Players of 260 lb or more (any position) also risk heat illness more in your high school practices, ×1.8 (`training/seat.rs`, `heat_week`).
- Does position matter? In the yearly roll (where it still runs, see below): QB 0.7, RB 1.4, WR 1.0, TE 1.1, OT 0.9, IOL 0.9, EDGE 1.0, DT 1.0, LB 1.1, CB 1.0, S 1.0, K/P/LS 0.25 (`positions.rs`, `PositionProfile.injury_risk`). In the engine only through how often and how hard he is hit. In practice specialists are 0.25 (`world/pro/games.rs`, `practice_injuries`; `training/seat.rs`, `practice_injuries`).
- Does turf matter? ×1.12 on artificial turf, wherever the game is played (`game/sim.rs`, `injury_check`). NFL fields follow each club's real turf seasons, a future new stadium turf if domed, neutral sites turf if closed (`world/finance/data.rs`, `TURF`; `world/finance/nfl.rs`, `turf`; `world/pro/games.rs`). College and high school fields are drawn per program or school and year from real shares that rise over time (FBS, smaller colleges, high schools), richer programs more often (`world/season.rs`, `field_turf`; `world/college.rs`, `conditions`; `world/hs_year.rs`, `hs_turf`). Rain and cold don't touch injury odds (they raise fumbles) (`game/sim.rs`, `hit_effects`).
- What is injury proneness? A hidden 1-20 trait, normal(10, 3.5) at creation; +1 after each major injury, capped 20 (`player.rs`, `trait_1_20`, `generate_prospect`; `development.rs`, `develop_season`).
- Does the medical staff shorten injuries? NFL regular-season and playoff game injuries and practice injuries: up to 12% shorter (`world/pro/games.rs`, `record`, `practice_injuries`); preseason game injuries don't read it (`record_preseason`). The yearly roll, where it still runs: up to 15% fewer games for minor and moderate ones (`development.rs`, `develop_season`, `ctx.medical`). Your college or high school practice injuries: not read (`training/seat.rs`, `practice_injuries`, `med = 1.0`).
- Does the strength staff make injuries rarer? Yes, muscle strains and backs only (minor bug 303): the guard = clamp(1 − 0.02 × (strength head − the level's typical head), 0.85, 1.15), 2% fewer a point above typical, 2% more a point below, at most 15% either way (proposed, not confirmed: the 2% and the cap). Typical is the division's mean over programs with players, or the mean of the NFL clubs playing this year; high school has none (guard 1). It multiplies the hamstring, back, groin, calf and quad weights in the mix, and the chance by (total + (guard − 1) × soft weights) / total, so every other kind's chance stays where it was and each level's totals stay at real (`game/sim.rs`, `strength_guard`, `guarded_mix`, `InjuryKind::soft_tissue`). Read in engine games (`GamePlayer.soft`, set by `world/season.rs`, `game_team`, and `world/pro/games.rs`), NFL practice (`practice_injuries`), college practices, yours and the AI's (`training/seat.rs`, `practice_guarded`, `guarded_practice_kind`; `training/week.rs`), and the yearly roll (`development.rs`, `develop_season`, from `strength_dev`). Strength work cuts sports injuries to about a third in trials (Lauersen 2014) and Nordic hamstring work halves strains (van Dyk 2019); every college and NFL staff lifts, so the gap between staffs is a small share of that.
- Is there re-injury? Only for a player you rush back through a second-opinion scene: 8% a week for eight weeks, 2-6 weeks out (`world/folk/life.rs`, `opinion_answer`, `rushed_week`). Nothing else raises odds after an injury except +1 proneness after a major one.
- Does pushing rehab do anything? Since talk facts lane L8 (proposed, not confirmed): at a school, the trainer's report holds each man rehabbing last season's surgery, major or moderate injury (`training.hurt`, "rehab from last season's injury") until fall camp, the spring game or two weeks into spring practice; pushed, a step sooner, and he carries 1.6x the practice injury risk for 8 weeks (`training/seat.rs`, `RUSHED_RISK`, `RUSHED_DAYS`). At a club, pushed brings each man back in three quarters of his time on the rushed rule above (`world/folk/takeover.rs`, `rehab_holds`, `rehab_back`).
- Is an injury ever longer than its usual range? Yes, rarely (realism audit item 14; proposed, not confirmed): a complication (a graft that fails, a bone that doesn't knit, nerve damage, symptoms that linger) multiplies its length by 1.3 to 2.5, most near the low end; 6% of torn ACLs, 5% of broken bones and foot injuries, 3% of high ankle sprains, 2% of concussions, 1% of the rest. Read from the length's own draw, so no other draw moves (`game/sim.rs`, `complication`, called by `injury_weeks`). A torn ACL can then run to 140 weeks; Jaylon Smith's 2016 knee cost about 20 months, Alex Smith's 2018 leg about two years.
- Can an injury end a career? Not directly. A major injury adds 1.5 wear, which lowers a pro's usual retirement age by 0.3 a point of wear above 6 (`development.rs`, `develop_season`; `world/pro/mod.rs`, end-of-season retirement). The class sim's own retirement (`career.rs`, `retires`) adds 12% after a major injury at 27 or older, but the world does not use it.
- Do AI draft boards read injuries? No reader found. The medical read is your staff's file only (`world/scouting/file.rs`, `medical_read`; shown by `crates/afm-game/src/lane_scouting.rs`).

## In games (the match engine)

- Where checked: every tackle in `hit_effects`, the ball carrier and each man who tackles him (a man joining the tackle hits at half the force), the sacked quarterback (`game/sim.rs`, `hit_effects`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, the sack, share 0.8, or 1.3 when the quarterback didn't see him), and a sliding quarterback when a late hit on him is flagged (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `late_on_slide`). Kick and interception returns run through the same tackles (`game/kicking.rs`).
- Never checked: a man in a block (unless he reaches out and tackles), the hit at the catch point (targeting included), the hit on a quarterback as he throws (roughing the passer included), the sacker, the late hitter, a hit that forces a fumble (`hit_effects` returns first) and an arm tackle the runner breaks (`first_hit`). There is no non-contact injury: hamstrings, groins and calves come only from a hit, and an Achilles is not a game injury kind.
- Force: (carrier mass × speed + tackler mass × speed) × 0.5 × (1 + 0.2 × share × his big-hit tendency lean) (`game/sim.rs`, `hit_effects`).
- Share: the carrier 1 + 0.4 × his contact tendency lean (min 0.2); the tackler 0.7 (`hit_effects`).
- Chance: `injury_scale × 0.022 × share × (force / 1700)^1.6 × prone × turf × tired × guarded` (guarded: the strength staff's factor, see above), prone = 0.55 + proneness / 18 (1 → 0.61, 10 → 1.11, 20 → 1.66), turf 1.12 or 1.0, `injury_scale` 1.0 (`game/sim.rs`, `injury_check`; `game/flow.rs`).
- Type mix (weights): bruise 23.2, ankle sprain 9.5, high ankle 3.6, knee sprain 7.9, ACL 1.7, shoulder 4.6, concussion 10.7, ribs 5.0, hand 6.1, fracture 3.1, back 2.8, hamstring 7.4, foot 3.1, groin 3.4, calf 3.0, neck 1.8, hip 1.7, quad 1.5 (`injury_check`). Sized so the week-plus ones match nflverse's shares by body part (knee 17.2%, ankle 14.3%, hamstring 12.9%, concussion 11.1%, shoulder 5.6%, foot 4.4%, groin 4.3%, calf 3.8%); foot, groin, calf, neck, hip and quad got their own kinds with minor bug 145 (knees had been 24.7% and ankles 21.1%). The list is `GAME_INJURIES`; hamstring, back, groin, calf and quad × the strength staff's guard.
- Length: `injury_weeks` gives (lo + (hi − lo) × u²) × the rare complication (most are short) × (1.3 − 0.03 × recovery) (recovery 10 → ×1.0, 20 → ×0.7, 1 → ×1.27) (`game/sim.rs`, `injury_weeks`, `injury_check`).
- Ranges in weeks: bruise 0-1, ankle 0-3, high ankle 2-7, hamstring 1-5, knee 1-6, ACL 36-56, shoulder 0-8, concussion 0.5-3, ribs 0-4, hand 0-5, fracture 4-12, back 0-4, foot 0.5-9.5, groin 0.5-5.5, calf 0.5-5.5, neck 0-10, hip 0-7, quad 0.5-6 (`injury_weeks`; the six new ranges and what each hampers are proposed, not confirmed).
- Playing on: never after a concussion, ACL or fracture; otherwise chance clamp(0.15 + 0.035 × toughness − 0.12 × weeks, 0, 0.95), toughness as a 1-20 read against the level (`injury_check`; `game/roster.rs`, `trait20`).
- Concussion protocol: caught 92% in the NFL, 80% in college, 60% in high school; one not caught plays on (`game/flow.rs`, `apply_injuries`).
- Playing hurt: the ratings his injury limits drop by its share each snap he plays, scrimmage and kicks alike (a hamstring speed and burst, a hand catching) (`game/sim.rs`, `hampered`, `InjuryKind::hampers`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`; `game/kicking.rs`). The rest are untouched.
- Out: removed for the rest of the game (`game/flow.rs`, `apply_injuries`, `out`). Nobody comes back in the same game: the only line that clears `out` brings back, at halftime, a man who sat the first half for a targeting ejection in his last game. A man playing on hurt is never pulled.
- Practice snaps on the engine clear the engine's injuries every snap (`game/flow.rs`, `practice_snaps`).

## After an NFL game

- Each injury not played through: days = ceil(weeks × (1 − 0.12 × medical z) × 7), out that many days from the day after the game (lane 4, B1: `world/pro/games.rs`, `record`). The injury goes to the team he played for (`res.injuries` holds the team; it was read by snap side, so a kick or turnover snap's injuries landed on the wrong team, oddity 28). Preseason games the same without the medical staff. Practice injuries: ceil(weeks × 7) days.
- Injured reserve when weeks ≥ the minimum: 6 before 2020, 4 from 2020 (`world/pro/games.rs`, `ir_minimum`).
- Preseason games: 6 weeks or more puts a vested veteran (4+ accrued seasons) on reserve now; anyone else waits for the cutdown (`record_preseason`). At the cutdown a kept player still out 6+ weeks goes to reserve (`world/pro/mod.rs`, `cutdown`); one cut while hurt gets an injury settlement, his base pay for the weeks out, as dead money (`world/pro/squad.rs`, `settle_injury`). A camp injury of 6+ weeks costs a borderline player 2 points in the cut ranking (`world/pro/mod.rs`, `cut_scores`).
- Players with `out_weeks > 0` don't dress, nor do your own club's players on the non-football injury list from report day (`world/pro/games.rs`, `actives`, `preseason_side`; `training/seat.rs`, `SeatTraining::hurt_out`).

## NFL practice injuries

- Each club on its own practice days from veterans' report to the Super Bowl, every healthy roster or practice-squad player, when that club's practice ends (the six-part day): in camp every day but a preseason game's and the day after, a sixth of the week's chance each; in season the three days before a game that aren't the day after one, a third each (`training/days.rs`, `nfl_practice`, `practice_at`; `world/pro/games.rs`, `practice_injuries`). See [clock.md](clock.md).
- Weekly chance (split over the practice days as above): base × prone × age × specialist × plan × load × guarded (the strength staff's factor; hamstring and back × the guard in the mix). Base 0.0155 before the cutdown, 0.009 after. Prone = 0.55 + proneness / 18. Age = 1 + 0.03 × max(age − 26, 0). Specialist 0.25. Plan = the club's week against an average staff's: clamp(sqrt((full-contact minutes + 10) / (neutral + 10)) × ((field minutes + 30) / (neutral + 30))^0.3, 0.5, 2.0) (`training/week.rs`, `team_week`, `risk`; stored in `training.club_risk`). Your own club's plan is the same read of your own week × (0.77 + 0.23 × limber), limber = clamp(1 − 0.1 × (mobility minutes − neutral) / (neutral + 15), 0.9, 1.1) (`training/seat.rs`, `week`, `my_risk`, `limber`; `training/week.rs`, `monday`). Load = 1 + 0.8 × (spike − 1.3) above a 1.3 spike (`training/mod.rs`, `Train::spike`).
- Mix: hamstring 22, ankle 15, knee 14, bruise 10, shoulder 7, back 6, hand 6, high ankle 4, concussion 4, ACL 2.5, ribs 2, fracture 2. Length: `injury_weeks` × (1.3 − 0.03 × recovery) × (1 − 0.12 × medical z), ceil; 0 weeks is no injury.
- Reserve: in camp 6+ weeks and vested; in season past `ir_minimum` and not on the practice squad.

## Your college or high school team's practices

Every AI college and high school team practises with injuries too (update 3, F13; the realism audit's item 2 had missed `training/week.rs`). Measured on the seed 1 world over 620 days on the engine: college practice made 33,289 injuries against 20,936 in games (61% in practice; the NCAA Injury Surveillance Program puts about half to 60% of college football injuries in practice), high school 118,553 against 219,742 (35%; high school surveillance puts roughly 40% in practice). Nothing was changed for it.

- Run weekly on staff weeks, from the planned week (`training/seat.rs`, `week`, `practice_injuries`). Every player not already hurt and not absent from a voluntary week rolls; heat illness too (`practice_injuries`, `heat_week`, `absent_ids`). AI college and high school teams roll the same chance in the weekly world pass, from their own week's block, practices, contact and mobility work; the injury goes in `training.others_hurt` and he sits the games until back (`training/week.rs`, `monday`; `practice_kind`). Both are rolled with Monday's week but wait for their practice day's end before they land, yours in `SeatTraining.pending`, the others' in `others_pending` (`training/days.rs`, `practice_at`).
- Rate per practice: camp 0.0037, spring 0.0023, season and postseason 0.00115, other 0.0008; high school × 0.4 (`training/seat.rs`, `practice_rate`).
- Chance over the week: 1 − (1 − rate × contact × prone × exposure × sqrt(soft))^practices (`injury_chance`), then × (0.77 + 0.23 × limber) for the week's mobility work (`practice_injuries`, `limber`). Contact = ((full-contact minutes + 10) / (neutral + 10))^0.7. Prone = 0.42 + proneness / 18. Exposure 0.25 for specialists. Soft = 1 + 1.5 × (spike − 1.3) above 1.3 (`soft_tissue`).
- Mix as the NFL practice mix, with hamstring weight × soft × limber and concussion and shoulder × contact (`practice_injuries`). In college, hamstring and back × the strength staff's guard, and the chance × `practice_guarded` so the other kinds stay put (`practice_guarded`, `guarded_practice_kind`).
- Length: days = round(`injury_weeks` × (1.3 − 0.03 × recovery) × 7), at least 1, starting on a random weekday of the week. Kept in `training.hurt` (`Hurt`), with a trainers' note.
- While hurt: his week keeps every off-field period except mat drills and testing (meetings, installs, film, the weight room, mobility, the rest of the classroom), plus 45 minutes of recovery on each day with any (`training/seat.rs`, `own_week`).
- He sits out every game until healed, at every level: `prepare` drops him and the game builders leave him out (`training/seat.rs`, `prepare`, `SeatTraining::hurt_out`; `world/college.rs`, `play_day`, `seat_setup`; `world/hs_year.rs`, `can_play`; `world/pro/games.rs`, `actives`, `preseason_side`).
- His status line shows the injury and the days or weeks left; an entry with no injury kind (a failed conditioning test, heat illness) shows as "Non-football injury list" (`crates/afm-game/src/views.rs`, `status_text`).
- His prep that week: limited (0.55 of the field work, all the classroom) when he is back within 3 days of Monday, else out (no field work, three quarters of the classroom). Out reads Unprepared or Underprepared for most players, and two poor weeks in a row start rust (`training/seat.rs`, `prep_detail`; `training/prep.rs`, `Status::there`; see [training.md](training.md)).
- Heat illness (your high school without a heat rule): see [training.md](training.md). Out 3 days, `kind: None` (`training/seat.rs`, `heat_week`).
- Failed conditioning test on report day: 3-10 days on the non-football injury list, `kind: None` (`training/seat.rs`, `report_day`; `training/offseason.rs`, `fail_chance`).
- An NFL seat team's practice injuries come from the league-wide NFL practice pass, not this one (`training/seat.rs`, `week`, `st.tier != Tier::Pro`).

## The yearly step's injury cost (every player, every checkpoint)

Realism audit item 1 (Cain's yes 2026-11-15): the injury that costs growth is the one he really had.

- The log: every game injury (college and high school `training/seat.rs`, `game_injuries`; NFL and preseason `world/pro/games.rs`, `record`, `record_preseason`; the other pro leagues `world/pro/other.rs`) and every practice injury (yours and the AI teams' as they land, `training/days.rs`, `practice_at`; NFL `practice_injuries`) adds itself to his `YearHurt` with its days out and the day: the worst one stands (by class, then days out), and the days out over all add up (`development.rs`, `YearHurt::add`). Played-through injuries, heat illness and a failed conditioning test don't go in. His step takes his entry (`world/season.rs`, `develop`; `world/pro/mod.rs`, `camp`); entries of men who reached no step leave after two years (pro camp).
- The class (proposed, not confirmed): under 7 days out a knock that costs nothing; 7-20 days minor; 21-55 moderate; 56 or more major; a torn ACL always major (`hurt_class`, `HURT_MINOR_DAYS`, `HURT_MODERATE_DAYS`, `HURT_MAJOR_DAYS`).
- Cost to the year, as the roll's: minor growth × 0.95 and snaps factor 0.9; moderate × 0.85 and 0.65; major × 0.5 and 0.2 (`develop_season`, `growth_multiplier`, `snaps_factor`). With weekly training, the growth multiplier is stored in the year's form and lands through the weeks (`training/mod.rs`, `Train::open`). His games are on the season rows already, counted game by game, so the step records none.
- A major injury heals by what it was (`major_heal`; proposed, not confirmed), on the injury-sensitive attributes (speed, acceleration, agility, balance, flexibility, jumping; `attributes.rs`, `injury_sensitive`):
  - A torn ACL: 55% heal fully (−1.5 to −5 now, back next year), 30% the same plus −1 to −3 on the ceilings for good, 15% lose their burst (`LostBurst`: −3 to −7 now and on the ceilings, nothing back).
  - A broken bone: 80% / 16% / 4%. Another leg injury long enough to be major (knee, ankle, high ankle, foot, hamstring, calf, quad, groin, hip): 75% / 20% / 5%.
  - Anything else (shoulder, neck, hand, ribs, concussion, back, bruise): no loss on the legs and no burst gone; the year's growth cost stands.
  - Every loss × the age hit: 1.0 under 24, 1.2 at 24-27, 1.6 at 28+ (`injury_age_hit`). Proneness +1 (max 20), wear +1.5.
- Which row names it: the season row it happened in. College and high school rows are named when the season ends (`mark_real_injury`); at the step, one from before his last season ended (more than 160 days back, `HURT_OFFSEASON_DAYS`) still unnamed goes on his last row (an NFL season's), and one since (spring ball, the summer) on the row the step opens (`world/season.rs`, `hurt_for_step`). A lost burst goes on the same row.
- The year back: a major injury that heals sets `YearHurt::rehab` for his next step, which returns min(room, 1.5-5 × age hit) on each sensitive attribute; the ceilings above them were kept from settling while he healed (`develop_season`, the year-back step and the tidy-up `rehab` rule).
- Measured (seed 1 world, played on 620 days from February 2026 on the engine, the 2027 steps, old roll against real injuries): injured share a step HS 12.4% → 8.9%, small college 13.0 → 21.0, FCS 14.0 → 23.3, FBS 15.4 → 24.4, NFL 18.9 → 30.2; major HS 1.38% → 0.61, FBS 1.60 → 1.86, NFL 1.96 → 2.15; burst lost HS 0.21% → 0.06, FBS 0.22 → 0.18, NFL 0.44 → 0.21. Majors by kind (FBS): torn ACL 1.02% of players a year, broken bone 0.50, foot 0.13, neck 0.07, shoulder 0.08. Average growth over the run moves under 0.05 overall at every college and high school age.
- The roll (kept where no season played on the engine, `ctx.hurt` None): chance level rate × position risk × (0.55 + 0.045 × proneness) × role × the strength staff's factor on the game mix's soft-tissue share; level rate HS 0.13, small college 0.16, FCS 0.17, FBS 0.19, NFL 0.24 (`level_injury_rate`); role 1.15 starter, 0.9 otherwise, 0.5 for a year without games or in trouble. Severity 62% minor, 27% moderate, 11% major (the major's chance kept against the guard). Games missed minor 0-2, moderate 2-6 (× (1.3 − 0.03 × recovery) × (1 − 0.15 × medical)), major half to all. A major heals as a torn ACL above. Its year back reads the last row's `MajorInjury` without `LostBurst`.
- Recovery trait: normal(10, 3.5) from his id, −0.1 to −0.4 a year from 30 (`player.rs`, `GameTraits::for_id`, `age_one_year`).
- Wear: only major injuries add it in the world (no other `wear +=` outside `career.rs`). Wear above 3 speeds physical decline (`development.rs`, ageing, `wear_mult`) and above 6 lowers a pro's retirement age.

## Recovery and lists (NFL)

- `out_days` drops one every day, all year, after the day's games, so the day plays the game its morning showed; a man who retired hurt heals too, so a loaded world heals as the original does (lane 4, B1: `world/pro/mod.rs`, `heal_day`, last in `nfl_year::day`), and `out_weeks` is the weeks still left, rounded up. The list of hurt pros is kept for the day and rebuilt from everyone each Tuesday and after a load; an older save's `out_weeks` turn into days the first morning. `heal_week` now counts only reserve and practice squad weeks.
- Back from reserve the night he heals, in season, if the club has returns left: none before 2012, 1 in 2012-16, 2 in 2017-19, unlimited 2020-21, 8 from 2022; else he sits out the season there (`world/pro/games.rs`, `ir_returns`; `world/pro/mod.rs`, `heal_day`). A full roster cuts its lowest-graded roster player to make room (health not checked).
- At the new league year a healed reserve player rejoins the roster; one still hurt moves to the reserve/PUP list and comes back the night he heals without using a return (`world/pro/mod.rs`, the league-year step, `pup`; `heal_day`).
- A club over the roster limit with too few healthy players to dress moves its longest injury to reserve (`world/pro/mod.rs`, `fill_roster`).
- Pros out 2+ weeks (other than yours, who train on your hurt week) train at a rehab ratio (strength 0.6, speed 0.4, conditioning 0.4, technique 0.1, contact 0, kicking 0.2, mental 0.5) at 70% attendance, load 150 units, and read as out for prep. An injury heals on Tuesday, after Monday's pass, so a pro with one week left practises and is read as full (`training/week.rs`, `monday`; `training/prep.rs`, `out_this_week`).
- Second opinions (your team): a player out 4+ weeks, or one with a major injury near fall camp, may ask; supporting it moves the estimate −1, 0 or +2 weeks; asking the trainer to rush him cuts it to three quarters unless the trainer's judgement is 14 or more (`world/folk/life.rs`, `opinions_week`, `opinion_answer`; the same as `second_opinion`, `would_rush` and `rush_back`, which live talks lane N5c also runs when you ask the team doctor in words: `docs/reference/how-it-works/live-talks.md`, "lane N5c").

## How injuries carry over

- High school to college: through the season rows and the hidden traits (proneness, wear, ceilings lost). In the world, recruiting doesn't read a senior-year injury; only the class sim does (`career.rs`, `simulate_cohort`, the senior-film note).
- College eligibility: a season where a major injury hit or half the season's games were missed is "hurt most of the season" (`world/eligibility.rs`, `hurt_most_of_season`). With 3 games or 30% of the team's games played at most, it is a medical hardship and doesn't count; a hurt redshirt year counts toward the two lost seasons that earn a sixth year. From 2026-27 Division I counts every year (no hardships) (`world/college.rs`, `settle_eligibility`; `world/eligibility.rs`, `hardship_games`).
- College to the NFL: the medical read on your staff's file lists each past moderate or major injury with games missed and a lost burst, a count of minor knocks, and doctors' notes when examined (combine, a visit or a local day). Concern = 0.45 × (proneness − 10) / 5 + 0.8 × majors + 0.25 × moderates − 0.2 × longevity / 2 + 0.3 × wear, plus noise of (0.35 examined, 0.8 not) × the staff's sharpness. Grades: cleared, minor concern (0.6+), major concern (1.4+), failed (2.4+) (`world/scouting/file.rs`, `medical_read`, `medical_words`).
- A projected first-rounder may skip the all-star week to avoid injury risk (`world/recruiting.rs`, `all_star_invites`).

## The trainers' memo in a college or high school inbox

A college or high school seat gets a memo from the head trainer the morning after a game that hurt anyone, and on Wednesdays while the season runs (off the season only when someone is out 14 days or more): who is out, what it was, how long ("5 d" up to a week, then weeks) and when he is back, the new injuries first, then the men still out, longest first. It reads the training desk's own record of who is out (`SeatTraining::hurt`: the day he was hurt, the day he is back, what it was) and raises no world event. After a game it offers to check in on each of them (a call each, the mood rises). A starter out four weeks or more makes it loud. Up to 25 rows (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_core/mail/trainer.rs`, `report`, `ROWS`). An NFL seat's report is the Wednesday injury report (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_core/mail.rs`, `injuries`). See [inbox.md](inbox.md).
