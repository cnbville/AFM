## Quick answers

- Who plays Mike, Sam and Will? Your depth order names the jobs and the engine keeps them: the base board's first three linebackers are SAM, MIKE and WILL in that order, its first three corners the two outside corners and the nickel, its first two safeties FS and SS (`spots.rs`, `board_jobs`, `named_jobs`; `game/flow.rs`, `Side::new`). Without your order (every AI staff), `best_order` puts the starters on the spots that rate highest, with a fill-in cost at a job he hasn't played. The staff reads body as one input there, never as a rule (Cain 2026-11-07): true speed where the job asks for range (Will, outside corner, free safety), size where it takes on blocks and hits (Sam, strong safety, nickel), height outside (`spots.rs`, `lean_value`). Sam is its own spot (Strength .2, Block Shedding .2, Man Coverage .15, Tackling .15, Pursuit .15, Blitzing .15); Will reads Speed .35, Pursuit .25, Zone Coverage .25, Blitzing .15; Mike as before. A man new to a job reads later and busts more until he has played it: +0.06 s on his read, +1.5 pts of busts and +3 pts of misfits at full, by how far the job is from his own (`fill_in` / 3, 0.6 of that once he has practised it this week), gone once it is in his played spots (`game/flow.rs`, `new_to_job`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `JOB_READ`, `JOB_BUST`, `JOB_MISFIT`). Your named men learn their jobs at spring practice and NFL camp (`world/systems.rs`, `set_spots`, `set_club_spots`). In the A/B test with a Will's build (228 lb, 4.56) at Sam and a Sam's (248, 4.68) at Will on every team, the man at Sam made 1.1 pts fewer run tackles a snap, missed 1.4 pts more and drew 0.9 pts more targets; the man at Will made 0.7 pts more run tackles and missed 1.7 pts fewer; the busts rose 0.19 pts a snap until both knew both jobs (`ab_check lb_swap`, `lb_swap_known`, 2026-11-08). The team's run defense got a little better (-0.06 yards a carry: the bigger man at Will plays the nickel snaps too) and its pass defense showed no clear cost, because a heavier linebacker's catch rate allowed does not rise yet (real +0.92 pts per 10 lb, part of minor bug 307). On the snap the Sam lines up strong side and takes the tight end in man, the Will weak side, the Mike in the middle with the A-gap on double-A pressure, the spy and the middle zone (`game/scrimmage.rs`).

- How is a throw's expected completion (behind CPOE) worked out? From its level's own model, fitted on the engine's throws: depth band, separation band and pressure, so each level's CPOE centres on zero (`game/stats.rs`, `XCOMP`, `expected_completion_at`; the stat book carries it as `x_cmp`). Details in records-and-data.md, Analytics.
- Do pregame and halftime talks change play? Yes, for every team: up to ±1.5 rating points on everything, ±2 discipline and concentration, ±2.5 composure and a late-hit share per player, fading through the half; a sideline word after a costly mistake adds its own (`game/talk.rs`, `land`, `Pulse`; `game/flow.rs`, `room_talk`, `give_word`). See [people.md](people.md), "Talks on game day and off it".
- What is "his level's typical man"? The mean ratings and body (height, weight, arm) of every player at his position on 64 synthetic teams of his level, measured once (`game/synthetic.rs`, `typical`, `typical_body`; the rosters `rules::trait_norm` was measured on). The reads added or resized for the A/B tests (minor bugs 274-289, 2026-11-07) are taken about him, so a level's typical player plays as he did and only the spread within the level changes: centred on NFL players, they took FBS yards an attempt from 7.12 to 6.77. In the NFL a typical passer has Speed 61, Decisions 82, Pocket Presence 81 and Arm Strength 80 and stands 6'3; a typical receiver 6'1 with 31-inch arms; a typical corner 5'11 1/2 with 30 1/2-inch arms. One pivot is a fixed number at the NFL: a tackle's reach in the hand fight (minor bug 288) is an inch of arm over 34 there, Cain's number, and moves at the other levels with their typical tackle's arm.
- What is "his level's typical starter"? The mean ratings of the men each of those 64 teams' depth charts start at his position (`roster::starters`: one passer, two corners, two safeties, two linebackers; `game/synthetic.rs`, `typical_starter`). The reads resized for the rest of minor bug 283 (2026-11-08) sit about him: starters rate above the dressed average, so a steeper slope about the dressed man lifted or sank every starting lineup (picks ran 2.6% a throw against 2.3%).
- Do players have good and bad days? Yes (minor bug 287, 2026-11-07): each man draws his form once a game on his own stream, N(0, 3) rating points on everything × clamp(1 + 0.6 × (10 - hidden Consistency) / 10, 0.2, 2), so 4.1 at Consistency 4 and 1.9 at 16, added to his edge on every scrimmage snap (`game/flow.rs`, `FORM_SD`, `Side::form`, through `hurt_edge_o`/`hurt_edge_d`); kicks and returns don't read it. Proposed, not confirmed: kept small because the engine's passers already varied from game to game a little more than real ones (true sd of EPA a dropback 0.167, nflverse 2021-24 0.154); at 5 points it added 1.4 points of runs stuffed at the line. Consistency still narrows each throw's and kick's scatter too.
- Does the elusiveness check read weight? Yes, since lane 4 (A3): every make-a-man-miss check takes off size_cost = (forty_weight(his weight) - forty_weight(typical)) / 0.0124 rating points, the way the forty reads weight (`game/sim.rs`, `size_cost`): the QB's sack escape (× 0.7, typical 222, `game/scrimmage.rs`), a QB run's moves (typical 222, `game/flow.rs`, `qb_run`, `qb_spy`) and a returner's danger (typical 200, `game/kicking.rs`). The first-contact miss no longer does (minor bug 279, 2026-11-07: real broken tackles don't move with weight once talent is known); a heavy carrier pays in burst, braking and cut instead. Weight also comes in once he is wrapped up: the broken-tackle roll and the pile (`game/sim.rs`, `fight_tick`).
- Where does weight count? Top speed, burst and cut (`game/roster.rs`, `forty`, `sprint_tau`, `accel`, `cut`); momentum and hit force (`game/sim.rs`, `hit_effects`); broken tackles, 0.08 a pound from 215 lb, and the wrap-up, (mass - 215) / 300 on both sides, and the pile's push by mass (`fight_tick`); blocks, 0.08 a pound from 300 lb, 280 for rushers and shedders (`block_base`, `rush_power`, `shed_power`, `anchor`); an interior pass rusher's get-off, 0.38 a pound off his level's typical man at his position (`engage`, minor bug 285); an edge's power move, 0.3 a Strength point over the typical edge's for each 10 lb over the typical edge's weight up to 20 lb, nothing at or under it (E3, `rush_power`, `EDGE_STRENGTH_MASS`, `EDGE_MASS_SPAN`); a long-armed edge's reach to the quarterback from his block (E2, below); an edge's burst on a pass rush reads the typical edge's when his own is lower (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `rush_feet`, minor bug 286), his weight his own; the jump at the catch point, -0.035 inch a pound over 220 (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `high_reach`); a contact-shy tackler passing on a man 15+ lb heavier (`game/sim.rs`, `tackles`).
- Where do height and arm length count? (Checked for the A/B tests, 2026-11-06; minor bugs 274-277 and 288 on 2026-11-07.) His jump at the catch point (standing reach 0.82 × height + arm + 4 inches, `game/scrimmage.rs`, `high_reach`); where a jump ball arrives (0.72 × his height, 0.95 × height + 6 on a long ball); a defender's reach to the ball at the catch point (0.025 yard an inch of height, 0.10 an inch of arm about his level's typical man at his position, `catch_point`, `D_REACH_HEIGHT`, `D_REACH_ARM`); the receiver's catch radius (0.0075 yard an inch of height, 0.01 an inch of arm, the same way); the passer's trust in a big receiver downfield and in the red zone (`quarterback`, `BIG_TRUST`) and a tall passer's over the middle (`OVER_LINE`); a tackle's pass set against speed and finesse, a rating point an inch of arm over 34 in the NFL, the pivot moving with each level's typical tackle (`game/sim.rs`, `block_base`); batted balls (the rusher's reach, 0.06 an inch of height and 0.08 an inch of arm; the passer's height no longer, real shows none); tackle reach (0.03 yard an inch of arm, `game/sim.rs`; an edge up to 0.3 more an inch over the typical edge's when he is quick, 0.15 a forty step of 0.15 s, E2, `close_reach`); an edge's speed move (0.2 a point an inch of height over the typical edge's for each Flexibility point over the typical edge's, E1); an edge's reach to the quarterback (0.18 yard an inch of arm over the typical edge's on his block, scaled by weight, E2, `sack_check`); and a carrier's reach for the line (`Tend::Reach`). Both feed Dynamic Links (Highpointer, Length, Low center, Pocket frame), which only count near the top of the position (`links.rs`). Not read: height in any block or in the pass rush outside an edge's bend, a receiver's or corner's height in the route, separation or the jam. What that does to the stats: `afm/ab-tests/` in the project folder.
- How fine is the sim? 22 tracked bodies, 10 ticks a second (`game/geom.rs`, `DT`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).
- What decides a sack? A free rusher within 1.25 yards, or one winning his block (w > 0.45) within 1.2, then an escape roll: 0.4 Elusiveness + 0.3 Agility + 0.3 Strength against 0.6 Tackling + 0.2 Strength + 0.2 Agility, + 0.5 a 10 Pocket Presence over his level's typical passer on the logit (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `sack_check`, `POCKET_ESCAPE`, minor bug 283).
- What counts as a pressure? The nearest rusher within 3.2 yards (an engaged one 1.5 yards farther unless he is winning) once 0.6 s are gone; the nearest and any free or winning rusher within 2.6 yards are credited (`quarterback`, `PRESS_NEAR`, `PRESS_FROM`, `PRESS_CAUSE`, minor bug 284). It is a label only: the quarterback's play reads his felt pressure, not this.
- What decides a catch? A base miss of max(0.15 - 0.00095 × Catching, 0.012), then how far off his frame the ball is, the fight within 1.4 yards, weather and a hard ball (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `catch_point`).
- What counts as a drop? An incompletion on a ball inside 0.8 of his catch radius with no defender within a yard of it (`catch_point`).
- Does the catch read Concentration? No; nor Toughness, Route Running or Speed (`catch_point`).
- How does a pick happen? Five ways: a tip in the throwing lane, a ball off target into a defender, a defender in front at the catch point, a breakup that sticks (6% of breakups), a ball off the receiver's hands (`catch_point`).
- What sets the fumble chance? 0.018 × (hit force / 1700)^1.5 × clamp(1 + 0.025 × (hit - Ball Security) - 0.005 × (hit - the hit of his level's typical man at his position), 0.25, 2.5), hit = the hitter's 0.5 Strength + 0.5 Tackling (so 0.020 a point for the hitter, 0.025 for the carrier, `FUMBLE_HIT`, minor bug 282), × a wet ball and the Strip tendency (`game/sim.rs`, `hit_effects`).
- What does fatigue cost? Each rating loses its fatigue share × tiredness (speed 10%, strength 12%, skills 6%), top speed 8%, burst and cut 10% when fully spent (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`).
- Is there momentum? Yes: -3 to +2 rating points on all his ratings, after turnovers, drops, sacks and touchdowns, softened by Composure (`game/flow.rs`, `edge_hit`, `decay_edges`).
- What does playing hurt cost? What his injury limits, as a share of the rating: ankle speed 8%, agility 12%, acceleration 8%; hamstring speed and acceleration 10%; knee agility 10%, speed 6%; shoulder tackling and arm strength 10%, run blocking and hand use 8%; ribs strength 6%, ball security 8%; hand catching 10%, ball security, hand use and the three accuracies 8%; back strength 8%, agility 6%; anything else speed 3%. On scrimmage snaps and kicks (`game/sim.rs`, `hampers`, `hampered`; `game/scrimmage.rs`; `game/kicking.rs`).
- How are Discipline, Composure, Concentration, Motor, Toughness read? On a 1-20 scale against the level: 10 + (rating - norm) / 2.6; norms NFL 67, FBS 48, FCS 42, small college 37, high school 29, plus Toughness +3, Motor +1.5, Composure +1, Concentration +4.5, Competitiveness +5.5, Physicality -0.5 (`game/rules.rs`, `trait_norm`; `game/roster.rs`, `trait20`).
- Are field goals tracked? No: a roll of the kick angle against the posts and the leg against the distance (`game/kicking.rs`, `field_goal`). Kick and punt returns are tracked plays (`run_return`).
- How do tendencies act? Each check reads lean = (tendency - 10.5) / 9.5, from -1 to +1; a followed instruction sets the tendency, stress slides it up to a third back toward his old habit (`game/sim.rs`, `tend`, `lean`).
- How much is a Dynamic Link worth? 5 rating points at full strength, only in its own situations; stacked links count the strongest in full, the next at half, a third at a quarter (`links.rs`, `edge`, `FULL`).
- When does a tired starter come out? Below his position's energy line (EDGE 0.90, DT 0.84, WR/TE 0.74, LB/CB/S 0.68, RB 0.60, others 0.5), if a fresh backup is within 60 × (line - energy) rating points, 600 × for an edge, and no other tired man has already been spelled by him (one backup spells one starter) (`game/flow.rs`, `threshold`, `pick`, `EDGE_LINE`, `EDGE_ALLOW`; minor bug 358: at 0.84 and 60 the best edges played 88-90% of the team's snaps, real p90 77%).
- Does a player's week of practice matter? Through his prep word, for every team: his busts and run misfits × 1.33 (Unprepared) to × 0.93 (Sharp), his read up to 0.1 s later, a quarterback up to a third longer on each read. It changes no rating (`game/roster.rs`, `GamePlayer.prep`; `training/prep.rs`, `game_factor`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`, `quarterback`). See [training.md](training.md).
- Is the win chance on screen what the coaches read? No: the screens' chance is its own model (`game/win_chance.rs`, `chance`); play calls still read `live::win_prob`, unchanged. See "The game boards' records and the win chance" below.
- Does the engine's speed work change any game? No. What it saves is work whose answer is already fixed or thrown away: a man's tendency read once a play, a block's edge kept until the block changes, buffers kept between snaps, a play caller's weights worked out once a game. Every draw, every float sum's order and every tie stays as it was. `engine_hash` proves it: the same games played on two builds must give the same fingerprint for every part of every game (`crates/afm-tools/src/bin/engine_hash.rs`). See "Speed: what is worked out once".

## How a snap runs

- `run_snap` builds the agents (offense first), aligns the defense, reads the play, rolls busts and misfits, gives every man a job, then ticks blocks, the quarterback, the defense, routes, the carrier, tackles and movement until the ball is dead (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).
- His ratings for the snap: rating × (1 - fatigue share × tiredness) + edge, clamped 1-99. The edge is momentum minus the fill-in cost of a spot he doesn't know (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`; `game/flow.rs`, `run_scrimmage_call`, `unfamiliar`).
- Top speed, burst and cut come from his raw ratings and weight; the edge doesn't move them, fatigue does (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`).
- Road noise: every offensive player loses 11 × crowd × focus points of Football IQ, focus = clamp(1.6 - 0.06 × Concentration20, 0.4, 1.6) (`run_snap`, `NOISE_LOAD`).
- Crowd, wherever this page uses it: the home crowd × the road offense's `prep.noise`, 0 at home or on a neutral site. `prep.noise` is 1 for AI teams; your week's crowd-noise days set yours, 0.9-1.15 (`game/flow.rs`, `home_crowd`; `training/seat.rs`, `week_habits`).
- Road receivers leave late on the silent count: + 0.38 × crowd × clamp(1 - 0.05 × (Concentration20 - 10), 0.5, 1.5) s on each route of a called pass, standing at the line before their release (`SILENT_RELEASE`, `silent_late`; 0.3 s and already running before every receiver had a release, minor bug 135). With the noise load, the busts and the silent count it is fitted to the real home margin: 2.3 NFL points over 6,000 `match_check` games at a 0.65 crowd (se 0.21; real 2.1, nflverse 2021-2025) and 2.8 FBS (se 0.25; real 2.4 in conference games); real road offenses lose it through the air (completions 63.8% against 65.2%, yards an attempt 6.95 against 7.15, runs about even), the engine's road passing loses 1.3 points of completions and 6.9% of the yards an attempt (minor bug 143: 0.8 and 1.2 points before; proposed, not confirmed).
- Seeing: a cone of half-angle 1.2 + 0.006 × (rating - 50) radians, read with Pocket Presence for the passer, Ball Carrier Vision for the carrier, Play Recognition for everyone else; anything within 2 yards is felt (`game/sim.rs`, `sees`).
- `pro()`: some quarterback, catch-radius and decision reads count every point from 68 up and a quarter of the gap below 68 (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `pro`).
- Chemistry is 0-1 with 0.45 as typical (`game/roster.rs`, `TYPICAL_CHEM`).
- The score moves habits (`game/flow.rs`, `mood`). late = clamp((quarter length - game left) / (0.67 × quarter length), 0, 1). Gamble: +2.5 × late down 9+, +1.5 × late behind with under a third of a quarter left, -2 × late up 9+, -1.5 × late ahead with under a third left. It adds to every tendency except Patient or downhill, Contact willingness and Gets his hands up (`tendencies.rs`, `Tend::gamble`; `game/sim.rs`, `tend`).
- Stakes = 0.5 in a playoff game + 0.4 × late when within 8 points with under 0.55 of a quarter left; stress = clamp(stakes + 0.8 × tiredness + 0.05 × (10 - Composure20), 0, 1) slides a habit stress / 3 of the way back to his old one (`mood`; `game/sim.rs`, `tend`).

### Reading the play

- Each defender's read time = max(0.12, 0.22 + 0.0075 × (100 - Play Recognition + level gap) + 0.12 × U - 0.14 × lean(Downhill) + fake + film + job + prep + motion + 0.12 × novelty) seconds (`run_snap`).
  - Level gap: the Play Recognition norm at his level minus FBS's (NFL +19, high school -19), so a typical defender reads alike at every level (`run_snap`; `game/rules.rs`, `trait_norm`).
  - Fake (play action or a draw): (0.15 + 0.012 × (100 - PR) × U) × sell × bite × max(1 + 0.9 × lean(Downhill), 0.1); sell = clamp(1 + 0.01 × (QB Ball Handling - 60), 0.6, 1.4); bite = clamp(1.5 - 0.05 × Discipline20, 0.5, 1.5).
  - Film: ±0.16 × keyed, sooner when what the look tells matches the play; keyed = belief lean × (0.5 + 0.5 × film study / 20) × clamp(PR / 80, 0.3, 1.2) (`run_snap`; `game/calling.rs`, `belief`).
  - Job: 0.12 × (0.9 - his knowledge of the front or coverage family). Jet or orbit motion: +0.10 × bite for linebackers.
  - Prep: 0.3 × (his prep factor - 1): +0.1 s Unprepared, +0.06 Underprepared, +0.024 Patchy, -0.0075 Prepared, -0.021 Sharp (`run_snap`; `training/prep.rs`, `game_factor`).
  - Downhill applies to linebackers and safeties only.

### Busts and misfits

- Bust chance = ((1 - know) × 0.05 + (60 - FIQ)₊ × 0.0006 + noise + check + install + lapse) × call × chem × prep (`run_snap`).
  - noise (road offense) = 0.06 × crowd × max(1.3 - FIQ / 100, 0.3) (minor bug 143: 0.03); check (defense against trips, bunch, empty or motion) = 0.04 × (1 - know).
  - install = 0.04 × (plan load - absorb)₊, absorb = clamp((FIQ - 30) / 45, 0.2, 1.3) (`game/roster.rs`, `absorb`, `plan`).
  - lapse = 0.03 × (1 - energy) × (10 - Concentration20) / 9.
  - call = clamp(1 - 0.006 × (Communication - 60), 0.75, 1.25) of the QB (offense), the center (the line), the best communicator among linebackers and safeties (defense).
  - chem (WR, TE, RB, CB, S) = 1 - 0.45 × (chemistry - 0.45).
  - prep = his week's prep factor from his latest word: None 1, Unprepared 1.33, Underprepared 1.2, Patchy 1.08, Prepared 0.975, Sharp 0.93 (`game/roster.rs`, `prep_of`; `training/prep.rs`, `game_factor`).
- Run-fit misfit (linebackers and DBs on runs) = clamp((0.035 + 0.002 × max(65 - PR, -15) + 0.06 × (1 - front knowledge) + 0.003 × (10 - Discipline20) + 0.04 misdirection + 0.03 jet + 0.05 × novelty) × prep, 0.01, 0.35). A misfit fills the wrong gap or comes late (`run_snap`, `defend`).

## Bodies and movement

- Forty = 5.56 - 0.0124 × (0.75 Speed + 0.25 Acceleration) + 0.0033 × (weight - 220), the combine fit without noise (`game/roster.rs`, `forty`).
- Top speed = 40 / max(forty - sprint_tau + 0.06, 3.4) yd/s; sprint_tau = clamp(0.78 + 0.003 × (60 - Acc) + 0.0006 × (weight - 220), 0.55, 1.0) (`top_speed`, `sprint_tau`).
- Accel = 4.65 + 0.008 a point of Acceleration above 60 (0.0046 below) - 0.006 × (weight - 220), clamped 2.2-7.5 yd/s² (`accel`).
- Cut = 0.99 + 0.004 a point of Agility above 60 (0.0022 below) - 0.002 × (weight - 220), clamped 0.4-1.7; Sudden adds 0.02 × share (`cut`; `game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`).
- Steering: speeding up follows the sprint curve, braking is accel × (1.2 + 0.7 × cut), a turn spends accel × cut sideways; Bend helps a tight turn at speed (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::steer`, `Agent::brake`).
- Bodies push apart below 0.8 yards, scaled by clamp(1 - 0.008 × (Traffic - 60), 0.55, 1.35) (`Agent::traffic`, `advance`).
- Burner: +1.25% top speed × share for the carrier 2+ yards past the line or a returner, a chaser behind the carrier, a route past 9 yards, a man defender trailing deep, the target of a ball in the air past 9 yards (`link_speeds`, `BURNER`).

## The raw burner's roles (lane 4, S10-S10d)

- Who he is: the fastest backup receiver or back by deep speed (0.4 Acceleration + 0.6 Speed, `game/roster.rs`, `deep_speed`) when he beats the top starting receiver by more than 1 and the starters' mean by more than 4; lean = clamp((his - mean - 2) / 10, 0, 1) (`roster.rs`, `burner`, kept on the depth chart).
- Returns: the returner rating reads 0.4 × deep speed in place of Speed (`roster.rs`). Punt gunners: the two best non-starting receivers, corners and safeties by 0.5 deep speed + 0.3 Release + 0.2 Strength, filled with corners when short (`game/flow.rs`, `Side::gunners`, `punt_units`).
- Snaps on offense: on a jet sweep he takes the motion man's place with chance 0.5 + 0.4 × lean; on a deep pass the Z (else the X) with 0.25 + 0.5 × lean, keyed per snap (`game/flow.rs`, `speed_package`).
- How the defense respects him (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `align_defense`, with the plan's fear, `Tactics::fear`): his corner's cushion + 6 × (fear - 0.5) in press, + 3 × fear off (a Cover 2 or Tampa 2 cloud corner keeps his); a slot corner over him + 3 × fear; the single-high safety leans toward him by clamp((his y - ball) × 0.3 × fear, -5, 5) and sits 2 × fear deeper; two-high safeties half the lean, 1.5 × fear deeper, the one on his side another fear yards (proposed, not confirmed).
- The proof (`match_check`, `AFM_BURNER=pts` adds pts to each team's second receiver's Speed and Acceleration): 3,000 NFL games, seed 7, +12 against +0. His teammates' catches under 10 air yards 12.38 to 12.71 a team game, their yards after the catch 4.97 to 5.01 a catch, two-high shells 59.4% to 62.5% of passes; his own catches 4.71 to 4.48 (bracketed more), points 26.30 to 26.42.

## Line blocking (run)

What it reads: run blocking, strength, hands, balance, burst and mass against shedding, hands, anchor, strength, recognition and mass. What it does not read: arm length in inches (only the Length link), height, Speed, the blocker's Agility or Football IQ.

- Start: get-off = 0.6 Acceleration + 0.4 Anticipation + 12 × lean(Snap timing) + Get-off + Length (defender); run set = 0.5 Run Blocking + 0.3 Acceleration + 0.2 Strength + Get-off + Length (blocker); w0 = -0.75 (`RUN_BLOCK_START`; -0.4 before minor bug 253, -0.1 before minor bug 164) + 0.006 × (get-off + silent count - set) + front fit + N(0, 0.18) (`game/sim.rs`, `engage`).
  - Silent count: a road line in its first second gives the defender 36 × crowd × focus (minor bug 143: 24), focus = clamp(1 - 0.05 × (Concentration20 - 10), 0.5, 1.5) (`engage`, `SILENT_COUNT`).
  - Front fit (DE/DT only): a two-gap DT (a 3-4 front) +0.05 against gap runs, -0.02 against zone; a one-gap lineman -0.02 against gap runs, +0.03 against zone (`front_fit`). Duo counts as zone here (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).
- Each tick: edge = shed power - block power; w += 0.035 × edge × 0.1 + 0.6 × √0.1 × N(0, 1) (`update_blocks`, `RUN_EDGE`).
  - Block power = 0.45 Run Blocking + 0.2 Strength + 0.15 Hand Use + 0.1 Balance + 0.1 Acceleration + 0.08 × (weight - 300) + Low center/Anchor (`block_base`, `block_gift`).
  - Shed power = 0.4 Block Shedding + 0.2 Hand Use + 0.15 Anchor + 0.1 Strength + 0.15 Play Recognition + 0.08 × (weight - 280) (`shed_power`).
  - Before his read time a run defender drifts 0.12 a second toward losing (`update_blocks`).
  - The runner within 2.2 yards and the block not sealed (w > -1): +2 × 0.9 × max(0.5 + w, 1.0) a second (1.0 before minor bug 253), the shed to make the play (`update_blocks`, `SHED_RUNNER`; before minor bug 164 only with w > 0, at 2 × 0.5 × max(0.5 + w, 0.2)).
  - A defender 1.5+ yards past the line (a linebacker someone climbed to) feels the runner from 3.5 yards and gets 2 × 1.0 × max(0.5 + w, 0.2) more (`SHED_SECOND`, `SHED_SECOND_RANGE`).
  - The runner 1.5+ yards past him: he lets go with chance 0.18 × clamp(1 + 0.04 × (Motor20 - 10) - 0.006 × (blocker Hand Use - 60), 0.5, 1.6) a tick.
  - A corner, nickel or safety who has read the run, with the runner past the line and more than 3 yards to his side: he works off the block with chance 0.6 a tick and pursues (`SPACE_BLOCK_GAP`, `SPACE_BLOCK_LEAVE`; minor bug 163); a linebacker with 0.8 (`LB_LEAVE`, bug 305: he was blocked to the whistle 1.7 times a run, now 1.0).
  - A linebacker 1.5+ yards past the line slips a climbing blocker's first hands 0.4 more often (`lands_block`, `LB_SLIP`, capped at 0.7).
- Double teams: edge -= 0.6 × the helper's block power - 20 - 0.3 × (anchor - 60); anchor = 0.6 Anchor + 0.2 Strength + 0.2 Balance + 0.08 × (weight - 300) + Anchor/Big man (`update_blocks`, `anchor`).
  - Combos: 2 on inside zone, zone read, duo and draw; 1 on outside zone, power, counter and iso (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_blocking`).
  - The helper climbs to his linebacker when w < -0.15 after 0.35 s, after 1.0 s, or when the linebacker is within 2 yards of the line or 2.5 of him (`run_snap`).
- w ≥ 1 is a shed. w ≤ -1 is a sealed block held at -1, or a pancake with chance 0.05 × finish a tick, finish = clamp(1 + 0.04 × (Physicality20 - 10) + 0.006 × (Strength - 60) - 0.008 × (defender Balance - 60), 0.4, 1.8) (`update_blocks`).
- The pair moves toward the carrier at 1.6 × w yd/s (`update_blocks`). The push fades with the block's age: driven back (w < 0) to nothing over 2 s, at most 1.6 yards (`RUN_PUSH_BACK`); driving his blocker back (w > 0), the push along the field over 1 s, while the slide toward the carrier goes on (`RUN_PUSH_UP`). Before minor bug 164 it never faded (a man pushed 11 yards).
- Blocks in space (climbs, pulls, screens, returns): a set, square blocker misses with clamp(0.06 + 0.004 × (slip - skill), 0.02, 0.25), any other with clamp(0.17 + 0.006 × (slip - skill) + 0.05 × (speed across - 2)₊, 0.05, 0.55); skill = 0.5 Blocking in Space + 0.5 Agility + Big man; slip = 0.5 Agility + 0.5 Block Shedding + Sudden. A DL moving under 3 yd/s, or anyone under 2 within 1.5 yards of the line (anywhere before minor bug 164), is always hit (`game/sim.rs`, `lands_block`, `PHONE_BOOTH`).
- Receivers' stalk blocks: the nearest man within 9 yards who isn't a DE or DT and stands 1.5+ yards off the line (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_blocking`; minor bug 164).

## Pass rush and pressure

What it reads: the rush move's ratings against the matching set, mass on the bull rush and in the get-off, a tackle's arm length against speed and finesse, Football IQ for the move pick, Motor for the counter. What it does not read: height outside an edge's bend (E1), the rusher's arm inches outside Length (and an edge's finish, under Sacks), the rusher's Speed outside the speed rush, a slow-burst edge's own Acceleration on his rush path (below).

- Start: w0 = -0.42 + 0.006 × (get-off + silent - pass set) + front fit + N(0, 0.18), pass set = 0.5 Pass Blocking + 0.3 Agility + 0.2 Football IQ + Get-off + Length; a two-gap DT starts -0.03 (`engage`, `front_fit`).
  - Pass-rush get-off (minor bugs 285, 286): a × Acceleration + (0.6 - a) × (T(Acceleration) + 0.8 (Finesse Rush - T) + 0.2 (Flexibility - T)) + 0.4 Anticipation - (0.64 + 2.7 for a defensive tackle) × a × (weight - his level's typical man's at his position) for an interior rusher (the tackle's two-gap read, `TWO_GAP`, minor bug 285) + 12 × lean(Snap timing) + Get-off + Length, a = 0.6, an edge on contain 0 (`EDGE_BURST`, 0.15 before the edge lane; Flexibility's part `EDGE_GETOFF_BEND`, 0.5 before E1), T his level's typical man at his position, so the typical edge gets off as before. The weight term is his Acceleration as his 10-yard split shows it at his weight (`links::ten_split`); an edge's weight already costs him on the arc (burst and cut, `game/roster.rs`).
- The move: the rusher takes the best of speed, power and finesse by (rush - block) + N(0, max(11 - 0.09 × FIQ, 2)) (`pick_move`).
  - Speed: 0.4 Finesse Rush + 0.2 Speed + 0.15 Acc + 0.15 Flexibility + 0.1 Hand Use + Bend (an edge on contain keeps a quarter of the Speed and Acceleration, `EDGE_ARC_LEGS`, half before the edge lane; the rest reads the typical man's Speed and Acceleration plus 0.65, 0.1 and 0.25 of his Finesse Rush, Flexibility and Hand Use over the typical man's, `EDGE_ARC_BEND`; his 0.15 Flexibility is 0.05 of his own plus 0.1 of the typical man's Flexibility and his Finesse Rush over the typical man's, `EDGE_SPEED_BEND`; and he adds 0.2 × (height - T height)₊ × (Flexibility - T), `EDGE_TALL_BEND`, E1: a tall man who bends gets his pads under the tackle's hands), against 0.5 Pass Blocking + 0.2 Agility + 0.15 Flexibility + 0.15 Hand Use + Big man + a tackle's (arm - 34 in the NFL, the pivot moving with each level's typical tackle) (`rush_power`, `block_base`, `block_gift`; minor bugs 286, 288).
  - Power: 0.4 Power Rush + 0.25 Strength + 0.15 Hand Use + 0.1 Balance + 0.1 Block Shedding + 0.08 × (weight - 280) + Anchor/Battering ram + for an edge 0.3 × clamp(weight - T weight, 0, 20) / 10 × (Strength - T) (E3, `EDGE_STRENGTH_MASS`, `EDGE_MASS_SPAN`: strength pays with mass, counted up to 20 lb over the typical edge, about the real heavy edge; at or under the typical weight Strength keeps its full value), against 0.4 PB + 0.3 Anchor + 0.15 Strength + 0.15 Balance + 0.08 × (weight - 300) + Anchor.
  - Finesse: 0.45 Finesse Rush + 0.25 Agility + 0.2 Hand Use + 0.1 Block Shedding + Big man/Sudden (an edge on contain 0.12 Agility, `EDGE_FINESSE_AGILITY`, the other 0.13 reading the typical man's Agility plus 0.6 and 0.4 of his Finesse Rush and Hand Use over the typical man's; minor bug 328), against 0.45 PB + 0.3 Hand Use + 0.15 Agility + 0.1 Football IQ + Big man/Length + a tackle's (arm - 34).
  - An edge on contain adds 10 × lean(Upfield); every rusher loses 2.5 × lean(Hands up) (`rush_power`).
- Each tick w += 0.032 × edge × 0.1 + 0.58 × √0.1 × N(0, 1) (`update_blocks`).
- An edge's burst on a pass rush (minor bug 286): on a pass play an edge on contain runs his rush path, before and past the line, on the quicker of his own burst (sprint build-up and grip) and the typical edge's at his weight and fatigue, so a man slower off the mark than the typical edge rushes as the typical edge does and a quicker one keeps his own (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `rush_feet`). The two are compared as bursts, so the day's form never decides it (his burst reads no form). His weight, turn, top speed and fatigue stay his own. Real edges' 10-yard split says nothing about their pressures once talent is known, so a slow first step costs nothing, while the quick freak keeps his edge (Cain 2026-11-08: no caps on rare freaks). Reading every edge's burst, weight and turn as the typical edge's put the A/B split and weight rows near real but failed the freak check: heavy edges took 35% of the top 10% of pressure seasons (11% of all), light ones none (8% of all; real 18% of the top, 19% of all), and as recruited 10 lb added +0.40 pts of pressures a snap (real flat).
- Counter: once, when w < -0.2 after 0.8 s, with chance clamp(0.45 + 0.008 × (FIQ - 60) + 0.03 × (Motor20 - 10), 0.15, 0.85); kept only if it beats the move he is on (`update_blocks`).
- A shed inside 2.5 s, with the QB still holding the ball, is a rush win. The beaten blocker can't square him again for clamp(0.8 + 0.15 if a speed rush - 0.010 × (Agility - 60) - 0.006 × (PB - 60), 0.35, 1.3) s and until he is back in front (`update_blocks`, `can_square`).
- The pocket: an engaged pair moves toward the QB at (0.25 + 0.65 × (w + 1) / 2) yd/s, × 1.4 on a bull rush, × 0.7 otherwise (`update_blocks`).
- Protection: each lineman takes the nearest rusher within 3.2 yards across; backs and tight ends take the rest (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `pass_protection`).
  - Help on the plan's rusher: a spare blocker slides to him; with none, a back or tight end within 5 yards across chips him, 0.6 s into his route, taking 0.25 × clamp(0.6 + PB / 150, 0.6, 1.3) off w and halving his speed (`slide_to`, `run_route`, `CHIP_TIME`, `CHIP_W`).
  - A stunt is passed off with clamp(0.35 + 0.45 × line chemistry + 0.004 × (the pair's FIQ - 60), 0.2, 0.92); the looper waits 0.45 s (`stunt_pickup`, `assign_defense`).
  - A back picks up a blitzer with clamp(0.85 + 0.01 × (FIQ - 60) + 0.004 × (PB - 60) - 0.008 × (blitzer's Blitzing - 60), 0.4, 0.98) (`picks_up`).
- Pressure on the QB: each free rusher he sees (or feels, chance 0.15 + 0.005 × Pocket Presence) within 4.5 yards adds (4.5 - d) / 4.5; an engaged rusher winning (w > 0.3) within 3 yards adds 1.0 × winning × (3 - d) / 3 (`POCKET_FEEL`; 0.6 before minor bug 358, when NFL sacks sat at 2.57 a team game, real 2.41). Pressured is logged when the nearest gets inside 3.2 yards after 0.6 s; the nearest and any free or winning rusher inside 2.6 are credited (`quarterback`, minor bug 284: the old rule, inside 2.6 from the snap with everyone inside 2.8 credited, counted a tackle over the center next to a quarterback under center, 60% of all pressures and 8.6 times PFR's for tackles).
- Batted balls: each rusher in the lane 1-8 yards ahead bats with 0.17 × (1 - offset) × reach × passer × arc × hands (`batter`, `BAT`).
  - reach = clamp(1 + 0.015 (Jumping - 60) + 0.08 (arm - 33.5) + 0.06 (height - 75) + 0.01 (Anticipation - 60) + 0.015 Length, 0.3, 2); passer = clamp(1 - 0.06 (T height - 75) - 0.008 (Quick Release + Pocket frame - 60), 0.4, 1.8), T his level's typical passer (his own height left out: real batted balls don't move with it, minor bug 277; the level's rate stays); arc = clamp(1.3 - distance / 30, 0.3, 1.2); hands = clamp(1 + 1.1 × lean(Hands up), 0.15, 2.4).

## Sacks

What it reads: who gets there (the blocks above), the rusher's reach (his Tackling and Pursuit, an edge's Flexibility, a long-armed edge's arm and weight, an edge's weight), the QB's elusiveness, agility and strength against the rusher's tackling, his Pocket Presence (minor bug 283), and the QB's weight (A3: - 0.7 × size_cost from 222 lb). What it does not read: an interior rusher's weight, the QB's Speed (that comes in through his clock), the rush ratings.

- A rusher tries when he is free within 1.25 + bend + finish + weight yards, or winning his block (w > 0.45) within 1.2 + arms + finish + weight, and hasn't tried in 0.6 s (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `sack_check`; the edge lane, 2026-11-08, bug 339, E1 and E2; weight from minor bug 358).
  - finish = 0.2 × (0.5 × his (0.5 Tackling + 0.5 Pursuit) + 0.5 × the same for his level's typical man at his position - 60)₊ / 10 (`FINISH_REACH`, `FINISH_MARK`, `FINISH_OWN`). Every rusher. An NFL edge is about 74 in both, an FBS one 54 and a high school one 33, so it lifts the NFL (sacks a pressure 0.22 to about 0.27, real 0.28) and barely moves college and high school. Read on his own in full, the best finishers ran away with it (34 sacks in a season; real best 22.5 and 23).
  - bend = clamp(0.2 × (Flexibility - T) / 10, -0.3, 0.6) for an edge on contain (`BEND_REACH`, E1): the man who bends the corner flattens to the quarterback. The 3-cone turns into sacks a pressure, not pressures.
  - arms = 0.18 × (arm - T arm)₊ × clamp(1 + (weight - T weight) / 13, 0, 3) for an edge (`ARM_REACH`, E2): the power rusher's long-arm stab. Short-armed men lose nothing; light men gain little.
  - weight = -0.07 × (weight - T weight) / 10 for an edge (`WEIGHT_REACH`, minor bug 358): real heavy edges finish less of their pressures (-0.49 pts of sacks a pressure per 10 lb held); the engine had them finishing more (+0.53), so heavy power rushers ran away with the sack tail. Pure, it is now -0.41. It reads no strength or force.
- Escape chance = sigmoid(-2.4 + (escape - tackle) / 12 - 1 if unseen + 1.2 if the rusher is still engaged) (`sack_check`).
  - escape = 0.4 Elusiveness + 0.3 Agility + 0.3 Strength (+ Pocket frame against an engaged rusher); tackle = 0.6 Tackling + 0.2 Strength + 0.2 Agility + Length.
- Strip sack = (0.06 seen, 0.16 unseen) × (1.3 - Ball Security / 120) × (1.2 from the blind side, 0.8 otherwise); the blind side is his left as a righty, his right as a lefty (`sack_check`).
- The nearest other rusher within 2.25 yards of the QB, free or winning his own pass block (w > 0.45), shares the sack (`SHARE_RADIUS`, minor bug 358: it was a second tackler within 1.1 yards, which never happened, so no sack was ever shared; real 10.8% of NFL sacks are, now 10.8%): half a sack (and half a tackle for loss) to each of the two, never more than two, as the NCAA and the NFL keep it; the man who brings him down has the tackle and the second an assist. A tackle for loss on a run is one to a lone tackler and half to each man in on it (`game/stats.rs`; minor bug 36: thirds and sixths showed in the record books). QB injury check at share 0.8 seen, 1.3 unseen, force = rusher mass × speed (`sack_check`).

## The quarterback: reads, decisions, the throw

What it reads: Play Recognition, Decisions, Pocket Presence, Composure, Football IQ, Eye Manipulation, Quick Release, Footwork, Anticipation, Arm Strength, the three accuracies, Touch, Throw on the Move, Speed (scrambles), hidden consistency, chemistry. What it does not read: Concentration, Toughness, height (except batted balls and the Pocket frame link).

- The drop: top of the drop at quick 1.25 s, medium 1.6, deep 2.1, boot 2.2, screen 1.4, bubble 0.75, +0.35 with play action, + clamp(-0.0015 × (Footwork - 60), -0.06, 0.08) (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).
  - A bad shotgun snap costs 0.3-0.6 s, chance clamp(0.004 - 0.0001 × (center's Shotgun Snap - 60), 0.001, 0.012); a fumbled snap is not modelled (`run_snap`).
- First read: the concept's order, except the best receiver (each man rated at his own spot, receiver, tight end, back or fullback; minor bug 137) is fed first with chance min(0.1 + 0.02 × his rating edge, 0.55), or the plan's target corner's man 50% of the time (`run_snap`, `FEED_BASE`, `FEED_MAX`).
- Time on each read = clamp(0.62 - 0.0045 × (iq - 50), 0.3, 0.95) × shown × (1 + 0.6 × lean(Hold)) × prep; iq = 0.5 PR + 0.5 Decisions; shown = (1 - 0.2 × (pre-snap read - 0.45)) × 1.15 if a disguise fooled him; prep = his prep factor, 1.33 Unprepared to 0.93 Sharp (`quarterback`).
- His clock = clamp(3.6 - 0.01 × (Ts Decisions - 60) - 0.004 × (Pocket Presence - 60), 3.1, 4.1), Ts his level's typical starter's (minor bug 283: within a level Decisions no longer shortens it, since a better decider already gets through his reads sooner and the shorter clock sent him to the checkdown early) + 0.3 on a deep drop + 1.0 × lean(Hold) + 0.2 × (Speed - his level's typical passer's) / 10 (61 in the NFL; `quarterback`, `LEGS_HOLD`; minor bug 276: real fast quarterbacks extend plays and take more sacks). The legs buy time for the throw more than for the run: his choice to take off waits half the legs' extra time on the clock and on the urgent line (`late_run`, `urgent_run`, `LEGS_RUN`). All of it and a fast man's scrambles came late and lost their extra yards; none of it and they rose 1.8 times real's slope.
- Hot read: pressure over 0.6 before 2 s sends him back to read 1 (always above 45 Football IQ, else 40%) (`quarterback`).
- Urgent: the first free rusher he knows of is under 0.85 - 0.004 × (Composure - 60) - 0.07 × (Speed - his level's typical passer's) / 10 + 0.1 × (0.6 × (Football IQ - Ts) + 0.4 × (Play Recognition - Ts)) / 10 s away: a passer who knows the pressure sees the blitzer sooner (`quarterback`, `LEGS_WAIT`, `BLITZ_ID`, `BLITZ_ID_LOOK`; minor bug 283).
- How open a man looks = window + size room + noise × N(0, 1) (`quarterback`, `window`, `size_window`).
  - window: the gap the nearest defender can't close in the ball's flight (no reaction time for his man defender, 0.3 s for others, + 0.3 × misread for one who sees the passer's eyes) and anyone under the throwing lane (+ 1.0 × misread yards of room he thinks that man leaves), capped at 8; misread = (Ts field read - his) / 10, field read = 0.7 Decisions + 0.3 Play Recognition, so a poor reader sees more room than there is and a good one less (`MISREAD_EYES`, `MISREAD_LANE`, `field_read`; minor bug 283); ball speed 16.5 + 0.13 × Arm Strength yd/s; flight 0.28 + distance / (speed × (1 - 0.005 × (distance - 20)₊)); a spot past 25 + 0.45 × Arm is never open (`window`).
  - size room: on downfield and red-zone throws, 0.07 yd an inch of high reach past 2 (max 1.2) × sense × hands; sense = clamp(0.6 + 0.01 × (0.5 FIQ + 0.5 Dec - 50), 0.4, 1.2), hands = clamp(0.6 + Contested Catch / 150, 0.6, 1.3) (`size_window`, `high_reach`).
  - noise = max(1.6 - 0.017 × pro(field read + Pocket frame), 0.2), field read = 0.7 Decisions + 0.3 Play Recognition (`READ_DECIDE`; it was half each, minor bug 283) × (1 + 0.6 × panic) × (1 - 0.15 × (pre-read - 0.45)) + 0.2 if fooled; panic = pressure × clamp(1.6 - 1.4 × Composure / 100, 0.15, 1.5) (`quarterback`).
- He throws when it looks above need (`quarterback`):
  - normal = 0.3 + caution - 0.18 × seconds since set - 0.44 × panic + 0.1 on read 1, caution = 0.02 × (Ts - T) + 0.008 × (T - 60) - 0.06 × (Decisions - Ts), T his level's typical passer's Decisions and Ts its typical starter's (`DECISIONS_CAUTION`, `DECISIONS_TRUST`, minor bug 283): the typical starter keeps his room, a better decider trusts a tighter window because he sees it as it is (at +0.02 a point, more room for the better man, better decisions threw shorter and held the ball into sacks, and EPA a dropback fell); quick game reads the first two routes as one picture.
  - late or out of reads = 1.35 - 0.2 × seconds since set - 0.44 × panic; urgent = 0.3 + caution - 0.44 × panic - 0.2.
  - need falls 0.05 a yard past 12 yards deep (max 1.2) and by lean(Gunslinger) (+0.4 × lean deeper than 12); before he is late, a holder wants 0.5 × lean(Hold) more, 0.9 more under 10 yards; a gunslinger prefers the deeper of two open men.
  - need also falls (minor bugs 275, 277, 280, 283; sizes proposed, not confirmed): past 15 yards, 0.4 × (receiver's deep speed - the level's typical receiver's) / 10 + 0.4 × (Arm Strength - the level's typical passer's) / 10 - 0.1 (`DEEP_LEGS`, `DEEP_ARM`, `DEEP_BASE`); inside (within 9 yards of the middle, 4+ deep) and from the pocket (not in the scramble drill, within 4 yards of the ball's spot), 0.7 × (passer's height - the level's typical passer's) / 2 (`OVER_LINE`); 5+ yards deep or in the red zone, 1.0 × ((height - his level's typical man's at his position) + 2 × (arm - his)) / 4 - 0.35 for the receiver, no base in high school (`BIG_TRUST`, `BIG_BASE`, `big_base`). The bases: he takes his best target, so a trust that differs from man to man makes him throw more even where it averages zero (without them the NFL's 20+ yard share went from 11.1% to 12.2% and throwaways from 2.3% to 1.7%).

## Routes against coverage (separation)

What it reads: Route Running, Release, Agility, Strength against Man/Zone Coverage, Press, Play Recognition, Anticipation, Agility, Flexibility. What it does not read: the receiver's height or weight (until the catch point), Speed beyond his own top speed.

## The catch and the contested catch

What it reads: Catching, Body Control, Jumping, height, arm length, Contested Catch, Strength, the Length and Highpointer links, Contact tendency over the middle, weather, the passer's Touch. What it does not read: Concentration, Toughness, Route Running, Speed, weight (except through the jump).

- In the lane: a defender within 1.1 yards of a short, flat ball's path touches it with 0.6 × (1 - gap / 1.1) × (0.5 + coverage / 200) × (1, or 0.35 without eyes on the QB) (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `catch_point`).
- Catch radius = 0.95 + 0.015 × (T height - 72) + 0.02 × (T arm - 32) + 0.0075 × (height - T height) + 0.004 × (pro(Jumping) - 50) + 0.01 × (arm - T arm) + 0.0015 × (pro(Body Control) - 60) yards, T his level's typical man at his position (`catch_point`; height and arm were 0.015 and 0.02 an inch, which T keeps, minor bug 275: real taller, longer receivers catch a smaller share, from the harder balls they get).
- Off target: past radius + reach (1.3 + 0.4 × clamp(nearest defender - 1.5, 0, 4)), out of bounds, or a jump ball over his high point by 2+ inches (`catch_point`).
- High reach = 0.82 × height + arm + 4 + vert × (1 - 0.35 × pace²) × (0.6 if he can't see the ball) inches; vert = max(20.9 + 0.182 × (max(Jumping, 0.95 × Acceleration) + Highpointer) - 0.035 × (weight - 220), 8) (`high_reach`).
- The defender at the catch point (nearest to the ball, reacted, within his reach 1.05 + 0.012 × (T height - 72) + 0.02 × (T arm - 31.5) + 0.025 × (height - T height) + 0.004 × (Jumping - 50) + 0.10 × (arm - T arm), +0.6 if he jumped the route, T his level's typical man at his position; minor bug 274, the slopes were 0.012 and 0.02, which T keeps): size = e^(-0.5 × va) × (0.3 if the ball is over him), va = clamp((receiver high reach - his) / 10, ±1.5) × clamp((ball height - 72) / 30, 0, 1) (`catch_point`).
  - Breakup = clamp(closeness × (0.39 + 0.004 × (Ts - 60) + 0.012 × (coverage - Ts) + 0.003 × (Contested Catch + Highpointer - 60) + 0.003 × (Jumping - 60)) × size, 0, 0.75), coverage his job's (Man or Zone) and Ts its typical starter's at his position (`BREAKUP_COVER`; it was the better of the two at 0.004 from 60, minor bug 283); 6% of breakups are picks (`PBU_PICK`) (0.42 before the hit below took its share).

League breakups and completions held (match_check).
- The catch = 1 - max(0.15 - 0.00095 × Catching, 0.012), then (`catch_point`):
  - away from his frame (past 0.9 of the radius): × (1 - 0.6 × clamp(1 - 0.005 × (Body Control + Length - 60), 0.75, 1.25) × clamp((reach share - 0.9) / 1.6, 0, 1)).
  - contested (defender within 1.4 yards of the ball): × (1 - 0.32 × (1 - d / 1.4) × (1.25 - fight / 100)), fight = 0.5 × (Contested Catch + Highpointer) + 0.25 Strength + 0.25 Jumping + 18 × va; over the middle a contact-shy man also loses 0.12 × (1 - d / 1.4) × (-lean(Contact))₊.
  - rain or snow × 0.97; cold × (1 - 0.03 × unused); a hard ball (over 24 yd/s, within a yard) × (1 - 0.015 × clamp(1 - 0.02 × (Touch - 60), 0.4, 1.6)).
- At the sideline he gets his feet down with clamp(0.35 + 0.006 × (0.6 Body Control + 0.4 FIQ) - 0.15 × (feet needed - 1), 0.1, 0.95); the NFL needs 2 (`run_snap`; `game/rules.rs`, `for_level`).

## Drops

- A drop is a missed catch with the ball inside 0.8 of his radius and no defender within a yard (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `catch_point`). It reads the same Catching base, so a 99 still misses 5.6% of such balls and a 60 9.3%.
- A drop costs him 1 point of momentum (`game/flow.rs`, `resolve`, `edge_hit`).

## Interceptions

What it reads: the defender's hands (Catching, raised half the gap from his level's norm to the NFL's), coverage, Jumping, height, arms, whether he sees the ball or jumped the route. What it does not read: the defender's Anticipation outside the route jump, the QB's Decisions after the throw.

- The man who picks it off runs it back as a ball carrier (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `defend` skips him; minor bug 132: he chased himself and stopped every tick, 0.005 pick sixes a team game against real 0.05).
- Hands = Catching + 0.5 × (Catching norm at the NFL - at his level) (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `ball_hands`, `HANDS_LEVEL`).
- A lane touch is a pick with 0.3 × hands / 70; an off-target ball within 0.8 × (1 + 0.01 × (Jumping - 50)) of a defender with 0.18 × hands / 70 (`catch_point`).
- In front at the catch point: clamp(FRONT_PICK × closeness × (0.45 + hands / 110) × (0.45 if he doesn't see the ball) × (3.5 if he jumped the route) × size, 0, 0.7) (`catch_point`).
- Off the receiver's hands to a defender within 3 yards: TIP_PICK × (1 - d / 3) × (0.5 + hands / 100); a breakup is picked PBU_PICK of the time (`catch_point`). Where picks come from (minor bug 283): FTN charting (NFL 2023-24) has 87% of picks on throws it calls interception-worthy, 53% of those picked and 0.33% of the rest; the engine spread them over every throw, so a careful passer saved few. FRONT_PICK 0.27 (0.22 before), TIP_PICK 0.065 (0.13), PBU_PICK 0.06 (unchanged), with the league's picks held.
- The thrower loses 1 point of momentum, the interceptor gains 1 (`game/flow.rs`, `resolve`).

## Run lanes and the ball carrier

What it reads: Ball Carrier Vision, his speed and cut, the Patience, Bounce and Contact tendencies, the blocks. What it does not read: weight, Strength or Elusiveness while he picks his path.

- Reads: zone read right with clamp(0.55 + 0.005 × Decisions, 0.5, 0.97); RPO pull right with clamp(0.6 + 0.0045 × (0.6 Dec + 0.4 PR), 0.6, 0.97); option pitch right with clamp(0.6 + 0.004 × the same iq, 0.6, 0.96) once the key is within 3.2 yards (`read_option`, `rpo_pull`, `option_pitch`).

Each pass rusher keeps his gap 0-4 from the line's spots and whether he was a down lineman, picked up at the snap or in the stunt, with the protection (man, slide left or right, max at 7+ blockers) (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `chart_rush`; `game/chart.rs`, `gap_at`). It draws no random numbers, so a game plays the same with it.
- **Timeouts**: every logged snap keeps both sides' timeouts (`game/flow.rs`, `new_record`; `game/stats.rs`, `PlayRecord::timeouts`).
- **Throws on the move**: every pass keeps whether the passer was moving when he let it go (rolling out, off his spot), so growth from results can read his throw-on-the-move rating; it changes nothing in the play (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `throw`; `game/stats.rs`, `PlayRecord::moving`; [development.md](development.md), "Growth from results").
- **Where a throw went**: every pass keeps where across the field it was snapped and where it was aimed, in yards from the left sideline (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `PlayRecord::throw_y`); an aim past a sideline is kept as aimed.
- **The film's throws**: your coming opponents' film keeps each pass's air yards, yards after the catch, where across the field it was snapped and got to (half yards), time to throw, separation, thrown away and dropped (`world/tape.rs`, `Throw::of`).
- **Names in your kept games**: at the year's turn, before the men who left football are dropped from the world, each of your kept games keeps the id, name and position of its men among them, so last season's pages still name them (`world/seat_names.rs`, `keep`, called from `world/mod.rs` beside `storylines::keep_names`).
- **Each level's numbers**: each engine game adds both sides to its level's tally for the season (dropbacks, pressure, sacks, throws by depth, air yards, time to throw, runs, yards before contact, stuffed, 10+), this season and last (`world/charts.rs`, `Tally::add_game`, `Charts::add`).
- **Drives, red zone trips and the clock held** (counts only): after every engine game the play log is cut into drives (each drive ends in a touchdown, a field goal or a miss, a punt, a safety, an interception, a fumble, a return for a score, downs, the half or the game) (`game/drives.rs`, `drives`, called from `game/flow.rs`, `play_game_ctl`, through `GameStats::count_drives`). A drive with a snap at the opponent's 20 or closer is a red zone trip; a trip ending in a touchdown is a red zone TD (`TeamStats::red_zone_trips`, `red_zone_tds`). The game clock between snaps goes to the side with the ball (a kickoff's time to the side that receives it; tries left out; the quarter's last snap gets the rest of the quarter), kept as `TeamStats::clock_possession` beside `time_of_possession`, the ball in play (`clock_possession`). Field goals and punts keep a bad snap (`PlayRecord::bad_snap`). The game day page reads the same drives.
- **The world log**: with a log open, every game at every level is written to disk as it is recorded (the game row, every snap for college, NFL and other-league engine games, situation totals by season), and memory does not grow; it draws nothing, so a world plays the same with it on or off (`world/wlog/games.rs`; the format in `docs/reference/how-it-works/world-log.md`, "Games, snaps and situations"). The college book keeps 19 more of the game line's numbers: field goals by distance band, kickoffs and touchbacks, punt yards, punts inside the 20 and touchbacks, QB hits, hurries, rush wins by speed, power and finesse, and the time to throw (`world/records.rs`, `stats!`, `StatSet::from_line`).
- **The win chance the screens show** is its own model, not the coaches' (`live::win_prob` is unchanged and still what calls read) (`game/win_chance.rs`, `chance`):
  - The drive on the field first: touchdown chance from the spot 0.15 + 0.65 × e^(-yards to go / 28); a field goal try 0.2 + 0.4 / (1 + e^((yards - (range - 8)) / 6)) times the level's make at that distance; both scaled so the drive is worth the down and distance's expected points, read between the table's 5-yard rows (`drive`, `ep_at`).
  - Where the other side starts after a stall: a punt netting 42 (a touchback at the 20) from outside range, the spot of the kick inside it, a mix of the two within 6 yards either side of range (`drive`).
  - A short clock, from the real spot: a touchdown needs one snap per 6 yards; after the first, a snap takes 6 s of play and 16 s more of running clock unless a timeout (or the two-minute warning, easing in over its last 30 s; the NFL and, from 2024, college's two-minute timeout, `Params::for_year`) stops it; the chance kept is 1 - e^(-1.6 × time left / time needed) (`drive`, `in_time`, `warning_stop`).
  - The half's last snap (when a second snap won't fit): a kick from the spot at the level's make there, or a shot at 0.55 × e^(-yards / 14), whichever is worth more (down 3 in the fourth, a tying kick counts half) (`drive`).
  - Late habits blend in with the clock: the leader or a tied side burns it from 7:00 of the fourth to fully at 3:00 (`MILK_FROM`, `MILK_RAMP`); the side behind in the fourth takes the best of three paces (usual, hurry, slow); from 6:00 of the half to 4:00 a side short of time plays for the kick (`KICK_FROM`, `KICK_RAMP`): given no touchdown it kicks once in range, from outside with chance e^(-yards past range / 25), × 0.45 on fourth down (`REACH`, `FOURTH_REACH`) (`chance`, `drive`).
  - The kick's make by distance, falling away as 1 - s² over the last 7 yards to the longest kick, 0 from there (`Params::fg_make`).
  - How long each ending takes: a touchdown drive 1 + yards / 8 snaps, a field goal drive the snaps to range plus two, a stall 4.5 snaps; 27 s a snap usual, 14 s in a hurry (then the short clock's own mean time to score), 36 s less 30 per timeout the other side spends when slow (the side behind in the fourth: the play clock plus 4 s a snap, less the play clock minus 2 per timeout, and a drive as long as one from its probe spot); a side burning the clock runs four downs before it scores (`drive`, `time_to_score`).
  - The rest of the game: the final margin is normal around the lead plus the pregame line × the share of the game left, with a spread of σ × √(time left × (1 + b) / game), b rising from 0 to 1 over the last ten minutes (`rest_sd`, `LATE_SPREAD`); σ 13.5 NFL, 16 FBS, 16.5 FCS and small college, 18 high school. Each ending's time is read at seven lengths, 0.55 to 1.45 of its usual, weighted 1-2-3-4-3-2-1 (`LENGTHS`) (`from_spot`, `Params::for_level`).
  - A spot's split is never read as worth less than one up to 25 yards further back, the clock still from the real spot: the drive's split has small kinks where kicks give way to punts (`chance`).
  - A leader in the fourth quarter whose kneels outlast the clock against the other side's timeouts (and the two-minute warning) has won: 0.999 (`kneel_burn`).
  - Overtime: the NFL's timed period as the end of a game; college and high school possession for possession from the level's spot (`overtime`). The log is followed round by round: a possession ends with its try, its kick or a turnover, or when the other side has the ball, and a side with the ball again starts a round (`Rounds`, `home_series`, `ot_rounds`). An answering side level wins with any score; a try ends the possession, so the other side is read from the spot, and an answering side's try settles the round (XP at the level's try worth, a two at 0.475) (`try_value`).
  - The game day bar: the same model before the next snap, try or kickoff (`home_now`, called from `crates/afm-game/src/lane_gameday.rs`, `live_wp`).
  - The pregame line: stored with each kept game when it is kept (the closing line, the playoff and Super Bowl lines included; 0 at a neutral site without one) (`world/seat.rs`, `seat_line`, `SeatGame::spread`); for an older game the home side's season page's line; else 1.7 points of home field in the NFL and 2.5 elsewhere, none at a neutral site (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_boards/flow.rs`, `Wp::of_game`, `Wp::new`, `home_field`).
  - With no line (high school and the other leagues always): the gap between the sides is a normal prior with sd `gap_sd` (6 NFL, 14 college, 17 high school), updated by the lead beyond the line's share so far against `sigma`; its mean moves the rest of the game's margin and its remaining spread widens it (`Params::without_line`, `from_spot`).

## Speed: what is worked out once

The engine speed lane (batch 4, 2026-11-08) keeps every game the same and does less work for it. On 3,000 synthetic games a level on two seeds, every game's result was the same and the time fell 7% at high school, 25% at FBS and 24% in the NFL (`engine_hash 3000 11`, 4 cores).

- A man's tendency is read once a play: its inputs are fixed while the play runs (`game/sim.rs`, `Sim::tend`, `tend_memo`).
- A block keeps the defender's edge until the blocker, his helper, the rush move or the rusher's arc changes (`game/sim.rs`, `update_blocks`, `Engagement::edge_memo`, never saved).
- Each tick moves every man and copies the field in one pass (`Sim::advance`).
- The ball carrier skips the threat sum for a heading that can't beat the best one so far (an exact bound), and draws his 15 noises first, in the old order (`run_carrier`).
- Fatigue weights come from a fixed table (`FATIGUE`, `Agent::new`).
- The trails and the quarterback's predicted spots keep their buffers between snaps on each thread (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `TRAILS`, `PREDICT_SPOTS`); a read's window from this tick is reused in the checkdown and the throw (`quarterback`).
- The play callers draw from their weights in place; the live pass concepts are one fixed list; the kickoff draft keeps each caller's weights per situation for the game (`game/calling.rs`, `weigh`, `live_concepts`, `CallMemo`).
- Each depth list's ratings, the Lungs recovery and each man's wind are read once a game (`game/flow.rs`, `Side`); each spot's lean is worked out once a player (`spots.rs`, `spot_ratings`).
- Batch 5 (2026-11-12, over main at 6c47622) works on the tick physics, where a high school game spends its time. On the same 3,000 games a level every game's result was again the same on two seeds, and the time fell a further 7% at high school, 5% at FBS and 5% in the NFL (`engine_hash 3000 11`, 4 cores, the machine alone, the middle of four rounds: 20.80 to 19.35 s, 30.10 to 28.45 s and 27.40 to 26.15 s):
  - The scan that keeps bodies apart tests a mover only against men in his one-yard band and the next one each way, along the field and across it; men two bands apart were always skipped by its test (`Sim::advance`, `band`).
  - A man's speed is kept with his velocity, so steering and moving take its root once (`Agent::vel_len`, `vel_memo`, never saved). The wanted speed's root is skipped when it is clearly under his top speed, and Bend's part of a turn is skipped for a man without the link (`Agent::steer`).
  - The vision cone is tested on the cosine with exact series bounds; the arc cosine is taken only within 1e-9 of the cone's edge (`acos_lt`, in `Sim::sees` and `run_carrier`).
  - The ball carrier scores his headings highest cap first, and drops one as soon as part of its threat (the nearest defender's share first) shows it can't win (`run_carrier`).
  - A tackler's reach from his arms, a quick edge's close reach and Length is worked out once a play (`Sim::reach_extra`, `reach_memo`); a man clearly out of reach all tick is skipped before the closest-approach math (`Sim::tackles`). A free rusher more than his own reach and 0.05 yards off the quarterback on either axis, beyond the tick's movement, is skipped the same way (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `sack_check`).
  - A pursuer in front of the runner breaks down without working out the intercept first, and Motor is read only for a man out of the play (`pursue_cap`). The rush move's edge from the pick is kept as the block's edge (`pick_move`, `engage`).
  - Distance tests take the root only near the line they test (`game/geom.rs`, `dist_sq`, `sq_lt`, `sq_ge`; `Nearest` in `game/scrimmage.rs`): escorts, pass and run blocks, the settle and climb scans, sacks, blitz threats, the option pitch, the scramble drill, `hurry_run` and `slide`.
  - Routes reuse their distance to the next point (`route_step`, `toward`); the quarterback's window skips a defender who can't close in time (`window`); the trails are one flat buffer (`Trails`); a deep zone's two verticals are picked without a list (`cover_inner`); kick returns turn the lineup in place (`game/kicking.rs`, `run_return`).
- The proof: `engine_hash [games] [seed] [hs|fbs|nfl]` plays the games `match_check` plays and prints one fingerprint for each part of a game's result (stats, injuries, film, talks and the rest), then the time. Two builds that give every game the same result print the same lines but the time (`crates/afm-tools/src/bin/engine_hash.rs`; the prints are `world/wlog/fp.rs`, `field_prints`). A world proof on a new seed follows, as for the world speed lane (see [world-log.md](world-log.md)).
