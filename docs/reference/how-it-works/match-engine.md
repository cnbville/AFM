## Quick answers

- Who plays Mike, Sam and Will? Your depth order names the jobs and the engine keeps them: the base board's first three linebackers are SAM, MIKE and WILL in that order, its first three corners the two outside corners and the nickel, its first two safeties FS and SS (`spots.rs`, `board_jobs`, `named_jobs`; `game/flow.rs`, `Side::new`). Without your order (every AI staff), `best_order` puts the starters on the spots that rate highest, with a fill-in cost at a job he hasn't played. The staff reads body as one input there, never as a rule (Cain 2026-11-07): true speed where the job asks for range (Will, outside corner, free safety), size where it takes on blocks and hits (Sam, strong safety, nickel), height outside (`spots.rs`, `lean_value`). Sam is its own spot (Strength .2, Block Shedding .2, Man Coverage .15, Tackling .15, Pursuit .15, Blitzing .15); Will reads Speed .35, Pursuit .25, Zone Coverage .25, Blitzing .15; Mike as before. A man new to a job reads later and busts more until he has played it: +0.06 s on his read, +1.5 pts of busts and +3 pts of misfits at full, by how far the job is from his own (`fill_in` / 3, 0.6 of that once he has practised it this week), gone once it is in his played spots (`game/flow.rs`, `new_to_job`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `JOB_READ`, `JOB_BUST`, `JOB_MISFIT`). Your named men learn their jobs at spring practice and NFL camp (`world/systems.rs`, `set_spots`, `set_club_spots`). In the A/B test with a Will's build (228 lb, 4.56) at Sam and a Sam's (248, 4.68) at Will on every team, the man at Sam made 1.1 pts fewer run tackles a snap, missed 1.4 pts more and drew 0.9 pts more targets; the man at Will made 0.7 pts more run tackles and missed 1.7 pts fewer; the busts rose 0.19 pts a snap until both knew both jobs (`ab_check lb_swap`, `lb_swap_known`, 2026-11-08). The team's run defense got a little better (-0.06 yards a carry: the bigger man at Will plays the nickel snaps too) and its pass defense showed no clear cost, because a heavier linebacker's catch rate allowed does not rise yet (real +0.92 pts per 10 lb, part of minor bug 307). On the snap the Sam lines up strong side and takes the tight end in man, the Will weak side, the Mike in the middle with the A-gap on double-A pressure, the spy and the middle zone (`game/scrimmage.rs`).
- Can a man who is out stay where you put him on your depth chart? Yes, for any reason he is out: hurt, on the non-football list, suspended or sitting by a team rule, academically ineligible. Your order keeps him (`World::set_seat_depth`), and on game day the team the engine dresses leaves him off (`world/season.rs`, `game_team`, `folk::rules::sitting`; `training/seat.rs`, `sit_hurt`), so `game/roster.rs`, `depth_chart` skips his id and the next man in your order plays his place until he is back; at a named spot the next men up fill it (see the next answer). Every view of your chart shows your order with him in it: the flat Depth chart (`afm-game`, `views.rs`, `depth`), the boards (`lane_training.rs`, `boards_with`) and the stadium view (`lane_squadboards/depth.rs`, `spots`). Before (Cain, 2026-11-15), the flat chart listed the order the engine dresses, so a starter out in the spring sat at the bottom and Move up never kept him higher. Where you haven't set the position, the staff's chart plays the next man and lists the man who is out after the men who dress. The flat chart's `listed` is how many men in the list dress today.
- Does your order name left tackle and right tackle, the guards and the center, or the left and right edge? The line, yes (playtest bug 12, 2026-11-13); the edges and inside tackles, no. Your order's first two tackles are LT and RT and its first three inside linemen LG, C and RG, in the board's slot order (`spots.rs`, `board_jobs`; `game/flow.rs`, `line_slots`): `Side::new` names each man to his slot at kickoff and `pick_line` puts him there on every snap he plays, whoever else is hurt or resting. He knows the spot from the week's practice, so he pays no fill-in cost there, as an AI staff's starters don't. A slot whose man can't dress, or that your order doesn't reach, goes to the next men up on the depth, each at the open slot that suits him (`best_order`), so your center stays at center when a guard is out (proposed, not confirmed). A package's line labels hold the same way: on your Jumbo and Goal line offense boards the man at LT, LG, C, RG or RT plays it (`pick_package`; they used to be re-sorted by fit; proposed, not confirmed). A man your order names on one half of the line stays there: a backup tackle you put at guard isn't taken to fill or spell a tackle spot (`Side::new`, `pick_line`). A man nobody named (a backup in for a starter, every man of a staff that names nobody) takes the open slot that fits him best with the others. A CFL club's order for the ratio names no spot where nobody set the position: it is ranked by rating and says only who starts, so that line is seated by fit (`GameTeam::ranked`, `world/pro/other.rs`). Every AI staff, and yours until you set the position, seats the line as before, by spot fit (LT reads pass blocking .4, agility .2; RT run blocking .4, strength .25: `spots.rs`, `lean`): `engine_hash` gives the same fingerprints as main at all three levels (400 games on seed 1, 200 on seed 7). The screens read the engine's own seating (`flow::seating`, `Seating::at`, `by_slot`): a position you haven't set shows the man the game will line up at each named spot, and a drop makes only the positions it changed yours (`afm-game`, `lane_training.rs`, `seated_first`, `board_set`). When you agree to start a young player he takes the starter's spot and nobody else moves (`spots::start_at`, `World::seat_start`; it used to put him first and push every man one spot over). Your named linemen learn their spots at spring practice and NFL camp like the other named jobs (`world/systems.rs`, `named_jobs`). In the A/B tests on games (`ab_check ot_swap nfl` and `c_swap`, 4,000 NFL games an arm, seed 1; the builds are Claude's picks, proposed, not confirmed), each team's pass protector (308 lb, Pass Blocking and Agility 6 up, Run Blocking and Strength 6 down) and run blocker (324 lb, the other way) were named right and then swapped on the board: the man at LT went from 308.1 to 324.0 lb and at RT from 324.0 to 308.2, so the side follows the board on almost every snap; pass blocks lost at LT rose from 19.8% to 27.0% (+7.2 points, band +6.9 to +7.6) and fell at RT from 15.4% to 9.5%; run blocks won at LT rose from 69.6% to 86.0%; the team's sacks went from 5.7% to 6.0% of dropbacks (+0.28, band +0.17 to +0.39) and pressured dropbacks from 23.1% to 23.9%, yards a carry no clear change. A center's build (302 lb, Shotgun Snap and Communication 8 up) and a guard's (318 lb, both 8 down) swapped put the guard at center (302.1 to 318.0 lb there) and the center at left guard, and the right guard stayed (318.0 lb in both arms): busts rose from 1.04% to 1.16% a snap (band +0.09 to +0.15), and with the heavier man snapping, pass blocks lost at center fell from 25.9% to 17.7% and pressured dropbacks from 24.7% to 23.6%. The per-spot rows are `oj_lt_wt` to `oj_rt_run` (`afm-tools`, `ab::line_jobs`). The two edges and the two inside tackles share one spot each (`Spot::EDGE`, `Spot::DT`), so their totals tie and `best_order` keeps the order `pick` hands it (your depth order, a tired man spelled): the first edge listed lines up at LE, the second at RE, and when one is spelled the other can change sides. With a linebacker, corner or safety order of yours, a named man who can't dress moves the men behind him up one job (the names follow the men who dress), and of the two outside corners the one earlier on the roster lines up on the left. These three are as they were, not changed here.

See "Fire on the field" below and [people.md](people.md), "Talks on game day and off it". Before, a talk added up to ±1.5 rating points to every rating.

Yes, by each level's rule and era: college a 15-yard foul from 2008 and an ejection from 2013, high school a foul from 2013, the NFL unnecessary roughness; thrown out for a flagrant hit at the NFL and in high school (`game/rules.rs`, `Targeting`; `game/sim.rs`, `hit_foul`). - Upper and lower body? Strength is his upper body and Lower Body his legs (physical study item 4b, Cain 2026-11-15; the split is on [body.md](body.md)). Where a read said Strength it now takes the half that does the work (Claude's sort, proposed, not confirmed). The legs: the run set and pass set at the start of a block, a run block's push (0.14 Lower Body + 0.06 Strength in place of 0.2 Strength), the bull rush's push (0.15 Lower Body + 0.1 Strength in place of 0.25) and an edge's strength with mass (E3, read on the legs), the anchor (pass block and double team), the pancake's finish, the carrier's drive and the pile's push, the thumper, a field goal's push up the middle and its protection (`game/sim.rs`, `engage`, `block_base`, `rush_power`, `anchor`, `update_blocks`, `fight_tick`; `game/kicking.rs`). The upper body: the hand fight in pass protection (now 0.05 Strength of it), sheds, the press and the jam, the release, the contested catch's fight and hold, the stiff arm and the forced fumble's hit. Both halves' mean (`Agent::whole`, `GamePlayer::whole`) where the whole body works: the wrap-up, the back's broken tackle, the passer's escape and the sack's tackle, the hit at the catch point, blocking in space and the hitter read (`game/sim.rs`, `game/scrimmage.rs`, `game/flow.rs`). A back injury now limits both halves 8%. The Anchor link reads his weight, legs and balance (`links.rs`, `Part::Lower`). The results fit's strength column was split in two halves of the old weight (`data/results_fit.csv`).
- What is "his level's typical man"? The mean ratings and body (height, weight, arm) of every player at his position on 64 synthetic teams of his level, measured once (`game/synthetic.rs`, `typical`, `typical_body`; the rosters `rules::trait_norm` was measured on). The reads added or resized for the A/B tests (minor bugs 274-289, 2026-11-07) are taken about him, so a level's typical player plays as he did and only the spread within the level changes: centred on NFL players, they took FBS yards an attempt from 7.12 to 6.77. In the NFL a typical passer has Speed 61, Decisions 82, Pocket Presence 81 and Arm Strength 80 and stands 6'3; a typical receiver 6'1 with 31-inch arms; a typical corner 5'11 1/2 with 30 1/2-inch arms. One pivot is a fixed number at the NFL: a tackle's reach in the hand fight (minor bug 288) is an inch of arm over 34 there, Cain's number, and moves at the other levels with their typical tackle's arm.
- What is "his level's typical starter"? The mean ratings of the men each of those 64 teams' depth charts start at his position (`roster::starters`: one passer, two corners, two safeties, two linebackers; `game/synthetic.rs`, `typical_starter`). The reads resized for the rest of minor bug 283 (2026-11-08) sit about him: starters rate above the dressed average, so a steeper slope about the dressed man lifted or sank every starting lineup (picks ran 2.6% a throw against 2.3%).
- Do players have good and bad days? Yes (minor bug 287, 2026-11-07): each man draws his form once a game on his own stream, N(0, 3) rating points on everything × clamp(1 + 0.6 × (10 - hidden Consistency) / 10, 0.2, 2), so 4.1 at Consistency 4 and 1.9 at 16, added to his edge on every scrimmage snap (`game/flow.rs`, `FORM_SD`, `Side::form`, through `hurt_edge_o`/`hurt_edge_d`); kicks and returns don't read it. Proposed, not confirmed: kept small because the engine's passers already varied from game to game a little more than real ones (true sd of EPA a dropback 0.167, nflverse 2021-24 0.154); at 5 points it added 1.4 points of runs stuffed at the line. Consistency still narrows each throw's and kick's scatter too.
- Does the elusiveness check read weight? Yes, since lane 4 (A3): every make-a-man-miss check takes off size_cost = (forty_weight(his weight) - forty_weight(typical)) / 0.0124 rating points, the way the forty reads weight (`game/sim.rs`, `size_cost`): the QB's sack escape (× 0.7, typical 222, `game/scrimmage.rs`), a QB run's moves (typical 222, `game/flow.rs`, `qb_run`, `qb_spy`) and a returner's danger (typical 200, `game/kicking.rs`). The first-contact miss no longer does (minor bug 279, 2026-11-07: real broken tackles don't move with weight once talent is known); a heavy carrier pays in burst, braking and cut instead. Weight also comes in once he is wrapped up: the broken-tackle roll and the pile (`game/sim.rs`, `fight_tick`).
- Where does weight count? Top speed, burst and cut (`game/roster.rs`, `forty`, `sprint_tau`, `accel`, `cut`); momentum and hit force (`game/sim.rs`, `hit_effects`); broken tackles, 0.08 a pound from 215 lb, and the wrap-up, (mass - 215) / 300 on both sides, and the pile's push by mass (`fight_tick`); blocks, 0.08 a pound from 300 lb, 280 for rushers and shedders (`block_base`, `rush_power`, `shed_power`, `anchor`); an interior pass rusher's get-off, 0.38 a pound off his level's typical man at his position (`engage`, minor bug 285); an edge's power move, 0.3 a Strength point over the typical edge's for each 10 lb over the typical edge's weight up to 20 lb, nothing at or under it (E3, `rush_power`, `EDGE_STRENGTH_MASS`, `EDGE_MASS_SPAN`); a long-armed edge's reach to the quarterback from his block (E2, below); an edge's burst on a pass rush reads the typical edge's when his own is lower (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `rush_feet`, minor bug 286), his weight his own; the jump at the catch point, -0.035 inch a pound over 220 (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `high_reach`); a contact-shy tackler passing on a man 15+ lb heavier (`game/sim.rs`, `tackles`).
- Where do height and arm length count? (Checked for the A/B tests, 2026-11-06; minor bugs 274-277 and 288 on 2026-11-07.) His jump at the catch point (standing reach 0.82 × height + arm + 4 inches, `game/scrimmage.rs`, `high_reach`); where a jump ball arrives (0.72 × his height, 0.95 × height + 6 on a long ball); a defender's reach to the ball at the catch point (0.025 yard an inch of height, 0.10 an inch of arm about his level's typical man at his position, `catch_point`, `D_REACH_HEIGHT`, `D_REACH_ARM`); the receiver's catch radius (0.0075 yard an inch of height, 0.01 an inch of arm, the same way); the passer's trust in a big receiver downfield and in the red zone (`quarterback`, `BIG_TRUST`) and a tall passer's over the middle (`OVER_LINE`); a tackle's pass set against speed and finesse, a rating point an inch of arm over 34 in the NFL, the pivot moving with each level's typical tackle (`game/sim.rs`, `block_base`); batted balls (the rusher's reach, 0.06 an inch of height and 0.08 an inch of arm; the passer's height no longer, real shows none); tackle reach (0.03 yard an inch of arm, `game/sim.rs`; an edge up to 0.3 more an inch over the typical edge's when he is quick, 0.15 a forty step of 0.15 s, E2, `close_reach`); an edge's speed move (0.2 a point an inch of height over the typical edge's for each Flexibility point over the typical edge's, E1); an edge's reach to the quarterback (0.18 yard an inch of arm over the typical edge's on his block, scaled by weight, E2, `sack_check`); and a carrier's reach for the line (`Tend::Reach`). Both feed Dynamic Links (Highpointer, Length, Low center, Pocket frame), which only count near the top of the position (`links.rs`). Not read: height in any block or in the pass rush outside an edge's bend, a receiver's or corner's height in the route, separation or the jam. What that does to the stats: `afm/ab-tests/` in the project folder.
- How fine is the sim? 22 tracked bodies, 10 ticks a second (`game/geom.rs`, `DT`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).
- What decides a sack? A free rusher within 1.25 yards, or one winning his block (w > 0.45) within 1.2, then an escape roll: 0.4 Elusiveness + 0.3 Agility + 0.3 Strength against 0.6 Tackling + 0.2 Strength + 0.2 Agility, + 0.5 a 10 Pocket Presence over his level's typical passer on the logit (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `sack_check`, `POCKET_ESCAPE`, minor bug 283).
- What counts as a pressure? The nearest rusher within 3.2 yards (an engaged one 1.5 yards farther unless he is winning) once 0.6 s are gone; the nearest and any free or winning rusher within 2.6 yards are credited (`quarterback`, `PRESS_NEAR`, `PRESS_FROM`, `PRESS_CAUSE`, minor bug 284). It is a label only: the quarterback's play reads his felt pressure, not this.
- What decides a catch? A base miss of max(0.15 - 0.00095 × Catching, 0.012), then how far off his frame the ball is, the fight within 1.4 yards, weather and a hard ball (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `catch_point`).
- What counts as a drop? An incompletion on a ball inside 0.8 of his catch radius with no defender within a yard of it (`catch_point`).
- Does the catch read Concentration? No; nor Toughness, Route Running or Speed (`catch_point`).
- How does a pick happen? Five ways: a tip in the throwing lane, a ball off target into a defender, a defender in front at the catch point, a breakup that sticks (6% of breakups), a ball off the receiver's hands (`catch_point`).
- What sets the fumble chance? 0.0064 × (hit force / (1,700 × his level's typical hit))^1.5 × clamp(1 + 0.025 × (his level's typical starting back's Ball Security - his) + 0.020 × (hit - the hit of his level's typical man at the hitter's position), 0.25, 2.5), hit = the hitter's 0.5 Strength + 0.5 Tackling (`FUMBLE_HIT`, minor bug 282), × 2.6 on a catch not yet tucked away, a wet ball and the Strip tendency (`game/sim.rs`, `hit_effects`; engine gaps item 7). Sacks, snaps and handoffs have their own rolls, and the ball stays live: see Fumbles.
- Is a fumble live? Yes (engine gaps item 4): it bounces, the men who see it go for it, a man falls on it in traffic or picks it up clear, and he runs with it by his level's rules; out of bounds, touchdowns, touchbacks and safeties as the rules have them (see Fumbles).
- What does fatigue cost? Each rating loses its fatigue share × tiredness (speed 10%, strength 12%, skills 6%), top speed 8%, burst and cut 10% when fully spent (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`).
- Is there momentum? Yes: -3 to +2 rating points on all his ratings, after turnovers, drops, sacks and touchdowns, softened by Composure (`game/flow.rs`, `edge_hit`, `decay_edges`).
- What does playing hurt cost? What his injury limits, as a share of the rating: ankle speed 8%, agility 12%, acceleration 8%; hamstring speed and acceleration 10%; knee agility 10%, speed 6%; shoulder tackling and arm strength 10%, run blocking and hand use 8%; ribs strength 6%, ball security 8%; hand catching 10%, ball security, hand use and the three accuracies 8%; back strength 8%, agility 6%; anything else speed 3%. On scrimmage snaps and kicks (`game/sim.rs`, `hampers`, `hampered`; `game/scrimmage.rs`; `game/kicking.rs`).
- How are Discipline, Composure, Concentration, Motor, Toughness read? On a 1-20 scale against the level: 10 + (rating - norm) / 2.6; norms NFL 67, FBS 48, FCS 42, small college 37, high school 29, plus Toughness +3, Motor +1.5, Composure +1, Concentration +4.5, Competitiveness +5.5, Physicality -0.5 (`game/rules.rs`, `trait_norm`; `game/roster.rs`, `trait20`).
- Are field goals tracked? The kick is a roll of its angle against the posts and the leg against the distance (`game/kicking.rs`, `field_goal`); the rush against it plays out man by man against the operation's clock (`place_block`, `punt_block`), and a blocked kick is a live ball, played out on the loose ball (`blocked_live`; engine gaps lane EG3, item 12). Kick and punt returns are tracked plays (`run_return`).
- How do tendencies act? Each check reads lean = (tendency - 10.5) / 9.5, from -1 to +1; a followed instruction sets the tendency, stress slides it up to a third back toward his old habit (`game/sim.rs`, `tend`, `lean`).
- How much is a Dynamic Link worth? 5 rating points at full strength, only in its own situations; stacked links count the strongest in full, the next at half, a third at a quarter (`links.rs`, `edge`, `FULL`).
- When does a tired starter come out? Below his position's energy line (EDGE 0.90, DT 0.84, WR/TE 0.74, LB/CB/S 0.68, RB 0.60, others 0.5), if a fresh backup is within 60 × (line - energy) rating points, 600 × for an edge, and no other tired man has already been spelled by him (one backup spells one starter) (`game/flow.rs`, `threshold`, `pick`, `EDGE_LINE`, `EDGE_ALLOW`; minor bug 358: at 0.84 and 60 the best edges played 88-90% of the team's snaps, real p90 77%).
- Does a player's week of practice matter? Through his prep word, for every team: his busts and run misfits × 1.33 (Unprepared) to × 0.93 (Sharp), his read up to 0.1 s later, a quarterback up to a third longer on each read. It changes no rating (`game/roster.rs`, `GamePlayer.prep`; `training/prep.rs`, `game_factor`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`, `quarterback`). See [training.md](training.md).
- Is the win chance on screen what the coaches read? No: the screens' chance is its own model (`game/win_chance.rs`, `chance`); play calls still read `live::win_prob`, unchanged. See "The game boards' records and the win chance" below.
- Does the engine's speed work change any game? No. What it saves is work whose answer is already fixed or thrown away: a man's tendency read once a play, a block's edge kept until the block changes, buffers kept between snaps, a play caller's weights worked out once a game. Every draw, every float sum's order and every tie stays as it was. `engine_hash` proves it: the same games played on two builds must give the same fingerprint for every part of every game (`crates/afm-tools/src/bin/engine_hash.rs`). See "Speed: what is worked out once".
- Can a man on a block make the tackle? Only while he is beating his blocker (w above 0), with an arm: 0.55 × w a try, inside his reach less 0.55 yards (`game/sim.rs`, `tackles`, `ARM_OUT`). A man on a block he isn't winning has to shed it first, and as the runner comes his shed reads the block: the pull off it is scaled by e^(edge / 9), so the man winning comes off fast and the man losing stays on (`update_blocks`, `SHED_EDGE`). The runner reads blocks the same way: a sealed man is no threat to him, an even block a quarter of a free man (`run_carrier`). So the line moves runs (scheme-fit lane SF3, 2026-11-17): Run Blocking +15 on one lineman adds 0.02 to 0.14 yards a carry at FBS (0.00 to 0.08 before) and Block Shedding +15 on a defensive tackle takes 0.08 to 0.12 off (0.01 to 0.02 before; `fit_check`, FBS seed 151, 13 and 5 arms). Details below under "Line blocking (run)" and "Pursuit, elusiveness, broken tackles and tackling"; the measures in `docs/match-engine.md`, "A blocked man is out of the play".

## How a snap runs

- `run_snap` builds the agents (offense first), aligns the defense, reads the play, rolls busts and misfits, gives every man a job, then ticks blocks, the quarterback, the defense, routes, the carrier, tackles and movement until the ball is dead (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).
- His ratings for the snap: rating × (1 - fatigue share × tiredness) + edge, clamped 1-99. The edge is momentum minus the fill-in cost of a spot he doesn't know (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`; `game/flow.rs`, `run_scrimmage_call`, `unfamiliar`).
- Top speed, burst and cut come from his raw ratings and weight; the edge doesn't move them, fatigue does (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`).
- Road noise: every offensive player loses 11 × crowd × focus points of Football IQ, focus = clamp(1.6 - 0.06 × Concentration20, 0.4, 1.6) (`run_snap`, `NOISE_LOAD`).
- Crowd, wherever this page uses it: the home crowd × the road offense's `prep.noise`, 0 at home or on a neutral site. `prep.noise` is 1 for AI teams; your week's crowd-noise days set yours, 0.9-1.15 (`game/flow.rs`, `home_crowd`; `training/seat.rs`, `week_habits`).
- Road receivers leave late on the silent count: + 0.38 × crowd × clamp(1 - 0.05 × (Concentration20 - 10), 0.5, 1.5) s on each route of a called pass, standing at the line before their release (`SILENT_RELEASE`, `silent_late`; 0.3 s and already running before every receiver had a release, minor bug 135). With the noise load, the busts and the silent count it is fitted to the real home margin: 2.3 NFL points over 6,000 `match_check` games at a 0.65 crowd (se 0.21; real 2.1, nflverse 2021-2025) and 2.8 FBS (se 0.25; real 2.4 in conference games); real road offenses lose it through the air (completions 63.8% against 65.2%, yards an attempt 6.95 against 7.15, runs about even), the engine's road passing loses 1.3 points of completions and 6.9% of the yards an attempt (minor bug 143: 0.8 and 1.2 points before; proposed, not confirmed).
- Seeing: a cone of half-angle 1.2 + 0.006 × (rating - 50) radians, read with Pocket Presence for the passer, Ball Carrier Vision for the carrier, Play Recognition for everyone else; anything within 2 yards is felt (`game/sim.rs`, `sees`).
- `pro()`: some quarterback, catch-radius and decision reads count every point from 68 up and a quarter of the gap below 68 (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `pro`).
- Chemistry is 0-1 with 0.45 as typical (`game/roster.rs`, `TYPICAL_CHEM`).
- The score moves habits (`game/flow.rs`, `mood`). late = clamp((quarter length - game left) / (0.67 × quarter length), 0, 1). Gamble: +2.5 × late down 9+, +1.5 × late behind with under a third of a quarter left, -2 × late up 9+, -1.5 × late ahead with under a third left. It adds to every tendency except Patient or downhill, Contact willingness and Gets his hands up (`tendencies.rs`, `Tend::gamble`; `game/sim.rs`, `tend`).
- Stakes = 0.5 in a playoff game + 0.4 × late when within 8 points with under 0.55 of a quarter left; stress = clamp(stakes + 0.8 × tiredness + 0.05 × (10 - Composure20), 0, 1) slides a habit stress / 3 of the way back to his old one (`mood`; `game/sim.rs`, `tend`).

### Reading the play

- Each defender's read time = max(0.12, 0.22 + 0.0075 × (100 - Play Recognition + level gap) + 0.12 × U - 0.14 × lean(Downhill) + fake + film + job + prep + motion + 0.12 × novelty) seconds (`run_snap`).
  - Level gap: the Play Recognition norm at his level minus FBS's (NFL +19, high school -19), so a typical defender reads alike at every level (`run_snap`; `game/rules.rs`, `trait_norm`).
  - Fake (play action or a draw): (0.15 + 0.012 × (100 - PR) × U) × sell × bite × max(1 + 0.9 × lean(Downhill), 0.1); sell = e^(0.01 × (QB Ball Handling - his level's typical starter's)), never capped (`SELL_PER_POINT`; scheme-fit item 3: it was clamp(1 + 0.01 × (Ball Handling - 60), 0.6, 1.4), so nearly every college and pro starter sold it alike); bite = clamp(1.5 - 0.05 × Discipline20, 0.5, 1.5). On play action the read time is also how long the fake holds him (below, "Play action").
  - Film: ±0.16 × keyed, sooner when what the look tells matches the play; keyed = belief lean × (0.5 + 0.5 × film study / 20) × clamp(PR / 80, 0.3, 1.2) (`run_snap`; `game/calling.rs`, `belief`).
  - Job: 0.12 × (0.9 - his knowledge of the front or coverage family). Jet or orbit motion: +0.10 × bite for linebackers.
  - Prep: 0.3 × (his prep factor - 1): +0.1 s Unprepared, +0.06 Underprepared, +0.024 Patchy, -0.0075 Prepared, -0.021 Sharp (`run_snap`; `training/prep.rs`, `game_factor`).
  - Downhill applies to linebackers and safeties only.

### Busts and misfits

- Bust chance = ((1 - know) × 0.05 + (60 - FIQ)₊ × 0.0006 + noise + check + install + lapse) × call × chem × prep (`run_snap`).
  - noise (road offense) = 0.06 × crowd × max(1.3 - FIQ / 100, 0.3) (minor bug 143: 0.03); check (defense against trips, bunch, empty or motion) = 0.04 × (1 - know).
  - install = 0.04 × (plan load - absorb)₊, absorb = clamp((FIQ - 30) / 45, 0.2, 1.3) (`game/roster.rs`, `absorb`, `plan`).
  - lapse = 0.03 × (1 - energy) × (10 - Concentration20) / 9.
  - call = clamp(1 - 0.006 × (Communication - 60), 0.75, 1.25) of the QB (offense), the center (the line), the best communicator among linebackers and safeties (defense).
  - chem (WR, TE, RB, CB, S) = 1 - 0.45 × (chemistry - 0.45).
  - prep = his week's prep factor from his latest word: None 1, Unprepared 1.33, Underprepared 1.2, Patchy 1.08, Prepared 0.975, Sharp 0.93 (`game/roster.rs`, `prep_of`; `training/prep.rs`, `game_factor`).
- Run-fit misfit (linebackers and DBs on runs) = clamp((0.035 + 0.002 × max(65 - PR, -15) + 0.06 × (1 - front knowledge) + 0.003 × (10 - Discipline20) + 0.04 misdirection + 0.03 jet + 0.05 × novelty) × prep, 0.01, 0.35). A misfit fills the wrong gap or comes late (`run_snap`, `defend`).

## Bodies and movement

- Forty = 5.56 - 0.0124 × (0.75 Speed + 0.25 Acceleration) + 0.0033 × (weight - 220), the combine fit without noise (`game/roster.rs`, `forty`).
- Top speed = 40 / max(forty - 0.92 × sprint_tau, 3.4) yd/s; sprint_tau = clamp(0.84 + 0.0015 × (60 - Acc) + 0.0004 × (weight - 220) + his first-step gift (sd 0.08), 0.5, 1.3), under (forty - 3.4) / 0.92 (`body.rs`, `top_speed`, `sprint_tau`, `start_knack`; the progression build's step 4b part 2: his split in the engine is his test day's, 0.04 s apart at the same forty as real; [body.md](body.md)).
- Accel = 4.65 + 0.008 a point of Acceleration above 60 (0.0046 below) - 0.006 × (weight - 220), clamped 2.2-7.5 yd/s² (`accel`).
- Cut = 0.99 + 0.004 a point of Agility above 60 (0.0022 below) - 0.002 × (weight - 220), clamped 0.4-1.7; Sudden adds 0.02 × share (`cut`; `game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`).
- Steering: speeding up follows the sprint curve, braking is accel × (1.2 + 0.7 × cut), a turn spends accel × cut sideways; Bend helps a tight turn at speed (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::steer`, `Agent::brake`).
- Bodies push apart below 0.8 yards, scaled by clamp(1 - 0.008 × (Traffic - 60), 0.55, 1.35) (`Agent::traffic`, `advance`).
- Burner: +1.25% top speed × share for the carrier 2+ yards past the line or a returner, a chaser behind the carrier, a route past 9 yards, a man defender trailing deep, the target of a ball in the air past 9 yards (`link_speeds`, `BURNER`).

## The raw burner's roles (lane 4, S10-S10d)

- Who he is: the fastest backup receiver or back by deep speed (0.4 Acceleration + 0.6 Speed, `game/roster.rs`, `deep_speed`) when he beats the top starting receiver by more than 1 and the starters' mean by more than 4; lean = clamp((his - mean - 2) / 10, 0, 1) (`roster.rs`, `burner`, kept on the depth chart).
- Returns: the returner rating reads 0.4 × deep speed in place of Speed (`roster.rs`). Punt gunners: the two best non-starting receivers, corners and safeties by 0.5 deep speed + 0.3 Release + 0.2 Strength, filled with corners when short (`game/flow.rs`, `Side::gunners`, `punt_units`).
- Snaps on offense: on a jet sweep he takes the motion man's place with chance 0.5 + 0.4 × lean; on a deep pass the Z (else the X) with 0.25 + 0.5 × lean, keyed per snap (`game/flow.rs`, `speed_package`).
- How the defense respects him (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `align_defense`, with the plan's fear, `Tactics::fear`): his corner's cushion + 6 × (fear - 0.5) in press, + 3 × fear off (a Cover 2 or Tampa 2 cloud corner keeps his); a slot corner over him + 3 × fear; the single-high safety leans toward him by clamp((his y - ball) × 0.3 × fear, -5, 5) and sits 2 × fear deeper; two-high safeties half the lean, 1.5 × fear deeper, the one on his side another fear yards (proposed, not confirmed).
- The proof (`match_check`, `AFM_BURNER=pts` adds pts to each team's second receiver's Speed and Acceleration): 3,000 NFL games, seed 7, +12 against +0. His teammates' catches under 10 air yards 12.38 to 12.71 a team game, their yards after the catch 4.97 to 5.01 a catch, two-high shells 59.4% to 62.5% of passes; his own catches 4.71 to 4.48 (bracketed more), points 26.30 to 26.42.

## Line blocking (run)

What it reads: run blocking, strength, hands, balance, burst and mass against shedding, hands, anchor, strength, recognition and mass. What it does not read: arm length in inches (only the Length link), height, Speed, the blocker's Agility or Football IQ.

- Start: get-off = 0.6 Acceleration + 0.4 Anticipation + 12 × lean(Snap timing) + Get-off + Length (defender); run set = 0.5 Run Blocking + 0.3 Acceleration + 0.2 Strength + Get-off + Length (blocker); w0 = -0.75 (`RUN_BLOCK_START`; -0.4 before minor bug 253, -0.1 before minor bug 164) + 0.006 × (get-off + silent count - set) + front fit + N(0, 0.18) (`game/sim.rs`, `engage`).
  - Silent count: a road line in its first second gives the defender 36 × crowd × focus (minor bug 143: 24), focus = clamp(1 - 0.05 × (Concentration20 - 10), 0.5, 1.5) (`engage`, `SILENT_COUNT`).
  - Front fit (DE/DT only): a two-gap DT (a 3-4 front) +0.05 against gap runs, -0.02 against zone; a one-gap lineman -0.02 against gap runs, +0.03 against zone (`front_fit`). Duo counts as zone here (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).
- Each tick: edge = shed power - block power; w += 0.035 × edge × 0.1 + 0.6 × √0.1 × N(0, 1) (`update_blocks`, `RUN_EDGE`).
  - Block power = 0.45 Run Blocking + 0.2 Strength + 0.15 Hand Use + 0.1 Balance + 0.1 Acceleration + 0.08 × (weight - 300) + Low center/Anchor (`block_base`, `block_gift`).
  - Shed power = 0.4 Block Shedding + 0.2 Hand Use + 0.15 Anchor + 0.1 Strength + 0.15 Play Recognition + 0.08 × (weight - 280) (`shed_power`).
  - Before his read time a run defender drifts 0.12 a second toward losing (`update_blocks`).
  - The runner within 2.2 yards and the block not sealed (w > -1): +2 × 0.9 × max(0.5 + w, 1.0) a second (1.0 before minor bug 253), the shed to make the play, × e^(edge / 9), so it reads who is winning the block: a man ahead of his blocker by 9 points comes off 2.7 times as hard as on an even block, one behind by 9 at 0.37 of it (`update_blocks`, `SHED_RUNNER`, `SHED_EDGE`; the same for every block before scheme-fit lane SF3; before minor bug 164 only with w > 0, at 2 × 0.5 × max(0.5 + w, 0.2)).
  - A defender 1.5+ yards past the line (a linebacker someone climbed to) feels the runner from 3.5 yards and gets 2 × 1.0 × max(0.5 + w, 0.2) more, by the same e^(edge / 9) (`SHED_SECOND`, `SHED_SECOND_RANGE`).
  - The runner 1.5+ yards past him: he lets go with chance 0.18 × clamp(1 + 0.04 × (Motor20 - 10) - 0.006 × (blocker Hand Use - 60), 0.5, 1.6) a tick.
  - A corner, nickel or safety who has read the run, with the runner past the line and more than 3 yards to his side: he works off the block with chance 0.6 a tick and pursues (`SPACE_BLOCK_GAP`, `SPACE_BLOCK_LEAVE`; minor bug 163); a linebacker with 0.8 (`LB_LEAVE`, bug 305: he was blocked to the whistle 1.7 times a run, now 1.0).
  - A linebacker 1.5+ yards past the line slips a climbing blocker's first hands 0.4 more often (`lands_block`, `LB_SLIP`, capped at 0.7).
- Double teams: edge -= 0.6 × the helper's block power - 20 - 0.3 × (anchor - 60); anchor = 0.6 Anchor + 0.2 Strength + 0.2 Balance + 0.08 × (weight - 300) + Anchor/Big man (`update_blocks`, `anchor`).
  - Combos: 2 on inside zone, zone read, duo and draw; 1 on outside zone, power, counter and iso (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_blocking`).
  - The helper climbs to his linebacker when w < -0.15 after 0.35 s, after 1.0 s, or when the linebacker is within 2 yards of the line or 2.5 of him (`run_snap`).
- w ≥ 1 is a shed. w ≤ -1 is a sealed block held at -1, or a pancake with chance 0.05 × finish a tick, finish = clamp(1 + 0.04 × (Physicality20 - 10) + 0.006 × (Strength - 60) - 0.008 × (defender Balance - 60), 0.4, 1.8) (`update_blocks`).
- The pair moves toward the carrier at 1.6 × w yd/s (`update_blocks`). The push fades with the block's age: driven back (w < 0) to nothing over 2 s, at most 1.6 yards (`RUN_PUSH_BACK`); driving his blocker back (w > 0), the push along the field over 1 s, while the slide toward the carrier goes on (`RUN_PUSH_UP`). Before minor bug 164 it never faded (a man pushed 11 yards).
- Blocks in space (climbs, pulls, screens, returns): a set, square blocker misses with clamp(0.06 + 0.004 × (slip - skill), 0.02, 0.25), any other with clamp(0.17 + 0.006 × (slip - skill) + 0.05 × (speed across - 2)₊, 0.05, 0.55); skill = 0.5 Blocking in Space + 0.5 Agility + Big man; slip = 0.5 Agility + 0.5 Block Shedding + Sudden. A DL moving under 3 yd/s, or anyone under 2 within 1.5 yards of the line (anywhere before minor bug 164), is always hit (`game/sim.rs`, `lands_block`, `PHONE_BOOTH`).
- Receivers' stalk blocks: the nearest man within 9 yards who isn't a DE or DT and stands 1.5+ yards off the line (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_blocking`; minor bug 164).

## Pass rush and pressure

What it reads: the rush move's ratings against the matching set, mass on the bull rush and in the get-off, a tackle's arm length against speed and finesse, Football IQ for the move pick, Motor for the counter. What it does not read: height outside an edge's bend (E1), the rusher's arm inches outside Length (and an edge's finish, under Sacks), the rusher's Speed outside the speed rush, a slow-burst edge's own Acceleration on his rush path (below).

- The set: each blocker takes the rusher across from him at the snap (`pass_protection`) and mirrors him from a yard in front of him toward the quarterback, never a step past 0.4 yards behind the line (`PASS_SET`, `hold_at`): he lets a blitzer from depth come to him (EG5: he used to walk up to meet one and took him on 2-3 yards downfield, and a pass thrown past the line found him there on one dropback in seven).
- Start: w0 = -0.42 + 0.006 × (get-off + silent - pass set) + front fit + N(0, 0.18), pass set = 0.5 Pass Blocking + 0.3 Agility + 0.2 Football IQ + Get-off + Length; a two-gap DT starts -0.03 (`engage`, `front_fit`).
  - Pass-rush get-off (minor bugs 285, 286): a × Acceleration + (0.6 - a) × (T(Acceleration) + 0.8 (Finesse Rush - T) + 0.2 (Flexibility - T)) + 0.4 Anticipation - (0.64 + 2.7 for a defensive tackle) × a × (weight - his level's typical man's at his position) for an interior rusher (the tackle's two-gap read, `TWO_GAP`, minor bug 285) + 12 × lean(Snap timing) + Get-off + Length, a = 0.6, an edge on contain 0 (`EDGE_BURST`, 0.15 before the edge lane; Flexibility's part `EDGE_GETOFF_BEND`, 0.5 before E1), T his level's typical man at his position, so the typical edge gets off as before. The weight term is his Acceleration as his 10-yard split shows it at his weight (`links::ten_split`); an edge's weight already costs him on the arc (burst and cut, `game/roster.rs`).
- The move: the rusher takes the best of speed, power and finesse by (rush - block) + N(0, max(11 - 0.09 × FIQ, 2)) (`pick_move`).
  - Speed: 0.4 Finesse Rush + 0.2 Speed + 0.15 Acc + 0.15 Flexibility + 0.1 Hand Use + Bend (an edge on contain keeps a quarter of the Speed and Acceleration, `EDGE_ARC_LEGS`, half before the edge lane; the rest reads the typical man's Speed and Acceleration plus 0.65, 0.1 and 0.25 of his Finesse Rush, Flexibility and Hand Use over the typical man's, `EDGE_ARC_BEND`; his 0.15 Flexibility is 0.05 of his own plus 0.1 of the typical man's Flexibility and his Finesse Rush over the typical man's, `EDGE_SPEED_BEND`; and he adds 0.2 × (height - T height)₊ × (Flexibility - T), `EDGE_TALL_BEND`, E1: a tall man who bends gets his pads under the tackle's hands), against 0.5 Pass Blocking + 0.2 Agility + 0.15 Flexibility + 0.15 Hand Use + Big man + a tackle's (arm - 34 in the NFL, the pivot moving with each level's typical tackle) (`rush_power`, `block_base`, `block_gift`; minor bugs 286, 288).
  - Power: 0.4 Power Rush + 0.25 Strength + 0.15 Hand Use + 0.1 Balance + 0.1 Block Shedding + 0.08 × (weight - 280) + Anchor/Battering ram + for an edge 0.3 × clamp(weight - T weight, 0, 20) / 10 × (Strength - T) (E3, `EDGE_STRENGTH_MASS`, `EDGE_MASS_SPAN`: strength pays with mass, counted up to 20 lb over the typical edge, about the real heavy edge; at or under the typical weight Strength keeps its full value), against 0.4 PB + 0.3 Anchor + 0.15 Strength + 0.15 Balance + 0.08 × (weight - 300) + Anchor.
  - Finesse: 0.45 Finesse Rush + 0.25 Agility + 0.2 Hand Use + 0.1 Block Shedding + Big man/Sudden (an edge on contain 0.12 Agility, `EDGE_FINESSE_AGILITY`, the other 0.13 reading the typical man's Agility plus 0.6 and 0.4 of his Finesse Rush and Hand Use over the typical man's; minor bug 328), against 0.45 PB + 0.3 Hand Use + 0.15 Agility + 0.1 Football IQ + Big man/Length + a tackle's (arm - 34).
  - An edge on contain adds 10 × lean(Upfield); every rusher loses 2.5 × lean(Hands up) (`rush_power`).
- Each tick w += 0.032 × edge × 0.1 + 0.58 × √0.1 × N(0, 1) (`update_blocks`).
- An edge's burst on a pass rush (minor bug 286): on a pass play an edge on contain runs his rush path, before and past the line, on the quicker of his own burst (sprint build-up and grip) and the typical edge's at his weight and fatigue, so a man slower off the mark than the typical edge rushes as the typical edge does and a quicker one keeps his own (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `rush_feet`). The two are compared as bursts, so the day's form never decides it (his burst reads no form). His weight, turn, top speed and fatigue stay his own. Real edges' 10-yard split says nothing about their pressures once talent is known, so a slow first step costs nothing, while the quick freak keeps his edge (Cain 2026-11-08: no caps on rare freaks). Reading every edge's burst, weight and turn as the typical edge's put the A/B split and weight rows near real but failed the freak check: heavy edges took 35% of the top 10% of pressure seasons (11% of all), light ones none (8% of all; real 18% of the top, 19% of all), and as recruited 10 lb added +0.40 pts of pressures a snap (real flat).
- Counter: once, when w < -0.2 after 0.8 s, with chance clamp(0.45 + 0.008 × (FIQ - 60) + 0.03 × (Motor20 - 10), 0.15, 0.85); kept only if it beats the move he is on (`update_blocks`).
- A shed inside 2.5 s, with the QB still holding the ball, is a rush win. The beaten blocker can't square him again for clamp(0.8 + 0.15 if a speed rush - 0.010 × (Agility - 60) - 0.006 × (PB - 60), 0.35, 1.3) s and until he is back in front (`update_blocks`, `can_square`).
- The pocket: an engaged pair moves toward the QB at (0.25 + 0.65 × (w + 1) / 2) yd/s, × 1.4 on a bull rush, × 0.7 otherwise (`update_blocks`). Once the quarterback no longer has the ball in the pocket (thrown, handed off or scrambling), the pair keeps driving toward the last spot he held it in the pocket (`Sim::pocket`), so it settles there; it used to drive at the blocker's own spot, a fixed heading that carried one pair 15 yards past the sideline on a long play (the film test, talk facts lane L8, 2026-11-14). Holding the pair still instead changed games more: FBS named lines held 75% of snaps against 85% on the line-order test's five seeds; the drive toward the pocket keeps it at the old level (76.6% against 77.3% pooled over 20 seeds).
- Protection: each lineman takes the nearest rusher within 3.2 yards across; backs and tight ends take the rest (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `pass_protection`). Off a run fake with the back in the protection it is a half-slide, and the boot has its own (below, "Play action").
  - Help on the plan's rusher: a spare blocker slides to him; with none, a back or tight end within 5 yards across chips him, 0.6 s into his route, taking 0.25 × clamp(0.6 + PB / 150, 0.6, 1.3) off w and halving his speed (`slide_to`, `run_route`, `CHIP_TIME`, `CHIP_W`).
  - A stunt is passed off with clamp(0.35 + 0.45 × line chemistry + 0.004 × (the pair's FIQ - 60), 0.2, 0.92); the looper waits 0.45 s (`stunt_pickup`, `assign_defense`).
  - A back picks up a blitzer with clamp(0.85 + 0.01 × (FIQ - 60) + 0.004 × (PB - 60) - 0.008 × (blitzer's Blitzing - 60), 0.4, 0.98) (`picks_up`).
- Pressure on the QB: each free rusher he sees (or feels, chance 0.15 + 0.005 × Pocket Presence) within 4.5 yards adds (4.5 - d) / 4.5; an engaged rusher winning (w > 0.3) within 3 yards adds 1.0 × winning × (3 - d) / 3 (`POCKET_FEEL`; 0.6 before minor bug 358, when NFL sacks sat at 2.57 a team game, real 2.41). Pressured is logged when the nearest gets inside 3.2 yards after 0.6 s; the nearest and any free or winning rusher inside 2.6 are credited (`quarterback`, minor bug 284: the old rule, inside 2.6 from the snap with everyone inside 2.8 credited, counted a tackle over the center next to a quarterback under center, 60% of all pressures and 8.6 times PFR's for tackles).
- Batted balls: each rusher in the lane 1-8 yards ahead bats with 0.17 × (1 - offset) × reach × passer × arc × hands (`batter`, `BAT`).
  - reach = clamp(1 + 0.015 (Jumping - 60) + 0.08 (arm - 33.5) + 0.06 (height - 75) + 0.01 (Anticipation - 60) + 0.015 Length, 0.3, 2); passer = clamp(1 - 0.06 (T height - 75) - 0.008 (Quick Release + Pocket frame - 60), 0.4, 1.8), T his level's typical passer (his own height left out: real batted balls don't move with it, minor bug 277; the level's rate stays); arc = clamp(1.3 - distance / 30, 0.3, 1.2); hands = clamp(1 + 1.1 × lean(Hands up), 0.15, 2.4).

- Chips on the chart (usage promises, 2026-11-14): a chip that lands on a rusher (`run_route`) sets his `CHIPPED` flag on the line chart (`game/chart.rs`, `Rusher::CHIPPED`, with `stunt()`, `lineman()` and `chipped()`); it changes nothing in the play. The usage promises' plan settings (free him up, send him, shots, feature, help, rush him) touch the rush, the blitz, the slide and the read order only in your games with a setting on, and draw no random number otherwise, so every other game plays the same (`tests/usage.rs`, `a_plan_that_keeps_nothing_changes_no_game_and_chips_are_saved`). Details on [play-calling.md](play-calling.md).
- The quarterback's freedom on the play log (lane N2, 2026-11-15): each snap keeps the passer's freedom at the line, `PlayRecord::freedom` (1 none, 2 a kill call, 3 full audibles; 0 not kept, for fakes and kneels), so a promise on his freedom can be judged. Your plan settings may raise his freedom, swap men on the lineup (`keep_lineup`) and release a back or tight end; with none set nothing changes and no number is drawn (`tests/usage_terms.rs`, `a_plan_naming_nobody_changes_no_game`).

## Sacks

What it reads: who gets there (the blocks above), the rusher's reach (his Tackling and Pursuit, an edge's Flexibility, a long-armed edge's arm and weight, an edge's weight), the QB's elusiveness, agility and strength against the rusher's tackling, his Pocket Presence (minor bug 283), and the QB's weight (A3: - 0.7 × size_cost from 222 lb). What it does not read: an interior rusher's weight, the QB's Speed (that comes in through his clock), the rush ratings.

- A rusher tries when he is free within 1.25 + bend + finish + weight yards, or winning his block (w > 0.45) within 1.2 + arms + finish + weight, and hasn't tried in 0.6 s (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `sack_check`; the edge lane, 2026-11-08, bug 339, E1 and E2; weight from minor bug 358).
  - finish = 0.2 × (0.5 × his (0.5 Tackling + 0.5 Pursuit) + 0.5 × the same for his level's typical man at his position - 60)₊ / 10 (`FINISH_REACH`, `FINISH_MARK`, `FINISH_OWN`). Every rusher. An NFL edge is about 74 in both, an FBS one 54 and a high school one 33, so it lifts the NFL (sacks a pressure 0.22 to about 0.27, real 0.28) and barely moves college and high school. Read on his own in full, the best finishers ran away with it (34 sacks in a season; real best 22.5 and 23).
  - bend = clamp(0.2 × (Flexibility - T) / 10, -0.3, 0.6) for an edge on contain (`BEND_REACH`, E1): the man who bends the corner flattens to the quarterback. The 3-cone turns into sacks a pressure, not pressures.
  - arms = 0.18 × (arm - T arm)₊ × clamp(1 + (weight - T weight) / 13, 0, 3) for an edge (`ARM_REACH`, E2): the power rusher's long-arm stab. Short-armed men lose nothing; light men gain little.
  - weight = -0.07 × (weight - T weight) / 10 for an edge (`WEIGHT_REACH`, minor bug 358): real heavy edges finish less of their pressures (-0.49 pts of sacks a pressure per 10 lb held); the engine had them finishing more (+0.53), so heavy power rushers ran away with the sack tail. Pure, it is now -0.41. It reads no strength or force.
- Escape chance = sigmoid(-2.4 + (escape - tackle) / 12 - 1 if unseen + 1.2 if the rusher is still engaged) (`sack_check`).
  - escape = 0.4 Elusiveness + 0.3 Agility + 0.3 Strength (+ Pocket frame against an engaged rusher); tackle = 0.6 Tackling + 0.2 Strength + 0.2 Agility + Length.
- Strip sack = 0.0635 × (2.3 if he never saw him coming) × the rusher's force against his level's typical sack × the Strip tendency × his Ball Security against his level's typical starting back's × 1.6 in his throwing motion × a wet ball (`sack_check`, `STRIP_SACK`; engine gaps item 6: it was (0.06 seen, 0.16 unseen) × (1.3 - Ball Security / 120) × 1.2 from the blind side, 4.7% of sacks, real 12.4%). See Fumbles.
- The nearest other rusher within 2.25 yards of the QB, free or winning his own pass block (w > 0.45), shares the sack (`SHARE_RADIUS`, minor bug 358: it was a second tackler within 1.1 yards, which never happened, so no sack was ever shared; real 10.8% of NFL sacks are, now 10.8%): half a sack (and half a tackle for loss) to each of the two, never more than two, as the NCAA and the NFL keep it; the man who brings him down has the tackle and the second an assist. A tackle for loss on a run is one to a lone tackler and half to each man in on it (`game/stats.rs`; minor bug 36: thirds and sixths showed in the record books). QB injury check at share 0.8 seen, 1.3 unseen, force = rusher mass × speed (`sack_check`).

## The quarterback: reads, decisions, the throw

What it reads: Play Recognition, Decisions, Pocket Presence, Composure, Football IQ, Eye Manipulation, Quick Release, Footwork, Anticipation, Arm Strength, the three accuracies, Touch, Throw on the Move, Speed (scrambles), hidden consistency, chemistry. What it does not read: Concentration, Toughness, height (except batted balls and the Pocket frame link).

- The drop: top of the drop at quick 1.25 s, medium 1.6, deep 2.1, boot 2.2, screen 1.4, bubble 0.75, +0.45 off a run fake (`PA_TIME`; 0.35 before scheme-fit item 3), + clamp(-0.0015 × (Footwork - 60), -0.06, 0.08) (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`). Off a fake he sets at least 2 yards past the mesh (`PA_PAST_MESH`), except on the boot.
  - A bad shotgun snap costs 0.3-0.6 s, chance clamp(0.004 - 0.0001 × (center's Shotgun Snap - 60), 0.001, 0.012), a run's mesh too; a snap on the ground under center or in the shotgun is a loose ball (`run_snap`, engine gaps item 5; see Fumbles).
- First read: the concept's order as his group lines up, except the best receiver is fed first with chance min(0.1 + 0.02 × his edge over the next best, 0.55), or the plan's target corner's man 50% of the time (`run_snap`, `FEED_BASE`, `FEED_MAX`). Each man is read as a receiver, every rating about his own position's typical starter at the level: 0.3 Catching, 0.3 Route Running, 0.15 Release, 0.1 Contested Catch, 0.1 Ball Tracking, 0.05 Body Control (`receiving_edge`, `FEED_READ`, `feed_chance`; scheme-fit item 25: his spot rating fed a back half again as often for any running rating +15, and a tight end for his run blocking as much as for his hands). The order as his group lines up (lane SF2, item 2; `game/playbook.rs`, `stand_in`, `reads_in`, `route_in`): each receiver the concept reads that the group lacks gives his place in the reads to the group's next tight end past the first (12 and 22: the second; 13: the second, then the third), who runs that receiver's route, except that in the slot's place he keeps only a tight end's own (the seam, the corner, the drag) and otherwise runs the concept's own short route for the second tight end, or the slot's route where the concept draws him none (Texas), and that one who lines up across the ball from the flanker whose place he takes (an inline tight end on the weak side) runs the concept's own route for him too (the boot's drag into the roll, not the flanker's corner away from it) (`route_in`, `strong_side`; the rule and each concept's places are in `play-calling.md`); max protection keeps them in, a screen keeps its own men and an RPO its own read. 11, 10 and 21 personnel read as before. On a throw that reads a tight end past the first, the staff puts its best receiving tight end (`receiving_edge`) in the tight ends' place the play reads first with the feed's chance, min(0.1 + 0.02 × his edge over the man the depth chart puts there, 0.55), swapping the two (`game/flow.rs`, `lineup`, a keyed draw; SF2's hand-off to lane SF4: by the depth chart alone the second tight end took the flanker's place, which many concepts read first, and out-drew the first). A run and an RPO keep the depth chart's places.
- Time on each read = clamp(0.62 - 0.0045 × (iq - 50), 0.3, 0.95) × shown × (1 + 0.6 × lean(Hold)) × prep; iq = 0.5 PR + 0.5 Decisions; shown = (1 - 0.2 × (pre-snap read - 0.45)) × 1.15 if a disguise fooled him; prep = his prep factor, 1.33 Unprepared to 0.93 Sharp (`quarterback`).
- His clock = clamp(3.6 - 0.01 × (Ts Decisions - 60) - 0.004 × (Pocket Presence - 60), 3.1, 4.1), Ts his level's typical starter's (minor bug 283: within a level Decisions no longer shortens it, since a better decider already gets through his reads sooner and the shorter clock sent him to the checkdown early) + 0.3 on a deep drop + 1.0 × lean(Hold) + 0.2 × (Speed - his level's typical passer's) / 10 (61 in the NFL; `quarterback`, `LEGS_HOLD`; minor bug 276: real fast quarterbacks extend plays and take more sacks). The legs buy time for the throw more than for the run: his choice to take off waits half the legs' extra time on the clock and on the urgent line (`late_run`, `urgent_run`, `LEGS_RUN`). All of it and a fast man's scrambles came late and lost their extra yards; none of it and they rose 1.8 times real's slope.
- Hot read: pressure over 0.6 before 2 s (2.45 s off a run fake, `HOT_BY` + `PA_TIME`) sends him back to read 1 (always above 45 Football IQ, else 40%) (`quarterback`).
- Urgent: the first free rusher he knows of is under 0.85 - 0.004 × (Composure - 60) - 0.07 × (Speed - his level's typical passer's) / 10 + 0.1 × (0.6 × (Football IQ - Ts) + 0.4 × (Play Recognition - Ts)) / 10 s away: a passer who knows the pressure sees the blitzer sooner (`quarterback`, `LEGS_WAIT`, `BLITZ_ID`, `BLITZ_ID_LOOK`; minor bug 283). Off a run fake, urgent lets him throw before he sets, once his eyes are back from the mesh: at the mesh in the shotgun and the pistol, 0.2 s after it under center (`PA_EYES_UC`; scheme-fit item 3: he was blind from the mesh to the top of his drop, so a blitzer nobody picked up was a sure sack). A throw he decides on in that window goes when it would have, even if the blitzer is picked up first; outside it he reads nothing new until a tenth of a second after he sets (`READ_START`).
- How open a man looks = window + size room + noise × N(0, 1) (`quarterback`, `window`, `size_window`).
  - window: the gap the nearest defender can't close in the ball's flight (no reaction time for his man defender, 0.3 s for others) and anyone under the throwing lane, capped at 8; misread = (Ts field read - his) / 10, field read = 0.7 Decisions + 0.3 Play Recognition (`field_read`; minor bug 283). A poorer reader than Ts gives a defender who sees his eyes + 0.3 × misread s more and the man in his lane + 1.0 × misread yards more room than they leave, so he sees more room than there is (`MISREAD_EYES`, `MISREAD_LANE`). A better reader sees each defender who sees his eyes break as he will (0.21 + 0.004 × (100 - PR) - cover + the zone turn, below), blended in from 0.3 s and all of it at 10 points of field read above Ts, and the lane as it is (`READ_SHARP`, `ball_break`; scheme-fit item 5: he used to see both tighter than they were, so Decisions +15 cut picks by throwing 0.16 to 0.37 yards shorter and scored no more; a typical or poorer reader keeps the 0.3 s, so only a better one reads the zone turn). The one man he still gives + 0.3 × misread is a defender underneath reading his eyes, between him and the spot (15% to 100% of the way) within 5 yards of the throw's line (`LURK_NEAR`); ball speed 16.5 + 0.13 × Arm Strength yd/s; flight 0.28 + distance / (speed × (1 - 0.005 × (distance - 20)₊)); a spot past 25 + 0.45 × Arm is never open (`window`).
  - size room: on downfield and red-zone throws, 0.07 yd an inch of high reach past 2 (max 1.2) × sense × hands; sense = clamp(0.6 + 0.01 × (0.5 FIQ + 0.5 Dec - 50), 0.4, 1.2), hands = clamp(0.6 + Contested Catch / 150, 0.6, 1.3) (`size_window`, `high_reach`).
  - noise = max(1.6 - 0.017 × pro(field read + Pocket frame), 0.2), field read = 0.7 Decisions + 0.3 Play Recognition (`READ_DECIDE`; it was half each, minor bug 283) × (1 + 0.6 × panic) × (1 - 0.15 × (pre-read - 0.45)) + 0.2 if fooled; panic = pressure × clamp(1.6 - 1.4 × Composure / 100, 0.15, 1.5) (`quarterback`).
- He throws when it looks above need (`quarterback`):
  - normal = 0.3 + caution - 0.18 × seconds since set - 0.44 × panic + 0.1 on read 1, caution = 0.02 × (Ts - T) + 0.008 × (T - 60) - 0.06 × (Decisions - Ts), T his level's typical passer's Decisions and Ts its typical starter's (`DECISIONS_CAUTION`, `DECISIONS_TRUST`, minor bug 283): the typical starter keeps his room, a better decider trusts a tighter window because he sees it as it is (at +0.02 a point, more room for the better man, better decisions threw shorter and held the ball into sacks, and EPA a dropback fell); quick game reads the first two routes as one picture.
  - late or out of reads = 1.35 - 0.2 × seconds since set - 0.44 × panic; urgent = 0.3 + caution - 0.44 × panic - 0.2.
  - need falls 0.05 a yard past 12 yards deep (max 1.2) and by lean(Gunslinger) (+0.4 × lean deeper than 12); before he is late, a holder wants 0.5 × lean(Hold) more, 0.9 more under 10 yards; a gunslinger prefers the deeper of two open men.
  - need also falls (minor bugs 275, 277, 280, 283; sizes proposed, not confirmed): past 15 yards, 0.4 × (receiver's deep speed - the level's typical receiver's) / 10 + 0.4 × (Arm Strength - the level's typical passer's) / 10 - 0.1 (`DEEP_LEGS`, `DEEP_ARM`, `DEEP_BASE`); inside (within 9 yards of the middle, 4+ deep) and from the pocket (not in the scramble drill, within 4 yards of the ball's spot), 0.7 × (passer's height - the level's typical passer's) / 2 (`OVER_LINE`); 5+ yards deep or in the red zone, 1.0 × ((height - his level's typical man's at his position) + 2 × (arm - his)) / 4 - 0.35 for the receiver, no base in high school (`BIG_TRUST`, `BIG_BASE`, `big_base`). The bases: he takes his best target, so a trust that differs from man to man makes him throw more even where it averages zero (without them the NFL's 20+ yard share went from 11.1% to 12.2% and throwaways from 2.3% to 1.7%).
- Checkdown: past clock + 0.7 × min(lean(Gunslinger), 0) with nobody open he takes a man within 7 yards with window ≥ 0.3; past clock + 0.5 - 0.4 × max(lean, 0) he forces it to anyone with window ≥ -1.2, unless his best man is still covered (window below 0) or nobody is there: then he gives up on the play with chance p0 = clamp(0.8 + 0.015 × (sense - 75), 0.05, 0.95) for his level's typical starter, sense = 0.5 pro(Decisions) + 0.5 pro(FIQ), moved on the log-odds 0.3 a 10 points of Decisions and 0.25 of Football IQ about him (`GIVE_UP_DECIDE`, `GIVE_UP_IQ`, minor bug 283), and throws it away as soon as no rusher can stop the flick, unless he runs first (minor bug 138) (`quarterback`, `GIVE_UP`).
- Release: clamp(0.34 - 0.00125 × (0.75 Quick Release + 0.25 Footwork - 60), 0.26, 0.42) s, 0.6× on screens. A free rusher who will reach him within it stops the throw (`quarterback`).
  - He lets it go clamp(0.25 - 0.0025 × (Anticipation - 50) + 0.12 × (0.45 - chemistry), 0, 0.6) s after he decides; heavy pressure skips the wait.
- Scramble: with nobody open or pressure > 0.5, a lane and 2+ s, chance clamp(0.03 + 0.004 × (pro(Speed) - 50), 0.01, 0.2) × max(1 + 1.2 × lean(Scramble), 0.1), × 3 once he has given up on the pass or been out of the pocket 1.2 s; with pressure > 1 and pro(Speed) > 75, 0.25 × that lean factor. An edge rushing upfield can run past him; a contain edge shuts his side from 3 + 0.8 × (-lean)₊ yards (`quarterback`).
- Out of the pocket, eyes downfield (minor bug 138): with the same lane, nobody open or pressure > 0.5 and 1.6+ s, chance 0.15 × clamp(1 - 0.5 × lean(Scramble), 0.3, 1.7) a tenth of a second that he breaks to the side with no free rusher, aiming 0.6 yards upfield for every 3 across, three such steps ahead so easing in never sets his pace, at 0.67 of his top speed (6.7 yd/s for the NFL's typical starter, the pace every passer had; 6.2 for a 5.2 forty and 7.1 for a 4.5: minor bug 409, `EXTEND_PACE`, `EXTEND_AHEAD`), never closer than 5 yards to the sideline. From then he is out of reads and takes whoever comes open (`quarterback`, `EXTEND`, `EXTEND_RUN`).
- Throwaway: always once he has given up (above); otherwise, with nobody open or pressure > 0.6 and a rusher on him or the play 0.5 s past his clock, 0.06 + 0.003 × pro(Ts Decisions) + 0.004 × (pro(Decisions) - pro(Ts)) + 0.12 outside the pocket (`THROW_AWAY_DECIDE`, minor bug 283). Inside the pocket a rusher who will reach him in half the release stops it (minor bug 138: it was the full release and 0.3 × the chance).
  - Intentional grounding (engine gaps lane EG5): inside the pocket the ball must reach a receiver's area. With none of his reads up in a route it can't; otherwise it misses with 0.105 × clamp(1 - 0.02 × (sense - Ts sense), 0.4, 1.8), sense = 0.5 pro(Decisions) + 0.5 pro(FIQ) (`GROUND_POCKET`). Outside the pocket it is legal once it reaches the line, but with a rusher on him the flick carries 29 + 0.1 × (Arm Strength - 60) yards, and one that dies short of the line is grounding (`FLICK_CARRY`). The crew flags 0.9 × its pass interference eye of them (`GROUND_SEEN`; `quarterback`). It was a flat 8% draw inside the pocket and never outside it.
- Accuracy: Short to 5 air yards, Medium at 15, Deep from 25, blended between; on a medium throw over a defender, Touch takes up to 0.3 of it (`pass_accuracy`, `throw`).
- Placement: the aim moves max(0.45 × accuracy / 100 + 1.0 × (field read - Ts) / 10 × near, 0) yards away from the nearest defender, near = clamp(1 - his distance from the spot / 4, 0, 1): with a defender close, a good reader puts it on the side away from him (`PLACE_READ`, `PLACE_NEAR`, minor bug 283). It stays 1.5 yards inside the sideline (`throw`).
- The ball in the air: the target works to where it lands and times his arrival, defenders within 25 yards break on it, but neither runs past a sideline or end line (0.3 yards inside, `feet_in`): a ball that sails out of bounds is caught only within his reach of the line, feet in.
- Everyone else stays on the field too: a receiver sitting down keeps 2.5 yards of room at the sideline and stays 1.5 yards inside the end line; a bust's few yards off and a jumped route's point over the top stay inside the lines like any route; the quarterback pulls up once the ball is out (he used to coast through the flight); punt gunners and their jammers start inside the lines (`run_route`, `jump_route`, `run_snap`, `kicking.rs`). None moved `match_check`'s NFL numbers past noise over 4,000 games.
- Miss sd = (0.25 + 0.036 × distance + 0.0007 × downfield²) × AIM_BASE × e^(0.005 × (110 - pro(Ts accuracy))) × e^(-k × (accuracy - Ts accuracy)) yards, Ts accuracy his level's typical starter's blend for the depth, k = 0.02 short, 0.025 medium, 0.014 deep, blended like the skills (`aim_sd`, `typical_accuracy`, `AIM_SHORT`, `AIM_MEDIUM`, `AIM_DEEP`; minor bug 283: on e^(0.005 × (110 - pro(accuracy))) alone Short Accuracy moved completion 0.05 of real starters' spread). AIM_BASE is 1.25 (1.3 before): the steeper slopes spread passers wider, and a wider miss costs more catches than a tighter one gains, so the base came down to hold each level's completion. Then × (`throw`):
  - pressure over 0.3: 1 + 0.9 × min(pressure, 1.5) × (1.35 - Composure / 100); moving over 3 yd/s: 1.25 - 0.002 × Throw on the Move.
  - wind 1 + 0.00129 × wind² / (1 + (wind / 20)²) × (1 + 0.01 × distance): almost nothing in a breeze, growing with the square of the wind, levelling off in a gale (engine gaps item 30, `WIND_MISS`, `WIND_GALE`; it was wind × (0.010 + 0.0001 × distance), minor bug 144); rain or snow 1 + 0.007 × distance, a wet ball missing more the farther it flies (item 30, `WET_THROW`; it was a flat 1.1); cold 1 + 0.08 × unused-to-cold.
  - hidden consistency 1.15 - 0.015 × consistency; chemistry 1 - 0.12 × (chemistry - 0.45).
  - along the line × (1 + 0.035 × (downfield - 12)₊) (minor bug 135; it was 0.022), and past 25 yards × 1.1 × clamp(1 + 0.01 × (75 - Arm), 0.8, 1.4).
- Roughing the passer: a free rusher closing who lands more than 0.4 s after the release draws it at 0.082, sooner at 0.0123, × (1.5 - Discipline20 / 20) × the crew's personal-foul eye × the book's share of the same hits it flags (`fouls::roughing_share`: the NFL 1, college and high school 0.62; EG5, it was 0.1 and 0.015 at every level) (`throw`).

## Play action (scheme-fit item 3)

What it reads: the passer's Ball Handling (how long the fake holds them) and Throw on the Move (the boot), each defender's Play Recognition, Discipline and Downhill habit (how long he bites), and the back's Football IQ and Pass Blocking against a blitzer's Blitzing (whether he drops the fake for him). What it does not read: the back's running ratings (any back sells the same fake), the line's run blocking.

- The tag: any dropback concept but the RPO halves and the three play-action concepts (which always fake) can carry a run fake, screens too (`OffCall::fake`, `OffCall::play_action`; `game/playbook.rs`, `takes_fake`). Who calls it and how often: [play-calling.md](play-calling.md), "Play action".
- The faker: the back, or the fullback with no back there, lined up in the backfield (more than 2 yards behind the line, within 4.5 yards of the ball across the field, at any depth). From an empty set, or with nobody there, the snap is a straight drop (`run_snap`).
- The mesh: 4 yards behind the line and 0.6 to the fake side at 0.8 s under center, 4.6 and 0.5 at 0.7 s in the pistol, 5.2 and 0.4 at 0.6 s in the shotgun (`PA_MESH_UC`, `PA_MESH_PISTOL`, `PA_MESH_GUN`). The fake goes to the strength side, away from it on the boot. The back times his path through the mesh, carries the fake 0.45 s toward a spot a yard past the line and 3 yards to the fake side (`PA_CARRY`, `PA_AIM`), then takes his job in the pass at once: his block, or his route from where the fake left him (`Job::Handoff` with `Sim::fake`). Until then he is nobody's target, the hot throw's included.
- The drop: 0.45 s more to the top of it (`PA_TIME`), at least 2 yards past the mesh (`PA_PAST_MESH`), except on the boot.
- Who it holds: every defender who keys the backfield plays the run he is shown until his own read time (above, "Reading the play"), kept as `Agent::fooled_until`: the rushers, the zone defenders, and a man on a tight end or a back. A man on a wide receiver keeps his eyes on his man. Until then, while the quarterback still holds the ball and hasn't taken off to run, the back carrying out the fake is the runner for him, and he fits it as a run (`defend`); rolling out on the boot or in the scramble drill doesn't end it. A corner's Peeks flaw counts on any faked snap and on an RPO the quarterback pulled, as against double moves (`jump_route`).
- A fooled rusher in a block plays it as the run: no counter move, no drive toward the passer, and the block's walk keeps only its noise (and the blocker's Re-fits his hands once the rep is past 0.3), so it stands about where it is until he reads the pass, or the quarterback throws or takes off to run; rolling out on the boot or in the scramble drill doesn't end it (`game/sim.rs`, `update_blocks`). His rush starts from there.
- The half-slide (`pa_protection`), with the back in the protection (his route a block or a check-release), not on the boot: on the fake side (more than 0.5 yards from the ball, `MAN_SIDE`) the guard and tackle block the down linemen over them, outside in, so the tackle has the end; the center and the far side slide, each taking the rusher nearest a spot 0.7 yards away from the fake (`SLIDE_GAP`) within 3.2 yards across (`PRO_REACH`); the back has the first rusher left on his side, from the center out. Anyone still free goes to a tight end or another back kept in, each taking the nearest one left (`protect_rest`), never to a lineman: a lineman the half-slide left without a man has nobody at the snap and picks up a free rusher who comes within 4.5 yards of him and 6 of the passer (`blitz_threat`). Anyone left after that is the quarterback's hot. When the back runs a route, the protection is the standard one.
- The back in the protection blocks a free rusher who comes within 2 yards of him on his path (`PA_FILL`), and from the mesh on his own man if that man comes free and he sees him (`picks_up`, above); otherwise he carries the fake, then blocks or check-releases.
- The boot (`boot_protection`): the standard protection, then the guard on the boot side pulls to the end man there (the rusher farthest outside on that side, more than 2 yards out); the blocker who had the end takes the guard's man, and if nobody had the end, a lineman with no man of his own blocks back on the guard's man. ## Routes against coverage (separation)

What it reads: Route Running, Release, Agility, Strength against Man/Zone Coverage, Press, Play Recognition, Anticipation, Agility, Flexibility; against zone, Route Running also turns the defender near him on the throw. What it does not read: the receiver's height or weight (until the catch point), Speed beyond his own top speed.

## The catch and the contested catch

What it reads: Catching, Body Control, Jumping, height, arm length, Contested Catch, Strength, the Length and Highpointer links, Contact tendency over the middle, weather, the passer's Touch. What it does not read: Concentration, Toughness, Route Running, Speed, weight (except through the jump).

- In the lane: a defender within 1.1 yards of a short, flat ball's path touches it with 0.6 × (1 - gap / 1.1) × (0.5 + coverage / 200) × (1, or 0.35 without eyes on the QB) (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `catch_point`).

It knocks the ball loose as a breakup, and draws unnecessary roughness on a defenseless receiver with 0.05 × max(1.35 - Discipline20 / 20, 0.2) × max(1 + 0.7 × lean(Big hit), 0.2). League breakups and completions held (match_check).
- The catch = 1 - max(0.15 - 0.00095 × Ts - 0.0016 × (Catching - Ts), 0.012), Ts his level's typical starter's Catching at his position, then (`catch_point`, `CATCH_HANDS`; scheme-fit item 6: it was 0.00095 a point from none, and the drops a target spread about half as much between men as real ones do; sized on that spread, men with 40 or more targets against FTN charting 2022-24):

## Drops

- A drop is a missed catch with the ball inside 0.8 of his radius and no defender within a yard (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `catch_point`). It reads the same catch, about his level's typical starter (`CATCH_HANDS`): an NFL wide receiver at the typical starter's 83 Catching misses 7.1% of such balls, at +15 4.7% and at -15 9.5%; at FBS (typical 62) 9.1%, and a 99 4.6% at the NFL and 3.2% at FBS (the 1.2% floor is out of reach). Before lane SF4 a 99 missed 5.6% and a 60 9.3% at every level. A miss counts as a drop only with nobody within a yard, so a more open man has more balls he can drop.
- A drop costs him 1 point of momentum (`game/flow.rs`, `resolve`, `edge_hit`).

## Interceptions

What it reads: the defender's hands (Catching, raised half the gap from his level's norm to the NFL's), coverage, Jumping, height, arms, whether he sees the ball or jumped the route. What it does not read: the defender's Anticipation outside the route jump, the QB's Decisions after the throw.

- The man who picks it off runs it back as a ball carrier (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `defend` skips him; minor bug 132: he chased himself and stopped every tick, 0.005 pick sixes a team game against real 0.05).
- Hands = Catching + 0.5 × (Catching norm at the NFL - at his level) (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `ball_hands`, `HANDS_LEVEL`).
- A lane touch is a pick with 0.3 × hands / 70; an off-target ball within 0.8 × (1 + 0.01 × (Jumping - 50)) of a defender with 0.18 × hands / 70 (`catch_point`).
- In front at the catch point: clamp(FRONT_PICK × closeness × (0.45 + hands / 110) × (0.45 if he doesn't see the ball) × (3.5 if he jumped the route) × size, 0, 0.7) (`catch_point`).
- Off the receiver's hands to a defender within 3 yards: TIP_PICK × (1 - d / 3) × (0.5 + hands / 100); a breakup is picked PBU_PICK of the time (`catch_point`). Where picks come from (minor bug 283): FTN charting (NFL 2023-24) has 87% of picks on throws it calls interception-worthy, 53% of those picked and 0.33% of the rest; the engine spread them over every throw, so a careful passer saved few. FRONT_PICK 0.27 (0.22 before), TIP_PICK 0.065 (0.13), PBU_PICK 0.06 (unchanged), with the league's picks held.
- The thrower loses 1 point of momentum, the interceptor gains 1 (`game/flow.rs`, `resolve`).

## YAC

- YAC = the spot minus where he caught it; yards before contact = first contact minus the line (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).

## Fumbles

What it reads: the hit's force against his level's typical hit, the hitter's Strength and Tackling, the carrier's Ball Security against his level's typical starting back's, a catch not yet tucked away, rain, snow and cold, the Strip tendency; on a sack whether the passer saw the rusher coming and his throwing motion; on a snap the quarterback's Ball Handling and the center's Shotgun Snap; at the loose ball each man's eyes, reactions, hands and feet, strength and Competitiveness. What it does not read: the carrier's Strength or Toughness, the hit's angle.

- In a hit (`game/sim.rs`, `hit_effects`; engine gaps item 7): 0.0064 × (force / (1,700 × level))^1.5 × clamp(1 + 0.025 × (T Ball Security - Ball Security) + 0.020 × (hit - T hit), 0.25, 2.5) × 2.6 inside 0.5 s of a catch or a pick × (1.35 in rain or snow) × (1 + 0.3 × unused-to-cold) × max(1 + 1.15 × lean(Strip), 0.1); T Ball Security is his level's typical starting back's whatever his position (one scale for every carrier), hit = the hitter's 0.5 Strength + 0.5 Tackling and T hit his level's typical man at his position's (`FUMBLE_BASE`, `FUMBLE_FORCE`, `FUMBLE_BS`, `FUMBLE_HIT`, `FRESH_CATCH`, `FRESH_CATCH_FUMBLE`). It was 0.018 with the carrier read against the hitter's typical man: NFL designed runs fumbled 1.45% of the time (real 0.75%).
- The level's typical hit (`hit_scale`), as a share of the NFL's, measured on `match_check`: college 0.82 a tackle and 0.96 a sack, high school 0.62 and 0.76; FCS and small colleges take FBS's (unmeasured, `match_check` plays neither). So a typical college or high school back fumbles about as often under his level's typical hit as an NFL back under an NFL one.
- Reaching the ball out: 0.035 × clamp(1 + 0.025 × (T Ball Security - Ball Security) + 0.020 × (hit - T hit), 0.3, 2.5) (`reach`).
- A strip sack (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `sack_check`; item 6): 0.0635 × (2.3 if he never saw the rusher) × force / (1,500 × level's sack) × max(1 + 1.15 × lean(Strip), 0.1) × clamp(1 + 0.025 × (T Ball Security - his), 0.4, 2) × (1.6 in his throwing motion) × the wet ball, force = the rusher's mass × speed (`STRIP_SACK`, `STRIP_SACK_FORCE`, `STRIP_UNSEEN`, `STRIP_MOTION`, `STRIP_BS`). He saw him if where the rusher was 0.35 s before is inside his view about his read, half-angle 1.2 + 0.006 × (Pocket Presence - 50) radians (`game/sim.rs`, `saw_coming`). One out of his hand in his motion is a Throw fumble, kept on the sack. The ball goes off with the passer and rusher's pile. His time to throw ends when the ball comes out, not with the return (`run_snap`).
- A fumbled snap (`run_snap`; item 5): under center 0.0049 × (0.7 fq + 0.3 fc), in the shotgun 0.00247 × (0.4 fq + 0.6 fc), × the wet ball; fq = clamp(1 + 0.03 × (T Ball Handling - the quarterback's), 0.4, 2.5), fc the same on the center's Shotgun Snap, T the level's typical starter (`SNAP_UNDER_CENTER`, `SNAP_SHOTGUN`, `SNAP_SLOPE`). Of the center's share, half under center and a quarter in the shotgun never get to the quarterback and are the center's fumble (`SNAP_WILD`). The ball is down 0.05-0.15 s after the snap under center, at his feet at 0.5-2.5 yd/s, and 0.3-0.4 s in the shotgun, off his hands at 1-3 yd/s back or past him at 4-8; he finds it 0.4 s later. A quarterback who picks up his own fumbled snap behind the line on a pass goes on with the play (`Sim::resume`). A bad shotgun snap that isn't fumbled costs a pass 0.3-0.6 s of the drop and a run the same off its mesh: clamp(0.004 - 0.0001 × (Shotgun Snap - 60), 0.001, 0.012).
- A handoff on the ground (`run_snap`): 0.0006 × clamp(1 + 0.03 × (0.65 × (T back's Ball Security - his) + 0.35 × (T Ball Handling - the quarterback's)), 0.4, 2.5) (`EXCHANGE`, `EXCHANGE_SLOPE`; 0.0025 × (1.6 - (0.65 Ball Security + 0.35 Ball Handling) / 100) before, one in 500); a pitch on the ground 0.012 × (1.6 - Catching / 100). Punt muffs: see Kicking.
- The loose ball (`fumble_going`, `loose_tick`; item 4): it pops at U(1, 12) yd/s in any direction on top of the pile's way (the carrier's and his tacklers' momentum), slows by e^(-0.5 t) and turns U(-1.4, 1.4) radians at each bounce (every 0.25-0.55 s, keeping 55-95% of its speed above 1 yd/s) (`LOOSE_POP`, `LOOSE_DRAG`). Blocks within 3 yards of it break up (`LOOSE_BLOCK`). - Getting it (`loose_grab`), nearest first, within 0.8 yards (a dive 1.8, on the ground 0.8): with traffic (a man of the other side on his feet within 2 yards of it in the NFL; in college and high school, where a man who falls on it is down, one less than half a yard farther from it than he is; on the ground within 1), off his feet, a ball faster than 6 yd/s or his own side's fumble, he falls on it with 0.6 × max(1 - speed / 10, 0.3) × his lunge (1 - 0.45 × (distance - 0.8)); with nobody there he runs onto it and picks it up with clamp(0.6 + 0.01 × (0.6 Catching + 0.4 Agility - T's), 0.15, 0.95) × max(1 - speed / 8, 0.2) (`scoop_chance`; `LOOSE_ROOM`, `LOOSE_RACE`, `LOOSE_FALL`, `LOOSE_SCOOP`). One he can't hold squirts off at 40% of its speed plus 1.5-4 yd/s. On the ground with the other side's nearest man within 1.2 yards, that man comes up with it at 0.15 × clamp(1 + 0.02 × (grip - his), 0.3, 2), grip = 0.5 Strength + 2.5 × Competitiveness20 (`LOOSE_RIP`). On the ground 3 s with nobody on it, the nearest man has it.
- Down where he has it (`loose_won`): on the ground in college and high school; in the NFL with a man of the other side within 1.5 yards as he gets it, or within a yard (a touch) before he is up 0.6 s later (`LOOSE_TOUCH`, `LOOSE_GETUP`, `LOOSE_TOUCH_DOWN`, `check_carrier`). The fumbling side covers its own ball up and stays down (real, 2.7% of the offense's recoveries go anywhere). Otherwise he runs with it, his side blocking and the other chasing, a kick return's fumble included (`game/kicking.rs`, `run_return`).
- Who may advance it (`game/rules.rs`, `FumbleRule`, `Rules::fumble_rule`; `game/flow.rs`, the snap's `FumbleSnap`): the defense always. In the NFL only the fumbler may advance his own team's fumble on fourth down, on a try and after the two-minute warning of either half; college on fourth down and a try; high school anyone (a state on college rules plays the NCAA's). A teammate's recovery then is dead, back at the spot of the fumble if he got it ahead of it. A muffed snap is anyone's. A high school try ends when the defense has it. The CFL: anyone, and a ball out of bounds is the team's that touched it last (`books.rs`).
- How it ends (`loose_end`): out of bounds between the goal lines it is the fumbling team's ball, back at the spot of the fumble when it went forward (the NFL, the NCAA, high school from 2025; not a muffed snap); over a goal line it is a touchback when the other side put it there and a safety when its own did. A recovery in the other side's end zone is a touchdown; the fumbling side's in its own a safety; the other side's in its own end zone a touchback, inside its 5 and carried in kept where he got it (the momentum rule), from farther out a safety. A lost fumble's yards run to where it came out; the return is the defense's, and the other side losing it back is a new series (`game/flow.rs`, `resolve`).
- A flag on the return (`resolve`): only the side with the ball blocks, so a holding or an illegal block by the defense is a foul on its own return, an interception's included. The ball stays its own, marked back its book's yards (10 at every level, `game/fouls.rs`, `penalty`) from the spot of the foul, or from where the return ended if that is short of it, half the distance to its own goal at most, and a score on the return comes off the board. Before lane EG2's fix the offense could take that foul and the ball back with it, and the score still counted (NFL interceptions 0.749 a team game before it, 0.771 after, real 0.756, on 2,000 `match_check` games before play action merged).
- On the play log (`PlayRecord::fumble`, `FumbleNote`: how, out of bounds, yards run with it; `recovered`; `fumble_rec` on the recovering man's line): "Fumble by X on the snap (on the sack, as he threw, on the handoff, on the pitch, reaching for the line), recovered by Y (defense) and run back 12 yards", or "out of bounds"; "Taken all the way back for a touchdown.", or with a flag that takes it off "Taken all the way back, but a flag takes the score off the board." (`why`). A ruled fumble the replay overturns takes its loose ball with it (`officiate`).

## Penalties

Each crew draws four multipliers, N(1, 0.15) clamped 0.6-1.5: holding, pass interference, pre-snap, personal fouls (`game/sim.rs`, `Crew::draw`).

The rule books (engine gaps lane EG5, `game/fouls.rs`). A game plays one of three books: the NFL's, the NCAA's (college, and high schools in Texas every season and in Massachusetts through 2018) and the NFHS's (every other high school). The other leagues keep the NFL's (`Rules::foul_book`, `Rules::high_school`; `world/hs_year.rs`, `world/front_office/school.rs`). Before EG5 one table with no level marked every foul (`FoulKind::yards`). Each foul's yards, first down and loss of down by book and season (`penalty`):
- Offensive holding, an illegal block and the offense's illegal use of hands: 10. Defensive holding and the defense's illegal hands: the NFL's 5 and a first down; college's 10, a first down on every call from 2022 and before only for a hold on a pass that crossed the line; the NFHS's 10. The NFL's illegal contact: 5 and a first down (college and high school have no such rule).
- Pass interference: the NFL's at the spot of the foul with a first down; college's at the spot, 15 yards from the previous spot at most, with a first down; the NFHS's 15 from the previous spot, a first down only through 2012. Offensive interference: the NFL's 10, college's and the NFHS's 15.
- Roughing the passer: 15 and a first down. Unnecessary roughness, face mask and targeting: 15, a first down except under NFHS rules. Unsportsmanlike conduct: 15, the defense's with a first down except under NFHS rules.
- False start, offside, neutral zone infraction, delay of game, illegal formation and ineligible downfield: 5. Too many men: 5; NFHS illegal participation 15.
- Intentional grounding, with the loss of the down: the NFL's 10 from the previous spot, or the spot of the pass when that is farther back; college's at the spot of the pass; the NFHS's 5 from it.

Where it is marked (`defense`, `offense`). The previous spot, except: a defensive foul during a run (or the run after a catch) that ended past the line, and any personal foul after a gain, from the end of the run, the play's yards standing (roughing the passer on a completion run past the line too). The offense's hold or illegal block behind the line goes from the previous spot (NFHS before 2023 from the spot of the foul), and one past the line during a run that ended past it from its own spot or the end of the run. The offense's hold, illegal block or grounding in its own end zone is a safety. Half the distance at every level: no penalty moves the ball more than half the way from its enforcement spot to the offending team's goal line (`half`). Pass interference keeps its own spots near the goal: the NFL's the 1 in the end zone, college's 15 or the 2, both half the distance from on or inside the 2. The offended side takes the better of the play and the best of the fouls by expected points; fouls by both sides offset and the down is played again (`game/flow.rs`, `resolve`). Pre-snap fouls halve from the ball (`no_play`). A foul by the defense on its own return (holding, an illegal block) is marked off the return instead (see Fumbles, "A flag on the return").

Kicks. A missed field goal the defense doesn't field goes over at the NFL's spot of the kick (the 20 when that is inside it), at college's previous spot (or the 20), and under NFHS rules as a scrimmage kick: a touchback at the 20 from the end zone, the defense's ball where a short one came down (`missed_fg_spot`; `game/flow.rs`, `field_goal`). A kickoff out of bounds: the NFL's 40; college's 35 through 2007, its 40 in 2008-2011, then 30 yards beyond the kick; the NFHS's 25 beyond it (`kick_out_spot`; `game/kicking.rs`, `kickoff`).

Live and dead at the snap (`live_at_snap`). The offense's illegal formation and the defense's twelfth man still on the field at the snap are live-ball fouls; so is the defense's offside at the snap in the NFL and college, a free play: the offense takes the better of the play and the 5 yards. Under NFHS rules offside is encroachment, dead. False starts, delays, neutral zone infractions and a twelfth man caught before the snap kill the play (`game/flow.rs`, `pre_snap_foul`, `TWELVE_DEAD`).

- Offensive holding: one check when a block first passes w 0.75: 0.044 × (1.7 - Discipline20 / 14) × clamp(1 + 0.012 × beat, 0.5, 1.6), beat = 0.5 × (rush or shed + defender Hand Use) - 0.5 × (blocking + blocker Hand Use). ## Fatigue

- Ratings fall by share × tiredness (tiredness = 1 - energy): Speed, Acceleration, Agility, Balance, Concentration 0.10; Strength 0.12; Flexibility, Jumping, Stamina, Discipline, Communication 0.06; Ball Carrier Vision and the reads (FIQ, PR, Anticipation, Decisions, Composure) 0.08; technical skills 0.06 (`attributes.rs`, `ATTRIBUTE_PROFILES`; `game/sim.rs`, `Agent::new`).
- Top speed × (1 - 0.08 × tiredness), accel × (1 - 0.1 × tiredness), sprint build-up × (1 + 0.1 × tiredness) (`Agent::new`).
- Work in a snap: running 0.035 × (0.2 + 0.8 × speed / 9) a tick; pass block 0.5 a second for the blocker, 1.0 for the rusher; run block 0.9 each; a runner in a pile 30 a second (`Agent::steer`, `update_blocks`, `fight_tick`).
- After the snap: energy -= 0.041 × work × max(1.55 - 0.011 × (Stamina + Lungs), 0.4) × (1 + road wear), floor 0.25 (`game/flow.rs`, `run_scrimmage_call`; `game/roster.rs`, `drain`).
- Recovery each snap: pace × (1 + 0.1 × Lungs share) × (0.012 on the field, 0.05 on the bench), pace = seconds between snaps / 37.5; +0.05 after the 1st and 3rd quarters, +0.35 at halftime, +0.15 for overtime, +0.02 at the two-minute warning, the defense's clock-stopping timeout and your timeouts; the offense's hurry-up timeout gives none (`run_scrimmage_call`, `recover`, `end_of_period`, `start_overtime`, `tick`, `after_snap_clock`, `user_timeout`).
- Kickoff energy is your week's `prep.fresh`, clamped 0.5-1 (1 for AI teams) (`game/flow.rs`, `Side::new`).
- Late in a drive a tired, unfocused player busts more (see Busts).

## Momentum

- -1 for a foul, an interception thrown, a drop or a fumble; -0.6 a missed tackle; +1 for an interception, a sack, a touchdown carry (`game/flow.rs`, `resolve`).

## Fire on the field (coach fire)

Design: `docs/design/coach-fire.md` (Cain 2026-11-17); what the build measured: `docs/match-engine.md`, "Coach fire" (`fire_check`). Every size here is Claude's, proposed, not confirmed. Fire is the 0-1 pitch a talk leaves him at (`game/talk.rs`, `Mind.fire`, `mind_now`, `land`); what it does is measured from where he came in, so a game with no talk plays as it would without fire.

- **His own pitch** (`talk::spot`): his position's (`ideal_fire`: kickers 0.34, QB 0.42, WR and CB 0.52, S 0.56, RB and TE 0.58, OT and IOL 0.62, LB 0.63, EDGE and DT 0.66), up by 0.035 × (10 - temperament) / 10 for a hot temper, 0.025 × (competitiveness - 10) / 10 for a fierce competitor and 0.03 × (Physicality20 - 10) / 10 for a hitter (at most 0.08 in all), down by 0.035 × (temperament - 10) / 10 for a calm temper and 0.03 × (10 - Composure20) / 10 for a man who rattles (at most 0.06). A quarterback adds 0.02 × his Gunslinger lean and 0.01 × his Scramble lean when they are over the middle and takes 0.02 and 0.015 when they are under it. A Branch-type safety (temperament 3, competitiveness 17, Physicality 17 on the 1-20 scale) plays best at 0.62, a calm pocket passer (temperament 16, Gunslinger 6, Scramble 5) at 0.38.
- **What a talk does** (`talk::fire_work`), from his fire going in (f0) to after it (f1), each within ±3 (`FIRE_CAP`): power and effort = 12 × (how far under his pitch he was - how far under it he is now) rating points (`POWER_PER_FIRE`); fine and thinking work = -12 × (how far past it he is now - how far past it he was) (`FINE_PER_FIRE`); the fire habits = 4 × the rise up to his pitch + 12 × the rise past it, tendency points (`HABITS_UNDER`, `HABITS_OVER`). Under his pitch more fire is more effort and costs no thinking; past it the effort holds and the fine work goes; a talk that cools a man who came in past his pitch gives fine work back, and one that cools a man under it costs effort.
- **Power and effort** (`game/sim.rs`, `Agent::fire_up`, from `run_snap`): Strength, Lower Body and Pursuit (`talk::POWER_WORK`); Motor on the 1-20 scale, power / 2.6, where it acts (the chase from behind, `Sim::trait20`); his first step, 0.4% a point (acceleration up, time to speed down; `FIRE_BURST`); his hit, 1% of its force a point (`FIRE_HIT`, `Sim::hit_force`, which also carries the fumble and injury checks).
- **Fine and thinking work**: Short, Medium and Deep Accuracy, Throw on the Move, Touch, Decisions, Play Recognition, Anticipation and Catching (`talk::FINE_WORK`), and Discipline and Concentration on the 1-20 scale, fine / 2.6, beside the talk's own focus (`Pulse::focus20`, read by the false start and offside checks too).
- **The fire habits** (`Tend::fiery`): Contact, Snap timing, Strip, Big hit, Downhill and Jump routes, plus the talk's habit points in `Sim::tend` and in the offside check's Snap timing (`game/flow.rs`, `pre_snap_foul`). An order he obeys still sets the number.
- Composure (±2.5), the talk's focus (±2) and the late-hit heat work as before. All of it fades by the talk's half-life; a sideline word adds to what is left (`Pulse::add`).
- **The landing's lift** (the screens' and the head coach's read of a talk, `Landing.lift`) is no longer added to every rating: it is the power and fine points weighed by his position's weights on each kind (`talk::work_shares`), plus 0.25 × the composure points, within ±3. Before (dialogue D4-D6), a talk added up to ±1.5 rating points to every rating through his momentum edge.
- **Targeting** (`game/rules.rs`, `Targeting`, `Rules::for_year`): college none to 2007, a 15-yard foul in 2008-2012 and a foul with an ejection from 2013 (NCAA); high school a 15-yard foul from 2013 (NFHS; thrown out only when flagrant); the NFL has no targeting foul (unnecessary roughness, and an ejection when flagrant). `FoulKind::Targeting` is 15 yards and a first down; a personal foul after a gain is enforced from the end of the run (`FoulKind::personal`).
- **The hits that draw it**: the late or extra hit on a tackle (unnecessary roughness, above: `game/sim.rs`, `tackled`), 9% of them led with the crown of the helmet (`HIGH_ON_TACKLE`; 7% before engine gaps lane EG5 brought the late hits it rides on to FBS's real count, refitted so FBS still calls about 0.04 targeting fouls a team game); the hit as the ball arrives at the catch point (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `catch_point`, `hit_at_catch`), a flag at 0.0425 × max(1.35 - Discipline20 / 20, 0.2) × max(1 + 0.7 × lean(Big hit), 0.2) × temper × the book's restraint (`CATCH_HIT_FOUL`, 0.05 before EG5), 70% of them high and helmet-led (`HIGH_AT_CATCH`); a sliding quarterback (`late_on_slide`): the nearest free defender within 2.5 yards closing at 4+ yards a second hits him at 0.255 × (1 - distance / 2.5) × min((closing - 4) / 3, 1) × the same Discipline, Big hit and temper terms × the restraint (`SLIDE_HIT`, 0.3 before EG5, a guess: no real count of flags on slides was found; about 1 in 100 of the test league's slides draws one), 60% high (`HIGH_ON_SLIDE`). The tackle gives nearly all of the hit fouls: on `match_check fbs 2000 1` the catch point flags 0.002 a team game and scrambles 0.012, against 0.45 on other plays (`AFM_GAPS=1` prints them). Temper = max(1 + 0.04 × (10 - temperament), 0.4) × max(1 + the talks' heat, 0.3) (`Sim::temper`). A high, helmet-led hit is targeting where the rule has it, else unnecessary roughness; a share 0.02 × temper of every such foul is flagrant and thrown out at every level (`FLAGRANT`, `Sim::hit_foul`; fitted to about 6 NFL ejections for hits a season: 21 ejections of every kind in 2023, Football Zebras). The catch point and the slide mark the man hit defenseless (the league's dearer fine). Fire reaches these flags three ways: the Big hit habit, the heat and Discipline (the fine work).
- **Thrown out** (`FireNotes.ejected`): he sits the rest of the game, the penalty taken or not (`game/flow.rs`, `fire_after`, `Side.out`). A college targeting ejection in the second half sits the first half of his next college game (`world/folk/rules.rs`, `game_flags`, `LeagueBook.half_out`, dropped after 400 days unserved; `world/folk/gametalk.rs`, `day_for`, college teams only; `talk::Day.first_half`, set out at kickoff and back at halftime).
- **A big hit** (`Sim::note_hit`: every tackle's hit, the sack, the hit at the catch point, the hit on a slide): the play's hardest hit at or over its level's big-hit force (`talk::big_hit`, `BIG_HIT`: NFL 2,120, FBS 1,810, the other college levels 1,680, high school 1,360, in the engine's pound-yards a second), the 97th percentile of plays with a hit on the engine's test rosters (`fire_check hits`, 400 games a level).
- **The spark** (`fire_after`): a big hit, a forced fumble or a fourth-down stop (a 4th down run, pass, sack or scramble with no first down, touchdown, turnover or safety) gives the man who made it +1 on the momentum scale (`edge_hit`) and every teammate on the field 0.4 × min(his fire / his pitch, 1) points of edge (`talk::SPARK`, `Pulse::catches`), within the edge's +2, fading with it (× 0.7 a snap). One spark a play, none on a play a penalty wiped out.
- **Heard footsteps**: the man a big hit lands on loses 3 × clamp((15 - (Toughness20 + Composure20) / 2) / 10, 0, 1) points of Contact (`talk::FLINCH`, within 3), keeping 0.95 of it a snap, about half gone after 14 snaps of the game (`FLINCH_KEEP`); a halftime talk doesn't take it away. It reads wherever Contact does: the short-arm over the middle, the slide, the sideline, the shy tackler.
- **On screen** (`PlayRecord.fire`, `FireNotes`; the play log's words in `crates/afm-game/src/views.rs`, `fire_text`): BIG HIT, his sideline erupting on a spark, a man whose fire habits were up a point or more (`talk::HOT`) on the play his habit goes for ("came in hot" on a big hit or a personal foul, "went for the ball hot" on a forced fumble, "jumped the route hot", "came downhill hot" for a linebacker or safety stopping a run at the line, "jumped the count, too hot" on offside), "heard footsteps" on a ball over the middle he didn't catch with a man within 1.4 yards and 0.5+ points of flinch (`FOOTSTEPS_TAG`), and EJECTED. Nothing shows a hidden number.

## In-game injuries

- Each tackle checks the carrier and each man who tackles him, the sack only the quarterback, a slide only when a late hit is flagged; the hit at the catch point, the hit as a quarterback throws and blocks are never checked (the full list in [injuries.md](injuries.md), "In games"). The chance: injury_scale (1.0 in games) × 0.022 × share × (force / 1700)^1.6 × (0.55 + proneness / 18) × 1.12 on turf; share 1 + 0.4 × lean(Contact) for the carrier, 0.7 for the tackler, 0.8 or 1.3 for a sacked QB (`game/sim.rs`, `injury_check`, `hit_effects`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `sack_check`).
- Kinds by weight: bruise 22, ankle sprain 13, knee 10, shoulder 9, concussion 8, hand 6, hamstring 6, high ankle 5, ribs 5, fracture 3, back 3, ACL 2.2 (`injury_check`).
- Weeks out = lo + (hi - lo) × U² × (1.3 - 0.03 × hidden recovery): bruise 0-1, ankle 0-3, high ankle 2-7, hamstring 1-5, knee 1-6, ACL 36-56, shoulder 0-8, concussion 0.5-3, ribs 0-4, hand 0-5, fracture 4-12, back 0-4 (`injury_weeks`).
- He plays on with clamp(0.15 + 0.035 × Toughness20 - 0.12 × weeks, 0, 0.95), never with a concussion, ACL or fracture; a concussion is caught 92% in the NFL, 80% in college, 60% in high school (`injury_check`; `game/flow.rs`, `apply_injuries`).
- Playing on costs 3 points off every rating (`run_scrimmage_call`).
- Code against the docs: `docs/world-model.md` (training lane 4's picks) says a poorly prepared player reads about a tenth of a second late per word below prepared and a quarterback's reads run up to 30% slower; the code adds 0.1 × (factor - 1) s (0.033 s Unprepared) and × (1 + 0.3 × (factor - 1)) (about 10%) (`run_snap`, `quarterback`).

## The game boards' records and the win chance

- **The line chart**, on your games only: a snap keeps a line chart when its game keeps its setup or is played live (`game/flow.rs`, `run_scrimmage_call`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `Snap::chart`). Each block's rep keeps both men, the leverage when it ended (× 50), its length in tenths of a second, the defender's last move (0 speed, 1 power, 2 finesse), pass, shed, double and pancake flags and both men's spots (`game/chart.rs`, `Rep::of`, called from `game/sim.rs`, `Sim::release`). Each pass rusher keeps his gap 0-4 from the line's spots and whether he was a down lineman, picked up at the snap or in the stunt, with the protection (man, slide left or right, max at 7+ blockers; a back who blocks after a play fake counts, with the man the half-slide gave him picked up) (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `chart_rush`; `game/chart.rs`, `gap_at`). It draws no random numbers, so a game plays the same with it.
- **Timeouts**: every logged snap keeps both sides' timeouts (`game/flow.rs`, `new_record`; `game/stats.rs`, `PlayRecord::timeouts`).
- **Throws on the move**: every pass keeps whether the passer was moving when he let it go (rolling out, off his spot), so growth from results can read his throw-on-the-move rating; it changes nothing in the play (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `throw`; `game/stats.rs`, `PlayRecord::moving`; [development.md](development.md), "Growth from results").
- **Where a throw went**: every pass keeps where across the field it was snapped and where it was aimed, in yards from the left sideline (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `PlayRecord::throw_y`); an aim past a sideline is kept as aimed.
- **The film's throws**: your coming opponents' film keeps each pass's air yards, yards after the catch, where across the field it was snapped and got to (half yards), time to throw, separation, thrown away and dropped (`world/tape.rs`, `Throw::of`).
- **Names in your kept games**: at the year's turn, before the men who left football are dropped from the world, each of your kept games keeps the id, name and position of its men among them, so last season's pages still name them (`world/seat_names.rs`, `keep`, called from `world/mod.rs` beside `storylines::keep_names`).
- **Each level's numbers**: each engine game adds both sides to its level's tally for the season (dropbacks, pressure, sacks, throws by depth, air yards, time to throw, runs, yards before contact, stuffed, 10+), this season and last (`world/charts.rs`, `Tally::add_game`, `Charts::add`).
- **Drives, red zone trips and the clock held** (counts only): after every engine game the play log is cut into drives (each drive ends in a touchdown, a field goal or a miss, a punt, a safety, an interception, a fumble, a return for a score, downs, the half or the game) (`game/drives.rs`, `drives`, called from `game/flow.rs`, `play_game_ctl`, through `GameStats::count_drives`). A drive with a snap at the opponent's 20 or closer is a red zone trip; a trip ending in a touchdown is a red zone TD (`TeamStats::red_zone_trips`, `red_zone_tds`). The game clock between snaps goes to the side with the ball (a kickoff's time to the side that receives it; tries left out; the quarter's last snap gets the rest of the quarter), kept as `TeamStats::clock_possession` beside `time_of_possession`, the ball in play (`clock_possession`). Field goals and punts keep a bad snap (`PlayRecord::bad_snap`). The game day page reads the same drives.
- **The world log**: with a log open, every game at every level is written to disk as it is recorded (the game row, every snap for college, NFL and other-league engine games, situation totals by season), and memory does not grow; it draws nothing, so a world plays the same with it on or off (`world/wlog/games.rs`; the format in `docs/reference/how-it-works/world-log.md`, "Games, snaps and situations"). The college book keeps 19 more of the game line's numbers: field goals by distance band, kickoffs and touchbacks, punt yards, punts inside the 20 and touchbacks, QB hits, hurries, rush wins by speed, power and finesse, and the time to throw (`world/records.rs`, `stats!`, `StatSet::from_line`).
- **The win chance the screens show** is its own model, not the coaches' (`live::win_prob` is unchanged and still what calls read) (`game/win_chance.rs`, `chance`):
  - The drive on the field first: touchdown chance from the spot 0.15 + 0.65 × e^(-yards to go / 28); a field goal try 0.2 + 0.4 / (1 + e^((yards - (range - 8)) / 6)) times the level's make at that distance; both scaled so the drive is worth the down and distance's expected points, read between the table's 5-yard rows (`drive`, `ep_at`).
  - Where the other side starts after a stall: a punt netting 42 (a touchback at the 20) from outside range, the spot of the kick inside it, a mix of the two within 6 yards either side of range (`drive`).
  - A short clock, from the real spot: a touchdown needs one snap per 6 yards; after the first, a snap takes 6 s of play and 16 s more of running clock unless a timeout (or the two-minute warning, easing in over its last 30 s; the NFL and, from 2024, college's two-minute timeout, `Params::for_year`) stops it; the chance kept is 1 - e^(-1.6 × time left / time needed) (`drive`, `in_time`, `warning_stop`).
  - The half's last snap (when a second snap won't fit): a kick from the spot at the level's make there, or a shot at 0.55 × e^(-yards / 14), whichever is worth more (down 3 in the fourth, a tying kick counts half) (`drive`). No engine play throws that shot: out of range the last snap is the usual two-minute call ([play-calling.md](play-calling.md), the end of a half).
  - Late habits blend in with the clock: the leader or a tied side burns it from 7:00 of the fourth to fully at 3:00 (`MILK_FROM`, `MILK_RAMP`); the side behind in the fourth takes the best of three paces (usual, hurry, slow); from 6:00 of the half to 4:00 a side short of time plays for the kick (`KICK_FROM`, `KICK_RAMP`): given no touchdown it kicks once in range, from outside with chance e^(-yards past range / 25), × 0.45 on fourth down (`REACH`, `FOURTH_REACH`) (`chance`, `drive`).
  - The kick's make by distance, falling away as 1 - s² over the last 7 yards to the longest kick, 0 from there (`Params::fg_make`).
  - How long each ending takes: a touchdown drive 1 + yards / 8 snaps, a field goal drive the snaps to range plus two, a stall 4.5 snaps; 27 s a snap usual, 14 s in a hurry (then the short clock's own mean time to score), 36 s less 30 per timeout the other side spends when slow (the side behind in the fourth: the play clock plus 4 s a snap, less the play clock minus 2 per timeout, and a drive as long as one from its probe spot); a side burning the clock runs four downs before it scores (`drive`, `time_to_score`).
  - The rest of the game: the final margin is normal around the lead plus the pregame line × the share of the game left, with a spread of σ × √(time left × (1 + b) / game), b rising from 0 to 1 over the last ten minutes (`rest_sd`, `LATE_SPREAD`); σ 13.5 NFL, 16 FBS, 16.5 FCS and small college, 18 high school. Each ending's time is read at seven lengths, 0.55 to 1.45 of its usual, weighted 1-2-3-4-3-2-1 (`LENGTHS`) (`from_spot`, `Params::for_level`).
  - A spot's split is never read as worth less than one up to 25 yards further back, the clock still from the real spot: the drive's split has small kinks where kicks give way to punts (`chance`).
  - A leader in the fourth quarter whose kneels outlast the clock against the other side's timeouts (and the two-minute warning) has won: 0.999 (`kneel_burn`).
  - Overtime: the NFL's timed period as the end of a game; college and high school possession for possession from the level's spot (`overtime`). The log is followed round by round: a possession ends with its try, its kick or a turnover, or when the other side has the ball, and a side with the ball again starts a round (`Rounds`, `home_series`, `ot_rounds`). An answering side level wins with any score; a try ends the possession, so the other side is read from the spot, and an answering side's try settles the round (XP at the level's try worth, a two at 0.475) (`try_value`).
  - The game day bar: the same model before the next snap, try or kickoff (`home_now`, called from `crates/afm-game/src/lane_gameday.rs`, `live_wp`).
  - The pregame line: stored with each kept game when it is kept (the closing line, the playoff and Super Bowl lines included; 0 at a neutral site without one) (`world/seat.rs`, `seat_line`, `SeatGame::spread`); for an older game the home side's season page's line; else 1.7 points of home field in the NFL and 2.5 elsewhere, none at a neutral site (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_boards/flow.rs`, `Wp::of_game`, `Wp::new`, `home_field`).
  - With no line (high school and the other leagues always): the gap between the sides is a normal prior with sd `gap_sd` (6 NFL, 14 college, 17 high school), updated by the lead beyond the line's share so far against `sigma`; its mean moves the rest of the game's margin and its remaining spread widens it (`Params::without_line`, `from_spot`).

## Speed: what is worked out once

The engine speed lane (batch 4, 2026-11-08) keeps every game the same and does less work for it. On 3,000 synthetic games a level on two seeds, every game's result was the same and the time fell 7% at high school, 25% at FBS and 24% in the NFL (`engine_hash 3000 11`, 4 cores).

- A man's tendency is read once a play: its inputs are fixed while the play runs (`game/sim.rs`, `Sim::tend`, `tend_memo`).
- A block keeps the defender's edge until the blocker, his helper, the rush move or the rusher's arc changes (`game/sim.rs`, `update_blocks`, `Engagement::edge_memo`, never saved).
- Each tick moves every man and copies the field in one pass (`Sim::advance`).
- The ball carrier skips the threat sum for a heading that can't beat the best one so far (an exact bound), and draws his 15 noises first, in the old order (`run_carrier`).
- Fatigue weights come from a fixed table (`FATIGUE`, `Agent::new`).
- The trails and the quarterback's predicted spots keep their buffers between snaps on each thread (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `TRAILS`, `PREDICT_SPOTS`); a read's window from this tick is reused in the checkdown and the throw (`quarterback`).
- The play callers draw from their weights in place; the live pass concepts are one fixed list; the kickoff draft keeps each caller's weights per situation for the game (`game/calling.rs`, `weigh`, `live_concepts`, `CallMemo`).
- Each depth list's ratings, the Lungs recovery and each man's wind are read once a game (`game/flow.rs`, `Side`); each spot's lean is worked out once a player (`spots.rs`, `spot_ratings`).
- Batch 5 (2026-11-12, over main at 6c47622) works on the tick physics, where a high school game spends its time. On the same 3,000 games a level every game's result was again the same on two seeds, and the time fell a further 7% at high school, 5% at FBS and 5% in the NFL (`engine_hash 3000 11`, 4 cores, the machine alone, the middle of four rounds: 20.80 to 19.35 s, 30.10 to 28.45 s and 27.40 to 26.15 s):
  - The scan that keeps bodies apart tests a mover only against men in his one-yard band and the next one each way, along the field and across it; men two bands apart were always skipped by its test (`Sim::advance`, `band`).
  - A man's speed is kept with his velocity, so steering and moving take its root once (`Agent::vel_len`, `vel_memo`, never saved). The wanted speed's root is skipped when it is clearly under his top speed, and Bend's part of a turn is skipped for a man without the link (`Agent::steer`).
  - The vision cone is tested on the cosine with exact series bounds; the arc cosine is taken only within 1e-9 of the cone's edge (`acos_lt`, in `Sim::sees` and `run_carrier`).
  - The ball carrier scores his headings highest cap first, and drops one as soon as part of its threat (the nearest defender's share first) shows it can't win (`run_carrier`).
  - A tackler's reach from his arms, a quick edge's close reach and Length is worked out once a play (`Sim::reach_extra`, `reach_memo`); a man clearly out of reach all tick is skipped before the closest-approach math (`Sim::tackles`). A free rusher more than his own reach and 0.05 yards off the quarterback on either axis, beyond the tick's movement, is skipped the same way (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `sack_check`).
  - A pursuer in front of the runner breaks down without working out the intercept first, and Motor is read only for a man out of the play (`pursue_cap`). The rush move's edge from the pick is kept as the block's edge (`pick_move`, `engage`).
  - Distance tests take the root only near the line they test (`game/geom.rs`, `dist_sq`, `sq_lt`, `sq_ge`; `Nearest` in `game/scrimmage.rs`): escorts, pass and run blocks, the settle and climb scans, sacks, blitz threats, the option pitch, the scramble drill, `hurry_run` and `slide`.
  - Routes reuse their distance to the next point (`route_step`, `toward`); the quarterback's window skips a defender who can't close in time (`window`); the trails are one flat buffer (`Trails`); a deep zone's two verticals are picked without a list (`cover_inner`); kick returns turn the lineup in place (`game/kicking.rs`, `run_return`).
- The proof: `engine_hash [games] [seed] [hs|fbs|nfl]` plays the games `match_check` plays and prints one fingerprint for each part of a game's result (stats, injuries, film, talks and the rest), then the time. Two builds that give every game the same result print the same lines but the time (`crates/afm-tools/src/bin/engine_hash.rs`; the prints are `world/wlog/fp.rs`, `field_prints`). A world proof on a new seed follows, as for the world speed lane (see [world-log.md](world-log.md)).
