## Quick answers

- Units? Height, arm, hand, wingspan in inches; weight in pounds (`body.rs`, `Body`).
- Is the stored body his body now? No. `Player.body` is the adult body he grows into; `body_now` turns it into his body at his age (`body.rs`, `body_now`). The engine, the combine and the player page all read `body_now` (`game/roster.rs`, `GamePlayer::from_player`; `body.rs`, `Drills::of`; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_core/pages.rs`, `measurables`).
- When does he reach full height? On the real growth curve (A15, the CDC charts' median boy): 7.6% short at 14, 2.2% at 16, 0.8% at 17 (about half an inch), 0.4% at 18, 0.2% at 19 and grown at 20, straight between years, with a late bloomer's curve 0.6 × bloom years later (`body.rs`, `HEIGHT_LEFT`, `height_left`, `body_now`).
- Does his body grow during the year? Yes (A16). His age for the body is `age + grow`, where `grow` is the years since his last birthday that the yearly step has not counted yet: the daily clock sets it from his birth date each day (0-2), and the yearly step takes one off when it adds a year (`world/clock.rs`, `grow_bodies`; `development.rs`, `develop_season`; `player.rs`, `Player.grow`). Before it the body read whole years, so a mid-season senior (17.7 on average) was built as 17.0. Players with no birth date (tests, the standalone class sim) keep 0.
- How much weight a year? By position group (A12): backs, receivers, defensive backs, kickers 80% at 14, 90% at 17, 94% as a freshman (18), full at 21; quarterbacks, tight ends, linebackers, edge rushers, snappers 89%, 93%, full at 21; tackles, guards, centers and defensive tackles 76% at 14, 87% at 17, 96% as a true freshman (18, A17: real freshman linemen 303 lb against seniors' 311), 97.5% at 19, 98.8% at 20, full at 22. Each player has his own offset from his group's share (A13) that fades to nothing by full (`body.rs`, `fill_share`, `FILL_SKILL`, `FILL_MID`, `FILL_LINE`, `fill_offset`).
- His own offset? sd 2 points of grown weight (3.2 for linemen), six tenths of it from his bloom (late bloomers light), and one player in seven drawn 2.5 times wider; fixed for life on the stream `id ^ 0xF111_0FF5`. So a Metcalf-type receiver arrives at 98% and a late-growing tackle at 78% who adds about 60 lb in college (`fill_offset`).
- How is the forty computed? 5.56 - 0.0124 × (0.75 × speed + 0.25 × acceleration) + 0.0033 × (max(weight, 175) - 220), then a stable track offset sd 0.04 and run noise sd 0.03, rounded to 0.01 (`body.rs`, `Drills::means`, `forty_weight`, `combine`, `tested`). Below 175 lb being lighter stops making him faster (S13); the engine's forty and the links read the same `forty_weight` (`game/roster.rs`, `forty`; `links.rs`, `forty`).
- Is speed tied to football talent? No (S1). Speed and acceleration are his own roll: his explosiveness on top of what boys built like him run (`athlete.rs`, `create`, `speed_base`, `SPEED_MIX`). At a spot whose overall counts speed, the roll leans toward his talent by just enough that among FBS players speed says nothing about skills (`speed_lean`): quarterbacks 0.25, tackles 0.32, defensive tackles 0.56, tight ends and linebackers 0.76, safeties 0.83, edge rushers 0.87, receivers and corners 0.89, backs 0.91. His other skills make up the rest of his talent, so a slow boy's skills sit higher and a raw burner's lower.
- Does a linebacker's or safety's body pull his skills? Yes, a little (safeties D, linebackers L3; Cain 2026-11-07): the generator moves 2 rating points a 10 lb over the combine's weight from Zone Coverage, Man Coverage and Pursuit to Tackling, Strength and Physicality at safety, and at linebacker 3 a 10 lb plus 1.5 a speed-roll sd under the mean to Tackling, Block Shedding and Strength, keeping his overall at his talent (`athlete.rs`, `body_lean`, `LEAN_S`, `LEAN_LB`, `LEAN_LB_SLOW`). A linebacker keeps half his frame's distance from the combine's weight (`lb_weight`, `LB_WEIGHT_SPREAD`; bug 310: the spread fell from 20 lb to 9, the heavy group from 259 to 250 lb, real 253), and synthetic NFL linebackers draw 0.6 sd lighter (`adult_body`). Sizes proposed, not confirmed.
- Does an edge's height pull his skills? It did, and bug 330 (the edge lane, 2026-11-08) moves it back: at the same overall the generator's 6-5½ edges had about 1.2 points an inch more Zone Coverage, Play Recognition, Pursuit and Anticipation and less Hand Use, Finesse Rush and Power Rush than 6-2 ones, so tall and long-armed edges rushed worse as recruited (-0.46 pts of pressures a snap an inch, real +0.02). A natural edge now moves 1.4 points an inch over the combine's height from those four to Hand Use (1.0), Finesse Rush (0.85) and Power Rush (0.55), his overall kept (`athlete.rs`, `edge_lean`, `EDGE_TALL_LEAN`; a short edge moves the other way). As recruited, height then costs -0.16 pts of pressures a snap an inch and arms -0.06 (main -0.46 and -0.26, real raw flat). The spread is untouched, so rare bodies with rare skills still come out. Proposed, not confirmed.
- How fast does he play? One number per player, from burst (acceleration) and top speed by position (S2b): interior linemen 70/30, edge and linebackers 60/40, backs, tight ends and quarterbacks 50/50, receivers, corners and safeties 40/60, none for kickers, punters and snappers (`body.rs`, `play_speed`). Stars, offers and depth charts read it from lane 4 on.
- Is the speed rating "speed for his size"? In effect, yes. The rating itself is never adjusted for size, but the stopwatch takes weight off on top: 0.0033 s per pound over 220 in the forty, and the same weight terms in the engine's top speed, burst and cuts (`body.rs`, `Drills::means`; `game/roster.rs`, `forty`, `sprint_tau`, `accel`, `cut`). So two 80-speed players run different forties at different weights, and one speed point is worth 0.0093 s, so 20 lb costs about 7 speed points of forty. A player who adds 20 lb while his speed rating grows 7 points runs the same forty (what the 2026-10-24 measurement found by class). Size also lowers speed at creation: boys built like linemen start from a lineman's mix of what their bodies run (`athlete.rs`, `speed_base`).
- Does the match engine use the same forty? Yes, the same formula without noise, then top speed = 40 / max(forty - 0.92 × sprint_tau, 3.4) yd/s (`body.rs`, `top_speed`; `game/roster.rs`, `forty`). He runs it 0.06 s quick in the engine, whatever his start.
- Is the 10-yard split the one he plays with? Yes, since the progression build's step 4b part 2 (physical study item 2, Cain 2026-11-15: "make sure you look at 10-y splits to, especially with big man"). A test day's split is his engine run at that day's forty, 0.1 s steps as `Agent::steer` runs him, plus 0.02 s of laser timing (`body.rs`, `ten_split`, `split_of`, `SPLIT_DAY_SD`). Before it, the test split was 0.375 × forty − 0.15 and the field one moved only with the ratings: men at the same forty and weight split 0.01 s apart (real 0.04).
- Is strength one rating? No, two since the progression build's step 3 (physical study item 4b, Cain 2026-11-15): Strength is his upper body and Lower Body his legs and hips (`attributes.rs`, `Attr::LowerBody`). They move together mostly but men differ: at creation the legs are 0.75 times the upper body's draw plus his own (`athlete.rs`, `lower_with`, `LOWER_TIE`; on its own keyed stream, so no other draw moves), and each grows on the same profile. A save from before carries legs as strong as his upper body (`attributes.rs`, the 67-value load). The overall splits each position's old strength weight between them (`positions.rs`, `lower_share`: backs, guards and centers and defensive tackles 0.6 legs; tackles and linebackers 0.55; receivers and corners 0.4; the rest 0.5). What the engine reads where is on [match-engine.md](match-engine.md), "Upper and lower body".
- How long are his arms? His spot's real drafted mean at its real height (MockDraftable 2005-2025: tackles 34.21 in, edges 33.38, corners 31.46), plus 0.20 in an inch of height for linemen (tackles, guards, defensive tackles, edges) and 0.37 for everyone else, plus his own part, sized so the spread is real's (`body.rs`, `LIMBS`, `arm_per_inch`, `limbs`). Hands and wingspan the same way: hands 0.08 in an inch of height, wingspan 2.03 in an inch of arm (`HAND_PER_INCH`, `WING_PER_ARM`). Before step 4a (physical study items 11-13) arms were 0.6-1.2 in short everywhere and followed height at 0.45 in an inch, and hands ran too even.
- What reads arm length? The bench (-0.8 rep per inch over 32.5) (`body.rs`, `Drills::means`); also the engine and links (`game/roster.rs`, `GamePlayer.arm`).
- What reads height in fit? Position fit: height costs from half a spread off the position's combine average, weight from 25 lb plus a spread (`athlete.rs`, `misfit`; A18 moved height from a whole spread).
- Does every player get the position's weight pull, or only movers? Every player: the team placement clears the history and calls `set_position` for each one (`athlete.rs`, the placement loop).
- Does lifting add weight? Yes (A4, A14). Each year from 15 to 25 every player's weight room pulls his adult weight toward 60% of the way from his frame to his position's weight (the combine average, less what lower talent takes off; `athlete::placed_weight`), a third of the remaining gap a year in college and the pros and an eighth in high school, × clamp(0.7 + 0.03 × work ethic, 0.7, 1.3) × (1 + 0.15 × strength staff support). It only adds and never goes past the position's weight, so a naturally big receiver keeps his size. A body project's pounds sit past it. Until step 4a (2026-11-16) it took every frame all the way to the position's weight, so the light half of each position was gone by the draft (drafted receivers 7.6 lb heavy, corners 3.5). Kickers, punters and snappers skip it (`development.rs`, "The weight room"; `athlete.rs`, `position_weight`, `placed_weight`). Your own players' body plans move them on top of it (`training/seat.rs`, `body_change`). The bad-weight event still adds 8-25 lb.
- What is the bad-weight event? Ages 18-32, 2.5% a year × a laxness factor: +8-25 lb, speed, acceleration and agility -1 to -3 now and ceilings -0.5 to -2 (`development.rs`, `develop_season`, `WeightGain`).
- Do high schoolers show arms and hands? No. The page shows arm, hand and wingspan only from college on, combine drills only for declared and pro players who were invited or drafted (`pages.rs`, `measurables`).
- Lefties? 10% throw left; 60% of lefties and 12% of righties kick left (`body.rs`, `LEFT_HANDED`, `RIGHTY_LEFT_FOOTED`).
- Where does the Body and testing tab get each number? The first known of: your staff's measuring and testing days (own players), the combine (declared and pro players who were invited, or a pro day for a drafted man who was not), the latest camp, showcase, pro day or combine your scouts saw, then the school's or team's listing (self-reported, height and weight only) (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_squadboards/body.rs`, `known`).
- What is a "combine moment"? The invited player whose combine forty beats 4.25 + 0.0042 × (weight - 180)₊ by the most makes one news item, if he beats it by more than 0.12 s (`world/scouting/predraft.rs`, `combine_moments`).

## The body record

`Body` fields: `height`, `weight` (adult playing weight, moved by position and plans), `arm`, `hand`, `wingspan`, `frame_weight` (the natural weight before any position program; never changed after creation), `left_handed`, `left_footed` (`body.rs`, `Body`).

Position sizes come from the real combine (nflverse 2000-2025, `data/real-world/combine_by_position.csv`, read into `SIZES`): e.g. QB 74.66 in / 221.6 lb, RB 70.72 / 217.5, WR 72.75 / 201.2, OT 77.69 / 315.6, IOL 75.81 / 310.6, DT 74.99 / 306.8, CB 71.35 / 192.7 (`body.rs`, `combine_size`). The CSV has no LS row, so LS falls back to the default 73 in (sd 2) / 230 lb (sd 20) (`body.rs`, `SIZES`).

## How a body is created

Normal creation is athlete first (`player.rs`, `generate_prospect` calls `athlete::create`):

1. Frame, as if he were a draft prospect (`athlete.rs`, `draw_frame`): h = normal(0,1).
   - 29%: lineman build, height 76.4 + 1.5h, weight 311 + 4h + normal(0, 15).
   - 71%: lean build, height 73.5 + 2.1h, weight 220 + 26 × (0.55h + 0.83 × normal(0,1)).
2. Talent shrinks it (`athlete.rs`, `talent_size`): size = clamp((talent - 72)/12, -2.5, 0.5); inches added = 0.7 × size × 1.6; pounds added = (0.3 + 1.3 × size) × 14. Talent 45: -2.5 in, -37 lb. Talent 72: +0 in, +4 lb. Talent 78+: +0.56 in, +13 lb.
3. His spot's size fit, once his spot is chosen (`athlete.rs`, `fitted_size`, `SIZE_FIT`; items 14 and 15): inches added and an extra height spread (backs, receivers, linebackers, corners and safeties only, on a stream keyed by his frame), pounds added, and the weight spread's scale about the combine's mean. For tackles, guards and defensive tackles the scale is only the light side's: the heavy side is spread 1.6 times (item 10, `HEAVY_SIDE`), since real linemen's heavy tail runs long (0.41% of FBS and FCS listings at 350+, drafted tackles' heaviest 1% 365 lb). It is applied after the spot is chosen, so who becomes a lineman stays as drawn. One stretch can't give real's narrow middle and long tail together, because the placement's weight program takes a heavy boy 60% of the way back to his position's weight (at most 25 lb): at 1.6 drafted tackles' heaviest 1% is 346 lb (real 365); 2.0 reaches 353 but drafts tackles at 16% of the class sim's picks (real 8%). The spots that share a frame squeeze each other's sizes without it (receivers' heights sd 1.51, real 2.26). Fitted on `body_sizes` so drafted men's heights and weights by spot land on the real combine's (proposed, not confirmed).
4. Height within 58-88 in, weight within 120-480 lb (`HEIGHT_RANGE`, `WEIGHT_RANGE`; item 9: no creation limits inside what people are).
5. Arms, hands, wingspan by his spot's real ones (`body.rs`, `Body::grown`, `limbs`; see the quick answer above).
6. A coach then places him; `set_position` moves his weight toward the position (see below).

Fixed-position creation (tests and tools, `ProspectOptions.position` set) uses `adult_body` instead, on its own stream `id ^ 0x5EEDB0D1` (`body.rs`, `adult_body`): size as above from potential; h_z = 0.7 × size + normal(0,1); w_z = 0.3 + 1.3 × size + 0.45 × strength_z - 0.3 × speed_z + 0.25 × h_z + normal(0, 0.8), where strength_z and speed_z are his ceilings' gaps from the position's scaled elite shape / 4 (`player.rs`, `generate_prospect`). Height = mean + sd × h_z, weight = mean + sd × w_z (in `HEIGHT_RANGE` and `WEIGHT_RANGE`). Arms, hands and wingspan come from `limbs`, as above.

## Weight by position

- On placement or a position move: weight = frame + clamp(0.6 × (target - frame), -25, +30), target = position combine mean + talent pounds (`athlete.rs`, `set_position`, `placed_weight`, `position_weight`). A receiver moved to tight end eats; a tight end moved to tackle eats more. Both ways, unlike the yearly weight room.
- K and P weigh what real ones do (A19): combine weight mean + combine sd × (frame - 220)/26, kept within 2.5 sd, so a big frame still makes a big kicker (drafted kickers 196 lb and punters 218 on 8 seeds; real NFL 194 and 213, combine 197 and 214). LS is clamped to 220-265 lb (`athlete.rs`, `set_position`, `LS_MIN_WEIGHT`, `LS_MAX_WEIGHT`); older saves are clamped on load (`world/seat.rs`, `World::after_load`).
- Listed sizes on scouting screens run tall and heavy: round(height + 0.25 + u) and round(weight + 3 + 6u), u uniform 0-1 per player (`world/scouting/file.rs`, `listed`; shown by `crates/afm-game/src/lane_scouting.rs`). The player page's measurables are true `body_now` values (`pages.rs`, `measurables`).

## How a body grows by age

`body_now(p)` (`body.rs`):
- age = `age + grow` (A16); left = `height_left(age - 0.6 × bloom)`; height = adult height × (1 - left); grown = 1 - left / left at 13.
- weight = adult weight × `fill_share(p, age)`: his position group's curve at his age (straight between whole years; 4 points less a year before 14) plus his own offset × fade, fade = clamp((full age - age)/(full age - 18), 0, 1), clamped 0.6-1.0. See Quick answers for the curves.
- arm and wingspan scale by height / adult height; hand × (0.9 + 0.1 × grown).
- Examples with bloom 0 for a 6'2" man: at 14, 5.6 in short; at 17, 0.6 in short and a receiver at 90% of his weight, a tackle at 87%; at 18, 0.3 in short and a tackle at 96%; full height at 20; receivers full at 21, tackles at 22.
- Bloom is normal(0, 1.1), clamped -2.5 to 3 (`player.rs`, `generate_prospect`). A late bloomer (+2) reaches full height at about 20 and arrives in college about 2 points lighter (3.5 for a lineman).
- What it does not read: level, coaching or nutrition (the weight room moves his adult weight instead).

## Physical growth in the yearly step

Physical attributes grow like the rest (`development.rs`, `GrowthCtx::rate`, `develop_season`), with these physical-only parts:
- High school: physical growth at full rate (×1.0) except strength ×0.6 (`hs_group_growth`, `HS_STRENGTH_GROWTH`). Technical ×0.65, mental ×0.7.
- College: the ×1.3 teaching boost (`COLLEGE_TEACHING`) skips physical attributes.
- Redshirt year: physical growth ×1.35.
- Strength staff and his professionalism: physical growth × (1 + 0.08 × clamp(`ctx.physical` − 0.06 × `strength_dev`, −1, 1)), so his own head's distance from the level's typical head comes out and only the typical head and his professionalism count here (`develop_season`, `physical_mult`).
- Strength staff's mix (minor bug 302): in college and the NFL, physical attributes read `physical_coaching` (the strength head in the position coach's share, around the level's typical head; see [development.md](development.md), "What the world feeds in") in place of coaching, in growth (`physical_boost` in place of `coaching_boost`, `GrowthCtx::rate`) and in the ceiling drive (`physical_extra`). High school and a year away keep coaching.
- Growth by development age (age - bloom): ×0.75 before the window, ×1.0 inside it, ×0.55^(years past) after (`development.rs`, `growth_age_factor`). Windows: speed 14-20, acceleration and agility 14-21, strength 15-24, stamina 14-24 (`attributes.rs`, `ATTRIBUTE_PROFILES`).
- Growth spurt: age ≤ 17, 9% a year: physical ceilings +2-6 (strength ×1.3, others ×0.8) (`develop_season`, `GrowthSpurt`).
- Body stalls: ages 18-20, chance 2.5% × e^(-0.7 × bloom) a year (early bloomers more): each physical ceiling drops to at most current + 0-2 and never rises again (`STALL_CHANCE`, `BodyStalled`).
- Major injury: each injury-sensitive attribute (speed, acceleration, agility, balance, flexibility, jumping) loses 1.5-5 × age hit now (age hit 1.0 under 24, 1.2 at 24-27, 1.6 at 28+); 55% heal fully (back next year), 30% keep a lasting 1-3 off the ceiling, 15% lose 3-7 for good (`LostBurst`) (`develop_season`, `injury_age_hit`).
- Speed with the body (S4): before 21 (and not after a stall), speed and acceleration are lifted to at least (0.66 + 0.30 × grown) of their ceilings at each checkpoint, grown = his height growth at the end of the year; the year's growth adds the rest. So a senior has about 95-96% of his top speed by senior spring and a late bloomer less (`development.rs`, `speed_with_body`).
- Decline: onset = decline age + longevity + the position aging offset clamped to ±1.5 for physical attributes, so a back's speed declines from 25.5, not 24.5 (F15). Speed 27, acceleration and agility 27, strength 31 (`development.rs`, `decline_onset`, `declines`). Physical loss × wear multiplier 1 + 0.06 × (wear - 3)₊ × clamp(1 - 0.15 × longevity, 0.5, 1.5); a major injury adds 1.5 wear, and every season played adds wear (F14; see [development.md](development.md)).
- Bad weight (18-32): chance 2.5% × lax, lax = clamp(1 + 0.12 × (10 - work ethic) + 0.06 × (10 - discipline/5), 0.3, 2.5). Effects in Quick answers (`develop_season`, `WeightGain`).

## The weight room and body plans (your own players)

- Practice families: Strength (strength and balance: the weight room), Speed (speed, acceleration, agility, flexibility, jumping), Conditioning (stamina) and others (`training/mod.rs`, `Family`). How much practice drives each family's growth: `PRACTICE_DRIVEN` [0.55, 0.3, 0.7, 0.5, 0.6, 0.5, 0.35]; a plan moves a family's year by at most 15% (`training/work.rs`, `PRACTICE_DRIVEN`, `PLAN_REACH`, `share`). Details belong to the training page.
- Body plan, each practice week: step toward the target weight of up to 1.5 lb (gain) or 1.2 lb (loss) × clamp(0.7 + 0.03 × work ethic, 0.7, 1.3) × fuel; fuel = clamp(1 + 0.15 × (nutrition minutes − an average staff's) / (theirs + 30), 0.9, 1.15) from the week's sessions with the dietitian (`training/seat.rs`, `week`).
- Each pound changes (`training/seat.rs`, `body_change`): adult weight by lbs × adult/now (within `WEIGHT_RANGE`), and attributes and ceilings by strength +0.05, anchor +0.04, speed -0.03, acceleration -0.02, agility -0.03, stamina -0.02. Ten pounds: +0.5 strength, -0.3 speed.
- Your strength staff's testing days use the combine fit: `Drills::expected` for his ratings and body now plus a test day's noise (reps sd 1.5, forty sd 0.03, shuttle sd 0.04 on top of his drill knack (S14) with the thin fast end, vertical sd 0.8 on top of his jump knack; no printed limits), the 10-yard split of that run (`split_of` at the day's forty, with his first-step gift, plus sd 0.02), and lifts from a max read at 220 lb and scaled by (weight / 220)^0.67: the bench max from his upper body with his lifting knack, the squat (1.45 times) and the clean (0.88 times) from the same read of his lower body without the knack (`training/seat.rs`, `test_row`). Details on [training.md](training.md).
- The normal weight room by body knack (everyday item 6, the progression build's step 3): every college and pro year from 18 to 22 his body knack moves his physical ceilings from the typical man's path, response r = 0.8 × his body knack z, each rating by clamp(r, −1, most / leash) × leash × the year's share. Upper-body strength takes a skewed response with no most (step 4c, physical study item 3: real training response is right-skewed, and a few big responders make the 40-rep benchers): r = 0.8 z + 0.3 × (max(z, 0)² − 0.5) (`response`, `RESPONSE_SKEW`); lower body keeps the plain one inside its leash (a skewed squat ran verticals to 56 inches). Strength and Lower Body each leash 7 and most 14, Speed 1, Acceleration and Jumping 1.5 (in the first two years only), Agility and Flexibility 2, Balance 3; shares 0.30, 0.25, 0.20, 0.15, 0.10 at 18-22 (burst 0.55 and 0.45 at 18-19). A ceiling never goes under his rating today, so a non-responder's gains just stop. High school and a year away from the team skip it (`progression.rs`, `weight_room`, `WEIGHT_ROOM`, `response`, `weight_room_share`). The response, the shares and the burst years are proposed, not confirmed; a body project adds on top: see [progression.md](progression.md), "Body projects" (`body_year`: the strength or speed leash by his body knack and frame, and the pounds the strength built carries).

## Combine drills

The expected result from ratings and his body now (`body.rs`, `Drills::of`, `Drills::means`), with w = weight now - 220:

- Forty = 5.56 - 0.0124 × (0.75 speed + 0.25 acceleration) + 0.0033 w.
- Vertical = 20.9 + 0.182 × spring - 0.035 w + his position's offset, spring = 0.8 × max(jumping, 0.95 × acceleration) + 0.2 × lower body (`JUMP_LEGS`; Cain 2026-11-15: the legs in the jumps).
- Broad = 87.6 + 0.448 × spring - 0.089 w + his position's offset (inches). The offsets (`VERT_OFFSET` -2.8 to +0.3 in, `BROAD_OFFSET` -8.7 to -1.1 in; physical study item 6) put each drafted position's averages on the real combine's: before them the broad ran 9 in long at quarterback, 5-6 at backs and 3-4 at receivers, corners and safeties.
- His jump knack (physical study item 5): one spring of his own for life on `id ^ 0x1A3B7` for both jumps (the broad's 0.7 with the vertical's, `JUMP_TOGETHER`), drawn from a plain normal (step 4c: the t of 5 degrees it had ran drafted verticals to 52 inches over 24 drafts and 44+ to 1.4 a draft, real best 46.5 in 25 years and 0.35 a draft; the normal gives 47.5 and 0.50), at each position's sd (`VERT_KNACK` 1.5-2.6 in, `BROAD_KNACK` 1.5-4.4 in), the part of real's spread the ratings and the day leave. Test days only, as the agility knacks (`jump_knack`, `jump_knack_sd`). All proposed, not confirmed.
- Bench (225 lb reps) = 8.05 + 0.137 × strength + 0.073 w - 0.8 × (arm - 32.5) + (0.35 × his weight / 249 − 0.137) × (strength − the drafted men's mean at his position) + his position's offset + his lifting knack (physical study items 3 and 4, minor bug 385; `BENCH_SLOPE` 0.35, `BENCH_WEIGHT`, `BENCH_CENTER`, `BENCH_OFFSET`, `lift_knack`). Within a position a strength point is worth reps by his weight (step 4c): reps at 225 follow the pounds on the bar (Epley), and a strength point is a share of his own body's pull, so a 305 lb tackle gets about 0.43 a point and a 200 lb receiver 0.28. With one slope for all (0.40) big men's bench spread 4.1-4.3 reps (real 4.8-5.5) and the skill spots' 4.9-5.7 (real 4.0-4.2). The means (QB 69.4 to DT 86.2) and offsets (−1.9 to +2.4 reps) are sixteen classes' drafted men (step 4c), so each drafted position benches the real combine's mean; the lifting knack is normal(0, 1.5) reps, his own for life on `id ^ 0x11F7B3C5`. Kickers, punters and snappers keep the old read. Strength here is his upper body. Measured in step 4c (`drill_tails 250000 24`, 6,168 drafted men): drafted bench spread 3.9-5.2 reps by position (real 4.0-5.5), each position's mean within 0.9 of real (linebackers 21.4 against 22.6); 35+ reps 4.7 a draft (real 3.5), 40+ 0.67 (real 0.77), best 51 (real best 51). Before step 4c: 35+ 0.8 a draft, 40+ none, with the 99 wall on strength. Proposed, not confirmed.
- 3-cone = 7.58 - 0.007 × bend + 0.0062 w + his position's offset, bend = 0.45 agility + 0.2 acceleration + 0.35 flexibility (E1, the edge lane 2026-11-08: 0.55, 0.25 and 0.2 before, so the 3-cone reads bend as scouts use it; each position's bend middle moved to keep its average 3-cone).
- Shuttle = 4.59 - 0.0045 × quick + 0.0039 w + his position's offset, quick = 0.7 agility + 0.3 acceleration. The offsets (`CONE_OFFSET` -0.06 to +0.14 s, `SHUTTLE_OFFSET` -0.07 to +0.12 s) put each drafted position's averages on the real combine's.
- The fast end thins out (physical study item 7, `thin_fast`): real 3-cone and shuttle tails are thinner than a bell, so past 0.9 sd under his position's real mean (`CONE_REAL`, `SHUTTLE_REAL`) a further stretch counts for less, never past 1.9 sd (`THIN_KNEE`, `THIN_ROOM`). It reads his number with his knack, before the day's noise, and also `Drills::expected` (`thin_drills`), the projections and your testing days' shuttle. Before it 3-cones of 6.50 or faster came 4.1 a draft (real 0.35 of the men who ran it) and the best stacked on the printed 6.40.
- Spread by position (S14): the drills spread as wide as the real combine at each position while the averages stay. Half of the missing spread comes from the ratings: bend and quick are stretched around the average drafted player at his position, bend' = mid + k × (bend - mid), k 3.2-4.1 for the 3-cone and 3.1-3.9 for the shuttle (edge 4.09 and 3.88 around bend 80.1 and quick 81.7), so one agility point is worth about 0.010-0.013 s of 3-cone (edge 0.013; 0.004 before the stretch). The rest is his knack for the drills, fixed for life on the stream `id ^ 0xC04E5`: 3-cone sd 0.15-0.25 s, shuttle 0.11-0.16 by position (an edge's two knacks go together 0.7, so drafted edges' 3-cone and shuttle go together 0.60, real 0.62; `EDGE_KNACK_TOGETHER`, E1; independent elsewhere), added on test days only (the combine, pro days, camps and your testing days' shuttle), not to `Drills::expected` (`body.rs`, `AGILE_SPREAD`, `Drills::bend`, `Drills::quick`, `drill_knack`). Kickers, punters and snappers: stretch 1, no knack. All from the ratings (stretch 5.2-7.2) would have made an average high school senior a second slower than before.

What they do not read: balance, stamina, any skill attribute, height (only through weight and arm).

Worked forties (no noise): speed and acceleration 90 at 200 lb = 4.38; 95 at 190 lb = 4.28; 60 at 310 lb = 5.11; 75 at 245 lb = 4.71.

Test day (`body.rs`, `combine`): forty + track offset (normal(0, 0.04), fixed per player from `id ^ 0x407AC4`, so some always run faster than they play) + normal(0, 0.03); vertical sd 1.5; broad sd 3.0; bench sd 2.0; 3-cone + knack + sd 0.08; shuttle + knack + sd 0.06. Vertical and broad add his jump knack first, the 3-cone and shuttle thin after their knack. Then `tested` rounds: the forty, 3-cone and shuttle to 0.01, bench whole reps, vertical to the half inch, broad whole inches. No printed limits (physical study item 8): only bounds no man reaches (forty 3.5-9, bench 0-99, vertical 0-70, broad 0-200, 3-cone 5-14, shuttle 3-9), so a broken number can't print; before, 4.20, 50 reps, 46 in, 145 in, 6.40 and 3.80 held real men's records off the page (Byron Jones's 147-inch broad). Your testing days drop theirs the same way (`training/seat.rs`, `test_row`).

- `Drills::expected` is the same without any noise: what a rating range means on the hover card (format: "About a 4.48-4.55 forty at 205 lb"). It plugs the range's ends into one rating and your read's middle into the others (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_ratings.rs`, `combine_line`).
- Which day: the player page uses one fixed day seeded `id ^ 0x00C0FFEE` (`pages.rs`, `measurables`). Scouting files seed per kind and year; a pro day forty runs 0.05 s quicker, a camp laser forty 0.04 s slower (`world/scouting/file.rs`, `tests`).

## Speed and size in the match engine

`GamePlayer` takes height, weight and arm from `body_now` (`game/roster.rs`, `from_player`).
- `forty()`: the combine formula, no noise or clamp.
- `top_speed()` = 40 / max(forty - 0.92 × sprint_tau, 3.4) yd/s; a 4.35 is about 10.7 yd/s (`body::top_speed`, `SPRINT_TAU_K`; 1.0 with a 0.06 s offset before step 4b part 2, which made a slow starter run his forty 0.03 s quicker than a quick one).
- `sprint_tau()` = clamp(0.84 + 0.0015 × (60 - acceleration) + 0.0004 × (weight - 220) + his first-step gift, 0.5, 1.3) s, held under (forty - 3.4) / 0.92 so top speed stays under 11.8 yd/s (`body::sprint_tau`). The gift is his own for life on `id ^ 0x57A27`, normal sd 0.08 held to 2.5 sd (`start_knack`, `START_KNACK`): about 0.036 s of split. Acceleration's pull halved from 0.003 a point, because the shared draw of speed and acceleration can't spread splits at the same forty; Cain's option A on the split card (2026-11-16).
- Measured (`split_check 250000 4`, 1,013 drafted men): every weight band's split within 0.01 s of real (1.55 under 200 lb to 1.81 over 315), the test split 0.343-0.345 of the forty (real 0.344-0.346), 0.037-0.042 s apart at the same forty (real 0.04), and the split moving 0.29-0.34 s a second of forty (real big men 0.28).
- The edge's rush step reads the typical edge's burst or his own, whichever is quicker, both without the gift (`scrimmage.rs`, `rush_feet`; real edges' split says nothing about their pressures, minor bug 286).
- `accel()` = 4.65 + 0.008 × (acc - 60) at 60+, 4.65 + 0.0046 × (acc - 60) below, minus 0.006 × (weight - 220), clamped 2.2-7.5 yd/s².
- `cut()` = 0.99 + 0.004 × (agility - 60) at 60+, 0.99 + 0.0022 × (agility - 60) below, minus 0.002 × (weight - 220), clamped 0.4-1.7.
So weight costs a big man straight-line speed, burst and cutting, with the same speed rating. The sack escape, a QB run's moves and a returner's danger read weight too (A3; `game/sim.rs`, `size_cost`; see [match-engine.md](match-engine.md)); the first tackler's miss check doesn't since minor bug 279 (2026-11-07: real broken tackles don't move with weight once talent is known), so a heavy carrier pays in burst, braking and cut instead.

## Recorded times (update 3, lane 4)

A high schooler's forty, 10-yard split, 100 m and 200 m are times on record, run at track meets, camps, showcases and his school's own testing (`world/times.rs`; when and where in [recruiting.md](recruiting.md), "Recorded times").
- Each run starts from his true Speed, Acceleration and real weight: run(a) = 5.56 - 0.0124 × (a × Speed + (1 - a) × Acceleration) + the forty's weight term; forty = run(0.75), split = 0.348 × run(0.25), 100 m = 2.5 × run(0.9) - 0.3, 200 m = 5 × run(0.9) - 0.35 (`base`). Day-to-day spread: forty 0.05 s, split 0.03, 100 m 0.08, 200 m 0.15 (`spread`). The split adds his first-step gift in seconds (`body::knack_split` at his forty), and a reader counts a split as 0.047 s wide (the day and the gift, which a staff can't tell from burst; `read_spread`).
- Hand timing reads 0.24 s fast (sd 0.10); a school's own listed forty another 0.05-0.15 (`HAND_FAST`, `HAND_SD`, `SCHOOL_FAST`). A meet or school reads the time against his listed weight, a camp against his measured one.
- A reader turns the times into a speed read by weighted least squares from his film read (sd 6): a hand time counts 0.4, a school forty 0.1. A young kid's read is corrected by his age's share of grown speed (`seen`, `typical_share`).

## Athletic traits at creation (athlete first)

`athlete.rs`, `draw_shape` builds an "elite shape" (what each attribute would be if he became elite) before any position:
- Physical shape: a blend of the 11 field positions' elite shapes, each weighted by body likeness × the position's HS share, likeness = exp(-0.5 × (dh² + dw²)) in combine sds (`body_likeness`). A 310-lb frame gets a lineman's speed shape (55-66), a 190-lb frame something near the corner's and receiver's mid 90s.
- Then his own explosiveness e = normal(0,1): speed, acceleration, jumping +4e + normal(0,3); agility +2.5e + normal(0, 3.5); balance +1.5e + normal(0,4); flexibility +1e + normal(0,5); strength and stamina + normal(0,5).
- Skill leanings: 39 independent gifts (arm, placement, hands, cover, leg, IQ, compete...) mixed per attribute, mapped by `leaning(z)` = 38 + 59 × Φ(1.2 × (z - 0.3)) (about 40-97). A kicking leg is shifted -0.4 and helped by a kicker's or punter's body; long snapping is shifted -0.6.
- Natural position: argmax over K, P and the field positions of overall(shape) × (1 - misfit) × `FIT_SCALE` (`best_fit`).
- Speed and acceleration ceilings (S1, S2, S11, S12; `create`): roll = lean × z(talent) + √(1 - lean²) × e, lean from `speed_lean` (see Quick answers). mix = the body-likeness blend of `SPEED_MIX` (what boys built like each position run before anyone picks them, set so drafted players run the real combine's forty), lead = the blend of each position's elite acceleration minus speed. Speed = mix - 0.25 × lead + 13 × roll; acceleration = mix + 0.75 × lead + 13 × roll. Natural kickers and punters use the kicker's (46.3) or punter's (50.8) mix with no lead and no lean, so they run about 4.91 like real ones.
- Other ceilings: scale = talent / fit. Attributes his position uses: talent + (shape - fit) × spread, spread = min(0.5 × scale, (94 - talent)/(top - fit)), then all of them move by the same amount so his overall at his natural spot is his talent; others shape × scale; all + normal(0, 4), clamped 1-99. Then skills only another position uses are trimmed until no other position rates over 95% of his natural one; the body and elusiveness are never trimmed (A2: elusiveness is rated for his size, so a Sewell keeps his moves).
- Talent = clamp(45 + 6.5z + 2.75 × (z - 1.6)₊², 20, 99) (`player.rs`, `talent_from_z`).
- Ratings at his first age: maturity = clamp(maturity_at_14 - 0.035 × bloom + 0.03 × (age - 14) + normal(0, 0.05), 0.2, 0.98); raw = ceiling × maturity; above 40 it is halved: 40 + (raw - 40) × 0.5; capped at the ceiling (`player.rs`, `generate_prospect`).

## The Body and testing tab (update 2, lane 9A)

What the game knows of a player's body, against three groups (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_squadboards/body.rs`):
- Sources, first known wins: your staff's measurements (height and weight; arms, hands and wingspan from college on) and the team's latest testing day, yours or the staff's before you (forty, split, vertical, shuttle, bench reps, and the lifts; `training::testing::seat_rows`); the combine (or a pro day for a drafted man it did not invite), seeded `id ^ 0x00C0FFEE` as on the player page; the latest camp, showcase, pro day or combine your scouts saw (`world/scouting/file.rs`, `tests`); the school's listing (`file.rs`, `listed`) or the team's. Only the listing is self-reported; the rest is marked verified (`known`).
- His level: his position at his level, from every player there through `Drills::expected` (no test-day noise): high schoolers in his state and graduating class, the nation's class when the state has under 40; college by FBS, FCS or smaller; NFL players on a roster (`level_pool`).
- Drafted: every drafted player at his position the world remembers in full (alumni with a record, drafted pros), each with his combine (`drafted_pool`).
- The real combine: the position's mean and spread from `data/real-world/combine_by_position.csv` (2000-2025), read as a normal curve (`REAL`, `phi`). Arms, hands, wingspan and the split have no real column.
- Percentiles: the share of the group his number beats (faster is better on the clock), ties counted half (`pct_in`).
- Athletic score on 0-10: the mean percentile over size (height, weight), speed (forty), burst (vertical, broad), agility (3-cone, shuttle) and bench, divided by 10, with at least three numbers known; "better than" scores each of the group the same way (`view`).
- Built like him: the five drafted players nearest in height, weight, forty, vertical and 3-cone, each in the drafted pool's spreads, with at least three shared; match = 100 - 18 × distance (`view`).
- Weight over time: his weight in each season's kept read (the ratings page, "Kept reads"), each of the team's testing days for him (the previous staff's too) and today.
- Testing history: every testing day the team has for him, newest first, with whose day it was (your baseline, your staff, the previous staff) and his 40, split, shuttle, vertical, bench (reps for a pro), squat, clean and weight; "rehab" for a day your trainers had him out (`view`, `tests`; the testing books are on [training.md](training.md), "Testing days"). Games missed hurt and seasons with an injury count only seasons played out (`view`, `record_season`).
- What it does not read: true ratings. The level cloud is drawn from each player's expected drills, the norms of his level, with no names.

## Projected test numbers (Cain 2026-11-14, items 1-6 and 8)

What he'd measure and run at a coming testing day, on the Body and testing tab's second switch and in the rating hover card (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_squadboards/projected.rs`; the tab's `targets` in `body.rs`, `projections`). Display only: nothing reads it in a world step, so worlds play the same per seed.
- Days (`targets`): Senior year for a high schooler (his last high school testing day, April 15 of his class year, while it is ahead); At the combine for high school and college (the first combine he's eligible for: three years after high school, and never one whose declaration deadline, January 15, has passed; a declared man's is this spring's, today once it has been run); Next year for a pro on a roster (a year from today). Years ahead are counted to the day.
- The read: the one that judges him (`reads::judging`: yours from your first own look, your staff's before), and its six physical ratings (speed, acceleration, agility, jumping, flexibility, strength; your own read of each where you have one, your staff's otherwise). Without all six there is no projection, and the tab says so.
- Ratings: each coming year every one of the six moves by drift + share × (ceiling read − the road's middle that season) (`road::mids`, the same middle the Shape tab draws), times the part of that year before the day (a year's growth lands through it). Drift and share per rating by age and level come from `world/scouting/physical.rs`, `physical_fit`: the world keeps no season record of single ratings, so it copies up to 15,000 of today's players at each level (high school, college, the NFL), lands their year, runs one more year of the world's own development step on the copies (`develop_season`, a neutral staff at coaching 10 and his last role), and fits each rating's change on his true gap (ceiling less overall) by least squares, share held to 0-1, 30 copies at least. A pro's age is read as his body's (age less his position's ageing offset, held to ±1.5), so a back of 27 is fitted with men whose bodies are as old. Nothing in the world changes; only the fit's totals and the copies' leftovers leave the module. A combine target keeps every year at the college fit.
- Weight: his known weight (a school's listing less 6 lb, the listing's average extra in `file::listed`; a team listing is his real weight) read as a share of his grown weight on his group's fill curve, the weight room's pull toward his position's weight for the ceiling read's talent each year from 15 to 25 (`development.rs`, `weight_room_room`, `weight_room_pace`, the same step the world runs, at an average work ethic), then the fill curve at his age on the day (`body.rs`, `fill_with`). Your own player with a body plan reaches its target at 1.5 lb a week up or 1.2 down, every week, and each pound against the weight he'd have reached without it moves speed −0.03, acceleration −0.02, agility −0.03 and strength +0.05 (`training/seat.rs`, `body_change`). Height on the median boy's curve (`height_share`, a school listing less 0.75 in); arms and wingspan grow with it. Unmeasured arms are read at the drafted men's arm-to-height ratio at his position.
- The drills: `Drills::raw` on the projected ratings, weight and arms (the combine's own formulas, before rounding), then rounded as a test day writes them. A declared man the combine didn't invite runs a pro day forty, 0.05 quicker.
- Anchoring on what he ran: for each drill with a real test (the combine or his pro day, a day your scouts saw, your testing day, a laser forty), the gap between the test (less the day's own shift: a scouts' pro day −0.05, a camp +0.04) and what his read stands for on that day (his read walked back along the fit by the years since, at the weight he'd have had) is carried forward, times persistent / (persistent + the day's noise² + years since × a year's growth²). Persistent is the read's doubt in that drill's units, plus his lasting knack where the day reads it (the track for the forty at the combine and scouts' days, sd 0.04; the jump knack, the vertical's on a testing day; the 3-cone and shuttle knacks, the shuttle's on a testing day). Hand times and listings never anchor.
- The band: the 25th and 75th of 400 draws around the middle. Each draw takes a ceiling from the ceiling read's sd, whole years of growth from the copies (all six ratings together, so a speed spurt comes with acceleration's), his hidden fill offset and bloom (`fill_offset_of`, `fill_own_draw`), and adds the day: the read's doubt and knack left after the anchor, and the combine's own test-day noise (`DAY_SD`).
- Percentiles and the athletic score recompute on the projected numbers against: this season's seniors at his position for Senior year (his state's with 40, the nation's otherwise), the last three drafts' picks at his position with their combine numbers (the men the combine tested lately) for At the combine, his level now for Next year; the drafted and the real combine as always (`target_pool`, `pct_with`, `scores_of`).
- On the tab: "Testing day: Now · Senior year · At the combine" (pros: Now · Next year). A projected row reads "4.47 projected (4.43-4.52) · now 4.58" in the dim style; the plot keeps his dot and adds a hollow ring where he's projected with a thin line between; his weight line runs dashed from today to the day. A note says whose read and which day.
- The hover card (`lane_ratings.rs`, `combine_ahead`): for speed, acceleration, agility, strength and jumping, "At the combine" shows the projected drills at the projected weight, on whose read; what he'd run today at today's weight follows as "Today". A pro keeps today's line.
- Confirmed (Cain, 2026-11-14): the senior testing day (April 15), the listing discount (its mean), coaching 10 for the fit's copies, 400 draws, 52 body-plan weeks a year, average work ethic for the weight room.
