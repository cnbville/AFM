## Quick answers

- How is a day played? As steps in clock order: 23 fixed hours plus every game's final whistle (the other pro leagues' too), each draft pick's minute, each practice's end and cutdown's deadline (`world/clock.rs`, `play_through`, `next_step`, `timed_steps`, `hour::FIXED`).
- When does a world day start and end? At 5:00 AM Eastern; it runs to 4:59 AM the next morning. A "day minute" counts minutes after 5:00 AM Eastern, 0 to 1439 (`calendar/zone.rs`, `DAY`, `et`, `moment`, `world_day`).
- Where does Continue stop? At your next section's first minute (5 AM, 8 AM, 1 PM, 4 PM, 7 PM, 10 PM on your local clock), or at a live moment inside a section (`world/sections.rs`, `next_stop`; `world/clock.rs`, `continue_until_needed`).
- What is a live moment? Your kickoff and final whistle (the Super Bowl's too), each draft night's start, the negotiating window, the new league year, the trade deadline, an hour before cutdown's deadline, signing day's 7 AM local and your meetings at their hours (`world/sections.rs`, `live_moments`, `seat_game_window`).
- Does a decision at a stop reach the hours after it? Yes. Standing at a stop at minute m means every step up to m has run and none after it, so a claim made at the 1 PM stop is in when the 4 PM claims run (`world/clock.rs`, `play_through`; test `a_stop_comes_before_the_hours_after_it`).
- Does stopping change the world? No. Stopping at every section with no choices plays the same steps as a straight day (test `sections_play_the_same_world`).
- What time zone is my seat in? Your team's town's: by state, with split states by town and longitude; Arizona keeps standard time all year; daylight saving by the US rules of each era (`calendar/zone.rs`, `Zone::of_place`, `daylight`; `world/sections.rs`, `team_zone`, `seat_zone`).
- Are the world's times Eastern? Yes. Every moment is stored Eastern; screens turn it into your local time (`world/sections.rs`, `local`, `clock_of`, `when_text`).
- When does a result show? At its final whistle: college kickoff plus 3 hours 15, NFL plus 3:05, high school 7 PM in the state's main zone plus 2:30 (your own high school game too), the other pro leagues 7 PM at the home club plus 3:05 (`world/clock.rs`, `hour::COLLEGE_GAME`, `NFL_GAME`, `HS_GAME`; `world/college.rs`, `final_whistle`; `world/nfl_year.rs`, `nfl_final`; `world/hs_year.rs`, `state_final`; `world/pro/other.rs`, `week_minute`, `games_at`).
- What does a line with no hour get? The moment of the step that posts it, or "all day" when it is posted for another day; the old 9:00 AM default is gone (`calendar/events.rs`, `Event::new`, `Calendar::post`).
- What hides a line until its hour? Screens show a line once its moment has come, and a line with no hour from the start of its day, the day's first stop included (so a line about your own move made there, or as your career starts, is on your desk at once); a scheduled one (a date, a window, a deadline, your day's slots and practice) shows ahead (`world/sections.rs`, `has_come`; `crates/afm-game/src/views.rs`, `shown`, `on_screen`). Lines posted after Eastern midnight are filed under the next date but belong to the world day before: the inbox, the news and the calendar's day panels read them there, and the year closes with them (`calendar/date.rs`, `Moment::world_date`; `calendar/events.rs`, `on_day`, `close_year`; `world/clock.rs`, `rollover`).
- How does holiday mode work? It passes the sections without stopping and comes back at the first section of the day you picked, or at the section after a huge moment (`world/clock.rs`, `holiday_to`).
- Where does a Jan 1 world stand? On the morning of January 1 of its start year, nothing of that day played, in its last history season's football year: the bowls, the playoff, the NFL's last weeks and signing day still to come, the January portal the next day (`World::jan1`, `history_left`, `play_history`; `WorldOptions::stop_jan1`). A world played on from Feb 9 is the same a year on: its played season becomes its history's last (`World::play_on_to`; `crates/afm-game/src/jobs.rs`, `play_on_file`).
- Which dates move a roster, and where does the Calendar say so? The day panel carries a "Roster" sentence on each (playtest bug 8): the sentences are built in `crates/afm-game/src/lane_calendar/notes.rs` (`all`) from the football year's `YearDates` and `RuleBook`, so a rule-book number or a moved date changes the sentence. Dates that had none get a line of their own (`Hit::own`: Class years move up, Draft withdrawals, Early enrollees arrive, Summer enrollment, New freshman class, Players age a year, Transfer day, Undrafted free agents sign, Every release goes on waivers). The college class label moves on January 2 (`calendar/anchors.rs`, `eligibility_day`; `world/clock.rs`, `office`, then `world/college.rs`, `settle_eligibility`), while the roster screen's year dots and seasons left read `w.year`, which turns the day after the Super Bowl (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_squadboards/roster.rs`, `years`). A high school grade moves on `hs_school_start` (`views.rs`, `class_text`). A cut leaves your roster at once at college (`world/front_office/college.rs`, `cut`) and at the NFL (`world/pro/mod.rs`, `release_with`); the NFL's AI clubs and your cutdown plan cut at the cutdown minute (`cutdown`); a high school has no cut.
- What does the top bar say? Your local date and section, "Fri 30 Oct, 7 PM" (`world/sections.rs`, `clock_text`; `crates/afm-game/src/views.rs`, `status`).
- What does a stop show? A card under the top bar: what came since the last stop, what you're doing now and what's due before the next stop (`crates/afm-game/src/views.rs`, `stopped`; `app/ui/screens/daystop.js`). Now and Due are two tiles side by side, with the next date under Due (`daystop.js`, `dsCard`).

## The steps of a day

`hour` in `world/clock.rs` names the fixed hours (Eastern): 5:00 dawn (the day's bookkeeping, all-day lines), 6:00 paper, 7:00 plan (Monday's training week), 8:00 early (Black Monday), 10:00 office, 11:00 wire, noon, 1:00 PM afternoon, 2:00 polls, 3:00 later, 4:00 deadline (claims, cutdown, tags, the league year), 5:00 market, 6:00 awards, 6:30 practice (bowl practices), 7:30 CFP rankings, 8:00 evening (recruiting, schedule release), 8:45 Heisman, 9:00 show, 10:30 night (portal decisions), 11:30 late night, 1:00 AM overnight (the college season's end, arrests, court cases), 4:00 AM late (stories, legacy, scouting, plans) and 4:30 AM heal.

`run_step` runs one step: the games whose final whistle is now first (`college::play_at`, `nfl_year::games_at`, `hs_year::games_at`, `pro::other::games_at`), then the draft picks and the practices due now, then the hour's own work, then the NFL's (`nfl_year::step`, `pro::step`) and the people's (`folk::step`). News written in the step gets the step's moment (`NewsItem.at`). What each college game means (the record chases, the stakes) is worked out once at dawn, before any kickoff (`world/college.rs`, `day_stakes`). Cutdown runs at its anchor's minute: 4 PM Eastern, 6 PM in 2026 (`pro/mod.rs`, `step`).

`advance_day` is the rest of today's steps, then the next morning (`end_day`); an open war room lets the staff make that night's picks (`scouting::live::finish`), as the tools and tests need. `run_to(d, m)` plays whole days to `d`, then `d` through minute `m`. While your war room holds the clock, no step runs (`play_through`).

`World.played` is how far today has run: 0 before the first step, m + 1 when standing at minute m.

## The weekly batches, by day

- NFL practice injuries: each club practices on its own days (camp: every day but a preseason game's and the day after; season: the three days before a game that aren't the day after one) and its injuries come when its practice ends, a sixth of the week's risk a camp day and a third a season day (`training/days.rs`, `nfl_practice`, `practice_at`; `pro/games.rs`, `practice_injuries`).
- College and high school practice injuries: rolled with Monday's week on a day the week practices (not a game day), they wait for that practice's end; heat illness at a high school practice waits the same way. One landing on a player already out keeps the longer layoff (`training/seat.rs`, `practice_injuries`, `heat_week`, `field_days`, `injury_day`, `SeatTraining.pending`; `training/week.rs`, `monday`, `TeamWeek.field_days`, `others_pending`; `training/days.rs`, `practice_at`). The week log counts only the injuries that have happened, and a staff note about a later day waits for it (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_training.rs`).
- The NFL's Tuesday: holdouts, heal week and the in-season trouble countdown stay weekly; arrests are daily overnight, rulings daily in office hours (`pro/mod.rs`, `step`; `people.rs`, `pro_arrests_day`, `pro_rulings_day`).
- Court cases open overnight every day, not on Wednesday (`folk/courts.rs`, `opens`).

## Practice hours

A team's practice starts at its staff's hour on its own clock: NFL 11:15 AM in season and 9:15 AM in camp, 2 hours 15 on the field; college 3:45 PM, or 8:45 AM for one program in five by custom, 2:45; high school 3:30 PM, or 7:30 AM before August 15, 3 hours (`training/days.rs`, `staff_start`, `practice_length`, `morning_program`, `summer`). At your seat you set the start yourself inside your level's rules (high school after a 3 PM bell in the school year, college 6 AM to a 9 PM finish, the NFL 8 AM to 3 PM) on the training week (`training/days.rs`, `practice_start`, `start_limits`; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_training.rs`, `practice_hour`, `set_practice_hour`). A change once today's practice is under way, or that would put it under way already, starts the next day (`SeatTraining.today_at`). On Sunday the training week plans the coming week, since Monday's plan runs at 7 AM Eastern, before a western seat's first Monday stop (`lane_training.rs`, `week_monday`).

## The draft's three days

Round 1 is the first night, rounds 2 and 3 the second, 4 to 7 the last day (`world/recruiting.rs`, `night_of`). Each night opens at its minute; each pick is made at its own minute and joins its club then, so no pick is on a roster, a page or the calendar before its minute (`draft_night`, `draft_due`, `commit_picks`, `pick_stamps`; the draft's steps in `draft_steps`). A trade made in the war room for a later pick is booked once, on that pick's night. The draft between nights is kept on the world (`World.draft_run`). The undrafted rush follows the last pick (`draft_end`). Your war room makes a night's picks with you and then waits for the next night while the clock runs on; you are never on the clock for the next night's pick (`world/scouting/live.rs`, `holds`, `on_clock`, `close_night`, `open_night`, `LiveDraft.waiting`). The public mocks and boards stay up until the last day, so the draft scores them (`world/scouting/public.rs`, `draft_year`).

## Your game day

Your kickoff and your final whistle are live moments; the sections between them pass while you coach (`world/sections.rs`, `next_stop`, `seat_game_length`). The sections before kickoff are game morning: the pregame talk is open, but the game kicks off only once its kickoff has come, from Continue or the game day screen (`world/sections.rs`, `kickoff_come`; `world/live_seat.rs`, `start_live`; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_gameday.rs`, `now`, `hub`; `app/ui/screens/gameday.js`). Once the day has booked your game, today has no game left to play (`world/sections.rs`, `seat_kept_on`; `SeatGame.whistle` keeps its final whistle). The stop card opens the game kept since the last stop (`crates/afm-game/src/views.rs`, `stopped`).

## Meetings and answers

A meeting keeps the hour its place line names ("the team room, 7:30 p.m."); otherwise the boss meets at 10 AM, parents and boosters at 7 PM, recruits by phone at 8 PM, a player or staff talk 15 minutes after practice, the weekly press at the program's own day (Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday) and hour (11 AM, noon or 1 PM), the postgame podium 15 minutes after the final whistle (`world/folk/scene.rs`, `add`, `hour_in`, `default_hour`; `world/folk/press.rs`, `press_day`, `press_hour`). A meeting dated today is handed to your staff only once its hour has passed (`world/folk/scene.rs`, `day`). A player's first answer to your NIL money comes at your next section (`world/front_office/college.rs`, `remember`, `Reaction.due`). Mail is written through the stop and shows once its stamp, on your local clock, has come; the welcome letter is stamped the moment your career starts (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_core/mail.rs`, `after_days`, `welcome`; `lane_core/desk.rs`, `shown`, `unread`). A day's letter written before the day was over takes the lines posted later that day and shows as new again. The recruiting desk's digest comes the Monday after its week, at 8 AM (`mail.rs`, `DIGEST_SPAN`). The stop card lists the letters filed at that stop (`views.rs`, `stopped`, `desk_ids`).

The winter rush (a job taken between rivalry week and national signing day, the Jan 1 start) gives every welcome meeting its own hour on your local clock in the first two days, from 7 AM (that day if 7 AM is still ahead, else the next): at a college the reaction 7:00, the athletic director 8:00, the holdover coordinators from 9:00 an hour each and then the position coaches, the press 14:00, the state of the team 15:30, the team meeting 19:30 (18:00 the next day if the team plays that day); day two the trainer 7:00, who might leave 8:00, the captains 9:30, the commits 11:00, a booster 12:30, the academic report 14:00, the beat reporters 15:00, the tour 16:00, the plan 17:00, boosters at 19:00 and the signees' calls from 20:30, four an evening, half an hour apart. The NFL's and the high school's are in `docs/game.md`, "Lane A" (`world/folk/takeover.rs`, `rush_schedule`, `signee_calls`).

## Speed

The world day costs the same in sections as in one go: the steps split one day's work, and none runs twice (`play_through`). What a stop adds is the game's own work at each stop: mail is written at every stop, and its linker looks up every name in the stop's lines among all people and coaches. It keeps a sorted index of each list's name hashes between stops, built again only when the list grows or shrinks or someone is renamed (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_core/links.rs`, `people_named`, `coaches_named`, `forget_names`; `world/mod.rs`, `World::rename`, `names_changed`). Measured on seed 1 on a 4-core thread machine, 13 to 16 quiet days in February 2026, medians: a quiet day's six stops take 0.89 s at a college seat (main's one day 0.76 s), 0.42 s at an NFL seat (0.52 s) and 0.55 s at a high school seat (0.56 s). The same day played whole takes 0.63, 0.43 and 0.44 s (`crates/afm-game/src/bin/seat_season.rs`, the quiet-day line; `AFM_SEAT_CMD=day` plays whole days).
