## Quick answers

- Is there a job interview? Yes, for head jobs: a talk with the panel (principal and AD, AD and president, owner and GM) before the search decides, worth +1.35 to -0.9 in the hiring score, plus a little for Aura; missing it is -1.0. Your coordinators also argue a change at the staff meeting the morning after each game, and overruling one whose unit struggles again costs his trust (`world/folk/dialogue.rs`, `interview_answer`, `interview_bonus`, `case_from`, `check_case`). See [people.md](people.md), "Talks on game day and off it".
- What scale are coach ratings on? 1-20 for every ability part, personality trait and reputation (`world/coach.rs`, `Ability`, `CoachPersonality`, `Coach`).
- Which ratings are hidden? All of `Ability` (teaching per group, scheme, game management, motivation, discipline, recruiting, network, relationships), the learning ceiling, sportsmanship, home state, decline age and rate, systems knowledge. The world shows reputation (`world/coach.rs`, `Coach` field notes).
- What does the game show of a coach? Reputation in words ("one of the best in the game" ≥ 17 ... "unproven" < 5) (`world/folk/mod.rs`, `rep_words`). Every coach's page has your read as ranges on the hidden 1-20 and his potential in words: a stranger is the whole scale, narrowing with your film, talk, interviews and honest words (`world/folk/coach_look.rs`; lane 18 below). Your own staff: ranges that narrow with seasons worked (`world/staff_read.rs`).
- How good are coaches by level? Core ~ N(mean, sd): HS 8.0/2.6, small college 9.5/2.3, FCS 10.5/2.2, FBS 12.0/2.3, NFL 13.5/2.0; each part = core + its own noise (`level_ability`, `generate_coach`).
- How does a coach affect development? Through one coaching number 1-20 per player: college = 0.2 HC + 0.25 coordinator's unit (his side's mean teaching and scheme) + 0.4 position coach (shared half and half with the coordinator in the group he came up in) + 0.15 × facilities term (`world/season.rs`, the stage match in the season step; C57, C60). Growth multiplier 0.75 + 0.025 × coaching, scaled by coachability (`development.rs`, `develop_season`).
- Does a head coach's overall matter on game day? Game management adds (GM − 10) × 0.12 to team strength in the quick results; on the engine the play callers' skill comes from scheme and game management (`world/season.rs`, `college_results`, `coach_caller`).
- Who calls plays? The coordinator, or the head coach with none; you if you choose to call a side (`world/season.rs`, `play_caller`).
- What does recruiting read from coaches? Head coach recruiting and relationships, position coach recruiting, every staffer's ties to high schools, the HS coach's network for exposure, and each coach's eye for boards (`world/recruiting.rs`, `run_signing_stage`; `Coach::evaluation`).
- What's a coach's "eye"? `evaluation` = 10 + 0.3 × (recruiting − 10) + 0.3 × (mean teaching − 10) + 3 × a fixed personal normal, 1-20; your own coach has his own number (`world/coach.rs`, `evaluation`).
- How does reputation move? Each season 0.3 × (head_coach_value + 12 × vs-expectation − rep), half speed for assistants, minus 0.6 × locker-room trouble; titles +3 (conference/state) or +5 (national) × headroom × how far the season beat the roster (`title_above`) (`season_reputation`, `title_reputation`). A graduate assistant, analyst or quality-control coach moves with his staff's season at an assistant's pace too (`junior_reputation`, from `world/college.rs`, `record_regular_season`, and `world/nfl_year.rs`, `black_monday`; minor bug 252).
- How are coaches hired? Ranked by `candidate_score` (reputation with recency bias + noisy reads of the parts the job needs), then each says yes or no by `weigh_offer`; the search calls up to 20 names (`world/staff.rs`, `fill_opening`). A college position job first goes to the program's own off-field man in that room when the head coach rates him ready (`ready_at_home`), and a head coach who moves brings men from his old staff (`bring_his_staff`). A step up the ladder counts by `promotion_worth` / 0.75 (ambition and age, as in your offers). Your own hires: lane 18 below.
- When are coaches fired? College: the AD's review after the regular season (`college_review`); in-season firings at every level on Sundays late Sep - mid Nov, head coaches, coordinators and position coaches (`in_season_firings`, `staff/midseason.rs`); NFL on Black Monday (`nfl_firing`) and in season on the Mondays after weeks 4-15, head coaches and coordinators (`nfl_in_season`); high school in late January (`hs_review`) and, rarely, in the fall (`hs_in_season`). See "Firings during the season" below.
- Is there a hiring bias? Yes, a hidden one against coaches of color in AI searches, biggest for head jobs, offensive coordinators and the quarterback and line rooms (`world/ethnicity.rs`, `hiring_bias`; applied in `fill_opening`). Your own hires never carry it.
- A coach's ethnicity? A former player keeps his own (so staffs lean toward the college players' mix); a high school job draws the group first from its state's pool and takes a former of that group. A made-up coach draws from his pool (`world/ethnicity.rs`, `coach_ethnicity`, `CoachPool`; all proposed, not confirmed): entering college and NFL coaches 27% of color in 1990 rising to 50% by 2015 (of them 82% Black, 8% Hispanic, 6% Polynesian, 4% Asian); a high school coach from his state's people at his birth year (`hs_coach_ethnicity`, see "New coaches"); the college head coaches in place in 1990 3% Black, their assistants 17% Black; the NFL head coaches of the 1996 start 90% white, 10% Black (3 of 30 then). Who gets hired then carries the hiring bias. Measured in two released worlds before minor bugs 161 and 242-244 were fixed: `afm/race-check/released-worlds.md` in the project folder.
- What's a coaching style? Five 1-20 dials (risk, freedom, physical, ball hunting, mould) from an AI coach's personality and ability, or set by you (`world/coach.rs`, `style`).
- Did a coach play? Every coach carries what he played (`Coach::played`: position, college or NFL grade, college, last season), shown in words on his page; 92.7% of FBS coaches played college football (`world/former.rs`, `college_share`, `made_up`; C2).
- What does playing do for a coach? His own position starts high in his teaching: an ex-pro 16-19 by how good a pro he was (journeyman to star), a former college player 13-15 (`Played::floor`, `apply`; C1, C2).
- Where do new coaches come from? From the world's own former players who went into coaching, his old coaches hiring him first; a coach is made up only when nobody fits (`former::draw`, `to_coach`; `world/staff.rs`, `new_coach_for`; C11).
- Name or standing? Standing (`reputation`) is what the trade thinks; name (`renown`) is what fans, boosters, recruits and small schools know. A college head search reads 0.1-0.6 name (more at a smaller job), a high school one half, assistants and the NFL none (`name_value`, `hiring_buzz_for`; `world/staff.rs`, `name_weight`; C10).
- Do coaches get worse with age? Yes. Learning stops at 58; decline starts at a personal age ~N(59, 3.5) and speeds up 15% a year (`age_one_year`).

## The coach

- `Ability` (`world/coach.rs`): `teaching[8]` per group (QB, RB, receivers, OL, DL, LB, DB, specialists), `scheme`, `game_management`, `motivation`, `discipline`, `recruiting`, `network`, `relationships`.
- Generation (`generate_coach`): half come up on offense; their side's teaching +1, the other −1. `came_up` is his best non-special group on his side. Network = core − 1 + N(0, 3). Ex-pros (HS 4%, NFL 35%, else 12%): network +3 to +6, teaching −0 to −1.5, ambition +2, reputation +1. A playing past then sets his own group's floor (below, "Former players and new coaches").
- Personality: ambition, loyalty, temper, each N(10, 3.5) (`CoachPersonality`).
- Learning ceiling = core + N(2, 2) − 0.05 per year over 30 (`learning_ceiling`). Your own coach's is 20 (`world/staff.rs`, `take_seat_as`).
- Reputation starts at head_coach_value + N(0, 2) (+1 ex-pro) (`generate_coach`).
- `head_coach_value` = 0.3 mean teaching + 0.2 scheme + 0.15 game management + 0.15 motivation + 0.1 discipline + 0.1 relationships (recruiting and network not in it) (`Ability::head_coach_value`).
- Sportsmanship (how soon starters come out of a decided game): 10 + 3.5 × keyed normal (`world/staff.rs`, `new_coach`).
- Your coach adds four ratings only you have as numbers: play calling, adaptation, evaluation, negotiating (`Yours`). An AI coach's calling comes from scheme and game management; his GM negotiates.

- **A retired coach in memory** (memory diet lane M2, item 9): a retired coach keeps his record, history, mentor, renown, fame, ratings (`ability`) and personality, which his page, the Hall, the trees, bonds and voice lines read. His working part (`coach::Work`: learning ceiling, recent seasons, prove-it, ties, units, sportsmanship, decline, contract, systems, stat buzz, style, freedom and your own ratings) is dropped at the year's cleanup and when a world opens (`Coach::retire_work`, `World::compact`); one who still holds an off-field post (`junior_now`) keeps it while he does. Code reads it as his own fields through `Deref`; a dropped part reads blank, and `AFM_TRIPWIRE=1` names any read or write of one. Files keep their shape (`world/coach_form.rs`: a flat file form in and a view out, in the old field order; a retired coach without his part writes it blank). The coaches store's print moves; results do not (Cain's yes, 2026-11-19).

## Ageing and learning

- Each year (`age_one_year`, run in `world/staff.rs`, `age_coaches` after the high school carousel): every part grows by pace × learn × min(room to ceiling / 6, 1) + N(0, 0.15), learn = 0.45 under 35, 0.2 to 47, 0.05 to 57, 0 after.
- Pace by job (`Coach::pace`; C59, C61): a position coach 1 in his group and 1/3 in the rest; a coordinator 1.5 in the group he came up in on his side, 1 in the rest of his side, 1/3 on the other side; a head coach 2/3 everywhere; out of work 1/3. A group he didn't come up in is × (0.6 + 0.8 × (adaptability − 1) / 19). Scheme: 1 for head coaches, coordinators, analysts and quality control, else 0.5; game management 1 for head coaches and coordinators, else 0.5; recruiting 1 at a college, else 1/3; the rest 1. A graduate assistant, analyst or quality-control coach grows like a position coach in his group.
- Adaptability (C38): hidden, 1-20, 10 + 3.5 × a normal keyed by his name and id (`Coach::adaptability`).
- Decline past `decline_from` (~N(59, 3.5), 52-68) at rate 0.08-0.35 × (1 + 0.15 × years past): recruiting ×1.2, motivation 1.0, game management 0.8, relationships 0.6, discipline 0.5, teaching 0.4. From 58 scheme loses 0.1-0.5 a year. Network never declines.
- Under a better head coach an assistant picks up 6% of each gap in teaching, scheme and network a year, and 15% of the gap in each system's knowledge (`age_one_year`; `world/systems.rs`, `learn_from`). Network +0.08 each year in a job.
- Visible teaching records fade ×0.85 a year; high school ties ×0.9 (dropped under 0.02) (`age_coaches`). Out-of-work coaches over 66 retire.

## Roles and what each one does

- Roles: head coach, offensive coordinator, defensive coordinator, a position coach per group (`Role`, `Group::coach_title`). College staffs keep all eleven as people; NFL teams keep HC, OC, DC; high schools keep the head coach and, at AI schools, an offensive and a defensive coordinator (`world/staff.rs`, `college_roles`, `nfl_roles`, `hs_staff_year`; C37).
- Junior posts are real coaches (C36; `Coach::junior`, `Junior`, `Post`; `world/staff.rs`, `junior_slots`, `new_junior`, `junior_year`): graduate assistants (FBS and FCS 4, else 2; $25K, $15K below the FCS; two years, then an analyst post at home half the time if one is open), analysts (FBS 2 + budget / $25M up to 12, else 1; $75K / $35K) and NFL quality-control coaches (2; $100K). A junior is drawn a tier below the level, aged 22-26 (GA), 24-34 (analyst) or 24-31 (QC), with a learning ceiling 1.5 ± 1.5 higher and reputation 4 lower; one in six analysts or QC coaches moves on a year; a young man two years without a job and no coaching behind him leaves coaching. A junior has no `job`; his post is `junior`, and his head coach is his mentor. His standing moves with his staff's season at an assistant's pace, and the season counts in his buzz (`Coach::junior_reputation`; before minor bug 252 it stayed at his first day's).
- Analysts from the street (`analysts_from_the_street`, in the junior year after the carousel; minor bug 252): coaches let go from an FBS or NFL staff since the last season, under 62, each take an analyst post 60% of the time; the FBS programs fill their open analyst posts best program first, each with the best-regarded of them, in the room he coached (Steve Sarkisian, Butch Jones and Mike Locksley were Alabama analysts between jobs; proposed, not confirmed). He keeps his standing.
- High school coordinators (C37; `new_hs_coordinator`, `hs_staff_year`): a new head coach keeps each half the time; one in twelve steps away a year; an empty post goes to an out-of-work high school or small-college coach from the state on that side 60% of the time, else a new man. Your own school's staff stays your seat's people (the old coordinators leave when you take the job, `take_seat_as`). An AI school's head coach calls the side he came up on and his coordinator the other (`world/hs_year.rs`). A coordinator's reputation moves with the season at an assistant's pace.
- Jobs the world still doesn't keep (NFL position coaches, an AI high school's position coaches) are drawn people with names, ages and pay, never stored (`world/folk/staffing.rs`, `drawn_staff`, `draw_person`); the staff screens list the real juniors and coordinators beside them with links.
- Support heads (strength, medical, analytics, academic, player engagement) are people on a market (realism audit item 10, 2026-11-15): each has a name, a hidden 1-20 rating, a birth year, his pay and the year he came, and a strength head is his head coach's man (`world/people.rs`, `Staffer`, `Support`, `staff_support`, `support_bosses`, `fill`). Engagement cuts player trouble by up to 12%.
  - **Pay**: a post offers its level's going rate (2024 dollars; strength: Power 4 $400K, Group of 5 $150K, FCS $80K, Division II/NAIA/JUCO $45K, Division III $40K, NFL $350K; medical, analytics, academic and engagement lower; `Dept::going_rate`) × (the team's money ÷ its level's median)^0.8 × the coaching pay index (`offer`). The money is a college's football support staff line for the head coach's hires (strength, analytics, engagement) and its whole athletic budget for the department's (the head trainer, academics); an NFL club's spending level ÷ 0.55. Pay keeps up with the index each year. All sizes proposed, not confirmed.
  - **The yearly market** (NFL camp, `pro_mood`): a head retires from 60 (6% + 4% a year past 60, all by 72), leaves football one year in 25, or is pushed out (one in four a year) when he sits 4 or more under what the post would draw fresh. Then every open post is filled, best-paying first: a fresh man the budget attracts (an assistant stepping up, ~N(5.5 + 7 × budget + 2 × era − 1, 2.8), `fresh_mean`, `FRESH_SHIFT` 1), the best man out of work who takes at least 70% of his old pay (yes 85% of the time), and the best heads elsewhere this pay moves by 25% or more (`POACH_RAISE`; up to three asked, yes 60% of the time, 25% for a strength head tied to his head coach). The best who says yes is hired at the offer (never less than he had); a head hired away opens his old post, filled in its turn. The men out of work leave the market after three years or at 66 (`SupportMarket.free`). Each year's moves are in `SupportMarket.years`.
  - **A new head coach** (each morning, `support_bosses`): when a team's head coach changes (an interim changes nothing: a college's has no head coach title, an NFL club's in-season interim is skipped until Black Monday decides, `permanent_boss`), the strength head is let go unless he is already the new man's, or kept one time in seven at a college and three in ten in the NFL. The new head coach's own strength coach (out of work, or at his old team) follows him nine times in ten, for at least his old pay, and his old post goes to the market; otherwise the post goes to the market and whoever is hired is the new man's.
  - **Measured** on seed 1's world (Nov 1 file, `support_check`): played on two seasons (Jan 2026 to Jan 2028) about 196 strength heads a year were let go by new head coaches, 64-70 came with their head coach, 20-22 were kept; 330-430 heads a year were hired away and 330-420 posts went to fresh men. On the market alone for 15 years (a stand-in carousel moving 18% of head coaches a year, `debug::support_market_years`) the lower levels held (Division II/NAIA/JUCO strength 9.5 → 9.0, Division III 8.8 → 8.8) and the money sorted the best up: FBS strength 11.9 → 13.7, NFL 10.2 → 13.4, NFL analytics 11.1 → 15.4, flat in the last six years. The FBS strength heads at the 20 richest staff lines rate 15.4 against 12.7 at the 20 poorest (13.3 against 12.2 before). The best-paid college strength heads earn about $850-900K in 2026 (real: Iowa's Chris Doyle about $800K in 2019).
- What reads the strength head? A head's z is (rating − 10) / 10 (`Support::z`). Since minor bug 302 it is the coaching for strength, speed and conditioning: in college it takes the position coach's 0.4 of the coaching mix for the physical attributes, in the NFL 0.4 of the staff's pull, read around the level's typical head (the division's mean over programs with players; the mean of the clubs playing this year), on their growth and their ceilings (`world/season.rs`, `develop`, `typical`; `world/pro/mod.rs`; `development.rs`, `physical_coaching`, `physical_boost`). `physical` (0.6 × strength z + 0.4 × professionalism z) speeds the weight room's weight gain by up to 15%, the head's full distance included, and, less his head's distance from the level's typical head (`physical − 0.06 × strength_dev`), physical growth by up to 8% (`world/people.rs`, `support_ctx`; `development.rs`, `develop_season`, `physical_mult`). Since minor bug 303 it guards muscle strains and backs: 2% fewer a point over the level's typical head, capped at 15% either way, in engine games, NFL practice, college practices and the yearly roll (`game/sim.rs`, `strength_guard`; [injuries.md](injuries.md)). The NFLPA report cards grade the weight room and the strength coaches from it (`world/finance/nfl.rs`, `report_cards`). Still not read: in-game fatigue and the report-day conditioning test (`training/offseason.rs`, `fail_chance`).
- How much is that? Before minor bug 302 (measured 2026-11-07 at 460d534: 3,000 players to age 30, the same players and random streams in each arm, a 1-20 head in college and the NFL against one rated 10) a strength head of 20 added +0.4 strength, +0.2 stamina, +0.04 speed and +0.05 overall by 22, while an elite position coach (20 against 10, college only) added +0.85 strength, +0.6 stamina, +0.4 speed and +0.6 overall by 22. Since (`tests/development.rs`, `strength_staff_drives_physical_growth`: 1,500 players from 18 to 22 at FBS, a typical mix of 13 and head of 10): a strength head of 20 adds +0.80 strength, +0.54 stamina, +0.37 speed and +0.15 overall, one of 5 costs −0.43, −0.26, −0.18 and −0.05, and a position coach of 20 adds +0.52 overall and nothing to strength, stamina or speed. A typical staff gives exactly what it did before. A medical head of 20 cuts the yearly roll's games missed in the seasons at 18 to 29 by 0.6 (9.2 to 8.6).

### Support departments

Built 2026-11-17 from `docs/design/support-departments.md` (Cain: "agree on all"). The code is `world/departments.rs`; the heads stay in `world/people.rs`. Every size below that the spec doesn't set is proposed, not confirmed.

- **The people.** Below its five heads each college and NFL club keeps stored people (`Support.people`, `departments::Aide`): name, she or he, a hidden 1-20 rating, birth year, pay, first year and his share of time on football. Jobs (`departments::Job`): assistant strength coach, strength intern, sports dietitian, sports scientist, assistant athletic trainer, team physician, behavioral health clinician, pain management specialist (NFL), academic adviser, tutor, player development, NFL analyst. College analysts are the junior analyst and quality-control posts already built (C36), counted at the market (`Support.ops`); a college's personnel and recruiting staff are section 7's (`scouting::readers::personnel`). Video and equipment staff have no ratings.
- **Sizes** (`Job::range`, `Job::era`, `target`): each job has a 2026 range per pay level (Power 4, Group of 5, FCS, Division II/NAIA/JUCO, Division III, NFL), the team picks inside it by its money against the level's median (`people::money_ratio`: a college's football staff line for the strength, analytics and engagement departments, its athletic budget for the trainers and academics; an NFL owner's spending level), times the era, plus a keyed half-person either way held year to year. Power 4: 2-3 strength assistants and 1-2 interns, 2-3 dietitians, 1-3 sports scientists, 3-5 trainers, 2 physicians, 0-1 clinician, 1-3 advisers, 4-10 tutors, 1-3 development. Group of 5: 2-3 strength assistants, 1 dietitian, 0-1 sports scientist, 2 trainers, 1 physician, 0-1 adviser, 2-4 tutors, 0-1 development. FCS: 1-2 strength assistants, 0-1 shared dietitian (half his time), 1-2 trainers, 1 physician, 1-2 tutors. Division II/NAIA/JUCO: 0-1 trainer and 0-1 tutor, each half on football. Division III: the heads alone. NFL: 2-4 strength assistants, 1-2 dietitians, 1-2 sports scientists, 3 trainers, 2 physicians, a clinician, a pain specialist, 1 development, 1-6 analysts. Eras: dietitians from about 2011-2020 (rare before the NCAA's unlimited meals and the NFL's hires), sports scientists 2012-2020, NFL clinicians and pain specialists from 2019 (a quarter of clubs had a clinician before), college clinicians 2014-2020, NFL analysts 2003-2023, development 2005-2016, academics 1990-2008, strength and medical people 1990-2010.
- **A head's share of his time** (`head_share`): Division II and below share the strength coach and the trainers with every sport (half on football; Division III's strength coach 0.4 and its one trainer 0.3).
- **The market** (`departments::yearly`, run at the end of `people::staff_support`, NFL camp): people retire from 60 (6% + 4% a year past 60, all by 72) or leave one in ten a year; pay keeps up with the coaching pay index; each job comes to the year's size (the weakest go, fresh people come). A fresh person is drawn N(the post's fresh head mean + his job's offset + a quarter of his head's distance from it, 2.8) (`hire`, `Job::offset`: strength assistants and trainers −1.5, interns −4, tutors −3, physicians +1). Pay is his head's going rate × the money × the index × his job's share (`Job::pay_share`). Women by the professions' shares (`Job::women`: dietitians 75%, clinicians and academic staff 60%, trainers 35%; heads from 3% in strength to 55% in academics, `people::hire`).
- **Stepping up** (`people::fill`): when a head's post opens, the market is canvassed first (a fresh head, the best man out of work who takes the pay, up to three heads elsewhere the pay moves, up to three ladder assistants elsewhere at the same level or one either side, yes 60%: an assistant trainer you raised becomes someone else's head trainer). Then his own staff's best man on the ladder (strength assistant, assistant trainer, adviser, development, NFL analyst) is asked last and wins if he is ready, within `READY_BELOW` (2.0 rating points) of what a fresh head at the post rates (the way a college staff's own off-field man is ready for a position job, `staff::ready_at_home`), or if he out-rates every man the market offered; yes 85% of the time (minor bug 422; before, he went first but had to out-rate the whole market with no credit for being known, and won 1-2 posts in 430). ### Development

- High school: coaching = 0.6 × head coach teaching of his group + 0.4 × staff, staff = 4 + 12 × school resources, or half that and half the side's coordinator's teaching of the group at an AI school (C37), or your real assistants at your school; no coach → 7 (`world/season.rs`, season step `Stage::HighSchool`).
- College (C57, C60): 0.2 × HC teaching + 0.25 × the coordinator's unit + 0.4 × position coach + 0.15 × (6 + 12 × facilities). The unit is the coordinator's `side_quality` (0.7 × mean teaching on his side + 0.3 × scheme), the same for every group of the side; in the group he came up in, the position coach's part is half the position coach and half the coordinator. Missing coaches fall back down the chain (no PC → coordinator → HC → 9) (`world/season.rs`).
- Prep school 12; a mission or military year 2.
- NFL: team coaching = 0.5 × HC head_coach_value + 0.25 × OC side quality + 0.25 × DC side quality, side quality = 0.7 × mean teaching on that side + 0.3 × scheme (no coordinator → 11) (`world/staff.rs`, `set_nfl_coaching`, `side_quality`; read in `world/pro/mod.rs`).
- In `development.rs`, `develop_season`: growth × (1 + (0.75 + 0.025 × coaching − 1) × (0.6 + 0.04 × coachability)); ceiling drive gets +0.35 × (coaching − 10)/10 and a coachability × coaching term; physical attributes read physical coaching in place of coaching in both (the strength head, above; minor bug 302); coaching under 8 risks a "poor coaching" year (4% per point under 8, up to 20%) that costs technique. See [development.md](development.md).
- Style and scheme pull habits: the head coach's style blended with the coordinator's (`tendencies::Style::blend` in the season step). See [tendencies.md](tendencies.md).
- What development does not read: recruiting, network, motivation (motivation goes to player trust, below), reputation.

### Games

- Play caller skill (`world/season.rs`, `coach_caller`): base 0.6 scheme + 0.4 game management, × (0.7 + 0.3 × how well he knows the installed system). Reading 0.7 scheme + 0.3 GM, situational 0.7 GM + 0.3 scheme, creativity = scheme, each ± a fixed personal spread; nerve and aggressiveness from ambition.
- `GameStaff` also carries the head coach's game management, discipline (strictness), sportsmanship and style; film study = 0.35 × (OC + DC scheme) + 7 × facilities (`game_team`).
- Systems: each spring the coordinator (or HC) installs the system he knows ≥ 0.5 that suits his players best, his read noise 0.8 × (20 − scheme)/10; a switch costs a year of learning (`world/systems.rs`, `spring_install`, `draw`). What suits his players (scheme-fit lane SF11): his starters (a quarterback, a back, three receivers, a tight end, two tackles, three inside linemen, two inside and two edge rushers, three linebackers, three corners, two safeties), the best by overall at each position, seated at the jobs that suit them together as a staff seats them (`spots::best_order`), each man's fit at his job summed in points a game (`roster_fit`). The head coach's guideline adds to a system's worth: firm 6, a lean 1.6, his call 0.3 points a game; the coverage guideline up to 4 toward the family whose man share is nearest, fading over 0.35. A lean or firm guideline lets him install a system he knows under 0.5 (`world/guide.rs`, `install_bonus`, `install_wanted`). NFL staffs weigh the same pull in knowledge units, the bonus / 6 (`nfl_bonus`; `world/systems.rs`, `nfl_install`). Your high school installs what your lean or firm guideline asks for at summer camp, once a season from August 1, knowing a changed system only as far as it shares the old one's playbook (`wanted_install`; `world/hs_year.rs`, `camp_install`, `school_install`; `world/systems.rs`, `overlap`).
- Fit (scheme-fit lane SF11, `world/systems.rs`, `fit_at`): what a man's ratings and weight are worth in each system at his job, from the table measured on the match engine (`data/fit_values.csv`, `fit_check values`: points a game for a rating point, job by job, so the free and strong safety, the Mike, Will and Sam and the outside and slot corner read their own), over the typical college player's at that job (`system_norms`, worked out each spring), less what a man as much better on every rating would gain there (fit is the shape of his game, not his level), against the average system of its kind. His job is the spot at his position his ratings and body suit best (`fit_job`; a fullback only when he clearly is one). One man's fit as recruiting, the market and the screens read it (`fits`, `on_rating_scale`) is that worth as the rating points at his job that would buy as much, in tenths, held to 1.5 either way.
- Guidelines (`world/guide.rs`, `head_guidelines`): an AI head coach sets one only where he leans clearly: the offense, front or coverage family he knows 0.8+ and 0.15 ahead of the next; pressure when his risk is 3+ from 10 (`habit_pressure`); a run share 0.05 above his best system's habit for a line or backs coach, 0.05 below for a quarterback or receivers coach, + 0.02 N, clamped 0.25-0.65; mixed tempo (no-huddle one in five) for an air raid or spread option man who knows it 0.85+. College and high school linebacker and secondary minds make a nickel front their base (4-2-5, 3-3-5) a quarter and about a third of the time, redrawn every four years. Wing-T never for the AI outside high school (`team_guidelines`).
- Firmness from his coaching style's mould: 13.5+ firm, 7.5 or less his coordinators' call, else a lean (`firmness`). A head coach calling a side himself has no guideline there; when you call a side, yours there are firm; an assistant's team plays to its head coach's, less any side he calls himself, as at every other team (`team_guidelines`, `firm_side`). Each level keeps only what its football allows: the wing-T at high school, no nickel base in the pros (`fit_level`).
- A coordinator asked off his systems calls them as well as he knows them: the defensive caller's fit is his knowledge blended toward the family the man guideline moves him to (more man: the man and pressure family; less: the zone family he knows best), by the share of snaps it moves (`man_fit`, `apply`).
- Quick results (no engine): team strength + (HC game management − 10) × 0.12 (`college_results`).
- NFL season: staff edge (coaching − 13) / 2.5 (`world/nfl_year.rs`).

## Reputation and the visible record

- `recent` holds the last six seasons' wins above expectation as a share of the season (`vs_expectation` = (wins − expected)/games) (`world/season.rs`, `TeamResult::vs_expectation`).
- Season update, at the end of the regular season (`world/college.rs`, `record_regular_season`; `world/hs_year.rs`; `world/nfl_year.rs`): rep += speed × 0.3 × (head_coach_value + 12 × vs − rep) − speed × 0.6 × locker room; speed 1 head, 0.5 assistant. College locker room = incidents / roster × 8, 0-1. NFL coordinators are judged on 0.5 × vs + a unit term from their side quality.
- Titles (`title_reputation`): +3 conference or state, +5 national, × headroom min((20 − rep)/8, 1) × `title_above` (0.5 + 4 × the season's result against expectation, the last entry of `recent`, 0.25-1.5): a title won as the roster should gains half, one 1.5 wins a 12-game season above it the full boost (program fortunes item 7, Cain 2026-11-24; sizes proposed, not confirmed). The stage (0.5 + 0.5 × stage) moves only his name.
- Hiring buzz = reputation + 10 × last season + 4 × the one before + stat buzz (`hiring_buzz`). Hotness adds `HOT_ABOVE` (6) × his last three seasons against expectation, summed (`above_three`), for college and NFL men; the employer's prestige, its titles and its rise no longer count (program fortunes item 6, Cain 2026-11-24; the size proposed, not confirmed). Other leagues: 4 × (win share − 0.5), +1.5 with a title (`world/staff.rs`, `hotness`).
- Unit records: how each group's players grew against everyone at their level and age (position coach credit 1, coordinator 0.5, HC 0.25; a high school HC 1 for every group), and how many moved up. `growth_read` = growth / (seasons + 8) (`world/coach.rs`, `UnitRecord`; `world/staff.rs`, `credit_teaching`; `credit_impact`). ## The coaching ladder and the carousel

- When: college carousel the Sunday after rivalry week; NFL firings on Black Monday, hires after the conference title games; high school carousel in late January, then everyone ages (`world/clock.rs`; `world/nfl_year.rs`; `world/staff.rs`, `Scope`).
- Each run (`carousel`): NCAA enforcement first (a show-cause order ends a college job); then retirements, early exits and reviews; then every open seat is queued and filled best job first; every hire opens the job he left (`hire_all`, `fill_opening`).
- Retirement: 60+, chance 0.04 + 0.03 per year over 60 (+ health scare). Under 60: health 0.08% + 0.025% per year over 40; burnout 0.8% × pressure² × tenure/10 × intensity × (1 + 0.5 per national title here, to 3). A 55-59 coach with 2+ national titles here walks away on top at 2% per title a year (`carousel`, `early_exit`).
- College searches decide on their own day: FBS head 4-22 days (2-10 after the regular season), FCS head 10-35 (5-21 after), smaller 14-45, coordinator 7-30, position coach 10-40; all close by fall camp (`schedule_search`, `college_searches_day`).
- Who's on the list (`candidate_pool`):
  - College head job: head coaches at the same level or one below, coordinators at the same level; the 15 hottest first, 40 random draws; for FBS also the 3 hottest NFL coordinators; D2/JUCO head jobs also 6 top high school coaches (prestige > 0.7); every college job except an FBS head job also draws 3 high school coaches with buzz (a state title in his last two seasons and 90%+ wins) (`hs_buzz`).
  - Coordinator: coordinators a level up, same or down on that side, head coaches a level down; 10 hottest first.
  - Position coach: same group sideways or a level either way, coordinators a level down on that side, state high school coaches with a good record at that group (an FBS search looks at one in a fifth of searches, no high school coordinators: 12 of 570 FBS on-field changes in 2021-22 came from high schools, 247Sports via FootballScoop; a smaller college 4 head coaches and 2 coordinators; minor bug 243, proposed, not confirmed), the program's own off-field men in that room and 3 others at random, the 3 best-regarded coaches let go from that room at this level since the last season (an FBS search also the NFL's; minor bug 252, proposed, not confirmed), 12% chance of a new ex-pro.
  - High school head job: 20 of the state's head coaches, the school's own coordinators, 8 of the state's, and a new ex-pro in one search in a hundred (about 130 of some 14,000 high school head coaches were ex-NFL in 2026, USA Today; at one in four the world's were 43% ex-pros; minor bug 242, proposed, not confirmed).
  - NFL: every NFL coordinator for a head job (same side for a coordinator job), out-of-work NFL coaches, the 8 (head) or 5 (coordinator) hottest FBS names, the 2 hottest other-league head coaches for a head job. A coordinator job also calls 4 of the league's position coaches, whom the world doesn't keep: each drawn from the rooms coordinators come up through (offense: quarterbacks 45%, receivers 30%, line 15%, backs 10%; defense: secondary 40%, linebackers 35%, line 25%), aged 34-54, his standing 1.5 below a new NFL coach's; the first is the club's own and gets +3 in the score; those passed over go back to the jobs the world doesn't keep (minor bug 244, proposed, not confirmed).
  - College head and coordinator jobs also call the 5 hottest out-of-work coaches who, in the last three seasons, held a head job at that level or above or in the NFL (from his own side for a coordinator job), or a coordinator job there (either side for a head job, the same side for a coordinator job) (`out_of_work`; minor bug 124: a fired head coach is a name every search knows, as Rich Rodriguez, Mike Leach and Butch Davis were).
  - Everyone: 8 random out-of-work coaches under 66 (a former FBS/FCS head coach won't take a high school job; a high school takes only a man who last worked in its state, and a public school only one who has coached in high school, the teaching certificate; minor bug 242). Show-cause coaches are barred. High school coordinator posts filled from the free list carry the same certificate rule (`hs_staff_year`).
  - How the lists are walked (world speed lane, same lists): a search walks the coaches still working, a list of every coach not retired taken at the start of each carousel, search day and other-league fill, plus every coach made since (`LiveCoaches`, `note_live`, `live`). Coaches are only ever added and never come out of retirement, so the list misses nobody, and every walk still skips the retired. The search's big walks (the out-of-work names, the program's own juniors, the let-go men in that room, the NFL and other-league pools) test each coach on all cores and keep index order (`live_where`); a college list looks only at the levels and slots that can fit the job (`candidate_pool`, `fits`).
- Score (`world/coach.rs`, `candidate_score`, `hiring_needs`): hiring buzz + needs.network × (read network − 10) × 1.2 + needs.teaching × (read teaching − 10) × 0.6 + needs.recruiting × (read recruiting − 10) × 0.6 + 2 × needs.network for an ex-pro + N(0, 1). Reads are ±2.5 noise.
  - Needs: high school network 0.6 × (1 − prestige) + 0.05, teaching 0.3 + 0.6 × prestige; college network 0.25 × (1 − prestige), teaching 0.35 + 0.25 × prestige, recruiting 0.4 − 0.1 × prestige; NFL teaching 0.6 only.

- A head coach who moves from one college head job to another brings his men: each assistant of his old staff follows him into the same role 55% of the time (35% to a smaller program), the man in that seat leaving ("let go by the new staff") and the old job opening (`bring_his_staff`, `his_old_program`; of 20 staffs whose head coach left for a Power 5 job since 2010, five assistants followed him, the next head coach kept two and two were left out, Winston-Salem Journal via FootballScoop; proposed, not confirmed). - Mentor: an assistant hired under a head coach joins his tree (`hire`).
- The years on a job: a job's `since` and a finished job's `to` are both the carousel's season (`cycle_year`: last season until fall camp opens), at every level but the other leagues, which keep the calendar year. - A coach's age comes from his birth date: a new coach and a former player who turns coach are born so that their age on 31 August of the coaching year is their drawn age, with the coaching year turning after January 20, the day coaches age (they age at the end of it, so a coach made that day counts the old season; `coach_age_season`, `new_coach_for`; `world/former.rs`, `to_coach`; minor bug S1).
- A program's season goes to the head coach of most of its games, as ESPN and the record books credit it: a coach fired during the season keeps it when he coached half or more of it, and his interim gets no season of his own (`world/college.rs`, `record_regular_season`, `fired_during`; minor bug 124). The interim still coaches the rest and his games count to his own job.
- A coach of color hired from an NFL coordinator job to a head job earns his old team the diversity picks (`hire`).

## Job security

### College (the athletic director)

- The AD has judgement and patience, each N(10, 3.5); 6% turnover a year, up to 18% when prestige slid over four years, +4% after ten years (`new_ad`, `ad_turnover`).
- The read (`college_review`): −(1.5 × last vs + 0.7 × the one before, his seasons here only) + N(0, 0.06 − 0.003 × (judgement − 10)) − direction × (0.1 in years 1-3, else 0.04) + (0.3 + 0.5 × prestige) × (0.4 − two-year win %) from year 3. Direction = half roster strength against what he inherited (4 points full), half his classes against the three before him (25% full).
- The bar: 0.06 + 0.012 × (patience − 10) − 0.04 × (prestige − 0.5), −0.03 for a new AD who didn't hire him.
- P(fire) = (read − bar) × (2 + 4 × prestige), max 0.85; year 1 only 10% after a 0.35 collapse; year 2 half and only after 0.22; year 3 × 0.75. FBS × 0.7 (`FBS_FIRE`, minor bug 124), FCS × 0.5, lower levels × 0.35. Buyout factor clamp(1.2 − buyout / (0.2 × athletic budget + boosters), 0.15, 1). Never while still playing for a title.
- Prove-it year: a loyal winner (5+ seasons, rarely left jobs, a title in his last eight seasons here or 70% over 50+ games) facing a firing gets an extension instead with chance 0.6 + 0.35 × excuse (draft losses, a young roster); a bad next year fires him (`earned_patience`, `excuse`).
- Contracts: 6 years at FBS prestige > 0.6, 5 at > 0.35, 4 FBS/FCS, 3 D2/NAIA/D3, 2 JUCO; pay = the search's price (see "Pay"; salary × (0.8 + 0.4 × rep/20) when no search set one); buyout share 70% big FBS, 50% FBS, 30% FCS, 20% D2/NAIA, 10% D3/JUCO, a fifth of that through 1995, then ramping to full by 2015 (`contract_years`, `buyout_share`, `hire`). A good year with two or fewer left renews at his market read (at least 5% more, within what the school can pay), and a hot name read 20%+ over his pay is extended early (realism audit item 6; before it, a flat 15%); an expiring deal after a poor year isn't renewed.
- In-season firing (every college level since realism audit item 8, from year 2): 5+ games, ≤ 30%, 0.15+ under expectation, already on a hot seat or prove-it; chance 0.35 × afford × era (0.4 before 2010) × (0.6 + 0.8 × prestige), half in year 2, × `FBS_FIRE` at the FBS, × 0.4 of it at the FCS and 0.2 below (`in_season_firings`). Assistants too: see "Firings during the season".
- A firing or departure reopens the head coach's verbal commitments (`world/recruiting.rs`, `decommit_after_firing`) and, from 2022, a coaching-change portal window. The players' portal lean takes the coaching-change push the day the head coach leaves, once a cycle (`world/college.rs`, `coach_gone`, from `leave`). One who leaves after the cycle's window opened (from six days after the FBS regular season until fall camp) opens a late window at his program five days after his successor is hired, 30 days long in 2022-24 and 15 from 2025 (`late_window_due`, `hire`; minor bug 88).
- A college head coach who leaves his job for another college head job at his level or above asks his own players along (one who takes a job a level down, or a fired coach rehired, takes no one): those tied to him (0.4 or more) who'd start or rotate there lean toward the portal and the new school wants them, whatever its depth chart says (`fill_opening`, `world/recruiting.rs`, `follow_coach`; minor bug 86). See [recruiting.md](recruiting.md), "The transfer portal".

P = (badness − 0.1) × 3.2 × patience, max 0.9, × 0 / 0.3 / 0.7 / 1 for tenure 0/1/2/3+. A kept coach fires each coordinator with chance (0.42 − win share) × 1.5, max 0.35 (`nfl_firing`).

## Pay

- The job's line, what that employer usually pays for the job, lo × (hi/lo)^prestige (`world/staff.rs`, `salary`): FBS head $0.6-12M, coordinator $0.25-2.5M, position coach $0.15-1.0M; FCS $0.15-0.7M / $80-250K / $50-120K; down to D3; high school $50-160K (teaching plus stipend); NFL head $5-14M, coordinator $1.2-4M, position $0.5-1.2M by owner, up to 2× in the 1990s. Since realism audit item 6 (Cain 2026-11-15) the line is only the employer's budget for the job; what a college or NFL coach is paid comes from the market below. High schools and the other leagues still pay their line.
- **The market** (`world/staff/pay.rs`; every size proposed, not confirmed):
  - What an employer can pay one man at most (`cap`): a college 7% of its athletic budget for a head coach, 1.6% for a coordinator, 0.6% for a position coach (about Kirby Smart's $13.3M on Georgia's budget, the best coordinators' $3M, the best position coaches' $1M+), never under 1.1 × the line; an NFL owner 1.6 × his line (Andy Reid, reported over $20M).
  - His market read (`refresh`, `read`, kept on the save in `Staffs::market`): the men of one level (FBS, FCS, the smaller colleges, the NFL) and one rung (head coach, coordinator, position coach) ranked by how hot they are (`hotness`: reputation and his last three seasons against expectation), the jobs ranked by their lines; the k-th hottest man is worth the k-th richest job's line × (1 + 0.25 × e^(−k/2)), so the top names go for more (1.25, 1.15, 1.09 ...). Refreshed before the college carousel, Black Monday and signing day.
  - A search's first offer (`offer`): the line × 1.15 for the first name on its list, 0.03 less a place, never under 0.85; at least his pay × 1.15 to move across or down (× 1.05 for a promotion) and his read when the job is on his own level and rung; at most what the employer can pay. A man out of work gets the line × his place × 0.9.
  - The counter (`auction`, from `weigh_offer`): when he means to go, his school (a college or NFL club the world runs, not your staff) counters 70% of the time if it wants him back (his last results at or over expectation, or reputation 13+): up to his read or pay × 1.25 within what it can pay; the suitor answers up to 1.3 × its first offer within what it can pay; the price lands 5% past the loser's top. He weighs the last bid against what he'd make staying on the same roll; if he stays he gets the raise and a year or two more on his deal (`kept_by_counter`, news for FBS and NFL head coaches and coordinators: "... keeps ... with a raise and a longer deal after ... came calling about its ... job").
  - He signs for the price the search paid (`fill_opening`, `pay::set`): a head coach's contract, an assistant's deal.
  - Results: a kept college head coach after a good year re-signs at his read (at least 5% more, never past what the school can pay; `renewed`), and a hot name read 20% or more over his pay is extended early (`underpaid`; Smart's 2022 and 2024 deals). NFL head coaches now sign deals too (five years, all of it owed on a firing; `nfl_contract`, `ensure_nfl_contracts` for older worlds), and a kept one after a winning year with a year or less left, or underpaid, gets three more years at his read (`nfl_kept`). An assistant whose deal runs out re-signs on signing day at his read when it says more than 3%, else +3%; your own staff's deals take the 3% (you redo them).
- Assistant deals (`world/folk/staffing.rs`, `deal_terms`, `sign`): pay × (0.8 + 0.4 × rep/20); coordinators at an FBS power program that season (`Program::power_conference`: today's four, the Pac-10/12 until 2023, the Big East until 2013, the Big Eight and Southwest Conference, Notre Dame, and before 1993 the major independents; minor bug 311) 3 years with 50% buyout, their position coaches 2 years 30%, other colleges 1 year, NFL 2-3 years. Signed for the search's price (above); renewed on signing day at his market read or +3% (`staffing::day`).

- **Your boss's yes to more staff money** (`world/folk/boss.rs`, `ask`, `AskKind::StaffMoney`; fixed 2026-11-15, size proposed, not confirmed): a yes adds a tenth (`STAFF_RAISE`) to `Staffs::pool_bonus`, which nothing read before this fix (it showed only on the staff screen). `staffing::staff_bonus(w, team)` gives the share for your own team only (0 for every other team, so AI worlds run exactly as before: only your seat asks). It is read through the same lines as the base pool:
  - **College**: the football support staff line of the athletic budget, what the department earns × its tier's share × how hard it pushes football × (1 + the raises) (`world/finance/college.rs`, `open_year`; "College spending from what it earns" below), from the day he says yes for the months still to come (`raise_staff`, like the recruiting raise) and in every later budget. The spending level (`Dept::level`, the two lines against a well-off program of the tier, up to `LEVEL_MAX` 4 as a guard) moves with it; the support heads' offers read the line itself (`world/people.rs`, `offer`), and a fresh hire is drawn at a level from facilities, division and √level, at most 1 (`hire_level`, `football_level`).
  - **NFL**: the assistants' pool inside the coaches' line (`22M × pay index × (0.8 + 0.4 × spend)`, `world/finance/nfl.rs`, `open_year`, `raise_staff`), and your club's support heads are hired at its owner's spending level × (1 + the raises) (`staff_support`).
  - **High school and other leagues**: no pool exists to raise (a school pays fixed stipends, the other leagues keep a lean room), so a yes there still counts for his next yes (`given_cut`) but moves no money.
  - The game has no hard payroll cap on your assistants: you can already offer any salary, and the cost of every deal lands in the coaches' pay line. The raise buys the support staff line, not extra offers.
  - A new job starts at none (`folk/takeover.rs`, `begin`).

## College spending from what it earns
