## Quick answers

- Who calls the plays? Each side's coordinator, the head coach when there is none, you when you take a side; a high school head coach calls both sides (`world/season.rs`, `play_caller`; `world/hs_year.rs`, `squad_team`).
- Does a good play caller get a bonus on the field? No. His skills only change which plays are called; the play then has to win its battles on the field (`game/calling.rs`, module doc).
- Where does a caller's quality come from? 0.6 scheme + 0.4 game management, times 0.7 + 0.3 × how well he knows the installed system (`world/season.rs`, `coach_caller`).
- How is run or pass picked? The real pass rate for the down, distance, score and clock, then the scheme's lean, aggressiveness, what has worked today and the game state (`game/calling.rs`, `call_offense`; `game/tables.rs`, `real_pass_rate`).
- Does the caller know what beats what? Yes: a table of what each concept gained against each coverage, and each run against each front, measured on the engine. He leans on it by trust(reading) = 0.55 × ((reading - 1) / 19)² (`game/calling.rs`, `CALL_VALUES_CSV`, `trust`).
- What does film study do? It sets how many of the opponent's snaps the staff breaks down (20 + 2.25 × film study), the error on what it counts, its matchup plan and the habits it targets (`Memory::prepare`, `make_plan`, `read_habit`).
- Do defenders key formations? Yes: what the offense has run from a look is a tell. A defender reads the play 0.16 s × his key sooner when the tell is right, and later when it lies (`Memory::belief`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).
- When does the quarterback audible? From a run into a box with 2+ more defenders than blockers (not when protecting a fourth-quarter lead in the last 5:00 or with the game won; minor bug 142), or from a pass against a light box showing two deep, with chance clamp((iq - 40) / 50, 0.05, 0.95), × (1 - 0.5 × crowd) on the road, if his head coach gives him the freedom (`game/flow.rs`, `run_scrimmage_call`).
- How is fourth down decided? Expected points of going, punting and kicking, read from the level and both teams (short yardage, the punter, the kicker; engine gaps item 20), then a habit: he goes at the real go rate, moved toward what the numbers say by how much sharper and bolder he is than his level's typical coach (`game/calling.rs`, `fourth_down`; minor bug 142: every coach used to follow the numbers on top of the real rate, 1.93 NFL tries a team game against 1.46).
- What happens on a half's last snap? A kick in range, else by the score, the spot and the time: a kneel, fades from inside 30 yards, a Hail Mary as far as the passer's arm gets it to the end zone, or, behind at the end of the game and too far for that, the laterals (`game/flow.rs`, `last_call`; engine gaps items 15 and 16). See "Fourth down, tries, onside kicks and the clock".
- When do starters come out? When the head coach's read of the comeback odds passes his level's steps: z = lead / (10 √(share of game left) + 3.25), read through his game management and moved by his sportsmanship (`game/garbage.rs`, `comeback_z`, `Coach::review`).
- Does any world team run the wing-T? Only your high school, when your system guideline asks for it (not "his call") on a power gap install; it is called at 0.75 of the play caller's power gap knowledge. AI teams install one of five offenses (West Coast, air raid, wide zone, power gap, spread option); synthetic high school test leagues also use the wing-T (`world/guide.rs`, `apply`, `wing_fit`; `world/systems.rs`, `OFFENSES`; `game/synthetic.rs`, `synthetic_league`).
- Do the head coach's guidelines bind the coordinators? No. Each pulls the coordinator's own habit toward the guideline by its firmness: firm 0.85, a lean 0.55, his call 0.15 of the gap; down, distance, score and clock still rule (`game/guide.rs`, `Firm::pull`, `expected`; `game/calling.rs`, `call_offense`, `call_defense`).
- Do AI head coaches set guidelines? Yes, from their own leanings and nature, so every team plays by the same rules (`world/guide.rs`, `head_guidelines`, `team_guidelines`).
- Do AI coordinators call designed plays? Yes, the ones on their team's sheets (dry or wet, the first eight): a play caller in that personnel weighs each design like the library concept it looks like, without the scheme-favorite lean, and runs it from its own backfield and set (`game/calling.rs`, `sheet_designs`, `pick_concept`, `call_offense`). Only your team draws designs, so only your coordinators have any. They also come from your sheet's boxes or from you (`game/sheet.rs`, `off_call`).
- Does the designer grade my play? No: it checks legality, your coordinator comments at his reading skill, and the play gains what its geometry gains (`game/designer.rs`, `legal`, `review`).
- Do new ideas fool defenses? In college only: a family new to the division, or one the team runs far more than its division, makes defenders read up to 0.12 s later. NFL and high school teams keep 0 (`world/trends.rs`, `team_novelty`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`).
- Does my game plan's run-pass lean do anything? The training plan's old lean does not: it is saved, but no engine code reads it (`training/seat.rs`, `GamePlan`, `prepare`). The identity's run share guideline does (`game/calling.rs`, `call_offense`).
- Do the rules change by era? Yes: every NFL and college game plays its year's book for the kickoff spot, the touchback, the extra point and overtime, playoffs included; college also for the clock: the two-minute timeout from 2024 and the clock stopped on every first down to 2022 (`game/rules.rs`, `Rules::for_year`; `game/flow.rs`, `quick_game_in`; see the rule books below). High school plays today's book. The other leagues' games use their own book for the season (`game/flow.rs`, `quick_game_book`; `game/books.rs`, `Rules::for_book`).

## Who calls and how good he is

- The caller is the side's coordinator, else the head coach; you, when your coach calls that side (`world/season.rs`, `play_caller`). A high school head coach calls both sides and installs the systems he knows best (`world/hs_year.rs`, `squad_team`).
- An AI caller's skills come from the calling coach's scheme (S) and game management (GM), both 1-20: skill = (0.6 S + 0.4 GM) × fit; reading = clamp(0.7 S + 0.3 GM + 2N, 1, 20) × fit; situational = clamp(0.7 GM + 0.3 S + 2N, 1, 20), with no fit; creativity = clamp(S + 3N, 1, 20) × fit. fit = 0.7 + 0.3 × his knowledge of the installed system, 0-1 (`world/season.rs`, `coach_caller`; the NFL copy is in `world/pro/games.rs`, `build_side`).
- Adaptation = his GM, aggressiveness = his ambition, nerve = clamp(0.04 × (ambition - 10) + 0.4N, -1, 1). Each N is keyed on the coach, so his spread is the same every game. With no coach at all: skill, reading and creativity 9 × 0.94 (NFL 10 × 0.94), situational and adaptation 9 (NFL 10), aggressiveness 10, nerve 0 (`coach_caller`).
- Your own calls: your play-calling rating replaces the 0.6 S + 0.4 GM base and shifts reading, situational and creativity by the same amount; your adaptation rating replaces GM (`world/season.rs`, `your_calls`).
- The head coach brings game management, strictness (his discipline) and sportsmanship (`world/season.rs`, `game_team`; `world/pro/games.rs`, `build_side`; `world/hs_year.rs`, `squad_team`).
- Film study, 1-20: college clamp(0.35 × (OC scheme + DC scheme) + 7 × facilities, 1, 20); NFL the same with 7 × clamp(0.85 + 0.15 × analytics z, 0.7, 1) in place of facilities; high school clamp(0.7 × his scheme + 7 × the school's resources, 1, 20) (same functions).
- Install, the share of the playbook in a normal week's plan: clamp(absorb(the roster's mean Football IQ) + miss + 0.01 × (OC aggressiveness - 10), 0.35, 1), miss = 0.15 × (1 - OC skill / 20) × N keyed on coach and year; high school has no miss (same functions).
- Nickel habit: clamp(0.88 + 0.08N, 0.55, 0.99) in college and high school, clamp(0.9 + 0.06N, 0.6, 0.99) in the NFL. Playbook size is 1.0 for every world team (same functions).

| Skill | On offense (`game/calling.rs`) | On defense (`game/calling.rs`) |
|---|---|---|

| nerve | agg = aggressiveness + 4 × nerve × pressure (`call_offense`, `pick_concept`, `fourth_down`) | not read |
| skill | not read in the call; the install's miss (`world/season.rs`, `game_team`) | simulated pressure weight, fire zone weight, disguise (`call_defense`) |

- trust(x) = 0.55 × clamp((x - 1) / 19, 0, 1)²: 0 at 1, 0.12 at 10, 0.55 at 20 (`trust`, `TRUST`).
- Fourth downs use the offensive caller's aggressiveness and nerve, tries his aggressiveness; the head coach adds only his game management, on fourth downs (`fourth_down`, `go_for_two`). His game management also sets the defense's late timeouts (`game/flow.rs`, `after_snap_clock`), how true his garbage-time read is (`game/garbage.rs`, `Coach::new`) and the NFL toss deferral (`game/live.rs`, `ai_toss`).
- Strictness cuts the quarterback's freedom at the line (`run_scrimmage_call`); its part in obeying instructions is on [tendencies.md](tendencies.md).

## Film and game prep

- A team's film is its season so far. Every run, pass, sack and scramble goes on both teams' film: runs and passes by look (personnel × backfield × set, 108 looks), pass and run concepts, coverages, blitzes, fronts and passes by down (`game/calling.rs`, `Film`; `game/flow.rs`, `resolve`).
- At the new season the film fades to 0.3, or 0.1 after a head coach change (`world/college.rs`, `start`; `world/pro/mod.rs`, `league_year`). High school teams carry no film, so their opponents prep from habits alone (`world/hs_year.rs`, `squad_team`).
- Prep, study = clamp(film study / 20, 0.05, 1) (`Memory::prepare`). What everyone knows: 12 × (0.4 + 0.6 study) snaps of the opponent's shell mix, its blitz rate clamp(0.24 + 0.012 × (DC aggressiveness - 10), 0.05, 0.6) and its pass rate 0.58 + scheme lean. The tape: at most 20 + 45 × study snaps of each side, each count × (1 + N(0, 0.35 × (1 - study))).
- Your own film study adds the tapes you watched at their true counts, with no cap (`Memory::study`; `world/live_seat.rs`, `seat_prep`).
- Habits targeted: seen = the group's true mean + N(0, 0.15 × max(21 - film study, 1)), lean = clamp((seen - 12.5) / 4, 0, 1) (`read_habit`; `game/flow.rs`, `play_game_ctl`). Against the starting 2 EDGE + 2 DT's Snap timing: the hard count comes on every 3rd and 4th down and at 3 or less to go, not only 3rd/4th and 2 or less, × (1 + 0.5 × lean) (`game/flow.rs`, `pre_snap_foul`). Against the 2 CB + 2 S's Jump routes: double moves cut their chance to jump the right route by 0.25 × lean (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `jump_route`).
- Against the 2 EDGE's Upfield: draws × (1 + 1.5 × lean) (`pick_run`). Against KR1's Return risk: with chance 0.6 × lean, if the leg reaches, the kickoff lands 1-4 yards deep in the end zone (`game/kicking.rs`, `kickoff`).
- Matchup plan, from depth-chart spot ratings seen with error N(0, 0.8 × max(20 - film study, 1)) (`make_plan`; `game/flow.rs`, `play_game_ctl`). Target corner: the weaker of their top 2 CBs, if more than 4 points below the other; his man becomes the first read 50% of the time (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`). Bracket: their best of 3 WRs, if more than 5 above our best of 2 CBs (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `assign_defense`).
- Spy: their QB's run threat above 0.6, with chance 0.4 + 0.03 × DC reading; run threat = clamp((0.6 Speed + 0.4 Elusiveness - size_cost(weight, 222) - 68) / 20 + 0.5 × (Scramble - 10.5) / 9.5, 0, 1) (`game/flow.rs`, `qb_spy`; the Scramble habit from minor bug 289, Cain's yes 2026-11-07: a QB who leaves the pocket often is spied more; the 0.5 proposed, not confirmed). Help: their best of 2 EDGE + 2 DT, if more than 3 above our 2 OTs' mean; the line slides and a back or tight end chips (`HELP_GAP`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `slide_to`).
- In-game memory, after each run, pass, sack or scramble with no accepted penalty: the offense keeps its runs, passes and deep throws (15+ air yards), each look's runs and passes and each run concept's results; the defense keeps the other offense's looks, concepts, passes, quick throws (under 2.4 s), deep EPA and run EPA. EPA is read off the level's own table (the NFL's, or the college one below the NFL), a touchdown worth 6.95 (`game/flow.rs`, `resolve`, `estimate_epa`, `ep_at`; `game/tables.rs`, `expected_points_in`).
- The defense's key on a look: league = 1 - (their passes + 2) / (their plays + 4); p_run = (runs from it + 3 × league) / (snaps from it + 3); sure = n / (n + 6); it returns (p_run - league, sure) (`Memory::belief`).

## The offensive call (`game/calling.rs`, `call_offense`)

- p_pass starts at the real pass rate by down 1-3 (fourth down uses third), distance, score and clock (late = the 4th quarter's last 7:00, two-minute = a half's last 2:00): nflverse 2021-25 for the NFL, the college table below it, defaults 0.58 and 0.5 (`game/tables.rs`, `real_pass_rate`; `Situation::clock`).
- Then + the scheme's lean (West Coast +0.02, air raid +0.09, wide zone -0.03, power gap -0.06, spread option -0.02, wing-T -0.25) + 0.006 × (agg - 10) + the book's lean, 0 at the American levels (`game/roster.rs`, `OffScheme::pass_lean`). agg = aggressiveness + 4 × nerve × pressure; pressure = clamp(-diff / 14, 0, 1) when behind in the second half, × 0.6 in Q3 (`Situation::pressure`).
- After 8+ plays: + clamp((pass EPA a play - run EPA a play - 0.12 + N(0, 0.25 × (1 - a))) × 0.22a, -0.12, 0.12), a = adaptation / 20.
- Weather: - max(0.007 × (wind - 13)₊, 0.04 in rain, 0.08 in snow) (minor bug 144: real offenses on 1st and 2nd down within 7 throw 51.8% in calm, about the same to 14 mph, 49.7% at 15-19 mph, 44.9% at 20+, 47.4% in rain and 42.6% in snow, nflverse 2021-2025; `game/flow.rs`, `weather_lean`, proposed, not confirmed).
- Hurry-up: p_pass at least 0.85. It is on in a half's last 2:00 from Q2 on when tied or behind, in Q4's last 5:00 when behind, and in Q2's last 2:00 at any score; never when easing at 0.5+ or conceded (`Situation::hurry`).
- Garbage time: leading, p_pass moves toward 0.22 by the ease share (0.44 on 3rd+ and 7+, 0.11 at 2 or less to go); conceded, 70% of the way to 0.45 (`RUN_OUT_PASS`, `CONCEDED_PASS`). 3rd and 4th and 1: at most 0.45. Clamped 0.03-0.97.
- What it does not read: the crowd, the defense on the field, or any player rating beyond the QB's run threat, the healthy TE count and whether the depth chart lists 2+ RBs (`OffCtx`; `game/flow.rs`, `snap_play_as`).
- The order of the call (lane SF2, item 1): run or pass (above); for a pass, a run fake or a straight dropback (below, "Play action"); then the personnel to fit that play; then the backfield and the set. Real play action comes from the run's groups, not the dropback's: 2024 NFL on 1st and 10, screens, RPOs and sneaks left out, 12 personnel is on 34% of runs, 37% of play action and 24% of straight dropbacks (nflverse with FTN charting).
- The concept is drawn among the concepts the group can run as the rule book fields it: one of its reads on the field, a second or third tight end's included (`pick_concept`, `playbook::readable`, `readable_library`, `Personnel::for_players`; 13 and 22 personnel have no flanker for the tunnel screen, while the CFL's and arena football's sets for them do). If none of them has a weight, he draws among them all.
- Personnel (`Groups::of`), with short = (3rd/4th and 2 or less) or at the 3 or closer, and tell = clamp(0.33 - 0.013 × creativity, 0.07, 0.33): hurry-up 11 (70%, NFL 95%) or 10; short yardage 12: 3, 22: 2, 13: 1.5, 21: 1.5; otherwise the scheme's weights below, the head coach's or your word's group leaned on (the guidelines, below), then each weight × h^heavy, h = max(1 + 2 × tell on runs and throws off a run fake, or 1 - 2 × tell on straight dropbacks, 0.2), heavy 10: -1, 11: 0, 12 and 21: 1.25, 13: 1.75, 22: 2.75 (`Personnel::heavy_fit`). The fit is the fielded group's: each weight goes to the group the roster can field first (`available`), so a lean to 13 with two healthy tight ends fits like 12. So the NFL's typical caller (creativity about 14, tell 0.15) brings 12 and 21 out on straight dropbacks 0.47 times as often, for their share of the runs and play action, as 11, 13 0.34 times and 22 0.19; real 2024 NFL on first and ten (screens, RPOs and sneaks left out; nflverse with FTN charting): 12 0.46, 21 0.47, 13 0.34 and 22 0.20. FBS's typical caller (13): 0.43, 0.31 and 0.16; one at creativity 10 (tell 0.20): 0.35, 0.23 and 0.10. Sized on first and ten, where the down doesn't pick the group: over all normal downs real teams keep their light groups on the field for passing downs' runs too (12 0.31, 21 0.22, 13 0.19, 22 0.09), and the call's group weights don't read the down (minor bug 418; lane SF2; Claude's sizes, proposed, not confirmed). The wing-T plays from its own sets whatever the call.

- Passes (`pick_concept`), short = 3 or less to go, red zone at the 20 or closer, goal at the 8 or closer. Depth: screens × 1.2 long, else 1.6; quick × 2.2 short, 0.7 long, else 1.5; medium × 1.4 long, else 0.9; deep × 1.1 long, 0.4 short, else 0.6.

The PA boot from the shotgun × 0.5 (it was play action × 1.4 on 1st down or short and × 0.5 otherwise, and × 0.3 in the hurry-up, before the fake had its own rate). From empty (a straight drop, since a fake never comes from empty): 6- or 7-man protection × 0.05, a back's route × 0.3.
- Scheme favorites × 2.2: air raid mesh, four verticals, stick, Y-cross, hitches, shallow cross, spacing; West Coast slant-flat, curl-flat, stick, RB screen, drive, spacing, Texas; wide zone PA boot, PA shot, PA dig, flood, drive, tunnel screen; power gap PA shot, curl-flat, dagger, smash, mills, Y-option; spread bubble screen, hitches, four verticals, RB screen, snag, levels; wing-T PA boot, PA shot, flood.

On 3rd and 4th, not short: × (1 + 0.8s) if one of the first two reads breaks at or past the sticks, else × (1 - 0.48s), s = clamp((situational - 4) / 14, 0, 1), reading the reads and routes as his group lines up and the rule book fields it, so a second tight end in the slot's place runs his own flat there (lane SF2; `sticks_depth`, `reads_in`, `route_in`; the memo keeps these weights by personnel on those downs and, for a group whose extra tight ends take missing receivers' places (12, 13 and 22), by its set, `route_set`). Deep, after 3+ deep throws today: × clamp(1 + deep EPA a throw × 0.4 × adaptation / 20, 0.5, 1.6).
- Aggressiveness: deep × (1 + 0.03 (agg - 10)), others × (1 - 0.009 (agg - 10)). Easing: deep and the PA shot × (1 - 0.3 × ease). The week's plan: non-favorites × clamp(playbook × plan share / 0.75, 0.2, 1.4) (`INSTALL`). Every weight is at least 0.01, but the RPO halves are never called alone, and on a straight drop the three play-action concepts (and any play-action design) weigh 0.
- Kill call: on 1st and 2nd down outside the hurry-up and short yardage, a second play goes in the huddle: a pass with a run or RPO (curl-flat at 8+ to go, else slant-flat), inside zone with a pass. The why line is the coordinator's reason, for example "they keep two safeties deep, so the box is light" on a run when over 55% of the coverages seen were two-high (`call_why`).

### Play action (scheme-fit item 3)

- Any dropback concept but the RPO halves and the three play-action concepts (which always fake) can carry a run fake, screens too (Cain, 2026-11-17: "fake on any dropback play"; `OffCall::fake`, `playbook::takes_fake`). The call's name says it: "play-action flood".
- When: decided with the throw, before the look, so a fake draws its personnel, backfield and set as a run does and a straight drop as a pass does (real NFL 2024: 53% of play-action dropbacks under center, as 51% of runs; 4% of straight drops). No fake from an empty set (no back to fake to): the straight drops carry the empty sets, so a dropback is in empty as often as before (`call_offense_memo`, `pick_set`). The self-scout's flip to a pass draws the fake again.
- How often (`fake_chance`): on the odds, the league's real habit for the down and distance, the score and clock, and the field (`tables::real_play_action_rate`, `data/real-world/nfl_play_action_rate.csv`: FTN charting with nflverse play-by-play, 2022-2025, 21.3% of dropbacks; first and ten in a tied game, outside the fourth quarter's last 7:00 and a half's last 2:00, more than 20 yards out 47.5% (48.3% behind by 1-7, 48.8% ahead by 1-7), third and 6 there 2.2%) × the scheme (wide zone 1.75, wing-T 1.6, power gap 1.35, West Coast 1.0, spread option 0.7, air raid 0.55; `fake_lean`) × (1 + your word to a player promised play action, `PA_LEAN`) × e^(trust(reading) × gain). College and high school read the NFL's habit: no college charting on file.
- gain (`fake_gain`): what the fake adds against the looks this caller expects, beyond what it adds on average, the mean over the concepts that can carry it of (its faked value - its straight value - the table's average fake gain for it), with the same look read as the concept weights (sharp = (reading + adaptation) / 40). A sharp caller fakes more against the looks the fake beats and less against the ones it doesn't.
- The concept: off a fake he draws among every concept but the RPO halves, valued on the table's faked rows ("play-action <name>", each shifted by its straight row's offset rather than centered on its own mean, so the fake's own gain or cost on each concept counts; the boot, the shot and the dig have no straight row and fake on every snap, so each is shifted by the mean of the straight concepts of its own depth instead, which keeps what the fake adds to them: minor bug 410, they were one of 27 each, about 2% of play action); on a straight drop among the rest (`concept_weights`, `fake_values`). The why line for his own throw off a fake: "they've been biting on the run"; a faked screen when the blitz rate he has seen, (blitzes + 1) / (snaps seen + 4), is over 0.3 keeps "they're blitzing, so a screen behind it", and a throw from the self-scout's flip keeps "breaking our run tendency from this look" (`call_why`).
- A sheet's throw: below, under "Playbooks, the install and your game plan" (`sheet::faked`, `sheet_fake_chance`). A run checked to a pass at the line is a straight drop; a throw checked to a run drops the fake.

- Then, with a = adaptation / 20. Two-high (Cover 2, Tampa 2, Cover 4, Cover 6, 2-man): × 1.5 long, × 0.55 at 2 or less to go; × (1 + a × (3 × deep hurt + 1.5 × (their pass share - 0.55)₊)); × (1 - min(2a × run hurt, 0.5)); × max(1 - 2 × lean_run, 0.2); × 1.6 against the hurry-up. Single-high and Cover 0: × (1 + 2.5a × run hurt); × max(1 + 2 × lean_run, 0.2).
- His safeties (safety A): two-high × e^(-0.15 × h), single-high × e^(0.15 × h), h the mean hitter read of his named safeties (weight over the level's typical safety in tens of pounds plus his hitting ratings' gap over his range ones in twenties; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `hitter`) less the level's typical FS and SS pair's (`game/synthetic.rs`, `typical_hitter`; `game/flow.rs`, `safety_hitter`), so a typical pair calls as before and a heavy pair rotates one down more often.
- Cover 0: × 2.5 at 2 or less to go or at the 10 or closer, × 0.3 long. Goal-line personnel: zones × 0.3. Leading (his ease): Cover 0 × (1 - ease), man × (1 - 0.5 × ease), two-high × (1 + ease).
- Deep hurt = max(their deep EPA / their plays today, 0) once they have more than 6 plays today; prep snaps are left out (`Memory::opp_game_plays`). Run hurt = max(their run EPA a run, 0) after more than 5 runs today. lean_run = belief × sure × (reading + adaptation) / 40, belief being the look's run share minus their usual one (`Memory::belief`).
- Blitz chance = clamp(0.24 + 0.012 × (aggressiveness - 10) - 0.2 × ease + heavy (0.02 a 10 lb of his named linebackers' and corners' mean weight over the level's typical men, capped ±0.06; `game/flow.rs`, `blitz_lean`, linebacker L1 and corner C1) + 0.1 on 3rd and 4-9 - 0.1 against the hurry-up + 0.5a × max(0.3 - quick share, 0) - back-off, 0.05, 0.7), a = sharp. Quick share = (their throws under 2.4 s today + 1.2) / (their plays today + 4): a four-play prior at 0.3, so it reads nothing on the first snaps (`call_defense`). Layered (lane 4, B3): an offense that holds the ball draws more blitzes, a quick-throwing one fewer, by back-off = 0.5a × max(quick share - 0.3, 0) × clamp((19 - aggressiveness) / 10, 0.1, 1): an aggressive coordinator (a Brian Flores at 18 or 19) barely backs off, a mild one fully. Cover 0 always sends the double-A.
- Pressure: LB blitz 4, DB blitz 2, double-A 1, simulated 2 (DC skill above 9) or 0.5, fire zone 1 + 0.1 × skill behind single-high zones only; in dime a double-A becomes a DB blitz. A snap that didn't blitz behind a zone becomes simulated pressure with back-off × 1.5 (skill above 9) or × 0.6 (B3). Disguise chance = clamp(0.05 + 0.025 × skill + 1.5 × back-off, 0.05, 0.7) × (1 - ease), so the backed-off snaps hide more and a quick passer still has to read zone from a disguised blitz.
- Burner (lane 4, S10): an offense with a raw burner on its depth chart (`roster.rs`, `burner`, below) runs jet motion × (1 + lean) and deep concepts × (1 + 0.3 × lean) (`pick_motion`, `pick_concept`; `OffCtx::burner`). The defense's plan reads how fast their receivers play deep (0.4 Acceleration + 0.6 Speed, `roster::deep_speed`; the top 3 receivers and their burner) through half the film error, against its top two corners: fear = clamp((fastest - corners - 2) / 8, 0, 1) (`make_plan`, `Plan::fear`). Fear adds two-high shells × (1 + 0.8 × fear) (`call_defense`), and the bracket goes to their best receiver by film read + 0.6 × his deep-speed edge over the corners, so a fast number two can draw it over a better number one.
- Stunts, on four-man and simulated rushes: clamp(0.12 + 0.008 × (aggressiveness - 10) + 0.08 long - 0.2 × lean_run, 0.03, 0.35); tackle-end 2, end-tackle 1.5, twist 1.2. - Fronts (`pick_front`): 4-3 over 4, under 3, wide 3 on 2nd+ and 7+ (else 1), bear 1.5 against 12, 21, 22 or 13 personnel or at 2 or less to go (else 0.3); 3-4 odd 4, tite 3, under 1, bear the same. lean_run above 0: bear and tite × (1 + 3 × lean_run). Today's best inside run (inside zone, duo, power, trap, iso; 3+ tries) boosts tite and bear × (1 + 4a × its EPA a carry); the best outside run (outside zone, toss, jet sweep, buck sweep) does the same for wide and over.
- A 3-4 staff's line plays two-gap on the snap (`game/flow.rs`, `run_scrimmage_call`, `two_gap`). What the call does not read: the defensive caller's situational, creativity and nerve; weather (the sheet does, below); the offense's players beyond the QB's run threat.

## Before the snap (`game/flow.rs`, `run_scrimmage_call`)

- Against a no-huddle offense the defense can't substitute (`frozen`). The box: linemen and linebackers, plus the strong safety unless the coverage is two-high. Blockers: 5 + tight ends and fullback. numbers = box - blockers.
- freedom = clamp(0.85 - 0.035 × strictness + 0.04 × min(experience, 5), 0.1, 0.95). The QB's iq = 0.5 Play Recognition + 0.5 Football IQ.
- A disguised single-high coverage fools him unless he wins clamp(0.5 + 0.02 × (iq - the DBs' mean Disguise), 0.1, 0.9). On the road, noise = 1 - 0.5 × crowd, crowd = the home crowd × the road offense's `prep.noise` (1 for AI teams, 0.9-1.15 from your week's crowd-noise days) (`home_crowd`; `training/seat.rs`, `week_habits`); sees = clamp((iq - 40) / 50, 0.05, 0.95) × noise.
- Audible, outside the hurry-up and kneels, with chance sees: a run into numbers of 2+ with more than 2 to go becomes a pass; a non-screen pass against numbers of -1 or less, with the shell showing two-high (two-high or disguised), not fooled and 6 or less to go, becomes a run.
- He takes the kill-call play if freedom is 0.35+. With freedom 0.7+ and no fitting kill call, he checks to the quick pass (slant-flat at 4 or less to go, else curl-flat) or inside zone (`quick_check`). Otherwise the call stands.
- pre_read = 0 if fooled, else clamp((iq - 45) / 50, 0, 1) × noise; across motion against man adds 0.35 × clamp(1 + 0.01 × (QB PR - the DBs' Disguise), 0.5, 1.5) unless the DC bumps. The QB's reads use it (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `quarterback`, `PRE_READ`).
- Passed to the snap (`scrimmage::Tactics`): the defense's key on the look and its film study; the offense's target corner and help; the defense's bracket; line chemistry; the called family's novelty; linemen allowed downfield (NFL 1, high school 2, college 3 yards); each side's plan load; double moves. - Fakes (engine gaps item 21; `game/flow.rs`, `fake_punt`, `staff_fake_punt`, `fake_field_goal`): a staff without your week's fake rule fakes a punt only when the return team shows the look and the staff reads it, 40-90 yards from goal, not protecting a lead in the last 5:00, once a game, with chance (on its own key) base × score × Q4 × boldness × 2.34 / 0.156 (`FAKE_LIFT`, `LOOK_READ`: 0.156 the engine's share of punts with the look read): base by yards to go 0.036 (2 or less), 0.011 (3-4), 0.008 (5-6), 0.005 (7-10), 0.0034 (11+); score behind 1.4, tied 0.57, ahead 1-8 1.04, ahead more 0.35; Q4 0.54; boldness clamp(aggressiveness / 10, 0.3, 2.0) (the real NFL rates by situation, 98 fakes in 10,520 punts, 2021-25). A fake field goal: fourth and 10 or less, never in a half's last 2:00, in overtime or on a kick that ties or wins late (Q4, 5:00 or less, down 0-3), once a game, with chance 0.0035 × boldness (`FAKE_FG`, proposed, not confirmed); the holder runs it or throws, half and half on its own key, from the kick's unit against the field goal block unit, who read it late (`fake_fg_play`, `Snap::fake_kick`). The defense, behind in Q4 under 3:00 and not conceded, calls timeout with chance clamp(0.4 + 0.03 GM, 0.4, 0.98). ## Garbage time (`game/garbage.rs`)

- At each drive start and each quarter (not in overtime) both head coaches read the game (`game/flow.rs`, `review`; `Coach::review`). z = lead / (10 × √(game left / game length) + 3.25) (`comeback_z`, `SWING`, `FLOOR`); the why line gives odds of 1 / (1 - Φ(|z|)) (`comeback_odds`).
- seen = z + bias + N(0, 0.015 × max(21 - GM, 0)), with bias = N(0, 0.03 × max(21 - GM, 0)) held for the whole game (`Coach::new`).

| Level | Reads from | Steps (z) | Top step | Ease from-full | Concede | Plays it out | FG-to-go base |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| High school | Q2 | 3.4, 3.9, 4.7 | 3 | 1.2-2.4 | 3.0 | 35% | 0.6 |
| College | Q3 | 4.3, 4.8, 5.7 | 3 | 1.2-2.4 | 3.4 | 45% | 0.55 |
| NFL | Q3 | 4.4, 5.4 | 2 | 1.4-2.8 | 5.2 | 60% | 0.25 |

- Leading, the steps and the ease line move by 0.15 × (10 - (sportsmanship + push)); push is -2 in a playoff race, -4 against a program that fired him (`habits`, `shift`). Behind, the steps move by +0.8 on the hot seat, +0.5 for a bowl push and his fight (4.5 if he plays it out, else N(0, 0.8)); the concede line moves by the hot seat and bowl push only (`steps`, `FIGHT_SD`, `PLAY_OUT_FIGHT`). The race, grudge, hot seat and bowl push exist only in college games (`world/season.rs`, `game_context`).
- A step comes back down only when the read falls 0.8 below its threshold, or the lead flips (`HOLD`). The lead back sits from 1.2 below the first step (`REST_BACK`). ease = clamp((|seen| - from) / (full - from), 0, 1) when leading; concede = behind and (a step taken, the concede line passed, or already conceded and within 0.8 of it).
- Who sits (`benched`): step 1 QB1, RB1, 3 WR, 2 LB, 3 CB, 2 S; step 2 TE1, 2 OT, 3 IOL, 2 EDGE, 2 DT; step 3 the second string. The first kicker and punter and the long snapper always stay.
- What reads it: the pass rate, deep shots, coverages, blitzes and disguise (`call_offense`, `pick_concept`, `call_defense`), the hurry-up (`Situation::hurry`), fourth downs (`fourth_down`; `game/flow.rs`, `garbage_fourth`), tries (`pat`), onside kicks (`kickoff`), tempo and timeouts (`after_snap_clock`) and kneels (`should_kneel`).

## The play designer (`game/designer.rs`)

- A design is a formation (motion included), each player's route, the reads in order, the protection, the drop and play action (`Design`).
- Legal (`legal`): 7 on the line (within 1 yard of the ball, the QB excluded), 4 backs at most, no route for a man covered up between the end men, every read runs a route, every route's man is in the personnel. A lineman (50-79) may report eligible and run a route only in the NFL; in college and high school his number keeps him ineligible (minor bug 236, B52: this page used to say the level was ignored).
- On saving, your offensive play caller reviews it and sees each flaw with chance clamp(reading / 20, 0.05, 0.95): two routes ending within 3 yards of each other; no route ending within 6 yards with fewer than 7 blockers, unless a screen; one route, unless a screen; no reads. With none seen he says "Looks good to me." (`review`; `crates/afm-game/src/lane_training.rs`, `design_save`).
- It becomes a concept: a screen or bubble drop is a screen; a deepest route of 7 yards or less is quick, 16 or less medium, else deep. It is numbered after the library (`to_concept`, `save`; `game/playbook.rs`, `register_concept`).
- Practice read: 2 snaps for each day with a team period, from the 35, against the team's own scout looks in turn (base Cover 3; nickel Cover 1 with an LB blitz; nickel Cover 4; base Cover 2; nickel Cover 3 match with simulated pressure). Average yards and completions add up over the weeks (`training/practice.rs`, `design_reads`; `practice`).
- A design's snaps go on film as the library concept they look most like: the same route by the same man 2, the same route by anyone 1, the same play action 2, drop 1, depth 1, protection 0.5. The concept tally, the opponent's concept memory and the family shares count it there (`game/playbook.rs`, `film_concept`; `game/calling.rs`, `Film::offense`; `game/flow.rs`, `resolve`).
- Legality reads the level: at every level seven on the line, four backs at most and a covered man ineligible; a lineman may run a route only in the NFL, from the end of the line or the backfield (he reports), never in college or high school (`game/designer.rs`, `legal`).
- Code and design doc differ: the doc (`docs/design/plays-and-tactics.md`, topic 8) also has a defense designer, motion and downfield rules and AI coaches inventing plays; the code has none of these. Opponents learn a design from film only as the library concept it looks like (`film_concept`).

## Ideas spreading (`world/trends.rs`)

- At college fall camp, before the film fades, each division's season goes on the trend lines: its family shares and pass rate; a division with under 100 offensive snaps of film is skipped (`record`; `world/college.rs`, `start`).
- Division novelty per family = clamp((share now - share 4+ seasons back) / max(share now, 0.02), 0, 1) × 0.8; 0 until a trend line 4 seasons back exists (`novelty`, `RISE_YEARS`).
- A team adds clamp((mine - division) / mine, 0, 1) × clamp(mine / 0.25, 0, 1) × 0.5 for each family it runs on more than 4% of snaps (fronts excluded), once it has 40+ snaps of film; capped at 1 (`team_novelty`). Only college teams get it (`world/season.rs`, `game_team`); NFL and high school teams keep 0 (`world/pro/games.rs`, `build_side`; `world/hs_year.rs`, `squad_team`).
- The snap reads only the called offensive family's novelty: defenders read 0.12 × novelty s later and misfit run fits 0.05 × novelty more often (`game/flow.rs`, `run_scrimmage_call`; `game/scrimmage.rs`, `run_snap`). Defense-family novelty is set but never read.
