## Quick answers

- Does a head coach's home visit matter? Yes: in the winter contact periods AI head coaches visit their commits and near recruits they offered, and the pitch adds -0.012 to +0.03 to that school's pull in the feel and on signing day; yours is a talk with whoever decides in his family (`world/folk/dialogue.rs`, `home_lean`, `ai_home_visit`; `world/recruiting.rs`, `recruiting_day`, `appeal`). See [people.md](people.md), "Talks on game day and off it".
- How many stars go out a class? On average, of a 250,000 class (times world scale): 46 five-stars and 301 four-stars (ESPN's 2013-2026 means, the counts the world's checks read; minor bug 269: the 247 Composite's 33 and 350 put five-stars far under), 2,800 three-stars, 3,236 two-stars; everyone else 0 ("unranked") (recruiting, `SERVICE_TIERS`). Each class's counts move with the services' standard (minor bug 102): a slow walk keyed by the world and the class (`STANDARD_CARRY` 0.8 of last class's lean carries over), each tier spread lognormally by its real class-to-class spread over the mean (ESPN 2013-2026: five-stars 0.26, four-stars 0.20; three-stars 0.10, proposed, not confirmed), the two-stars filling the rated pool to its usual size (`service_tiers`, `STANDARD_SPREAD`; used by `run_signing_stage`, `rate_underclass`, `service_stars`). The standalone class sim keeps 2,000 and 4,000 (`career.rs`, `PIPELINE.star_shares`).
- What do the services rank on? 0.25 × film + 0.75 × their upside projection, minus 12 × (1 - seen)², plus a small physical-gift term, plus 6 film-SD × his offer sheet; seen is the larger of his visibility and his offer sheet (recruiting, `star_score`, `OFFER_READ`).
- What is the offer sheet? 0-1: the school holding his scholarship commitment, or before he commits his best scholarship offer (early offers too), weighted by the level he reads in it (`recruit_level`: FBS 1, Group of 5 0.85, FCS 0.55, JUCO 0.35, D2 0.3, NAIA 0.25, D3 0.2) × (0.5 + 0.5 × the program's prestige at its level) (recruiting, `offer_sheet`). An SEC offer moves a kid about a tier; an FCS-only list keeps him below the FBS signees; a Group of 5 commitment reads as that school, whatever else offered.
- Do stars read true ability? Partly. Film is his season's play (`last_performance`, else overall); while his state's high school season is being played it is last season's, giving way to this one by the share of the regular season played (minor bug 20: the season's row is written before its games; `film_raw`, `hs_played`). The projection is a 12-accuracy scouting read of potential plus a lasting service bias (`film_raw`, `service_bias`; `scouting.rs`, `scout_player`).
- Do the services see a physical freak? Late and weakly: 0.15 film-SD per full Dynamic Link, times visibility. Staffs see it at 0.5 SD (`SERVICE_GIFT`, `STAFF_GIFT`). His speed and burst in those links come from his recorded times and his film, never his hidden ceilings (lane 4, S6f: `world/times.rs`, `seen`, `seen_links`; the other parts still read 0.96 × his ceilings, a doubt).
- Does timed speed earn stars? Yes (S5): the fastest 1 in 100 at his position in his class on the recorded burst-and-speed mix (`body::play_speed`, so edge rushers, tackles and linebackers read the split more, S2a) gets at least 3 stars, 1 in 1,000 at least 4; each tier's lowest-ranked kids without a floor step down so the quotas hold (`times::speed_floors`, `service_stars_floored`). Only an electronic time (laser or a fully timed track meet) qualifies.
- Do burners get offers? A desperate school (S7, S7a) adds 1.0 film-SD for a kid on the S5 list in its state or footprint (1.5 with two or more signs), and sees him off the track lists whatever his visibility. Signs: a position the class plan still needs after December's signing day, a head coach at a third of the pressure or more, and being a Group of 5 or FCS school, whose good players keep moving up (proposed, not confirmed: by level, not each school's own losses; `BURNER_EVAL`, `HOT_SEAT`).
- Does exposure matter? Yes. Visibility (0-1) cuts his star score and decides which staffs see him at all (`run_signing_stage`, `visibility`, `see`).
- Are kickers rated by the services? Capped at 3 stars. Their real stars come from the kicking and snapping camps (`SERVICE_SPECIALIST_MAX`, `camp_rankings`, `recruiting_stars`).
- How does a recruit pick a school? He holds the best offer by a utility of level, brand, money, closeness, playing time, academics, development and ties, plus room at his position (`utility_fit`, `RosterView::fit`, the deferred-acceptance loop in `run_signing_stage`).
- Whose wants decide? His decider's (`family.decider_wants`): himself, a parent, grandparents or a handler. In the portal he uses his own wants (`portal_day`, `own_wants`).
- Can I call, visit and promise a transfer? Yes, as a recruit: a call a day, a campus visit and a home visit, a promise of a starting job or a role, and he says yes on the spot when your offer clears his own bar; every AI staff calls him and his finalists host him by the same code. See "Portal recruiting and the satisfaction bar" below.
- Does a recruit wait for his wave when your offer is enough? No: a recruit of the next class who holds your offer commits on the spot once you lead his race by his own bar (`front_office/college.rs`, `board_check`).
- How far does distance matter? Closeness = 1 / (1 + (miles/300)²): 1 at home, 0.5 at 300 miles, 0.12 at 800 (`closeness`).
- Does the head coach's recruiting rating matter? Yes, in the relationship a staff builds: 0.006 × (HC skill - 10) × time, HC skill = 0.6 recruiting + 0.4 relationships; the position coach's recruiting at 0.003 (`run_signing_stage`, `relationships`).
- Does the coach's network matter? Yes, for exposure: a high school coach's network / 20 is 0.2 of a recruit's visibility (`run_signing_stage`, `network`).
- When do recruits commit? Each gets a timing from personality and family: one of five summer/fall waves (Mar 20, Apr 30, Jun 15, Aug 31, Oct 31), the early period, or February (`commit_timing`; `world/clock.rs`, `commit_wave`).
- What's a decommit? A commitment that loses its place or is beaten at a signing stage, or a fired coach's commit reopening: 45% of 5-stars, 35% 4, 25% 3, 15% others, times loyalty and closeness (`decommit_after_firing`).
- What decides a portal entry? A yearly "lean" built from role, how buried he is and dated events, then a uniform draw against lean × era factor (`world/college.rs`, `lean_checkpoint`; recruiting, `portal_open`).
- How is NIL paid? On July 1, each collective's budget × 0.6 is split over the roster: starters take most of it, by their position's going rate, their stars and how they played against the level's starters; a newcomer is paid on his stars. Early enrollees are paid from January and transfers from the day they land (`nil_payouts`, `nil_value`, `nil_weight`; minor bug 100). Each player's share is his weight over the roster's: role last season (starter 1, rotation 0.25, else 0.02; with no college season yet 0.2 × star factor) × his position's going rate / a receiver's (interior line 0.58 of it, defensive tackles 0.73; minor bug 335) × star factor^0.2 × e^(0.03 × his play over the level's starter median at his spot, ±15 points). That split is the going rate. Your own program pays your deals instead, and every player of yours has one (below, "Every NIL dollar is yours").
- What NIL does a recruit get offered? Position market rate × a star factor × (budget / $10M)^0.8, capped by the collective. A school pays within the going rate's top for his position (`world/ncaa.rs`, `position_nil_max`) unless it's buying: a reach program whose NIL money ranks at least 10 places above its prestige in its division (`BUY_RANKS`) pays a 4- or 5-star near the top of its board (rank < 0.3) past it, up to 6% of its money (`BUY_SHARE`) (`offer_for`, `buying`, `position_nil`; minor bug 61). The established powers never buy. No recruit has a ceiling of his own, as in real life.
- How much NIL can you still promise? The budget less your deals for players still on your roster, less the offers out to recruits and portal targets and the signees' numbers until they arrive: the deals, NIL and portal offers check it, and the NIL board shows it (`world/front_office/college.rs`, `budget`). In two pots each pot has its own: the revenue share (`left`) and the collective (`coll_left`, your collective deals as written).
- What grades does he need? Division I 2.3 core GPA to qualify, 2.0-2.299 academic redshirt, under 2.0 can't enroll; Division II 2.2; JUCO and Division III anyone; NAIA 2.0, below it an academic redshirt (proposed, not confirmed). Warnings say "academic line" (`ncaa_tier`; minor bug 192, B8).
- When do the services first rank a class? From its sophomore year: four updates a year (21 Jan, 12 May, 14 Jul, 20 Oct) for the classes behind the one the signing stages rate, which they take over at 20 March of its junior year (`world/scouting/services.rs`, `update_days`, `underclasses`; recruiting, `rate_underclass`).
- Is there a composite? From 2012, a 0.70-1.00 rating read off each class's national order: 5 stars from .9834, 4 from .89, 3 from .797, 2 from .70 (`world/scouting/services.rs`, `composite`, `BANDS`, `COMPOSITE_FROM`).
- Who predicts where a recruit goes? Each service's insider, from 2011, every Tuesday (`world/scouting/services.rs`, `insiders_week`).
- Do walk-ons exist? Yes: planned preferred walk-ons, local walk-on offers in February and on the late signing days, open tryouts, and fall-camp scholarships for walk-ons who played their way into the two-deep here; at your program the tryout and the scholarships are your calls (`class_plan`, `run_signing_stage`, `camp_walk_ons`; `world/front_office/walk_ons.rs`).
- Is there a deadline to pick a school in the portal? No (minor bug 337, real since 2025): an entrant who finds no school keeps looking, a week at a time, until fall camp (see "Spring and summer roster moves").
- Can a recruit still sign after February's signing day? Yes: an unsigned senior or last-year junior college player whom some staff had on its board or offered signs on the late signing days, every Wednesday until summer enrollment (see "Spring and summer roster moves").
- Can a college coach cut a player? Yes, your own, from his player page: he is off the roster and out of your games that day and enters the next transfer window (see Cuts) (`world/front_office/college.rs`, `cut`).
- What does a hunted recruit get? The hunter puts him first on its board, holds his scholarship (the others at his spot share one fewer; he still fits as a star would), gives him its head coach's time 1.8 and, an AI program, bids at least 6% of its collective's budget; a hunting program's other targets get 0.12 less of its head coach's time. A school's hunt pulls a transfer 1.8 times as hard. All of it in [hunt.md](hunt.md) (`world/recruiting.rs`, `run_signing_stage`, `high_school_transfers`).

## High schools and being seen

- Each state has its real top programs plus generated schools in real towns. Prestige 0-1; a powerhouse is prestige ≥ 0.75. Real schools start at 0.8 + 0.04 per rank (top five) + 0.08 if national (`world/high_school.rs`, `generate_state_schools`, `HighSchool::is_powerhouse`).
- Eight national powerhouses (IMG Academy, Mater Dei, St. John Bosco, Bishop Gorman, St. Frances, Duncanville, Buford, St. Thomas Aquinas) pull from out of state (`NATIONAL_POWERHOUSES`).
- IMG Academy is a boarding school with no neighbourhood (minor bug 126): no new 14-year-old is placed there by where he lives (`World::add_class_aged` takes the other school drawn). Its staff fill 25 places a class (`BOARDING_CLASS`, proposed, not confirmed): the freshmen from the new class on high school class day (`boarding_freshmen`, from `hs_year`), and the open places at ages 15-17 each June before any other transfer. The board is every state's standouts of that age (top 3%) not already at a powerhouse, read with 2 points of noise, best first. Each offer carries aid; the family decides on 0.3 × prestige gain, the coach's name, ×1.4 with NIL at home or there, × (0.5 + 0.5 × family money), × (1 − close-to-home × miles / 800, at least 0.3; `BOARDING_MILES`, proposed, not confirmed), the local coach, home stability, a crowded depth chart, competitiveness and ×1.8 if IMG hunts him, at most 40%, on keyed draws. A boarder keeps his home state and doesn't sit out (`recruiting`, `boarding_intake`, `boarding`).
- High school transfers (yearly, `hs_transfer_day`): standouts are the top 3% of their state's age 15-17. Pull = 0.3 × prestige gain, ×1.4 in an NIL state, cut to 0.35× by a strong local coach (relationships ≥ 13 and reputation ≥ 11), cut by home stability, private-school cost, a crowded depth chart (×0.5), lifted by competitiveness. Capped at 40%. The other national powerhouses take a state's very best (within 2 points of its top) 15% of the time; any move to another state, a hunter's too, moves the family and he plays at once. The state's rule sets how much of an in-state move he sits (`recruiting`, `high_school_transfers`; `world/high_school.rs`, `transfer_rule`).
- Homesick boarders go home (up to 30% a year); buried powerhouse backups at 16-17 transfer out 12% of the time (a buried IMG boarder goes back to his home state); 0.4% reclassify up, 0.5% repeat a year (`high_school_transfers`).
- Visibility, 0-1, recomputed at every signing stage (`run_signing_stage`):
  `0.05 + 0.3 × school exposure + 0.2 × coach network/20 + 0.15 × camps + 0.25 × tape + traffic + viral + bloodline + honors + your promos`.
  - School exposure: his school's prestige (prep school 0.6, JUCO mover 0.7).
  - Coach network: his high school coach's (JUCO: the college head coach's); 0.4 with none.
  - Camps: 0.3 + 0.35 × the camps and showcases he has tested at (two or more is full; lane 4, S6d: `times::tested_at`). Family money and distance decide who goes (`world/times.rs`). Tape: how far his film sits above the class mean, 0-1. Traffic: 0.1 × (state culture + 0.6).
  - Viral clip: +0.25, about 2% of kids (6% with a social-media parent); it gets a first look only.
  - Bloodline: NFL father +0.15, college +0.05. All-state honors: +0.04 each, up to 2.
  - Your calls as a high school coach (`world/scouting/hs.rs`, `promos`).
- What visibility does not read: his true potential, his body directly (the gift is separate), his grades.

## Star ratings and the services

- Film is aligned by position: each position's film is rescaled to the class-wide mean and SD, so every position's top looks the same (`run_signing_stage`, `pos_mean_sd`).
- The services' projection: a scouting read at accuracy 12, HS level, plus `service_bias` = 1.6 points per year older than the middle of his class (±1.5 years) + a lasting 6-point-SD misread keyed to the player (9 before lane 4, S9). Each new look moves halfway toward what they now see; a JUCO mover is a fresh read (`service_bias`, `SERVICE_MATURITY`, `SERVICE_MISREAD`). Only the services carry it, and the staffs' offers put much of it right through the offer sheet. Minor bug 101 (2026-10-31) tried a 7-point misread shared with every staff's film: five-stars drafted fell from 79% to 61% (real 63%), but staffs then placed boys less by what they are: FBS rosters lost 1.5 points of true talent, four-stars drafted fell from 22.5% to 17% (real 22%), FBS receivers went from 63% to 59% Black (real 64%) and more colleges had a pick, so it was taken back. Five-stars drafted (79%) and unrated draft picks (4% against a real 25%) stay open: they need late bloomers, not a worse read.
- Ratings happen at each summer wave and at the early period (or January enrollees before 2017); each rating replaces the last. February adds no new stars (`run_signing_stage`, `rate_now`). The younger classes are rated at the services' own four updates (next section).
- Specialists: K and P are ranked by the camps on their overall + 1.5 SD noise among those who attend (attendance 0.3 + 0.7 × money). Camp tiers by national rank: 5 stars top 25, 4 to 100, 3 to 250, 2 to 500. Long snappers: top 12/40/100/250. A school's best non-QB/K/P snapper with 0 service stars and LS build is found once a class and moved to LS (`camp_rankings`, `CAMP_TIERS`, `SNAP_TIERS`).
- Composite class rankings (signing days): 5★ = 99, 4★ = 92, 3★ = 85, 2★ = 78, else 70; a class scores Σ rating × exp(-n² / 200) down its signees, best first. Walk-ons and specialists don't count. Only FBS is headlined. The score is kept per program in `class_scores`, which athletic directors read (`class_rankings_news`).

## Recorded times (lane 4, speed update S6-S6f)

- Stored when he runs them, in `World::times` by player id: the event (forty, 10-yard split, 100 m, 200 m), how it was timed (laser, fully automatic, hand), seconds as printed, the date, where, and the weight the time is read against: his listed weight at a meet or his school, his measured one at a camp or showcase (`world/times.rs`, `Time`). He always runs on his real weight. A meet keeps his season's best by event and watch; the school lists one forty a year. A kid's times go when he leaves high school (July 1).
- The times, before noise: forty 5.56 - 0.0124 × (0.75 Speed + 0.25 Acceleration) + `forty_weight`; split 0.348 × the same at a quarter Speed; 100 m 2.5 × the forty at 0.9 Speed - 0.3; 200 m 5 × that - 0.35 (`base`). Noise: laser forty 0.05 s, split 0.03, fully timed 100 m 0.08, 200 m 0.15. Hand times run 0.24 s fast with 0.10 more spread (Mann et al., JSCR 2015) and print to a tenth on the track; a school's own forty another 0.05-0.15 s fast.
- Spring track (S6a): who runs is drawn once a spring: 45% of receivers, backs, corners and safeties at an average speed, 18% of quarterbacks, tight ends, linebackers and edge rushers, 4% of linemen, 5% of kickers, × clamp(0.5 + 0.035 × (0.9 Speed + 0.1 Acceleration - 62), 0.2, 1.6). Meets every Saturday from the second of March to the second of May; he runs 70% of them, a meet is fully timed three times in four (by school), the 100 m always and the 200 m 60% of the time. State meets the third Saturday of May: each state's 16 fastest 100 m runners, fully timed, 70% of them the 200 m too. All proposed, not confirmed.
- Camps and showcases (S6d, minor bug 26): the next three classes (a rising senior at full rate, a junior 0.8, a sophomore 0.3), not specialists. College camps every June day: a kid goes at (0.2 + 2 × visibility + 1 if offered + 0.3 × stars) × class rate × (0.3 + 0.7 × money) camps a June, at a school that offered him (60%) or an FBS or FCS school in his state; more than 400 miles needs money ≥ 0.5. Regional showcases on eight Sundays (Texas, Florida, Georgia, California, Ohio, North Carolina, Louisiana, Pennsylvania): rated, offered or a 100 m of 11.0 or better, within 500 miles, at 0.45 × class rate × (0.3 + 0.7 × money). National finals the fourth Sunday of June: the next class's top 150 on the services' read (85% go) and its 40 fastest timed (60%). Each tests a laser forty and split; your staff gets a Showcase look at the ones on your board. Your own camps are your staff's (`scouting/dept.rs`).
- School forties (S6c): August 20, hand-timed, for juniors and seniors who are rated, offered or at visibility 0.35 or more.
- The read (S6f, `seen`): his film's look at his Speed and Acceleration (6 points of noise, by year) as a prior, then every time in a weighted least squares fit of both, each moved to his age today on the typical speed curve (`development::speed_with_body` at an average bloom). Electronic times weigh in full, hand times 0.4, a school's forty 0.1. Grown: today's read × 0.96 / that curve's share. One shared read for every staff and service.

## The services before the signing stages

`world/scouting/services.rs` and recruiting, `rate_underclass`:

- Update days: 21 January (winter), 12 May (spring), 14 July (summer), 20 October (fall). Each ranks every class from the one after the signing stages' class down to this year's sophomores (`update_days`, `underclasses`, `underclass_update`).
- Who is looked at: the class's top 8% by film, plus every kid with stars, camp stars, all-state honors, an offer or a commitment; a class under 10 kids is skipped. Each is read the way a senior is (film and projection, each new look moving halfway), on keyed streams that leave the kids' own untouched (`rate_underclass`).
- How deep a list goes, as shares of a full class's 5/4/3/2-star quotas: a sophomore class 0.6/0.6/0.25/0, after its sophomore spring and summer 1.0/0.9/0.5/0.1, a junior class 1.0/1.0/0.7/0.25 (`reach`).
- Each class's national order (stars, then the services' read) is kept with the one before, for the rank movement, and each service's list with it (`record`, `ClassRanks`). An old save catches up once, on loading only, rated as of the latest update (`catch_up`); a page never rates.
- Each service's own list, made at each rating and kept until the next (`boards_of`; read by `service_boards`): the composite plus its lean minus the services' mean lean on the kid (so the composite is their average), the lean a lasting misread of SD 0.012 + 0.0015 × (20 − skill) plus a little of the update's own, and its bias, read from what a desk can see (minor bug 27): big programs +0.006 for a kid with 3+ power-conference offers or 15+ offers in all; viral +0.006 for a recorded laser forty of 4.45 or less or a fully automatic 100 m of 10.75 or less; size up to ±0.006 from his listed weight; a 10-game last season +0.004. Stars by the composite's bands on that rating (`stars_of`), a specialist 3 at most; 60-100% as deep into the 2-stars as the shared list (`lean`).
- Underclass kickers, punters and snappers: no services' stars; the kicking camps rank those they saw on the camps' tiers, as short as the services' list (`recruiting::underclass_camps`).
- Insiders: picks on the open 4- and 5-stars and on 3-stars with 6+ offers (no specialists), in the two classes signing next. The read is the kid's race (`world/front_office/college.rs`, `race_at`) plus the insider's misread of each school (SD 0.018 + 0.0025 × (20 − skill), mostly lasting). A first pick needs a 0.08 lead (0.025 within 75 days of his decision), a change 0.06; a pick on a school that fell out of his top three is moved like a first pick or taken back. Confidence 50 + 550 × the lead, capped 50-95 (`insiders_week`). Judged when he commits; six years of records (`judge`, `insider_record`).
- Top schools: announced within 90 days of his decision day when 6+ schools offer and he is a 4-star or has 10+: 10 schools (14+ offers), 8 (10+) or 5; cut to 5 or 3 in the last 30 days. The list is kept by program, never in his order (`announced`). A list whose leader is now off it, or more than half gone from his race, is dropped (`insiders_week`).
- His decision day: his commit timing's date (a summer wave, the early signing period's Wednesday from `calendar::anchors::early_signing`, or February's first Wednesday) (`decision_day`). Screens show it only as a read: nothing without a signal, then a window (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_find/race.rs`, `decides_read`).

## Offers before the waves

- Five dated offer passes per class (`early_offers`, `EARLY_OFFER_PASSES`; dates in `world/clock.rs`): Jan 15 of the sophomore year, Jun 15 camps, Sep 1 junior year, Jan 15 after the junior season, junior days + 14. Only the top 6% of a class on film is looked at, ranked 0.6 film + 0.4 service read.
- Offers held by rank r: floor(most × e^(-r / scale)): by the last pass 24 for the top prospect, 21 for the 100th, 7 for the 1,000th, 0 for the 3,000th (the code comment says about 8 and 1, before the floor). The first three passes carry a quarter of their offers through 2000, then about 0.067 more a year, all from 2012 (the comment says before 2005 and by 2015).
- Which programs offer: FBS and FCS only, scored -6 × (how far the program sits above his projected level) + 1.5 × footprint in his state + 0.5 × closeness + noise.
- The first school to offer adds +0.02 to the relationship, later early offers +0.01 (`run_signing_stage`, `early_offer`).
- Your own offer is an early one when it comes before his class's first commitment wave (March 20 of his junior year, `recruiting.rs`, `first_wave`, from `clock::COMMIT_WAVES`), as the staffs' last dated pass at junior days is; before Cain's NIL bug report (2026-11-06) only one before his class became the class signing next counted, so a junior offered after February's signing day was never remembered as the first, and his file's "His offers in order" said "No offers yet" beside "Your offer is his only one" (`world/front_office/college.rs`, `offer_at`). The file's list now shows every offer: the dated early ones in order, then the signing stages' ("Later") (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_frontoffice/college.rs`, `file`).

## How a staff builds its board

- Who gets looked at: the top 25% of the class by max(film, projection - 6), plus anyone committed or rated 2+ stars (`run_signing_stage`, `interest`).
- Who a staff sees (`see`): (reach + 1.2 × local) × visibility × 1.6 + bonus, where reach = max(its region in his state + 0.6 × its pipeline there, `FAR_READ`[stars] × footprint[state]) (minor bug 246: outside its region a staff reads unrated and 1-star kids at 0.1 of its footprint, 2-stars 0.3, 3-stars 0.7, blue chips 1; before, 29 of North Dakota State's 52 players from 500+ miles were unrated Californians, Texans and Arizonans), local = max(HS pipeline, 0.6 × area pipeline, 0.7 × staff ties to his school); bonus 1.0 for 4-5 stars, else 0.15 × stars × reach (minor bug 246: before, a flat 0.15 × stars put every 3-star in the country on about half of all FBS boards, and Iowa's filled from Georgia, Florida and Alabama). Below 1.0 it's a roll. A staff always sees its own commits and every 2+ star in its own state.
- Your own coach starts with the high school ties his road brings (update 3, lane 2): a high school coach about 30 schools in his state, a college assistant about 25 in his recruiting area, a playing road his own high school (`crates/afm-game/src/setup.rs`, `road_ties`; see [staff-and-coaches.md](staff-and-coaches.md), "Your coach at the start"). They count in `see` and the signing stage like any staffer's ties.
- Skipped: D2/NAIA/D3 don't board 4-5 stars, FCS doesn't board 5-stars; NCAA non-qualifiers; below the school's own admissions bar unless a special admit (qualifier, GPA trending up, work ethic and coachability reads, stable home or mentor, no trouble, and the school has special admits left).
- Evaluation (`eval`): 0.6 × (film + noise) + 0.4 × (projection + noise) + 0.3 × film-SD × system fit + 0.5 × film-SD × gift seen × eye factor + 0.5 × film-SD × each of the services' stars above two (not specialists; `STAR_EVAL`) − 4 × grade risk × (1 − academic support) × (1 − 0.6 × hot seat) + 5 film-SD for its own commits.
  - Noise SD = 3.5 − 0.15 × (0.6 × position coach eye + 0.4 × HC eye − 10), clamped 1-6. Eye = `Coach::evaluation` (`world/coach.rs`).
  - Grade risk = (2.6 − GPA)/0.6 clamped 0-1; academic support = 0.3 + 0.7 × facilities (`academic_support`).
- Boards are per position, up to 40 × (planned signees + walk-ons + 1) deep. A JUCO takes at most 15 out-of-state recruits (`JUCO_OUT_OF_STATE_OFFERS`).

## What a recruit weighs

Utility of a school (`utility_fit`), each grade on 0-1 (`world/program.rs`, `GradeCat`):

- Level: 1.6 × the level he reads × (0.5 + wants.prestige + wants.development). Level value: FBS 1.0, FCS 0.55, JUCO 0.35, D2 0.3, NAIA 0.25, D3 0.2 (`level_value`). He reads an FBS school outside the power conferences that year (the Pac-12 counts until 2023, the Big East until 2013, Notre Dame always, and before 1993 the major independents: Boston College, Florida State, Miami, Penn State, Pittsburgh, Rutgers, South Carolina, Syracuse, Temple, Virginia Tech, West Virginia; minor bug 311, that list proposed, not confirmed) as 0.85 of FBS (`recruit_level`, `GROUP_OF_FIVE_LEVEL`, `Program::power_conference`); the portal's choice reads it the same way.
- Brand: (0.25 + wants.prestige) × prestige_rel (capped at 0.85, so every program at or above 85% of the 1996 leader reads the same name; dynasty item 8, `NAME_TIE`) × (0.4 + 0.6 × the level he reads).
- Prestige grades: wants.prestige × mean(title chances, stadium, tradition, brand exposure). Facilities: 0.1 × grade.
- Money: wants.money × need × (1 − 0.35 × ambition drive) × 1.5 × (NIL / $1M)^0.6. Need = 0.25 + 0.75 × (1 − family money) (+0.2 after a family crisis).
- Ambitious families instead weigh the path to the pros: 0.35 × drive × wants.money on development and playing time.
- Closeness: (wants.close_to_home, +0.15 after a death or lost job) × closeness(miles) × his home weight.
- Home weight (minor bug 89, `home_weight`): 0.3 + 0.7 × e^(−offers / 4), offers = his offers (the early ones and the last stage's) from power-conference programs outside his home state; none 1, three 0.63, five 0.50, ten 0.36 (`HOME_FADE_FLOOR`, `HOME_FADE_OFFERS`). Sized so blue chips sign in their home state like real (41%, 5-stars 45%): 40-41% (5-stars 46-56%) on two 30-year worlds. It scales closeness, his stay-home lean and his childhood team; a transfer in the portal keeps 1. The reasons a recruit shows (`reason_parts`, `reason_weights`) use it too.
- Playing time grade × (0.6 + 0.4 × his standing on that board); academics grade × (0.5 + 0.5 × GPA factor); development = mean(pro pipeline, coach prestige).
- Small pulls, by the aid rule of the season he would arrive (`world/ncaa.rs`, `aid`; minor bug 297): no aid at all (the Pioneer League, MAAC football 1993-2007) −0.8 × (1 − 0.6 × money), need-based aid only (the Ivy League; the Colonial League from 1986 and the Patriot League through 2012, Fordham through 2009, Georgetown always) −0.4; HS pipeline +0.12 × strength; the program's week of stories (`story_push`); his own stay-home lean (0 for 20% of kids, else 0.04-0.40, the band drawn 0.8 by chance and 0.6 from his family: home stability and a parent or grandparent deciding, read against his own class; `world/mod.rs`, `draw_home_ties`) in his home state × his home weight; his childhood team +0.18 × his home weight; an older brother there +0.15 (or −0.05); a system that shows him off up to +0.06; 5-stars get +0.25 × brand³ × the level he reads, with the brand tied at 0.85 as above (`FIVE_STAR_PULL`).
- Room at his position (`RosterView::fit`): he judges himself at overall + 6, against players with at most 2 college seasons played (they'll still be there) who'll be 2 points better. Room = 1 − 0.5 × ahead/starters, or negative when buried. Weight (0.06 + 0.2 × wants.playing_time) × early weight (1 + 0.08 per star over 2) (`room_weight`). Plus the supporting cast his position leans on (a QB's receivers and line), weight 0.04 + 0.15 × wants.development.
- A quarterback also reads his path to the job (`recruit_fit`, `qb_rivals`; minor bug 130): the rivals in the room by pedigree (a five-star 1, a four-star 0.5, the starter at least 0.5), each times the share of the recruit's first three seasons he'll still be there, at most 2, gone if declared or in the portal; his fit falls by 1 × that × the room weight (`QB_PEDIGREE`, `QB_STARTER`, `QB_RIVALS_CAP`, `QB_PATH`, proposed, not confirmed). One quarterback plays: blue-chip quarterbacks had piled into rooms that already held young blue-chip quarterbacks (a stacking index of 1.24-1.36 at the 10 programs with the most blue chips, real 0.67; sportsdataverse cfb_recruits 2005-2025).
- Ties on top: the relationship the staff built, his own feel for the school (SD 0.07, `FEEL_SD`), and the pull of a commitment (`fixed_at`, `pull`).
- Walk-on places carry no money, cost tuition (0.8 × (1 − 0.6 × money), ×1.77 out of state; free where the league gives no athletic aid that season (`ncaa::aid`: the Pioneer League, MAAC football, the Ivy League, the Colonial and Patriot Leagues before their scholarships, Georgetown); Division III walk-ons still pay it) and, against a real scholarship, 1.4 × level value of what he turns down (specialists exempt) (`walk_on_cost`, `scholarship_given_up`).
- What it does not read: coach ratings directly (only through coach prestige grade, relationships and pipelines), the program's true roster strength beyond the units, his own true potential.

Who decides (`world/background.rs`, `generate_family`): himself 45%, a parent (or grandparents) 45%, a handler 6-12%. A parent adds money, closeness and academics to the boy's wants; grandparents closeness and academics; a handler money. A signing note says "Linfield was more a parent's choice than his own" (the school named, minor bug 116) when his own wants would have picked another offer by 0.06 (`run_signing_stage`, `steered`). Steered players get a one-time 45% portal chance after year one (`portal_open`); one who lands somewhere ("free at last to choose for himself") decides for himself from then on: his decider becomes himself and the decider's wants his own (`portal_day`, minor bug 116: a later JUCO move up read as the family's choice again).

## Relationships and visits

- Each staff recruits 3 per planned spot + 3 at each position (`RECRUITED_PER_SPOT`, `RECRUITED_EXTRA`). The head coach's own targets are the top of each spot.
- Relationship = known × (0.025 × HC time + 0.006 × (HC skill − 10) × HC time + 0.003 × (PC recruiting − 10) × PC time) + 0.03 × staff tie to his school + 0.02 if committed there + early-offer bonus + 0.05 if his high school head coach of the last year is on the staff (C17) + 0.004 × contacts + chemistry (SD 0.03). Known = 0.35 + 0.65 × visibility. Home-state effort 1.4, out of state 0.85. Visited (official, any contact, or within 250 miles) gives full time (`run_signing_stage`, `relationships`).
- Official visits: five max to June 30, 2023; since July 1, 2023 no total, one a school, though he still takes about five (`OFFICIAL_VISITS`, `official_cap`; minor bug 185, B1). An FBS school hosts at most 56 official visitors a cycle (Aug 1-Jul 31; Apr 1-Mar 31 from April 2020), 70 from April 2023, no limit from April 2024; FCS has no school limit (proposed, not confirmed) (`school_official_cap`, `officials_hosted`; your visit request says so; minor bug 186, B2). He takes them to the five schools he likes best, or the ones he really visited on their dates. A Summer(0) committer took none; Summer(1-3) took none before 2018 (junior officials), Summer(1) two after (`officials`).
- Dated visits (`recruiting_day`): official visits on Fridays outside dead periods, for the next class from Sep 1 (juniors Apr 1 - Jun 30 from 2018); fall officials go to a program at home that Saturday; 12% a weekend. Game-day unofficials within 250 miles 25%; drop-ins in evaluation weeks 20% (40% for his commit); junior days on Saturdays Feb-Mar to early offerers within 500 miles, 30%.
- Periods (`calendar/mod.rs`, `recruiting_period`): Feb-Mar quiet, mid-April contact, late May dead, late June-July dead, Sep-Nov evaluation, late Nov - mid Dec contact, dead around each signing day, January contact.

## Signing: the stages

- Stages (`SignStage`, dates `world/clock.rs`): five commitment waves Summer(0-4) (the enum's comment says 0-3; the code has five) on Mar 20, Apr 30, Jun 15, Aug 31, Oct 31; Early (early signing day; third Wednesday of December 2017-23, first Wednesday from 2024); JanuaryEnrollees (before 2017 only); Final (first Wednesday of February).
- Each stage runs the same matching: programs offer down their boards in rounds; each recruit holds the best offer and trades up; an offer at a full position is made only for a recruit about (1 − 0.75 × prestige_rel) film-SD better than the best left at an open spot, at most 2 over the plan; a D-I staff always finds 2 extra places for a 5-star (`next_offer`, `OVER_PLAN`, `OVER_CLASS`).
- Bidding: a school near the top of its board (rank < 0.3) can raise its NIL offer 1.5× to beat a held offer, within its cap and the going rate's top, or for a buying school and a 4- or 5-star, 6% of its money (`run_signing_stage`, bidding block).
- Who decides at each stage: only recruits whose `commit_timing` has come. Commitment timing z = 0.8 × noise + 0.25 decides himself − 0.3 social-media parent − 0.3 handler + 0.03 × (love of game − 10) + 0.15 older brother + 4★ 0.15 / ≤2★ −0.2; cut points 1.34, 0.81, −0.11, −0.35, −0.57, −1.1 (`commit_timing`). A summer commitment is announced on a day of its own before the next wave (`announce_commitments`).
- A commitment holds +10 utility through the summer waves; at signing stages it holds by stickiness 0.18 × loyalty (halved when the head coach changed) (`pull`, `stickiness`).
- Waits for February (`waits_for_february`): 50% with a new head coach, 60% borderline students, 40% of 5-stars with a show-minded family, 3% others.
- January enrollees: era 3% to 15% (2000-17), 30% + 2.5%/yr to 45% after; ×1.6 5★, 1.3 4★, 0.6 ≤2★; ×0.35 under a 2.6 GPA; FCS ×0.4 (`early_enrollee_chance`).
- February only: a commit whose place ran out signs where there's room (no NIL), or walks on; then D-I staffs offer open walk-on places to the best unsigned ≤2★ seniors in their state (prestige order) (`run_signing_stage`, `scrambled`, preferred walk-ons).
- Not signed: on February's signing day an unsigned senior, prep player or junior college player in his last year there whom some staff had on its board or offered keeps looking (the late pool, `cal.late`); the rest, and the late pool left on the last late day, go as before: JUCO movers stop after two years; prep school if good film (2.2 SD above mean) and GPA under 2.3, chance 0.25 + 0.4 × money; otherwise done (`run_signing_stage`, `None` branch, `keeps_looking`).
- Enrollment: January enrollees in January, everyone else in June; a JUCO mover keeps at most 2 seasons used (`enroll`).

His last role counts only from a season here (`walk_ons::in_two_deep`, his last `college_staff` entry at this program): before lane B a freshman walk-on's high school role counted, so most House-era FBS preferred walk-ons went on aid at their first camp. At your program your staff names them in your inbox and the call is yours (`walk_ons::camp_word`). Open tryouts: 8% of FBS/FCS programs a year, 16% below, take the best unsigned new graduate in their state; never at your program, where you hold your own (`camp_walk_ons`, `tryout_sticks`).

## Cuts (minor bug 60)

- Who: a college head coach cuts any player on his roster not already in the portal, at any time, from the player page's "Cut him" (`crates/afm-game/src/lane_frontoffice/college.rs`, `college_cut`; `world/front_office/college.rs`, `cut`, `cut_words`). AI staffs don't cut.
- His aid (`world/ncaa.rs`, `aid_kept`): a walk-on has none. A scholarship player at a power conference FBS school from the 2015 season keeps it while he stays in school (the autonomy rule); anywhere else, and before, it is a one-year award that isn't renewed. Division III gives no athletic aid.
- The limit (`takes_a_place`): a cut player never counts. He leaves at the next window, so his place goes to next year's class under either limit. (A guaranteed scholarship player who stayed in school without football would still count; the world has nobody who does: he transfers or leaves football.)
- That day: off the roster (`World::rebuild_rosters` skips him while `Person::cut_here` holds), out of the season's games ("cut from the team"), his NIL deal and your deal for him gone, a note on his page, news when he is a 4-star or a starter. His page reads "Cut by <school>, waiting for the transfer window".
- The next window (`portal_open`): any winter, spring or graduate window takes him unless his eligibility is done. He enters that day, is never held for a playoff run, can land at his level or below (never up), and never returns; with no offer he leaves football. Once he lands elsewhere the cut no longer holds there.

## The transfer portal

- The lean (`world/college.rs`, `lean_checkpoint`): set at fall camp from role (backup 0.18, rotation 0.07, starter 0.07/3, redshirt 0.12 × early weight), × buried (1 + 0.12 × (overall − level starter median), 0.6-2), × competitiveness, × 1.12 homesick (900+ miles). Midseason: 4+ games ×0.85, none ×1.15, mood and loyalty. Coaching change ×1.8 the day his head coach leaves, once a cycle (`PIPELINE.portal_entry.coaching_change`; `world/college.rs`, `coach_gone`, called from `staff::leave`; before minor bug 88 it waited for a checkpoint after the carousel, so a coach who left later shook nobody). Bowl ×0.97, no bowl ×1.08. A blue-chip signed at his spot ×1.2. A poor NIL deal at your program ×1.35 (`nil_payouts`).
- Before the winter window the head coach talks rotation players into staying: × (1 − 0.03 × (relationships − 10)), 0.75-1.25 (`portal_open`, `sway`).
- Who enters (`portal_open`): dismissed players always; a school dropping football frees everyone; a standout below FBS moving up (`PIPELINE.transfer_chance` 0.15, ×1.6 when his collective is under $2M) when his play, level-discounted, beats the level above's starter median + 1; steered players 45%; anyone else on u < lean × era; an FBS starter at a program whose collective is under $1M leaves for money with chance 0.24 × his money want (NIL era only). Teammates from his high school leaving lift the lean ×1.15.
- Era factor (`era_factor`): permission era 0.5, early portal 0.8-0.9, 2021-22 1.0, 2023 1.2, 2024 1.45, January-window era 1.45 × 1.3.
- Windows (`world/clock.rs`, `portal_windows`): none before 2022 (players leave after their last game); 2022-24 a December window and an April one; from 2025 one January window only: Jan 2-16 for the 2025 season, then 10 days from the first weekday on or after Jan 2 (Jan 4-13 in 2027) (`calendar/anchors.rs`, `january_window`; minor bugs 196-200, B12-B16). A head coach change opens a 30-day (2022-24) or 15-day (2025+) window five days after the carousel, its first day counted (`world/folk/takeover.rs`; `front_office/college.rs`, `next_window` names your own on the Handbook task and the needs line, and the carousel's window only when your own head coach changed that cycle). A head coach who leaves after that (from six days after the FBS regular season until fall camp) opens a late window of the same length (its first day counted) at his program five days after his successor is hired (`world/staff.rs`, `late_window_due`, `hire`; `World::late_windows`; minor bug 88). Graduate transfers after May commencement. Players on teams still in the playoff are held until they're out.
- Your players talk to you first (`portal_open`, `ask_first`, `intents_day`): at your program, with you as head coach, a lean-driven entrant (the lean rule, a teammate leaving, spring depth, a graduate) doesn't enter on the window's day. He asks to see you: a one-on-one about his future, "He asked for this meeting" (`world/folk/talks.rs`, `portal_ask`). He opens with his own line in his mood (`talk.open.portal.sour`, `good`, `flat`; no mood part that assumes you called him in), many keyed to why he means to leave (`reason=snaps`, `coach`, `money`, `up`, `choice`, `grad`; `recruiting::intent_reason`). Before minor bug 155 that part had no lines, so he said only a mood line. Up to six talks a day, best first; a one-on-one already on your calendar counts (proposed, not confirmed: six a day). He decides on his own entry day, the day after the talk at the earliest, and enters only if his lean is still above the bar his draw set (u / era, or the same draw over that window's rate). Each talk move changes his lean ×0.7-1.3 (`talks.rs`, `one_on_one_answer`); a talk handed to staff becomes his position coach's word, ×0.85-1.05 (`takeover::position_coach_word`); a desk sit-down (`front_office/college.rs`, `keep_talk`) lands at once. A playoff team's held player talks once his season is over (`release_held`). Every other program's players enter on the window's day as before. Before 2026-10-28 every entrant was decided on the window's first day, so a takeover's flight-risk one-on-ones came after the decisions.
- A late window (yours in February, or an AI head coach who left after the cycle's window) belongs to the season just played (`portal_open`: label = year − 1 before fall camp). One that opens after that cycle's winter window draws only from the players who stayed through it: a player enters on u < (min(1, lean now × era) − s) / (1 − s), where s = min(1, his lean on the winter window's first day × era) (`lean_on`, the product of his lean's dated factors). A career has its own seed, so January's draw can't be reused. One that opens before the winter window (a December late window from 2025, when the winter window opens January 2) draws on u < lean × era with the same draw the winter window will use, so a player isn't drawn twice in a cycle. Before 2026-10-28 the window drew everyone afresh (a second full cycle at ×1.8) and a junior in his last season counted as out of eligibility. Measured (`coach_change_check 5 2026 6`, six FBS programs at 7-29 to 13-25 in their last three seasons, nobody holding the talks): 21.7 entered after your hire before, 13.2 after, on top of a January window of 14-32; AI programs with a fired coach lose 31-32 a cycle, those without a change 20.4.
- A player who has decided (landed, gone home or quit) has his lean set to 0 (`portal_day`): a later window that offseason (spring, or a coaching change where he landed) doesn't draw him again.
- Landing (`portal_day`): a program wants him if he'd make its two-deep (three-deep with roster room) or beats its backups at an open starting spot (a Division II, NAIA or Division III staff only for an entrant from inside its footprint (0.2 or more, `SMALL_PORTAL_REACH`), one playing in its state, or one it has ties to; minor bug 246: every program with a place called before, and 19% of Division III's transfers came from 1,000 miles or more), adds ≤ 10, under its limit, counted on next season's roster: the players who return and the recruits signed for it, not its seniors with no season left, its own players in the portal or its junior college players signed with a four-year school, plus the day's landings (earlier days' are on the roster by then) (`counted_rosters`; minor bug 131: counting this season's roster, a full FBS roster in December had no room, so four- and five-star freshmen who left landed at FCS and an FCS standout moving up could find no FBS school); his play is read with a level discount (FCS 1, D2/JUCO 5, NAIA 6, D3 7) (`competition_discount`). Bar: the level's weakest-quarter starter − 2 (−3 more for a step down). He picks by own-wants utility + room; first choice falls through at ~1% per day waited. Two levels down or more (an FBS player at D2 or below, an FCS player at the NAIA or below) he goes only for the love of the game: he keeps those schools with chance (love of the game / 20)², else most finish their degree (`STEP_DOWN_POW`, proposed, not confirmed; minor bug 99: about one FBS entrant in five landed below FCS, real about 2%). A dismissed player restarts wherever he can.

## Program grades, prestige and pipelines

- Prestige 0-1 = 0.15 tradition + 0.3 recent + 0.12 × coach rep/20 × √level scale + 0.12 facilities + 0.11 × √(boosters/$25M) + 0.12 fans + 0.08 conference (`world/program.rs`, `prestige_of`). prestige_rel = prestige / the division leader's prestige when history began (`world/staff.rs`, `update_programs`).
- How a program starts (minor bugs 294-296): from its real past in the season it starts, 1984 for the game's worlds (`world/program.rs`, `program_from_row`; `world/data.rs`). Recent = level scale × (0.2 + 0.8 × its win share over the five seasons before, ties half), from `college_start_records.csv` (1979-83; James Madison 22-30 → 0.42, not its 2021-25 0.80); with no record known 0.5, and a world that takes over in 2026 reads the 2021-25 record (`start_record`, `NO_START_RECORD`). Tradition = level scale × (0.25 + 0.12 × √titles + 0.25 if a power program) + noise, titles being the real national titles won before it starts at every level (`college_titles.csv`, `titles_before`): a title won below its level counts by that level's scale over its own (a Division II title is 0.56 of an FCS one), one won above it as one of its own (proposed, not confirmed). `titles` starts at that count and `titles_before` keeps them by level for the screens ("10 FCS titles · 5 Division II titles", `crates/afm-game/src/setup.rs`, `college_titles_text`). Seats = its real seats that season (`college_seats.csv`, `real_seats`), else today's; they then follow the real seats each season through 2025 (`apply_history`; a season with none known keeps the last), today's seats (`colleges.csv`) in 2026, and they hold from then on (the world's college projects build facilities, not stadiums: `finance/college.rs`, `facility_decisions`). Facilities and fans start from those seats (`stadium_share`).
- Twelve grades, re-ranked within each division to 55-99 each year; fast grades (title chances, playing time, coach prestige, NIL) move 80% of the way, steady ones 15% (`update_programs`, `move_grade`, `GradeCat::speed`).
- Footprint per state: max(local reach, national reach, overflow spill) + 0.6 × pipeline (`rebuild_footprint`; minor bug 246, all sizes proposed, not confirmed):
  - Local (`region`): 1 − miles / reach, reach = 150 + 700 × level reach × prestige_rel² + 150 × fans (`HOME_REACH_MILES`, `FAN_REACH_MILES`; it was 1,100 and 250, about 1,100 miles for a strong name). Level reach: FBS 1, FCS 0.55, JUCO 0.6, D2 and NAIA 0.3, D3 0.25. Miles run from the campus to each other state's center, 60 in its own state (`campus_miles`; from the home state's center before, so North Dakota State in Fargo read Minnesota as 300 miles off).
  - National: 0.35 × the level's reach × (0.25 + 0.75 × the state's overflow) (`NATIONAL_FLOOR`). FBS: ((prestige_rel − 0.55) / 0.45)², 0 at or below 0.55 (`NATIONAL_FROM`; Georgia at 0.98 0.91, Iowa at 0.84 0.42, Utah at 0.64 0.04). FCS 0.3 × the same. JUCO 0.36 × prestige_rel³. D2 and NAIA 0.01 × prestige_rel³, D3 0.005 × prestige_rel³. Before, every level read 0.35 × level reach² × prestige_rel³ in every state alike.
  - Overflow spill: the size × the state's overflow × (1 − miles / 1,800) (`SPILL_MILES`); Group of 5 0.45 (`SPILL_FBS`), power programs 0.2 (`SPILL_POWER`), FCS 0.2 (`SPILL_FCS`), none below. A state's overflow is the square root of (its FBS signees a year − 14 × its power programs), over the most (`world/mod.rs`, `overflow_by_state`).
  - A save from before minor bug 246 rebuilds every footprint when it loads (`World::after_load`, `mix_rev`).
- Where the talent grows (`world/mod.rs`, `state_culture`): each boy's talent draw centres on his state's shift, the log of (its share of FBS signees a year / its share of the class) / 2.6, between −0.6 and 0.5, plus a fit to the real rosters (`TALENT_FIT`, minor bug 246): the shift that gives each state its share of Division I players by home state on the 2025 rosters (cfbfastR). The signee counts miss the upper Midwest's players, so Iowa, Wisconsin and Minnesota grew 47-61% of their real share of Division I players and Mississippi 171%. Fitted in two steps on full-scale worlds (seeds 62, 63): each step adds ln(real share / world share) / 2, × n / (n + 40) for a state with n real players, at most 0.5 either way, centred so the country's talent stays the same. A save from before it grows its next classes from the new shifts (`refit_cultures`).
- Pipelines grow only by impact (all-conference, a draft pick, an undrafted signing): state +0.12 × weight × room, high school +0.25, area +0.12; they fade 0.03 a year after 3 quiet years. The same impact adds 0.2 × weight to the staff's ties to that high school (`record_impact`, `fade_pipelines`; recruiting, `credit_impact`). Both grow in the spin-up years too, from pipelines seeded when the world is made (`program::seed_pipelines`, again when recording starts); only the coaches' visible record waits for recorded history (minor bug 131: a short world's first draft classes were recruited with no pipelines at all).
