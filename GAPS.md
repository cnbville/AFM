# Gaps

Gaps found while fixing bugs or building, so they carry over between threads (Cain, 2026-11-17). Each: what's missing, why it matters, impact. Remove an entry once it's built. Nothing here is agreed until Cain says yes.

## Engine

- **Screens line up under center too often, and play action throws too deep** (low-medium). Screens are drawn after the look: 24.0% under center on the NFL test league (real 18.7%), where a drop from the gun is now at real. Play action's air yards an attempt are 12.5 (real 9.2) and 5.7% of its throws are behind the line (real 14.8%), with the boot still below its real share. Minor bugs 410 and 411, 2026-11-18; the screens belong in `calling.rs`, `concept_weights` (a screen from under center weighs more).
- **Straight drops from the gun expose the pass rush** (medium). With drops lined up as real, NFL sacks are 1.43 a team game (real 2.41) and points 26.1; the pass rush is lane SF8's.
- **The engine's instructions read a freelance trust, not his trust in the coach** (medium). `GamePlayer.trust` is 0.5 for every player the world sends to a game, so `tendencies::obey_chance` never sees how much he believes his own coach; only the new bond part (`GamePlayer.bond`, `obey_trust`) reaches it. The built `Person::trust` could feed it directly. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Build coach and player bonds").
- **The press ranks only finished rooms** (low). `race::press_score` reads `Campus.level` and `since`, so a building that has broken ground adds nothing until it opens, and the Pecking order screen can't say "read as 10th since the groundbreaking" as board 5 draws it. Recruits' groundbreaking pull (facilities lane #281) is separate. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Build Capital screen CS5"); needs Cain's call on whether a building under way counts for the press.
- **Fumbles go out of bounds less than half as often as real** (medium). 3.8% of NFL scrimmage fumbles (real 8.5%; after a catch 10.0% against 19.0%, designed runs 0.9% against 5.8%). The loose ball goes out wherever it reaches the line, but the engine's plays seldom end near the sideline (designed runs out of bounds 1.1% of the time, real 5.5%), so its fumbles come out far from it. It keeps the defense a little ahead on recoveries after a catch (58% against 49%). Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Build engine lane EG2 ball security").
- **Who comes up with a strip sack** (medium). The NFL's sack fumbles go to the defense 46% of the time (real 50%) and back to the passer 21% (15%), so they are lost 44% of the time (real 51%): a fifth of the 17% that lost fumbles are short of real (most of the rest is the sacks themselves, 1.37 a team game against 2.41, the pass rush of lane SF8 and the quarterback off script of EG7). Its catch fumbles are run back 44% of the time (real 31%) and its sacks' 22% (33%), where the totals are near real (31% and 28%). Fumbled snaps are lost 15% of the time (real 25%). Same thread.
- **A passer hit as he throws never fumbles** (low). The strip in his motion comes only on a sack (`sack_check`); the hit on the throw rolls nothing, so passes without a catch fumble 0.021 a team game (real 0.036), all of them interception returns. EG1 adds that hit for injuries. Same thread.
- **A quarterback's designed runs fumble a third of the real rate** (low). 0.66% of them over seven NFL seeds (15,385 runs), real 2.12% (nflverse 2021-25), where backs are at real (0.69%, real 0.70%). The tackle's fumble reads him like any carrier, by his Ball Security against the level's typical starting back's; why real passers fumble three times as often on their own runs (sneaks into a pile, option keeps, his Ball Security as a carrier) is not measured yet. At the engine's few designed quarterback runs (0.55 a team game, real 1.69; item 10, lane SF10) it misses under 0.01 fumbles a team game, and 0.025 at the real number of runs. Same thread.
- **A muffed punt is a roll** (medium). The kicking team gets 45-50% of muffs at the spot and nobody advances one (`game/kicking.rs`, `punt`), where a fumbled return now plays out live. Same thread. Lane EG3 made blocked kicks live on the same loose ball but left muffs as they were (outside its items).
- **A fumbled return the return team downs in its own end zone is a touchback** (low). Real, a ball the return team put there itself is a safety. A kick's result holds no safety (`game/kicking.rs`, `run_return`), so the loose ball's safety is kept as a touchback. Rare. Same thread; outside lane EG3's items.
- **A flag on the return in the return team's own end zone is marked from its 1** (low). The defense's holding or illegal block on its own return is marked from the spot of the foul, but a spot behind its own goal line is moved up to the 1 (`game/flow.rs`, `resolve`, the return's foul); every level's book makes it a safety, as EG5 does for the offense's foul in its own end zone (`fouls::offense`). Found 2026-11-19 (thread "Build engine lane EG5 rule books"), merging EG2.
- **College runs fumbles back for scores less often than real, and college and high school have no full real count** (low). The engine plays them on the NFL's per-chance rates read against each level (the level's typical hit, `hit_scale`, and its typical starting back). FBS against FBS in 2023 (cfbfastR's play text) lost 0.43 fumbles a team game and ran 0.044 back for touchdowns, 10% of its lost fumbles (the NFL's 7%); the engine loses 0.40 and scores 0.021, its defenders scooping whenever they are first to the ball (`LOOSE_RACE`; scooping in closer traffic scores more but loses fewer). The text holds 0.81 fumbles a team game with 53% lost, where the engine fumbles 0.95 and loses 42%; the text may leave out fumbles the offense falls on, so its total isn't a target. High school has no source. FCS and small colleges take FBS's typical hit, unmeasured (`match_check` plays neither). Same thread.
- **Synthetic players draw their flaws from 14-year-old habits, then get level habits** (low). `game/synthetic.rs` runs `craft::ensure` (whose leans read `p.tend`: patience, downhill, upfield, gunslinger, jump routes) before `Tendencies::at_level` replaces `p.tend`, so a change to age-14 centres redraws every synthetic roster's flaws and moves seed-tuned tests (2026-11-19: `a_first_half_sitter_comes_back_at_halftime`, `free_him_up_and_send_him_move_his_rushes`). Reading the level habits first would be the truer order but moves every synthetic league and every check, so it needs its own measure.
- **Tests built on `tests::prospects` take ids from the global counter** (low). `player.rs` `NEXT_ID`; none reads tendencies today (`fourteen_year_olds_spread_wide_around_the_middle` runs on fixed ids).

- **A ball off target costs nothing after the catch** (medium). Real yards after the catch fall 0.37 a catch in rain and 0.28 in a 15-19 mph wind (nflverse 2021-25), as receivers reach back or stop for a wet or blown ball; the engine's rise a little (5.06 to 5.18 in rain): placement reads only whether he catches it (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `catch_point`), and the cleaner throws are the ones caught. It moves every pass, not only the weather's, so it needs its own measure. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Fix DL rotation and weather size"); a slower first step after a wet catch was tried there and did nothing.
- **Footing is read nowhere** (low). Rain, snow and mud don't touch a man's burst or cuts. Cutting both sides' burst and cut 10% on a wet field raised yards after the catch by 0.5 a catch in this engine (real -0.37), because tacklers lose their closing burst more than a carrier loses his cut; footing needs the open-field tackle's angle read first. Same thread.
- **No edges inside on passing downs** (low). Real 4-3 teams line up 1.83 inside men a snap (nflverse snap counts 2023-24) as edges slide inside on third and long; the engine's fronts always field two tackles (three in a 3-4 base), so the second tackle plays 63% of snaps (real 54% on 4-3 teams) after the staff's rotation (item 29). An AI pass-rush package would close it. Same thread.
- **A rested tackle doesn't win back his backup's snaps** (medium). Since the inside line rotates by plan (engine gaps item 29), defenses give up 0.3 points a team game more at the NFL and FBS and 0.4 in high school (match_check over main ea85aad, seed 1): the backups play more and the starters' rest barely shows on the field. Real staffs rotate because a fresh rusher wins more; the energy cost of a long series on a big man is the place to look (`game/flow.rs`, the energy line; `game/sim.rs`, how energy reads). Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Fix DL rotation and weather size").
- **Edges' third and fourth men still play only on fatigue** (low). The inside line rotates by plan since item 29; edges keep bug 358's fatigue rotation, so the top two play 78% and 71% of snaps a game (real 67% and 45%, PFR labels, some off-ball linebackers among them). Bug 368's edge side. Same thread.
- **Heat is read nowhere** (low). A hot, humid game tires nobody faster (`Conditions.temperature` reads only kicks and cold). Item 30's note; same thread.

- **Return breakaways still short** (medium). Kickoff returns of 40+ are 2.5% of returns (real 5.0%) and punt returns of 20+ 4.4% (8.0%) on 2,000 NFL games over lane EG5, from 1.6% and 2.6% before lane EG3 gave returns the scrimmage tackle's reach. The averages are about right (punts 9.2 yards, real 9.45; kickoffs 26.4, real 25.9), so what is missing is the run past the second level: on punts the first contact comes 5-9 yards upfield (the jammers and the cover men arriving at the catch), and on kickoffs the two safeties and the kicker stop nearly every return that breaks the first wave. One safety deep gave too many kickoff touchdowns. Found in lane EG3.
- **No fouls on punts that are not returned** (low). Real return teams are flagged for holding on 3.2% of fair-caught or downed punts (nflverse 2025, 0.067 a team game); the engine's return fouls ride only on a return (`kicking::run_return`). Kickoffs short of the landing zone or out of bounds are audit item 28. Found in lane EG3.
- **The cover men's face masks on punts at a fifth of real** (low). 0.003 a team game (real 0.012, nflverse 2025), where the rest of the return fouls are at real by kind since lane EG3 sized them over lane EG5 (kickoffs' holding 0.225 against 0.223, blocks in the back 0.034 against 0.033; punts' holding 0.084 against 0.088, blocks in the back 0.059 against 0.053 with blindside blocks). The face mask is the tackle's draw, and a punt's tackles come at the catch. Found in lane EG3.
- **The half's last kick reads the range alone** (low-medium). Tries of 55+ in a half's last 30 seconds are 0.030 a team game (real 0.060 in 2024-25) and the rest 0.155 (0.125): the half-end branch of `flow::scrimmage` kicks only inside `kicker_range`, with nothing for a last chance. It goes with lane EG4's half's last snap. Found in lane EG3. Lane EG4's last snap, which kicks up to 6 yards past the range when the kick beats a Hail Mary, leaves it at 0.030 (measured over both, 2026-11-19): the shortfall is in reaching a long kick with time left, not the last call.
- **Long kicks in the wind are tried a little less and made too rarely** (low). In a 17 mph wind the NFL tries 3% fewer kicks of 50+ (real 13% fewer in 15+ mph) and makes 62% of them (real 78%: real coaches pick the ones the wind carries), over main 569e61f. The wind's aim, 1.2% wider a mph (`kicking::fg_spread`), may be too wide. At 25 F the tries fall 25% (real 26%) and the makes are 66% (59%). Found in lane EG3.
- **NFL kicks of 55+ made 66% of the time** (low). Real 59.7%; 50-54 70.3% (73.4%). The coaches pick their long kicks by each kicker's estimate, so the real selection (a few strong legs try most of them) is the likely gap. Found in lane EG3.
- **Blocked kicks run back for scores below real** (low). Blocked punts 21% (real 17 of 51, 33%), field goals 0.7% (8 of 101, 7.9%), on 2,000 NFL games over lane EG5; the punting team keeps none of its blocked punts. The ball comes down among the rushers, and the kicking team covers it first. Found in lane EG3.
- **High school field goals blocked 8.2% of tries** (low). From 1.4%, by the low trajectory and slow operation of high school kickers and snappers (blocked kicks 0.166 a team game, FBS 0.086, the NFL 0.071, real NFL 0.072). No real high school rate was found to check it against. Found in lane EG3.
- **College tries few kicks of 50+** (low). FBS 0.058 a team game (0.163 before lane EG3), made a little above the real rate (58.3%, real 50.6%); there is no real count of college tries by distance yet. The punt's value reads the NFL's net (41 yards, the pin at 88) at every level (`calling::punt_start`), which goes with audit item 20. Found in lane EG3.
- **Fouls on a kick play aren't drawn live** (medium). Offside at a kick's snap, running into or roughing the kicker or holder, and the kicking team's holds are never called; EG5 books only the dead-ball fouls before a kick's snap and the two kickoff fouls, so all accepted penalties come to 5.18 a team game at the NFL (real 6.00) with scrimmage plays at 5.01 (5.24; `match_check nfl 2000 1` over main 569e61f, with lane EG2). The return team's fouls are bug 403 in EG3, which can size these with them. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Build engine lane EG5 rule books").
- **Ineligible downfield short of real** (low-medium). 0.051 accepted a team game at the NFL (real 0.106, on every kind of play; over main 569e61f): RPOs draw 0.042 and screens 0.020 (nearly all play-action screens, whose fake holds the throw while the line releases), but dropbacks, other play action and scrambles give almost none, because a pass blocker sets behind the line, SF1's play-action line pass-sets behind it too (real lines show the run and some drift past the limit), and nobody drifts downfield while the passer scrambles. The scramble is EG7's; a line that shows the run on the fake has no lane. Same thread.
- **Few delays to back up a punt** (low). 0.020 a team game at the NFL (real 0.066; over main 569e61f), at the real share from each spot, because the engine punts less from the other side's 30-50 than real teams do (it tries more long field goals, item 11 in EG3, and its fourth-down sums are item 20 in EG4). Delays on fourth down come to 0.075 (0.142). Same thread.
- **The defense's illegal use of hands comes only from coverage** (low). A pass rusher's hands to the blocker's face, much of the real defensive count, is never drawn; the defense's calls come from the hand fight in man coverage, so the league total (0.099, real 0.110) is right but who draws it is not. Same thread.
- **The defense holds only in man coverage** (low-medium). Its holding, illegal contact and hands all come from the hand fight in man coverage (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `HAND_FIGHT`), sized on seed 1's test league, which plays man on 31% of dropbacks like real (NGS 2025). Real holds also come against zone (22% of the defense's holds on dropbacks, with 2023-24's labels, which call 45% of dropbacks man) and on runs (15% of all its holds; nflverse with FTN's labels, 2023-24). So the count moves with a league's man share more than real does: on seeds 2-4 of `match_check nfl 2000`, whose leagues play man on 25-28%, the defense's accepted holding is 0.19-0.26 a team game (seed 1 0.285; real 0.287-0.387 by season, nflverse 2021-25), while the offense's stays at 0.89-1.04 over the four seeds (real 0.825-1.028). The hold against zone and on runs belongs with the coverage jobs (SF7), which then sizes the hand fight again on the four seeds' mean. Found 2026-11-19 (thread "Build engine lane EG5 rule books").
- **FBS holding high and unsportsmanlike conduct low against the play text** (low-medium). FBS holding by both sides 1.56 accepted a team game against about 1.15 in cfbfastR's 2023-24 play text (FBS against FBS, kick plays in), unsportsmanlike conduct 0.17 (0.23) and pass interference by both sides 0.59 (0.67). The text count misses about one foul in nine, so the FBS sizes wait for a cleaner count. Same thread.
- **FBS false starts 10-20% high before fourth down** (low). 0.48, 0.35 and 0.37 a team game on first, second and third down against real 0.40, 0.32 and 0.31 (cfbfastR 2023-24, FBS against FBS); main was 0.49, 0.36 and 0.23. First and second down's rate a snap is main's own; fourth down is 27% short (0.11, real 0.15) with the back-ups. Same thread.
- **Neutral zone infractions and kickoffs out of bounds a little high** (low). At the NFL 0.255 and 0.081 accepted a team game (real 0.226 and 0.070, nflverse 2021-25 and 2025), on `match_check nfl 2000 1`. A defender's jump splits into a neutral zone infraction before the snap and offside at it by a fixed share (`JUMP_EARLY`, `JUMP_EARLY_HARD` against a hard count), and a kickoff goes out of bounds by the kicker's kickoff technique (`kicking.rs`, `kickoff`); neither was refitted after SF1. Same thread.
- **The hit at the catch point barely draws a flag** (medium). On `match_check fbs 2000 1` the catch point gives 0.002 flagged hits a team game and scrambles 0.012, against 0.45 on every other play (the late hit on a tackle), so nearly all college targeting comes from tackles, where real targeting is mostly on defenseless receivers; EG5 kept the total in its real band by refitting the tackle's helmet-first share (`HIGH_ON_TACKLE` 0.09). Count how often `hit_at_catch` lands at all before sizing `CATCH_HIT_FOUL` again; the catch point is SF5's. Same thread.
- **The play log names a defender's dead-ball jump the NFL's way** (low). College calls a defender who jumps into the neutral zone before the snap offside (cfbfastR's play text has no neutral zone infraction), and the NFHS calls it encroachment, but the engine books the first as a neutral zone infraction and the second as offside, so the names read wrong in college and high school play-by-play; the yards and the dead ball are right. A name by book (`FoulKind::name` reads no book) would fix it. Same thread.
- **No ejection for a second unsportsmanlike conduct** (low). The NFL (from 2016) and college throw a man out for his second; EG5 calls the fouls but never counts them, and unsportsmanlike conduct after a turnover or a score's return is never drawn. Same thread.

### The engine gaps audit (agreed)

Cain agreed to all 30 on 2026-11-17 ("agreed"). What gets built and in which lane is in `docs/design/engine-gaps.md` (lanes EG1-EG7, item 10 in SF10, items 29 and 30 as small fixes); each lane removes its entries here when it merges. The audit (thread "Find engine gaps beyond scheme fit") checked real NFL play-by-play (nflverse 2021-25) and the repo's college targets against `match_check` on main, each code claim checked twice. Numbers, sources and code lines are in the project folder at `afm/engine-gaps/report.md` (items 1-30).

- **Injuries only in tackles and sacks** (high). Blocks, the hit at the catch point and the hit on a throwing quarterback never roll one, so backs take 35% of game injuries (real about 8%) and offensive linemen 0.2% (real about 18%). Item 1.
- **No non-contact injuries** (high). About 70% of NFL ACL tears come without contact, and hamstrings are sprinting injuries; the engine draws every injury at a hit. Item 2.
- **Nobody comes back in the same game** (medium). A hurt man plays on hurt or is out; most real in-game injuries cost a few snaps, and a bruise sends about half its men out for good. Item 3.
- **The red zone plays loose** (high). NFL red zone completion 65.9% (real 53.0%), a touchdown on 26.5% of red zone throws (19.0%), 2.37 points a drive (2.00). Item 8.
- **Quarterbacks extend few plays** (high). The scramble drill is built but gets few passers out before the play is dead: 2.5% of throws come after 3.94 s (real 10%), sacks 5.5% of dropbacks (6.8%), scrambles 1.60 a team game (1.97). Item 9. After SF1 and EG5 (2026-11-18): sacks 4.1% of dropbacks and 1.54 a team game (4.4% and 1.65 on main after SF1), since a pass blocker now lets a blitzer come to him instead of walking up to meet him (pressure 20.0% of dropbacks, real 22.2%; sacks per pressure 0.199, real 0.283); scrambles 1.33, throws after 3.94 s 2.3%; throwaways 0.49 a team game (real 1.38), so intentional grounding, flagged at the real rate a throwaway, stays at 0.040 (real 0.077).
- **Few designed quarterback runs** (medium). 0.54 a team game (real 1.69); the test league's passers are typical, so SF10 measures it on world rosters. Item 10.
- **The analysts' fourth-down model counts a conversion near the goal as a touchdown** (low). `decide::Model::fourth_at` scores a converted fourth down as a touchdown whenever the band's mean gain on converted plays reaches the goal (17.1 yards for 7-10 to go at the NFL), so going is overstated near the goal line: on 4th and 9 at their 12 early in a tied game the model sits within a hair of the kick, and a refit over lane EG4 flipped it to go (nfl4th kicks; `tests::decide` pins it), so the tables were kept. A converted play could end at its gain, capped at the goal, or in the end zone by the gains' spread (proposed, not confirmed). Found in lane EG4, 2026-11-19.
- **A half's last 5 s take one snap** (low-medium). Real teams often fit two (a quick throw at the end zone, then the kick). Of the pool's second-quarter snaps (5 s or less) the engine kicks 35% (real 51%) and kneels 51% (18%); the pool is 0.47 snaps a game (real 0.74) and 0.9% are spikes (8.5%). Items 15 and 17, left by lane EG4 (2026-11-19, over lane EG3).
- **The fourth quarter's last snap throws deep more and kicks less** (low). Of the pool, deep shots are 9.7% (real 2.7%) and field goals 5.1% (11.1%), likely from where late drives stand when time runs out rather than the call (not measured). Lane EG4.
- **Few untimed downs at 0:00** (low). One in 100 NFL games (real one in 68): the Hail Mary's crowd (`scrimmage::crowd_ball`) skips the catch point where lane EG5 reads pass interference, so it never draws a flag. Item 22, lane EG4.
- **Few plays reach the sideline** (medium). Designed runs end out of bounds 1.2% of the time (real 5.5%), 0.6% when ahead in the last 5:00 (2.3%): the rest of S6.2 (the wide run's path toward the sideline, the short outs), left to SF4 (routes) and SF9 (long runs). Item 18, lane EG4.
- **Laterals a little often** (low). On 29% of the fourth quarter's last passes from 40+ out at the NFL (real 22%), 41% in college and 58% at high school (no real numbers there yet). Item 16, lane EG4.
- **A player back from a first-half sit does not win his job back** (low). When another man at his spot is hurt in the first half, the next man takes the job and the sitter, the better player, plays about one snap in the second half (coach fire item 4; found 2026-11-19 on seed 5 of `tests::coach_fire`, which now reads eight games). The lineup should be named again at halftime.

## Logic

- **A snapper's knack gives one boy in ten at FBS a long snapper's race odds** (medium; needs a design call). A boy with the knack draws his ethnicity with the snapper row (94% white; `ethnicity::snapper_knack`, A25), but on a full-scale world (seed 281, main 27bc372) 1,544 of 15,444 FBS players carry that row and only 261 of them snap: 209 defensive backs, 266 linemen, 243 defensive linemen, 158 linebackers, 128 receivers, 73 quarterbacks. Each position's odds are fitted to make up for its knack boys, so after the 2026-11-19 refit a back born as a back draws about 92% Black where a real room is 78%, and any lane that moves who reaches a position moves its mix. Drawing the knack boys with their own position's row would move every position 3 to 6 points more Black before a refit (FBS DBs 79%, RBs 78%). A narrower knack (closer to the 1-2% of boys who snap) or a draw that blends the two rows would fix it; it needs Cain's call, then a refit of every row on one world. Found 2026-11-19 (thread "Refit college DB and QB race mix").
- **Talks as a shortcut cover a player's moves and a staff duty, not every screen yet** (medium). `live_talk/persona/actions.rs` `ROWS` runs projects, focus, a position move, a depth spot, a redshirt, the reserve list, a scholarship, the transfer block, the scout squad, your players list, the recruiting board and a staff duty. Not yet from a talk: an NFL contract step (an extension, a cut, a restructure), special teams boards, the team's practice plan, scouting assignments, a recruit's visit day beyond the desk's first open date. Each needs a row with the screen's own command and check.
- **A money line read as both an answer and an offer lists twice** (low). "I can go to 970,000" came as an `answer` counter and an `offer` in one reading (replay `donte_haggle`, 2026-11-19), so "Settled so far" holds two open offers for one number and the voice once asked for "a staff job on the table" in the same breath as "I'd take that".
- **A coach's walk-away can sit close to his ask, so some haggles are two rounds** (low). Damann asked $1.15 million with a walk-away near $970,000 (`hiring::would_take` on your terms); 860,000 then 970,000 closed it in two rounds. Real coordinators' gaps aren't measured; the pace and decay are proposed, not confirmed (`persona::haggle`).
- **NFL fines may now fall short of the real count** (low-medium). The league office's share of flagged hits it fines (`world/folk/rules.rs`, `fine_share`) was fitted to 250-350 fines a season on main's hit fouls, which ran a half high at the NFL (unnecessary roughness 0.43 a team game, real 0.27); EG5 brings them to real (0.28), so the world may fine fewer than 250 a season. Measure on the next world and refit there. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Build engine lane EG5 rule books").
- **A coach of yours can't see last season if it was played before you took the seat** (high). His unit's numbers and form (`claims::rooms::unit`, `points_of`) read only `w.seat_games`, the games kept since you arrived. In a new career every holdover's unit has no numbers and no good or poor read, so the voice gets nothing to analyse, the holdover meeting can't narrate his unit's year, and the keep or let go talk can't let you blame his unit (`takeover.rs`, `staffing.rs`). The world already keeps every team's season in the squads, games and players tallies. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Give coaches real depth when asked why"); fix proposed there.
- **A coach hired after last season reads as "one season" here** (medium). `rooms::unit` sets `years` to `(w.year - first_season + 1).max(1)`, so a man who hasn't coached a game here gets 1, and his card says he's been the coordinator "one season". Robert Leonard, hired after a 2-10 year, took the blame for it. Same thread.
- **July's payday under the paid-set offers is not measured yet** (medium). Since 2026-11-19 a school offers money only to recruits its July paid set takes, never under his place's revenue share, so a Power 4 class takes about $4-5M of a program's July money where it took $1.5-2M (seed 1, class of 2027; top classes $8.2-10.8M against real $6-9M+). The rest of the roster splits what is left (`roster_split`), so returning backups and maybe starters get less; nobody has read July 2027 against the College Sports Commission's medians and The NIL Standard. One `nil_scheme run <world> 2027 8` on main against the branch before it would read it. Thread "Pay only next year's paid recruits".
- **A paid two-star backup at a Power 4 school signs for what a three-star does** (low). The floor is his role's revenue share, read by place, not stars, so two-stars in the paid set signed for a median $113K against three-stars' $124K (seed 1). The College Sports Commission pays by role too, but a two-star at a Power 4 school is rarely a planned backup. Same thread.
- **A school's paid set fills from its room before the stage, not as it signs** (low). Every recruit a school bids on in one signing stage reads the same paid set (`PaidLine` from the room before the matching), so a Group of 5 class that signs several projected starters the same day can take more places than the set has; July then pays its deal holders first. Same thread.
- **A keep-him offer takes a revenue-share place first** (medium). July's paid set puts every deal holder ahead of starters (`roster_split`), a keep-him offer's holder too, though it is only a floor on his split; by January nearly every Group of 5 man holds one, so the set fills by place among holders.
