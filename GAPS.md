# Gaps

Gaps found while fixing bugs or building, so they carry over between threads (Cain, 2026-11-17). Each: what's missing, why it matters, impact. Remove an entry once it's built. Nothing here is agreed until Cain says yes.

## Engine

- **Screens line up under center too often, and play action throws too deep** (low-medium). Screens are drawn after the look: 24.0% under center on the NFL test league (real 18.7%), where a drop from the gun is now at real. Play action's air yards an attempt are 12.5 (real 9.2) and 5.7% of its throws are behind the line (real 14.8%), with the boot still below its real share. Minor bugs 410 and 411, 2026-11-18; the screens belong in `calling.rs`, `concept_weights` (a screen from under center weighs more).
- **Straight drops from the gun expose the pass rush** (medium). With drops lined up as real, NFL sacks are 1.43 a team game (real 2.41) and points 26.1; the pass rush is lane SF8's.
- **The engine's instructions read a freelance trust, not his trust in the coach** (medium). `GamePlayer.trust` is 0.5 for every player the world sends to a game, so `tendencies::obey_chance` never sees how much he believes his own coach; only the new bond part (`GamePlayer.bond`, `obey_trust`) reaches it. The built `Person::trust` could feed it directly. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Build coach and player bonds").
- **The press ranks only finished rooms** (low). `race::press_score` reads `Campus.level` and `since`, so a building that has broken ground adds nothing until it opens, and the Pecking order screen can't say "read as 10th since the groundbreaking" as board 5 draws it. Recruits' groundbreaking pull (facilities lane #281) is separate. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Build Capital screen CS5"); needs Cain's call on whether a building under way counts for the press.
- **Playing on or coming back costs nothing past the game** (medium). A man who plays on hurt or comes back from the sideline keeps the same injury and the same length; real players risk making it worse (a sprain into a tear, a hamstring strained again), so going back in is a gamble the engine never takes. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Build engine lane EG1 injuries").
- **The sideline call reads the injury and his toughness only** (medium). Whether he goes back in doesn't read the medical staff's judgement, the score, the stakes or a playoff game; section 9 brings your own call, and the AI's should read its staff (support departments) and the game. Same thread.
- **The sideline counts plays through halftime** (low). A man who goes off late in the second quarter sits his plays into the third, though the trainers have him for the whole break. Same thread.
- **No trench pile** (low). Men go to the ground only at the tackle, the sack, a pancake and a missed dive, so the roll-ups in the scrum at the line come from the block's time (`Sim::wear`, the grind) rather than from bodies falling. Same thread.
- **The loose ball's scrum hurts nobody** (low). A man diving on a fumble and the men he lands among, under lane EG2's loose ball, are not checked as a pile (`Sim::pile_checks` runs only where the man tackled or sacked goes down); as at a pancake, little weight goes down there, so it would add little. Found 2026-11-19, merging EG2; same thread.
- **Heat, cold and the turf's era don't move injury odds** (low). Strains don't rise in the cold, and `TURF_NON_CONTACT` is one number from the 1966 AstroTurf to today's infilled fields. Same thread.
- **Concussion checks aren't kept past the game** (low). `GameResult.checks` holds each game's cleared checks, but the world log, the reader and the game pages don't read them. Same thread.
- **The world's college and high school games run more injuries than the test leagues** (low). On world seed 271 (scale 0.05, 2023-2026), EG1 lifts week-plus injuries 15% a college team game (0.963 to 1.103) and 19% a high school one (0.664 to 0.792), where the FBS and high school test leagues moved 0.1% and 5% on the default seed (3.6% and 7.3% on four seeds). At college about 40% of the rise is the turf's lean (60% of its games are on turf, where strains and plants run 1.45 times grass) and 60% is on grass, mostly piles: they hurt 0.25 men a team game against the FBS test league's 0.18 on the same 67 plays, every other cause within 4%. In high school every cause runs above the test league's (blocks 0.50 against 0.39 a team game, piles 0.19 against 0.15), as the man tackled did on main (1.24 against 1.07 injuries a team game). Not measured why (how many men reach the ball, their weight, the rosters). Same thread.
- **The levels' game injuries draw together** (medium). On four `match_check` seeds EG1 moves week-plus injuries a team game from 1.091 to 1.061 in the NFL, 0.947 to 0.981 at FBS and 0.544 to 0.584 in high school, so the NFL runs 1.08 times FBS (main 1.15) and FBS 1.68 times high school (main 1.74); over main 590101e (SF1, items 29 and 30) 1.098 to 1.058, 0.953 to 1.004 and 0.542 to 0.599, the NFL 1.05 times FBS and FBS 1.68 times high school; over main ad17262 (minor bugs 409-412) 1.080 to 1.070, 0.957 to 1.007 and 0.544 to 0.609, the NFL 1.06 times FBS (main 1.13) and FBS 1.65 times high school (main 1.76); over main 569e61f (EG2, ball security) 1.103 to 1.079, 0.953 to 0.993 and 0.562 to 0.610, the NFL 1.09 times FBS (main 1.16) and FBS 1.63 times high school (main 1.70). Real college games run 2.5 times high school's (Kerr et al. 2018: 32.1 and 12.7 injuries costing a day per 1,000 players a game). On main the order came from the man tackled alone, which falls with lighter, slower men; after, piles fall least from level to level (on the default seed FBS piles run 1.14 times the NFL's and high school's 1.09, 1.31 a play, as more men reach the ball). Not tuned: a share for one level would be a fudge. Next: measure how many men reach the ball at each level against real tackle counts, and whether a pile's weight should fall with the level's bodies (`docs/match-engine.md`, lane EG1). Same thread.
- **Fumbles go out of bounds less than half as often as real** (medium). 3.8% of NFL scrimmage fumbles (real 8.5%; after a catch 10.0% against 19.0%, designed runs 0.9% against 5.8%). The loose ball goes out wherever it reaches the line, but the engine's plays seldom end near the sideline (designed runs out of bounds 1.1% of the time, real 5.5%), so its fumbles come out far from it. It keeps the defense a little ahead on recoveries after a catch (58% against 49%). Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Build engine lane EG2 ball security").
- **Who comes up with a strip sack** (medium). The NFL's sack fumbles go to the defense 46% of the time (real 50%) and back to the passer 21% (15%), so they are lost 44% of the time (real 51%): a fifth of the 17% that lost fumbles are short of real (most of the rest is the sacks themselves, 1.37 a team game against 2.41, the pass rush of lane SF8 and the quarterback off script of EG7). Its catch fumbles are run back 44% of the time (real 31%) and its sacks' 22% (33%), where the totals are near real (31% and 28%). Fumbled snaps are lost 15% of the time (real 25%). Same thread.
- **A passer hit as he throws never fumbles** (low). The strip in his motion comes only on a sack (`sack_check`); the hit on the throw rolls nothing, so passes without a catch fumble 0.021 a team game (real 0.036), all of them interception returns. EG1 adds that hit for injuries. Same thread.
- **A quarterback's designed runs fumble a third of the real rate** (low). 0.66% of them over seven NFL seeds (15,385 runs), real 2.12% (nflverse 2021-25), where backs are at real (0.69%, real 0.70%). The tackle's fumble reads him like any carrier, by his Ball Security against the level's typical starting back's; why real passers fumble three times as often on their own runs (sneaks into a pile, option keeps, his Ball Security as a carrier) is not measured yet. At the engine's few designed quarterback runs (0.55 a team game, real 1.69; item 10, lane SF10) it misses under 0.01 fumbles a team game, and 0.025 at the real number of runs. Same thread.
- **A muffed punt is a roll** (medium). The kicking team gets 45-50% of muffs at the spot and nobody advances one (`game/kicking.rs`, `punt`), where a fumbled return now plays out live. Same thread; it goes with EG3's kick returns.
- **A fumbled return the return team downs in its own end zone is a touchback** (low). Real, a ball the return team put there itself is a safety. A kick's result holds no safety (`game/kicking.rs`, `run_return`), so the loose ball's safety is kept as a touchback. Rare. Same thread; it goes with EG3's kick returns.
- **College runs fumbles back for scores less often than real, and college and high school have no full real count** (low). The engine plays them on the NFL's per-chance rates read against each level (the level's typical hit, `hit_scale`, and its typical starting back). FBS against FBS in 2023 (cfbfastR's play text) lost 0.43 fumbles a team game and ran 0.044 back for touchdowns, 10% of its lost fumbles (the NFL's 7%); the engine loses 0.40 and scores 0.021, its defenders scooping whenever they are first to the ball (`LOOSE_RACE`; scooping in closer traffic scores more but loses fewer). The text holds 0.81 fumbles a team game with 53% lost, where the engine fumbles 0.95 and loses 42%; the text may leave out fumbles the offense falls on, so its total isn't a target. High school has no source. FCS and small colleges take FBS's typical hit, unmeasured (`match_check` plays neither). Same thread.
- **Synthetic players draw their flaws from 14-year-old habits, then get level habits** (low). `game/synthetic.rs` runs `craft::ensure` (whose leans read `p.tend`: patience, downhill, upfield, gunslinger, jump routes) before `Tendencies::at_level` replaces `p.tend`, so a change to age-14 centres redraws every synthetic roster's flaws and moves seed-tuned tests (2026-11-19: `a_first_half_sitter_comes_back_at_halftime`, `free_him_up_and_send_him_move_his_rushes`). Reading the level habits first would be the truer order but moves every synthetic league and every check, so it needs its own measure.
- **Tests built on `tests::prospects` take ids from the global counter** (low). `player.rs` `NEXT_ID`; none reads tendencies today (`fourteen_year_olds_spread_wide_around_the_middle` runs on fixed ids).

- **A ball off target costs nothing after the catch** (medium). Real yards after the catch fall 0.37 a catch in rain and 0.28 in a 15-19 mph wind (nflverse 2021-25), as receivers reach back or stop for a wet or blown ball; the engine's rise a little (5.06 to 5.18 in rain): placement reads only whether he catches it (`game/scrimmage.rs`, `catch_point`), and the cleaner throws are the ones caught. It moves every pass, not only the weather's, so it needs its own measure. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Fix DL rotation and weather size"); a slower first step after a wet catch was tried there and did nothing.
- **Footing is read nowhere** (low). Rain, snow and mud don't touch a man's burst or cuts. Cutting both sides' burst and cut 10% on a wet field raised yards after the catch by 0.5 a catch in this engine (real -0.37), because tacklers lose their closing burst more than a carrier loses his cut; footing needs the open-field tackle's angle read first. Same thread.
- **No edges inside on passing downs** (low). Real 4-3 teams line up 1.83 inside men a snap (nflverse snap counts 2023-24) as edges slide inside on third and long; the engine's fronts always field two tackles (three in a 3-4 base), so the second tackle plays 63% of snaps (real 54% on 4-3 teams) after the staff's rotation (item 29). An AI pass-rush package would close it. Same thread.
- **A rested tackle doesn't win back his backup's snaps** (medium). Since the inside line rotates by plan (engine gaps item 29), defenses give up 0.3 points a team game more at the NFL and FBS and 0.4 in high school (match_check over main ea85aad, seed 1): the backups play more and the starters' rest barely shows on the field. Real staffs rotate because a fresh rusher wins more; the energy cost of a long series on a big man is the place to look (`game/flow.rs`, the energy line; `game/sim.rs`, how energy reads). Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Fix DL rotation and weather size").
- **Edges' third and fourth men still play only on fatigue** (low). The inside line rotates by plan since item 29; edges keep bug 358's fatigue rotation, so the top two play 78% and 71% of snaps a game (real 67% and 45%, PFR labels, some off-ball linebackers among them). Bug 368's edge side. Same thread.
- **Heat is read nowhere** (low). A hot, humid game tires nobody faster (`Conditions.temperature` reads only kicks and cold). Item 30's note; same thread.

### The engine gaps audit (agreed)

Cain agreed to all 30 on 2026-11-17 ("agreed"). What gets built and in which lane is in `docs/design/engine-gaps.md` (lanes EG1-EG7, item 10 in SF10, items 29 and 30 as small fixes); each lane removes its entries here when it merges. The audit (thread "Find engine gaps beyond scheme fit") checked real NFL play-by-play (nflverse 2021-25) and the repo's college targets against `match_check` on main, each code claim checked twice. Numbers, sources and code lines are in the project folder at `afm/engine-gaps/report.md` (items 1-30).

- **The red zone plays loose** (high). NFL red zone completion 65.9% (real 53.0%), a touchdown on 26.5% of red zone throws (19.0%), 2.37 points a drive (2.00). Item 8.
- **Quarterbacks extend few plays** (high). The scramble drill is built but gets few passers out before the play is dead: 2.5% of throws come after 3.94 s (real 10%), sacks 5.5% of dropbacks (6.8%), scrambles 1.60 a team game (1.97). Item 9.
- **Few designed quarterback runs** (medium). 0.54 a team game (real 1.69); the test league's passers are typical, so SF10 measures it on world rosters. Item 10.
- **Long field goals made too often** (medium). NFL 55+ made 70.9% (real 59.7%) and tried twice as often; FBS 40-49 75.2% (65.2%), 50+ 65.9% (50.6%). Item 11.
- **Blocked kicks at a third of real, and never returned** (medium). Field goals 0.58% (real 1.89%), punts 0.18% (0.48%), extra points 0.26% (0.70%); a kick's block is a formula, not a tracked play, and real blocked punts go back for a touchdown a third of the time. Item 12.
- **Few long returns** (medium). Kickoff returns of 40+ 1.8% (real 5.0%), punt returns of 20+ 2.8% (8.0%). Item 13.
- **The kicking range ignores the weather** (low-medium). The engine tries 8% more kicks of 50+ in a 17 mph wind; real teams try 13% fewer in 15+ mph wind and 26% fewer at 32 F or colder. Item 14.
- **A half's last snaps are ordinary calls** (medium). No Hail Mary, and a tied team never kneels. Of the second quarter's snaps with 5 s or less, field goals are 31% (real 51%) and kneels 2% (18%); and it almost never spikes (0.1% of those late snaps, real 5.2%). The screens' win chance credits a shot no play throws. Item 15.
- **No laterals on the last play** (low). Item 16.
- **The clock before halftime** (low-medium). Real defenses call 1.35 timeouts a game in the last 4:00 of the second quarter; the staff's timeout needs the fourth quarter (`after_snap_clock`), and every offense hurries in the half's last 2:00 whatever its spot or score. Item 17.
- **Nobody protects a late lead** (medium). The carrier's sideline choice never reads the score or clock; real runs by the team ahead in the last 5:00 end out of bounds 2.3% of the time (5.8% earlier). Goes with S6.2. Item 18.
- **Two-point tries short** (low-medium). 5.0% of tries (real 9.8%), made at the real rate. Item 19.
- **Fourth-down sums mostly use the NFL's numbers** (low-medium). The expected points and the go habit are the level's, but the conversion chance reads yards to go only, a punt is always 41 net and a miss goes at the NFL's spot at every level (`fourth_down`, `fourth_ev`). Item 20.
- **AI staffs never fake a kick** (low). Real 0.036 fake punts a team game; no fake field goal exists. Item 21.
- **No untimed down or 10-second runoff** (low). Item 22.
- **Penalties mark the NFL's yards at every level** (medium). College defensive holding is 10 yards; in high school (the NFHS states; Texas plays college rules), pass interference, holding, face masks and roughness carry no automatic first down (only roughing the passer, kicker, holder or snapper do). Item 23.
- **Penalties near the goal lines** (low-medium). Defensive fouls halve only when they'd reach the goal line, from the line of scrimmage; a foul in the offense's own end zone is never a safety (real 12 of 65 safeties in 2021-25). Item 24.
- **Kick rules by level** (low). A missed field goal goes over at the NFL's spot and a kickoff out of bounds at the 40 at every level (college: the line of scrimmage, and a kickoff out of bounds by era from the kick). Item 25.
- **Ineligible man downfield called about four times too often** (medium, a bug). 0.383 a team game (real 0.106), all on RPOs; minor bug 406. Item 26.
- **Pass interference short** (medium). 0.352 a team game at 12.5 yards (real 0.507 at 15.8). Item 27.
- **Fouls missing or off** (low). The defense's too many men, illegal contact, illegal use of hands and live-ball offside don't exist; kickoffs out of bounds or short of the landing zone are played but never flagged; grounding 0.015 a team game (real 0.080); false starts and delay of game short, the formation group long, and illegal blocks in the back about ten times real on scrimmage plays. Item 28.

## Logic

- **A coach of yours can't see last season if it was played before you took the seat** (high). His unit's numbers and form (`claims::rooms::unit`, `points_of`) read only `w.seat_games`, the games kept since you arrived. In a new career every holdover's unit has no numbers and no good or poor read, so the voice gets nothing to analyse, the holdover meeting can't narrate his unit's year, and the keep or let go talk can't let you blame his unit (`takeover.rs`, `staffing.rs`). The world already keeps every team's season in the squads, games and players tallies. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Give coaches real depth when asked why"); fix proposed there.
- **A coach hired after last season reads as "one season" here** (medium). `rooms::unit` sets `years` to `(w.year - first_season + 1).max(1)`, so a man who hasn't coached a game here gets 1, and his card says he's been the coordinator "one season". Robert Leonard, hired after a 2-10 year, took the blame for it. Same thread.
- **July's payday under the paid-set offers is not measured yet** (medium). Since 2026-11-19 a school offers money only to recruits its July paid set takes, never under his place's revenue share, so a Power 4 class takes about $4-5M of a program's July money where it took $1.5-2M (seed 1, class of 2027; top classes $8.2-10.8M against real $6-9M+). The rest of the roster splits what is left (`roster_split`), so returning backups and maybe starters get less; nobody has read July 2027 against the College Sports Commission's medians and The NIL Standard. One `nil_scheme run <world> 2027 8` on main against the branch before it would read it. Thread "Pay only next year's paid recruits".
- **A paid two-star backup at a Power 4 school signs for what a three-star does** (low). The floor is his role's revenue share, read by place, not stars, so two-stars in the paid set signed for a median $113K against three-stars' $124K (seed 1). The College Sports Commission pays by role too, but a two-star at a Power 4 school is rarely a planned backup. Same thread.
- **A school's paid set fills from its room before the stage, not as it signs** (low). Every recruit a school bids on in one signing stage reads the same paid set (`PaidLine` from the room before the matching), so a Group of 5 class that signs several projected starters the same day can take more places than the set has; July then pays its deal holders first. Same thread.
- **A keep-him offer takes a revenue-share place first** (medium). July's paid set puts every deal holder ahead of starters (`roster_split`), a keep-him offer's holder too, though it is only a floor on his split; by January nearly every Group of 5 man holds one, so the set fills by place among holders. Signees no longer take places this way: since 2026-11-19 only recruits the paid set takes on merit get a deal (`PaidLine`). Found in the thread "Fix NIL pay gaps after the market fit".
- **A world saved before signees carried deals keeps no room for your class on its first year** (low). The roster split keeps room for a class to come from the share AI schools paid their arrivals last July (`NilBook::new_share`). The shipped seed 1 and seed 41 worlds signed their 2026 classes without deals, so July 2026 reads only transfers, and your desk's roster split for 2026-27 leaves little room for your class. The world rebuild fixes it. Same thread.
- **A position coach's year is only a growth read** (medium). His room's own numbers on the field (the depth counts of his players) never reach his talk. Same thread.
- **High school rooms name no captains** (low). `people::settle_room` runs only for college programs and NFL clubs, so a high school team has no captains to lead a players-only meeting, take the pregame talk or be promised a captaincy (`captain::open` refuses it there). Real high school teams name captains each season, by the coach or a vote. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Make captain promises count").
- **AI staffs never promise a captaincy** (low). `ai_promise.rs` makes only playing-time promises, so no AI coach keeps or breaks a captain promise and no AI room pays for one named over its vote. Same thread.
- **The promise plan setting has no switch on screen** (medium). The agenda sends each promise's `plan` (what keeps it and `off`), and `people_promise_plan` turns it off, but no screen in `app/ui` shows it, so a usage or captain promise can only be kept the default way. Same thread.
- **A coaching-change transfer window keeps no dates once it opens** (low). `World.coach_window` is cleared the day it opens, so a talk can name the window while it waits but not when it closes once it's open; a late window keeps its dates (`late_windows`). Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Make talks know the time of year").
- **Writing a blank value makes a person's kept part** (medium). Since memory diet lane M1, a person's college part and calendar rest exist only once written (`world/stored.rs`). A new loop that resets a field for everyone (`p.cal.x = None` over all people) quietly makes the part for a million high schoolers and gives back about 0.4 GB, with no failing test unless the tiny world's share check trips. `memory_season` prints the kept counts; a quick-CI check on a small world's high schoolers after a season would catch it. Lane M1 thread.
- **World files still write every blank field of every person** (low). About 120 field names per person, 986,000 of them high schoolers with nothing in most: skipping blank parts would make files and loads smaller, but it changes the shape the World Reader reads. Lane M1 thread.
- **Resident memory sits about 0.4 GB above the live heap in season** (medium). After the allocator hands back free pages, the seed 181 world holds 5.19 GB of live data but 5.6 GB resident at the first game (main: 6.44 and 6.43). Freed space is scattered across pages still in use. Worth measuring on the Mac, whose allocator differs, before any fix. Lane M1 thread.
- **A person's school, state and college indexes are 8-byte numbers** (low). About 2,100 places read them as `usize`; a typed index would let them be 2-4 bytes (about 0.03 GB). Lane M1 thread.
- **Retired pros' careers are kept whole in memory and read whole by the Data pages** (medium). Memory diet item 3 wanted alumni on disk or packed (about 0.13-0.23 GB at full size), but `lane_data/analytics.rs`, `careers.rs`, `finder.rs`, `lane_squadboards/arc.rs` and `body.rs` read a full `ProCareer` for every retired pro, so a finder or leaderboard page would unpack about 18,000 careers (about 2 seconds). A summary kept in memory (seasons' year, team, games, stat line, honors) for those pages, with the full career packed or on disk, would do it; it needs a design for what the pages read. Lane M2 thread.
- **Men who left football and retired coaches cannot be kept compact without moving the stores' prints** (low). Memory diet items 6 and 9 (about 0.13 and 0.10 GB): dropping any part of a `Stage::Done` person or a retired coach changes the people and coaches prints. Either a stored form that expands when written, or Cain's yes that those two prints may move while results stay the same. Lane M2 thread.
- **The open tallies' rows are running sums read while the season runs** (low). Memory diet item 10 (0.1 GB in September, 0.3 GB December to February): packing finished weeks means every Data page read merges the packed weeks back, so it needs a design for those reads first. Lane M2 thread.
