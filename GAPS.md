# Gaps

Gaps found while fixing bugs or building, so they carry over between threads (Cain, 2026-11-17). Each: what's missing, why it matters, impact. Remove an entry once it's built. Nothing here is agreed until Cain says yes.

## Engine

- **The engine's instructions read a freelance trust, not his trust in the coach** (medium). `GamePlayer.trust` is 0.5 for every player the world sends to a game, so `tendencies::obey_chance` never sees how much he believes his own coach; only the new bond part (`GamePlayer.bond`, `obey_trust`) reaches it. The built `Person::trust` could feed it directly. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Build coach and player bonds").
- **The press ranks only finished rooms** (low). `race::press_score` reads `Campus.level` and `since`, so a building that has broken ground adds nothing until it opens, and the Pecking order screen can't say "read as 10th since the groundbreaking" as board 5 draws it. Recruits' groundbreaking pull (facilities lane #281) is separate. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Build Capital screen CS5"); needs Cain's call on whether a building under way counts for the press.
- **A project's minutes don't come out of the room's week** (medium). Design item 8 says a project takes the position coach's one-on-one minutes, so the rest of the room gets a little less. Today the 60 minutes only set the project's pace (`development.rs`, `GrowthCtx::rate`); nobody else's individual time shrinks, for you or the AI. Same thread.

### The engine gaps audit (agreed)

Cain agreed to all 30 on 2026-11-17 ("agreed"). What gets built and in which lane is in `docs/design/engine-gaps.md` (lanes EG1-EG7, item 10 in SF10, items 29 and 30 as small fixes); each lane removes its entries here when it merges. The audit (thread "Find engine gaps beyond scheme fit") checked real NFL play-by-play (nflverse 2021-25) and the repo's college targets against `match_check` on main, each code claim checked twice. Numbers, sources and code lines are in the project folder at `afm/engine-gaps/report.md` (items 1-30).

- **Injuries only in tackles and sacks** (high). Blocks, the hit at the catch point and the hit on a throwing quarterback never roll one, so backs take 35% of game injuries (real about 8%) and offensive linemen 0.2% (real about 18%). Item 1.
- **No non-contact injuries** (high). About 70% of NFL ACL tears come without contact, and hamstrings are sprinting injuries; the engine draws every injury at a hit. Item 2.
- **Nobody comes back in the same game** (medium). A hurt man plays on hurt or is out; most real in-game injuries cost a few snaps, and a bruise sends about half its men out for good. Item 3.
- **Fumbles are dead where they're fallen on** (high). No fumble is ever returned (real 0.032 return touchdowns a team game, a third of the defense's touchdowns from scrimmage), a defensive recovery in the end zone is first and goal at the 1 instead of a touchdown, and none goes out of bounds (real 8.5%). Item 4.
- **No fumbled snaps** (high). Real 0.185 a team game, 16% of all fumbles; a bad shotgun snap only costs time (`run_snap`). Item 5.
- **Strip sacks at a third of real** (high). 4.1% of sacks fumble, real 12.4%. Item 6.
- **Backs fumble about twice as often as real** (medium). 1.4% of designed runs, real 0.75%. Item 7.
- **The red zone plays loose** (high). NFL red zone completion 65.9% (real 53.0%), a touchdown on 26.5% of red zone throws (19.0%), 2.37 points a drive (2.00). Item 8.
- **Quarterbacks extend few plays** (high). The scramble drill is built but gets few passers out before the play is dead: 2.5% of throws come after 3.94 s (real 10%), sacks 5.5% of dropbacks (6.8%), scrambles 1.60 a team game (1.97). Item 9.
- **Few designed quarterback runs** (medium). 0.54 a team game (real 1.69); the test league's passers are typical, so SF10 measures it on world rosters. Item 10.
- **Long field goals made too often** (medium). NFL 55+ made 70.9% (real 59.7%) and tried twice as often; FBS 40-49 75.2% (65.2%), 50+ 65.9% (50.6%). Item 11.
- **Blocked kicks at a third of real, and never returned** (medium). Field goals 0.58% (real 1.89%), punts 0.18% (0.48%), extra points 0.26% (0.70%); a kick's block is a formula, not a tracked play, and real blocked punts go back for a touchdown a third of the time. Item 12.
- **Few long returns** (medium). Kickoff returns of 40+ 1.8% (real 5.0%), punt returns of 20+ 2.8% (8.0%). Item 13.
- **The kicking range ignores the weather** (low-medium). The engine tries 8% more kicks of 50+ in a 17 mph wind; real teams try 13% fewer in 15+ mph wind and 26% fewer at 32 F or colder. Item 14.
- **A half's last snaps are ordinary calls** (medium). No Hail Mary, and a tied team never kneels. Of the second quarter's snaps with 5 s or less, field goals are 31% (real 51%) and kneels 2% (18%); and it almost never spikes (0.1% of those late snaps, real 5.2%). The screens' win chance credits a shot no play throws. Item 15.
- **No laterals on the last play** (low). Item 16.
- **The clock before halftime** (low-medium). Real defenses call 1.35 timeouts a game in the last 4:00 of the second quarter; the staff's timeout needs the fourth quarter (`after_snap_clock`), and every offense hurries in the half's last 2:00 whatever its spot or score. Item 17.
- **Nobody protects a late lead** (medium). The carrier's sideline choice never reads the score or clock; real runs by the team ahead in the last 5:00 end out of bounds 2.3% of the time (5.8% earlier). Goes with S6.2. Item 18.
- **Two-point tries short** (low-medium). 5.0% of tries (real 9.8%), made at the real rate. Item 19.
- **Fourth-down sums mostly use the NFL's numbers** (low-medium). The expected points and the go habit are the level's, but the conversion chance reads yards to go only, a punt is always 41 net and a miss goes at the NFL's spot at every level (`fourth_down`, `fourth_ev`). Item 20.
- **AI staffs never fake a kick** (low). Real 0.036 fake punts a team game; no fake field goal exists. Item 21.
- **No untimed down or 10-second runoff** (low). Item 22.
- **Penalties mark the NFL's yards at every level** (medium). College defensive holding is 10 yards; in high school (the NFHS states; Texas plays college rules), pass interference, holding, face masks and roughness carry no automatic first down (only roughing the passer, kicker, holder or snapper do). Item 23.
- **Penalties near the goal lines** (low-medium). Defensive fouls halve only when they'd reach the goal line, from the line of scrimmage; a foul in the offense's own end zone is never a safety (real 12 of 65 safeties in 2021-25). Item 24.
- **Kick rules by level** (low). A missed field goal goes over at the NFL's spot and a kickoff out of bounds at the 40 at every level (college: the line of scrimmage, and a kickoff out of bounds by era from the kick). Item 25.
- **Ineligible man downfield called about four times too often** (medium, a bug). 0.383 a team game (real 0.106), all on RPOs; minor bug 406. Item 26.
- **Pass interference short** (medium). 0.352 a team game at 12.5 yards (real 0.507 at 15.8). Item 27.
- **Fouls missing or off** (low). The defense's too many men, illegal contact, illegal use of hands and live-ball offside don't exist; kickoffs out of bounds or short of the landing zone are played but never flagged; grounding 0.015 a team game (real 0.080); false starts and delay of game short, the formation group long, and illegal blocks in the back about ten times real on scrimmage plays. Item 28.
- **Interior linemen rotate too little** (low-medium). The top two DTs play 78% of snaps (real 68% and 50%). Item 29.
- **Rain and wind a little small** (low). Rain costs 1.9 points a team (real 3.4), 17 mph wind 2.0 (2.8). Item 30.

## Logic

- **A coach of yours can't see last season if it was played before you took the seat** (high). His unit's numbers and form (`claims::rooms::unit`, `points_of`) read only `w.seat_games`, the games kept since you arrived. In a new career every holdover's unit has no numbers and no good or poor read, so the voice gets nothing to analyse, the holdover meeting can't narrate his unit's year, and the keep or let go talk can't let you blame his unit (`takeover.rs`, `staffing.rs`). The world already keeps every team's season in the squads, games and players tallies. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Give coaches real depth when asked why"); fix proposed there.
- **A coach hired after last season reads as "one season" here** (medium). `rooms::unit` sets `years` to `(w.year - first_season + 1).max(1)`, so a man who hasn't coached a game here gets 1, and his card says he's been the coordinator "one season". Robert Leonard, hired after a 2-10 year, took the blame for it. Same thread.
- **Your going rate is 0.6 of what an AI school pays the same player** (high). `front_office::college::splits` hands out 0.6 of the school's revenue share and of the collective's money (`NIL_ROSTER`, kept for your offers), while an AI school's July 1 pays its roster all of both (`recruiting::nil_payouts`, `NIL_ROSTER_TWO`). So the desk's "roster split" for a starter reads well under what the same man earns at a rival, and "under half the going rate" warnings fire late. Found 2026-11-17 (thread "Pay inherited players their market price"); not changed there.
- **A player's stay read doesn't see the deal you set for next year** (medium). `stay_read` weighs his NIL income (last July's, brand money included) and December's keep-him offer, not your desk deal, so a raise you give in the spring doesn't hold him in the spring window until July 1 pays it. It also counts his own brand money as pay. Same thread.
- **AI schools pay rotation players and backups the split, never their market price** (medium). Only starters get December's keep-him offer (`nil_bid::keep_offers`); everyone else shares what the fixed deals leave. A breakout rotation player a rival would pay more is underpaid on every AI roster. The world check proposed in the same thread measures it.
- **`nil_scheme` plays its seasons on the stand-in, which spreads play wider than the engine** (medium). Both its modes set `engine_from` to 2100 for speed, so every number read after the first season (market prices, keep-him offers, transfers' deals, July 2027's payday) prices play from stand-in games: on the seed 1 world Power 4 starters 10 or more points over their median went from 43% (July 2026, engine history) to 50% (July 2027), and the starting quarterbacks' market price from $1.99M to $2.67M. Lane A's 2027 fit ("non-QB starters 17-39% high") was read the same way. A run on the engine costs about 2 minutes a season more per level played. Found in the thread "Make inherited NIL deals keep market price".
- **Group of 5 signees are paid a few thousand dollars after the 2b refit** (low). Their offers read the school's whole budget × the spot's share × stars, so a Group of 5 four-star signs for a median $3K; reports put Group of 5 signees in the five figures when paid at all. Found in the thread "Make inherited NIL deals keep market price".
- **A position coach's year is only a growth read** (medium). His room's own numbers on the field (the depth counts of his players) never reach his talk. Same thread.
- **"You'll be a captain" is only a pride hit in a talk** (medium). The ear has no captaincy term (`usage`, `terms`), so the promise binds you to nothing, he can't judge it later, and the room never hears it; real captains are usually voted by the team, so a coach promising it is promising something he may not control. `takeover::captains_of` already names captains. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Make talks know the time of year").
- **A coaching-change transfer window keeps no dates once it opens** (low). `World.coach_window` is cleared the day it opens, so a talk can name the window while it waits but not when it closes once it's open; a late window keeps its dates (`late_windows`). Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Make talks know the time of year").
- **Brothers are not people to each other** (medium). `Family::older_brother_at` names a program, not a person, so a younger brother can't commit to the coach his brother was close to, and the bond's "his little brother commits to you" (`docs/design/coach-bonds.md`, section 7) isn't built. Same thread.
- **No bonds with strength coaches or NFL position coaches** (medium). Strength coaches see a player more than anyone in the off-season, and NFL clubs store no position coaches. They wait for support departments and stored NFL staffs. Same thread.
- **Your own effort in a bond is talks only** (low). `bonds::you_effort` is 1.0: what you do counts only as one-on-ones (a week of contact each) and your choices at the moments, not time you give him in your week (the design's funeral "a day of your week"). Same thread.
- **The returns are not built** (low). A former player's summer visits, his hall of fame asking you to present him, naming a son after you, and a hometown discount in NFL free agency beyond the built "guys follow you" (design section 7). Same thread.
- **A coach leaving doesn't shake a high-need kid's home stability** (low). The design's "loses stability (-0.5 x closeness)" isn't built: only his mood, a Left moment, a year guarded and a year of trouble risk. The stability field is his background, so it needs a call on whether a coach's leaving may change it. Same thread.
- **No "I'm not going anywhere" promise** (low). A close player hurt by a coach leaving is the price of the bond, but you can't promise to stay. The promises lane has no kind for it. Same thread.
- **Bonds are bigger in memory than the design said** (medium). On a quarter-scale world (seed 261, 4 years) the store holds about 965,000 bonds and 669,000 moments, about 38 MB, so roughly 150 MB at full scale against the design's 10-20 MB. Most rows are Just met pairs with coaches who never got close. Candidate for the memory diet: drop a Just met bond once the pair parts, cap moments sooner. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Build coach and player bonds", the world measure).
- **No word when a project clicks, stalls or is dropped** (medium). A click, a dead end your staff would see, or a project dropped because he moved to a position that doesn't work the skill (`progression::project_year`) sends no note to the inbox or the staff notes. Same thread ("Let me start development projects").
- **The knack clues in Reports** (medium). The progression design puts the knack's vague clues on the Reports sub-tab; only the one-word read on the projects block exists. Same thread.
- **Projects the staff before you set run until you end them** (low). The AI's dead-end rule (`ai_keeps`) doesn't run on your team, so a holdover project keeps costing his other technique a quarter of its growth. The screens mark it "set before you", but no note tells you. Same thread.

- **Few NFL players re-sign before free agency** (medium). Main re-signs about 23% of expiring veterans before the market opens (91 of 395 on seed 1, 2026); NG4 lowers it to 21% (83 of 394), since a club that values him well under what other clubs would pay no longer gets a free 18% off. Real clubs keep more of their own: their offers before free agency read the same `make_offer` as a stranger's, with no premium for knowing the man. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Make negotiation respond to arguments").
- **Good veterans left unsigned at cutdown** (medium). 71 players rated 78+ on main (80 with NG4), most 30 or older. The market's first offers open at 88% of his ideal (`make_offer`, `climb`), and once clubs fill their room nobody bids on a capable 31-year-old. Same thread.
- **The guarantee floor is still a flat cliff** (low). `raw_score` caps any offer at 0.84 when the guarantee is under 75% of his ask and his security goal is over half, whoever he is. Item 1 replaced only the money cliff. Same thread.
- **No pro trusts an AI head coach** (low). One trust (item 2) at an AI club is his trust in the club alone; only the bond part reads the AI head coach. Same thread.

- **A college player reads your points at a flat 0.5** (low). `points::skill` gives an agent's skill to a pro's side, but a college player answering for himself, his family decider or his handler all read a cherry-pick at a middling 0.5, whoever they are. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Make negotiation respond to arguments").
- **A GM hears no true points** (low). Stats count in your college and pro players' money talks (and with a pro's agent), and film and depth count with a recruit; a stat said to another club's GM in a trade talk counts nothing. Same thread.

- **A recruit's hard truth doesn't cost your case a playing-time point** (low). Item 7 says your case loses one when you tell a recruit he'd be behind; only his trust moves (`points::recruit_land`). The case's playing-time point already reads the depth chart, so the truth told changes nothing there. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Make negotiation respond to arguments").
- **Points are found by words** (low). A film or depth point to a recruit and a stat point to a player are matched on the words of the ear's claim (`points::recruit_read`, `stat_for`), so a point said in other words counts nothing. Same thread.
- **A help promise's "new man" is this league year's** (low). `talks::help_kept` reads `ProCareer::new_team` and this year's first two rounds, not the day you made the promise, so a man signed before you said it counts. Same thread.

- **A season's tape barely moves a college price** (medium). NG1's season read adds 0.3 x his tape, but the tape is shrunk for credibility as growth shrinks it, so on seed 1's 2026 season 90% of players move less than 0.13 points (under half a percent of price) and only 16 of 47,659 move more than half a point. A season of interceptions still costs little in the portal. A stronger read (less shrink for the market than for growth, or the stat line itself) needs Cain's call. Found 2026-11-18 (thread "Make negotiation respond to arguments").
- **The rival's race shows only the signs the engine records** (medium). A wavering commit's signs are the sign-in sheet, his visits and early offers (`race::watch`). Board 6 also draws "he heard about him first" (the order the news went, today on the lead card only), a kid's post at a rival's camp and what a person told you as dated, numbered signs of their own. Without them a race with a quiet rival reads thin. Found 2026-11-18 (lane S6).
- **No coordinator's pick on a rival's claim** (low). Board 6 marks one answer "Coley's pick", but no code gives the coordinator an opinion on a race answer; the staff meeting (lane G10) is where it would come from. Favors are shipped off (lane G11b), so no answer spends one. Found 2026-11-18 (lane S6).
- **A rival's page shows only kids of yours who waver** (low). Races a rival runs for kids you only recruit (no commit yet) aren't stored: `Race` keeps wavers and claims, so his page can't list them. The kid's race lives in `front_office::college::race_at`. Found 2026-11-18 (lane S6).

## UI

- **The Pecking order shows your conference only** (low). A state rival outside the conference has its rumors in "What your donors hear" but no row, and the table never ranks you against your state. Found in the same thread (CS5); the press order is built per conference (CP31).
- **No pecking order for high school or NFL seats** (medium). The tab row reads "Your seat" for them (CS6), but nothing ranks a district's or an owner's rooms. Same thread.
- **No project column on the Squad's Development view** (low). You see who is on a project only on the Training screen's room count or each player's own tab. Same thread ("Let me start development projects").
