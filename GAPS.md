# Gaps

Gaps found while fixing bugs or building, so they carry over between threads (Cain, 2026-11-17). Each: what's missing, why it matters, impact. Remove an entry once it's built. Nothing here is agreed until Cain says yes.

## Engine

- **Screens line up under center too often, and play action throws too deep** (low-medium). Screens are drawn after the look: 24.0% under center on the NFL test league (real 18.7%), where a drop from the gun is now at real. Play action's air yards an attempt are 12.5 (real 9.2) and 5.7% of its throws are behind the line (real 14.8%), with the boot still below its real share. Minor bugs 410 and 411, 2026-11-18; the screens belong in `calling.rs`, `concept_weights` (a screen from under center weighs more).
- **NFL sacks are a fifth short of real** (medium). After lane EG7 (the quarterback off script, 2026-11-22; NFL test league, 2,000 games on seed 1) passers take 1.89 sacks a team game (real 2.41; main 1.60), on 5.2% of dropbacks (6.8%), at 19.7% pressure (PFR 22.2%) and 0.257 sacks a pressure (0.283); FBS 1.92 (cfbfastR 2.00). The feel for a rusher he can't see and his hot are built; what is left is time: he still gets the ball out inside 2.5 s on 68% of throws (real 50%) and pressure comes at 2.42 s (real 2.9), as receivers come open early (man coverage, below), so few plays last long enough for a coverage sack. With the feel alone (before his hot) sacks were at real, but 31% of them came from the man the protection left free. Found in lane EG7.
- **Pocket Presence pays little** (low-medium). On lane EG7's `fit_check` (2026-11-22, the audit's typical teams) the passer's Pocket Presence +15 takes 0.36 points off the NFL sack rate (main 0.53) and 0.38 at FBS (0.51), and its points fall inside the noise (+0.06 a game at the NFL, main +0.34; FBS +0.19, main +0.13), where his overall weighs it 2.0 (`docs/reference/how-it-works/ratings.md`). It reads the feel for a rusher he can't see (10% more a 10 points, `FEEL_SLOPE`, proposed) and the escape at the sack; on main it also found his hot, which his Football IQ and Play Recognition read now. Real pocket presence is also the step-up and the slide that buy time against a rusher he can see, which the engine reads only as the slide's size. Found in lane EG7.
- **Linebackers' blitzes get home half as often as real** (medium). Off-ball linebackers get 0.067 pressures a blitz on the NFL test league (1,000 games) and the typical team's 0.058 and 0.068 at the NFL and 0.054 and 0.066 at FBS (`fit_check`, West Coast and man pressure; real 0.140, PFR 2018-24). A lineman picks him up on 77% of his blitzes and 4.1% of those become pressure, a back on 11% (7.4%), and he comes free on 11.5% (23.5%). Since SF8 Blitzing's size is about right (a Mike's +15 gets 41% and 61% more a blitz at the NFL); the level isn't. Found 2026-11-20 (thread "Build engine lane SF8").
- **The Will's blitz gets home a quarter as often as the Mike's** (medium). On the NFL test league the Mike gets 0.099 pressures a blitz, the Sam 0.098 and the Will 0.029 (the typical team's Will 0.022 and 0.028, its Mike 0.097 and 0.109); real Mikes, Sams and Wills pressure about as often (0.74%, 0.77% and 0.72% of snaps, `lb_blitzer`'s note). A lineman picks up 85% of the Will's blitzes (the Mike's 72%) and 1.2% of those become pressure (the Mike's 7.5%); why was not looked into. Same thread.
- **A defensive back's rush almost never gets home** (medium). On the NFL test league corners, nickels and safeties rush 3.67 times a team game and get 0.016 pressures a rush; three in four of those rushes are never blocked and get home 1.7% of the time (a linebacker unblocked 23.5%), a free safety's 0.5%. Where they start and when they arrive was not looked into. So Blitzing +15 on a nickel or a strong safety moves his pressures a blitz only 0.004 to 0.014. Same thread.
- **Edges get fewer pressures than tackles** (medium). The typical team's first edge gets 10.2 pressures a season at the NFL and 8.1 at FBS against its first tackle's 17.4 and 13.5 (`fit_check`, West Coast), though he wins as many reps (7.6 a hundred against 6.8 at the NFL): 27% to 34% of an edge's wins become his pressure, 50% to 53% of a tackle's. On the NFL test league an edge's bull rush win comes off 5.3 yards from the passer (a tackle's 4.5) and his wins come at about 2.0 s (a tackle's 1.6 to 1.7 s), with about 0.8 s left before the throw. Real edges get more pressures than tackles. Same thread.
- **At FBS a quick tackle's Finesse Rush adds sacks but few pressures** (low). The first tackle's Finesse Rush +15 adds 0.84 sacks a season and 0.5 pressures (not past the noise; NFL 2.2 and 3.5), on 2.1 more wins a hundred reps (NFL 3.2); why fewer of his wins come at FBS was not looked into. Same thread.
- **Safeties and linebackers end the wrong runs** (medium). After lane SF9b (4,000 NFL games on seed 11, 2026-11-21) safeties end 43.3% of the backs' 5-9 yard runs (real 23.3%, nflverse 2024) and 56.4% of the 10-19 (46.8%); linebackers end 20.5% of the 5-9 (26.2%) and 7.0% of the 10-19 (15.8%) but 12.2% of the 20+ (7.2%), and corners 9.9% of the 5-9 (15.1%). The 5-9 yard runs are 28.2% of carries (24.5%). The alley safety still meets most runs that get past the line, and linebackers flowing to the ball arrive late on the middle runs and run down too many long ones from behind. Found in lane SF9b (thread "Fix open-field tackles on long runs").

- **Few passers get out of the pocket, and few scramble** (high). Lane EG7 (2026-11-22) built the feel, his hot, the call at the end of his reads, the drill from the break-out with its own clock, and plaster, but on the NFL test league only 5.3% of dropbacks end out of the pocket (real 18.8%, FTN charting 2023-25; main 6.3%), 3.6% pressured (10.7%), so the targets that ride on it stay short: 2.7% of throws after 3.94 s (real 10%), time to throw 2.49 s (NGS 2.71), scrambles 0.91 a team game (1.97; main 1.31) and throwaways 0.7% of throws (4.1%; intentional grounding with them). Main's scrambles came in good part from passers fleeing a free man they found late; with his hot known he throws hot, as real passers do, and what is left are the scrambles from coverage that holds and pockets that cave. Three causes, each traced in the lane: the escape (a passer who leaves a caving pocket from a standstill is caught: escapes tried in the lane were sacked on 20% to 34% of those dropbacks, real 15% of pressured passers out of the pocket, since the sack check reads his elusiveness only at the tackle, not a step-up or a spin against the first man), the boot (1.2% of dropbacks; real boots and rollouts are a good part of the share out of the pocket, the calling's), and receivers open early (above), so few reads run out. Out of the pocket his throws are easier than real: pressured and out of it, 68% complete for 8.5 yards an attempt (real 52% and 4.95), where real throws on the run are rushed and off platform. Near the goal it shows most: 1.0% of throws inside the 5 are thrown away (real 7.8%, FTN charting 2022-25) and 0.7% from 6 to 10 (7.6%). Item 9, lane EG7.
- **The analysts' fourth-down model counts a conversion near the goal as a touchdown** (low). `decide::Model::fourth_at` scores a converted fourth down as a touchdown whenever the band's mean gain on converted plays reaches the goal (17.1 yards for 7-10 to go at the NFL), so going is overstated near the goal line: on 4th and 9 at their 12 early in a tied game the model sits within a hair of the kick, and a refit over lane EG4 flipped it to go (nfl4th kicks; `tests::decide` pins it), so the tables were kept; lane SF10's refit (2026-11-21) keeps the kick there at the NFL and FBS. A converted play could end at its gain, capped at the goal, or in the end zone by the gains' spread (proposed, not confirmed). Found in lane EG4, 2026-11-19.
- **A half's last 5 s take one snap** (low-medium). Real teams often fit two (a quick throw at the end zone, then the kick). Of the pool's second-quarter snaps (5 s or less) the engine kicks 35% (real 51%) and kneels 51% (18%); the pool is 0.47 snaps a game (real 0.74) and 0.9% are spikes (8.5%). Items 15 and 17, left by lane EG4 (2026-11-19, over lane EG3).

- **Few plays reach the sideline** (medium). ## Logic

- **A recruit's leverage reaches only his tone, a sting and a dodge** (medium). Since 2026-11-22 a kid a clear step below you opens eager and takes a hard truth or an unanswered question at half (`pitch::reach`, `recruit::EAGER_EAR`). Nothing else reads it: a coveted recruit with better offers takes a sting or a dodge at full, not more; his patience and heat (`feel`) and the read on your points ignore it; a step measured on program prestige alone misses a kid whose dream school is smaller. Found in thread "Fix recruit call reasoning on accuracy".
- **A coach's answer that goes at a recruit's question sideways counts as a dodge** (low). Asked "Were your QB guys good enough against the best to get noticed?", a line about how you'd develop him is read as not answering (`EAR_RULES`, dodged): the rule asks for an answer to the question asked. A real kid would hear part of an answer in it. Same thread.
- **A guarded recruit hides his wants from the chips but tells them in a typed talk** (medium). In the chips one recruit in four keeps his wants close (`pitch::guarded`) and a money-minded one may dress them up as development (`pitch::dresser`); in a typed talk a question on his wants or his role is answered from his true wants on N1's openness odds alone (`knowing::wants_known`, `telling::tell`), with neither rule. So the same kid tells you more by typing than by the chips, and your file now takes what he says (2026-11-22). Found in thread "Fix recruit call contradictions".
- **Five copies of a high school class in words** (low). `front_office::school::grade`, `lane_find::college::class_words`, `lane_scouting::class_words`, `ground::leads::class_word` and `lane_hunt::class_words` each work out a high schooler's grade, two on the school year's first day and two on `dates.season`; the talks' copy read the calendar year and called every senior a junior from August to December (fixed 2026-11-22). - **An AI staff over its limit at camp cuts more than it tells in winter** (low). On the small world, staff urgency adds about one FBS entrant a program a cycle, all of it from camp trims (8.3% of FBS entrants are cut to the limit). The cap on winter pushes (`TOLD_SHARE`, a quarter of the expected flow) holds the told men inside the flow, so the rest of an over-full roster waits for camp. Found 2026-11-21 (thread "Build staff urgency to move on").
