# Keys, most specific first:
#   sl.<kind>.<beat>.<extra>, sl.<family>.<beat>.<extra>, sl.any.<beat>.<extra>,
#   sl.<kind>.<beat>, sl.<family>.<beat>, sl.any.<beat>
#   endings: sl.<kind>.end.<ending>, sl.<kind>.end, sl.<family>.end, sl.any.end
#   legacy and chapters: sl.<kind>.legacy.<ending>, sl.<kind>.legacy, sl.<family>.legacy
#                        sl.<kind>.chapter.<ending>, sl.<kind>.chapter, sl.<family>.chapter

#   jab, post (.paper before 2008), quote (.boss .ask .spin .spin_straight), peak,
#   memo (.up .down .even .gap: your staff, in private), fizzle, end, legacy, chapter,
#   callback.memento (a shelf piece).
# Placeholders: {team} {city} {state} {other} {other_short} {lead} {lead_last} {lead_first}
#   {lead_pos} {rival} {rival_last} {rival_first} {rival_pos} {coach} {coach_last} {boss}
#   {boss_title} {record} {weeks} {nth_week} {since} {question} {writer} {outlet} {season}
#   {year} {word} and the kind's own: {money} {through} {made} {tried} {stat} {mark}
