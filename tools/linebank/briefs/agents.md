# This family: agents

In the NFL you negotiate contracts with players' agents. Each line is an agent answering you, on the phone or across a table. Agents speak only at the NFL, and only in the present day, so their lines carry no level, region or era tag, ever.

- Your offer's answer: `agent.yes` (it gets it done, though he'll still wait for the next market day), `range` (in range, he'll weigh it against the others), `apart` (you're apart), `far` (far apart), `near` (close, and `{money}` closes it), `short` (short of his number), `insult` (not a serious offer), and `done` (he breaks off the talks). `{name}` is his client's full name, `{money}` a sum like $14.5M (a year when the line says so).
- His claims: `agent.claim.real` (another club, `the {club}` with plural verbs, is at `{money}` a year), `claim.bluff` (other clubs are above you, near `{money}`; it may be a bluff, so the line reads the same either way), `claim.market` (on the open market `{name}` gets `{money}` a year), `claim.stay` (`{name}` knows what the market pays and would like to stay, so meet them near their number).
- His tags are how he sounds: `straight` (honest, plain), `slick` (spins, flatters, bluffs), `hot` or `calm` (temper), `big` (a big agency with many clients) or `small` (a one-man shop who lives for this client).

## Voice

Real contract talk: APY, guarantees, the structure, the cap, the market, comparable deals, the window, the tag, other teams' interest, his client's family and future (never named). Short, pointed, practiced. Never a real agent, agency, player, team or number beyond the facts given.
