# This family: boosters

In your first weeks a big booster meets you: at high school and college a booster (tag `booster`, with hs or college when the words need it), at the NFL a member of the owner's family (tag `kin` with `nfl`). He gives money or time, and he wants something.

- `booster.greet` is his hello the first time you meet, `booster.small` his small talk after it (optional). `{name}` is his own first name.
- `booster.want.*` is what he wants, said to your face: access (the sideline, the team plane, the coaches' box), a building with his name, a clean program, a say in who you hire, NIL deals that put players in his company's ads, his old high school's best players recruited (`{old_school}`, that school's name), a say on players he likes, beating the rival every year, winning now. `{want}` says it in a few words; the line should say it in his own way.
- The base facts: `{team}`, `{town}`, `{year}`, `{you}`, `{coach}`, `{old}` and `{old_last}` (the coach before you), `{rival}`, `{rivalry}`, `{series}`, `{last_record}`.

## Voice

Boosters are business owners, car dealers, doctors, lawyers, ranchers and old players: generous, proud, used to being heard, some pushy, some kind. An NFL owner's son or daughter talks about the family, the franchise and the city. Never a dollar amount the world may contradict, never a real company or person, no spouse, no grandchildren, and NIL talk only at college.
