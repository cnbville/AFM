# This family: the room

The everyday life of your program off the field: how players take your team rules, the parents who call, and your first weeks in a new job.

- `rules.react.fair`, `harsh` and `soft` are a player's reaction when you've handled a team rule (being late, a missed class or meeting, a fight, a curfew): when he thinks you were fair, harsh or soft. He says it to you. `{coach}` is you. A high school or college player stays respectful even when he thinks it was harsh; talking back carries `brash` (an NFL ego or temper, or a college player with both who doesn't trust you yet; never `hs`). Never name the rule or the punishment: the part doesn't know them.
- `parents.call.open` and `parents.call.point` are a loud or online parent calling you because their boy, `{name}`, is unhappy with his role and playing time: the opening, then an optional second line with their point. The game shows the caller as his father, mother, grandmother or guardian, so the line never says which, and says "my boy", "our son" or "him" at most. It happens at high school and college. Facts: `{name}`, `{full}` (his full name), `{coach}`, `{you}`, `{team}`, `{friend}` (a teammate's first name).
- `parents.playing_time` is a high school father at the parents' meeting in your first weeks who wants his son to play more ("my son" is fine).
- `takeover.meeting.you_open` is you, opening your first team meeting in a new job; `{count}` is how many players are in the room. Your words, in your own voice, at your level.
- `takeover.meeting.react_*` is a player's reaction to that meeting, by your message: compete (every job is open), honour (you honour what was built), winnow (we win now, the standard is a title), purge (some of you won't be here), plain (who you are, how you work, then practice).
- `takeover.leader.*` is one of the team's leaders meeting you: close to the old coach, an ego, steady, or waiting to see.
- `takeover.holdover.pitch` and `detail` are an assistant from the old staff, `{role}`, making his case to stay on your staff, then one more detail about his work.
- `takeover.oldcoach.greet` is, at a high school, the coach before you, still at the school, greeting you.
- The base facts: `{team}`, `{town}`, `{year}`, `{you}`, `{coach}`, `{old}` and `{old_last}` (the coach before you; never in the old coach's own greeting), `{rival}`, `{rivalry}`, `{series}`, `{last_record}`.

## Voice

A team in transition: guarded, hopeful, loyal to the old coach, testing the new one. Parents are protective and convinced; they mean well or don't. Old assistants are proud of their work and need the job. Keep the levels' worlds apart: high school parents, teachers and Friday nights; college recruiting, scholarships and the portal; NFL contracts, the 53 and the front office.
