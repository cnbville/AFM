Photo face proof

The same three players as the GAN proof, now drawn by a current photo model as media-day headshots at 17, 22, 28 and 34, in that year's team jersey. The age-22 photo is drawn from a text description (heritage, position, height, weight, beard). Every other age is drawn from the same description for that year plus a face lock that copies his face from the age-22 photo. Same person compares each photo with the one at 22 using the same face recognition model as the GAN proof: 1.00 is identical, and about 0.4 and above usually counts as the same person. Min is the measured time to draw that one photo. The 3D heads (style E) are on the face styles page, since they turn in 3D and can't be pasted in as photos.

How it was made

Photo model: RealVisXL V5.0 Lightning, a photoreal fine-tune of Stable Diffusion XL that needs only 6 steps, at 896 by 1152 pixels. Face lock: InstantID (its face adapter at 0.65 strength, its face-landmark guide at 0.5, prompt guidance 1.5), fed the face reading of the age-22 photo. PhotoMaker V2 was tried first as the face lock and failed: same-person scores of 0.09 to 0.15, a different man every time. Jersey lettering and numbers are garbled, as agreed; they would be put on top later. Code: the "photo model" scripts in the faces proof folder.
